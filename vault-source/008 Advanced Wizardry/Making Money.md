# Making Money
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


A Wizard has unusually high gold demand:

- Spell copying
- Scrolls
- Components
- Crafting
- Planar Binding
- Simulacrum
- Clone
- Bastion projects
- Magical infrastructure

The 2024 PHB supplies standard spellcasting-service prices, but not a guaranteed stream of paying customers. Separate **explicit Bastion trade returns**, **listed service prices**, and **DM-dependent sales demand**. See [[Wizard Services#Standard service prices]].

## Reliable Sources

### Adventuring

Still the most reliable source of large one-time wealth:

- Coin
- Gems
- Art objects
- Magic items
- Captured spellbooks
- Scrolls
- Valuable information

### Bastion Income

If the campaign uses Bastions, productive facilities can generate value while you adventure.

See [[Bastion Crafting]] for facility outputs, investment, and trade-cycle timing. Remaining away without magical communication normally means a Maintain order rather than continuing every productive order automatically; see [[Hirelings#Remote orders and ongoing work]].

## Crafting for Sale

Possible products:

- Spell Scrolls
- Common / Uncommon magic items
- Mundane crafted goods
- Alchemical products

Profit depends on:

- Buyer availability
- Sale price
- Crafting time
- Material cost
- Tool access

Do not assume every crafted magic item can be instantly sold at listed value.

## Spellcasting Services

See [[Wizard Services]].

Strong commercial services include:

- Identification
- Communication
- Divination
- Fabrication
- Teleportation
- Magical security
- Planar travel

Price should reflect:

**component cost + time + risk + spell-slot opportunity cost + rarity of the service**

## Fabricate

Fabricate can convert raw materials into finished goods when the spell and tool-proficiency requirements are met.

It can be economically powerful, but infinite-arbitrage assumptions fail if the world lacks:

- Buyers
- Raw materials
- Demand
- Legal permission
- Market capacity

Treat it as legitimate production, not automatic infinite money.

## Information for Profit

A Wizard can sell:

- Research
- Identification
- Historical / magical expertise
- Divination
- Scrying
- Locate-object services
- Magical investigation

These often require less material investment than crafting.

## High-Level Markets

High-level services can be worth enormous amounts in the right setting:

- Teleport
- Plane Shift
- Magical stronghold security
- Long-range communication
- Rare divination
- Specialized crafting
- Planar expertise

The hard part becomes finding clients who both **need the service and can afford it**.

## Best Principle

> Use magic to solve expensive problems for people who already have money.

Do not spend a 1,000 gp component to earn 200 gp.
