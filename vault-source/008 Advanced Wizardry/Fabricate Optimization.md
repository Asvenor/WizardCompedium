# Fabricate Optimization
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


> **Fabricate converts raw materials into finished mundane objects in 10 minutes.**
>
> Its real optimization value is not simply "making money." It converts **materials + tool proficiency + a 4th-level spell slot** into rapid manufacturing, infrastructure, equipment, and problem-solving.

---

# Core Rules

| Property | Fabricate |
|---|---|
| **Spell Level** | 4th |
| **Casting Time** | 10 minutes |
| **Range** | 120 feet |
| **Duration** | Instantaneous |
| **Concentration** | Required during its 10-minute casting; no ongoing Concentration after completion |
| **Primary Function** | Convert raw materials into products made from those materials |

## What Fabricate Can Do

Fabricate converts raw materials into finished products made from the **same material**.

Examples include:

- Trees → wooden bridge
- Hemp → rope
- Flax or wool → clothing
- Metal → metal objects
- Stone → stone objects

The quality of the finished product depends on the quality of the raw materials.

You must have a **sufficient quantity of appropriate raw material** available.

---

# Size Limits

The normal maximum size is:

**Large or smaller**

The spell defines this as fitting within a **10-foot Cube or eight connected 5-foot Cubes**. The connected-cube alternative can matter for a narrow bridge; Large is not permission for an arbitrarily long structure.

provided enough raw material is available.

However, if working with:

- Metal
- Stone
- Other mineral substances

the finished product is limited to:

**Medium or smaller**

For this spell, that means fitting within a **5-foot Cube**. The raw materials must be visible and within 120 feet when selected.

This restriction matters considerably for large-scale construction.

### Practical Consequence

Fabricate is excellent for:

- Doors
- Barricades
- Furniture
- Platforms
- Containers
- Equipment
- Components
- Small structures

but it cannot simply produce enormous metal or stone structures with one casting.

---

# Hard Limitations

Fabricate cannot:

- Create creatures
- Create magic items
- Create something from nothing
- Convert one material into a different material
- Ignore the requirement for sufficient raw material
- Ignore its size restrictions
- Automatically provide craftsmanship you lack
- Treat a creature as an automatically valid supply of raw material or turn the spell into an instant-kill attack

The spell reshapes existing material.

It does **not** transmute material.

The 2024 text explicitly forbids **creating creatures or magic items**. It does not repeat the older edition's separate sentence about transmuting them. Whether a proposed input actually counts as raw material is still a source-text/DM question; the spell supplies no hostile-creature targeting, damage, or saving-throw procedure.

**Iron remains iron.**

**Wood remains wood.**

**Stone remains stone.**

---

# Tool Proficiency

Tool proficiency is one of the most important optimization components of Fabricate.

However:

> **Fabricate does not require tool proficiency for every finished object.**

The restriction applies when creating something that normally requires a **high degree of craftsmanship**.

The spell specifically gives examples such as:

- Weapons
- Armor

For those objects, you must have proficiency with the appropriate type of **Artisan's Tools**.

---

# High-Value Tool Proficiencies

| Tool | Potential Fabricate Uses |
|---|---|
| **Smith's Tools** | Weapons, armor, sophisticated metalwork, metal components |
| **Carpenter's Tools** | Skilled wooden construction, furniture, structural components |
| **Mason's Tools** | Skilled stonework, masonry, defensive construction |
| **Woodcarver's Tools** | Detailed wooden objects, wooden weapons and components |
| **Weaver's Tools** | Clothing, textiles, canvas, finished fabric products |
| **Leatherworker's Tools** | Leather armor, containers, clothing, finished leather goods |
| **Glassblower's Tools** | Bottles, vessels, glass objects and specialized glasswork |
| **Jeweler's Tools** | Jewelry, gems, precious-metal objects and precision work |
| **Potter's Tools** | Ceramic containers and finished ceramic objects |

The exact proficiency required should follow the normal tool associated with creating the object.

If no obvious tool exists, the DM determines what proficiency is appropriate.

# Fabricate vs. Normal Crafting

This distinction is important under the **2024 crafting rules**.

## Normal Mundane Crafting

Normal crafting generally requires:

- Appropriate tool
- Proficiency with that tool
- Appropriate raw materials
- Raw materials worth **half the item's purchase price**
- Significant crafting time

Crafting progresses according to the normal crafting rules.

## Fabricate

Fabricate instead has its own spell procedure:

**Raw materials**

↓

**10-minute casting**

↓

**Finished mundane object**

The spell requires:

- Appropriate raw material
- Sufficient quantity
- Appropriate tool proficiency when high craftsmanship is required

### The Major Advantage

Fabricate can compress an enormous amount of **production time** into a single 10-minute casting.

This is the spell's most reliable economic and logistical advantage.

---

# Fabricate Economics

Fabricate can potentially be used for rapid mundane manufacturing.

Possible production includes:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Tools
- Jewelry
- Furniture
- Clothing
- Containers
- Construction components
- Trade goods
- Replacement equipment

However, there is an important distinction between:

**Production efficiency**

and

**Infinite-money exploit**

Fabricate unquestionably provides extraordinary production speed.

The profitability is less absolute.

---

## Raw-Material Ambiguity

The spell requires:

> A sufficient quantity of appropriate raw material.

It does not independently specify a GP value for those materials.

The normal 2024 crafting system has its own raw-material cost rules.

Exactly how those rules interact with Fabricate can therefore depend on the campaign's interpretation.

> [!warning] DM-Dependent Economics
> Do not automatically assume:
>
> **50% market-value raw materials → Fabricate → 100% market-value finished item → guaranteed 50% profit**
>
> unless the campaign explicitly uses that interpretation.

Even if allowed mechanically, actual profit still depends on:

- Raw-material prices
- Material availability
- Buyers
- Demand
- Selling prices
- Local economy
- Transportation
- Legality
- Competition

# Economic Optimization

The safest economic use of Fabricate is not necessarily mass-producing goods for sale.

It is often more valuable to **avoid paying someone else to manufacture something**.

### Example

Instead of:

**Buy finished equipment**

you may be able to:

**Acquire raw materials → Fabricate finished equipment**

This can provide value even when selling fabricated products is restricted or economically unrealistic.

### High-Value Economic Uses

- Manufacture your own equipment
- Manufacture party equipment
- Process valuable recovered materials
- Produce goods where skilled labor is unavailable
- Manufacture urgently needed items
- Convert bulky materials into more useful forms
- Produce standardized goods rapidly
- Support Bastion operations
- Supply hirelings or followers
- Reduce dependence on settlements

# Adventuring Uses

Fabricate is much more than an economic spell.

Its 10-minute casting time allows a prepared Wizard to manufacture solutions during an adventure.

## Battlefield Preparation

Before combat, potentially create:

- Barricades
- Cover
- Defensive walls or partitions
- Doors
- Platforms
- Obstacles
- Chokepoint structures

The exact object must still obey the spell's material and size restrictions.

---

## Bridges & Traversal

The spell explicitly supports the concept of converting trees into a wooden bridge.

This makes Fabricate useful for:

- Crossing gaps
- Crossing rivers
- Creating platforms
- Building ramps
- Creating walkways
- Improving difficult terrain

When sufficient material exists, environmental obstacles can become manufacturing problems.

---

# Containers & Transportation

Fabricate can rapidly create mundane storage and transportation infrastructure.

Examples:

- Crates
- Boxes
- Barrels
- Chests
- Frames
- Shelving
- Carts or components where appropriate
- Specialized containers

This becomes particularly useful for a Wizard carrying:

- Spellbooks
- Material components
- Crafting supplies
- Treasure
- Corpses
- Summoning materials
- Laboratory equipment

---

# Restraints & Security

With appropriate materials and craftsmanship, Fabricate may be useful for creating:

- Chains
- Manacles
- Bars
- Doors
- Locks or lock components
- Cages
- Reinforced containers
- Barricades

This can support:

- [[Planar Binding]]
- Prisoner management
- Bastion security
- Creature containment
- Laboratory security

Fabricate itself does not make these objects magical or impossible to break.

# Bastion & Infrastructure

Fabricate becomes considerably stronger when the Wizard has a permanent base.

Potential uses include rapidly producing:

- Furniture
- Shelving
- Doors
- Workshop equipment
- Storage
- Laboratory components
- Defensive structures
- Replacement building components
- Containers
- Equipment for Hirelings

This can dramatically reduce the logistical difficulty of establishing Wizard infrastructure.

# Fabricate + Wall of Stone

These spells solve different parts of construction.

## Wall of Stone

Provides large quantities of permanent stone structure when its permanence conditions are satisfied.

It must merge with and be supported by existing stone, and a span over 20 feet requires smaller panels for supports. The wall becomes permanent and cannot be dispelled only after the full **10 minutes of Concentration**. Do not assume magically produced walls automatically satisfy the separate Bastion Defensive Wall purchase/benefit rules; agree on that construction interaction with the DM.

## Fabricate

Can reshape available material into more specialized finished objects.

Together they support a Wizard interested in:

- Fortifications
- Laboratories
- Bastions
- Defensive infrastructure
- Construction projects

> [!warning]
> Fabricate still obeys its normal mineral-object size restriction. Wall of Stone does not remove that limitation.

---

# Fabricate + Downtime

Fabricate converts spell slots into manufacturing capacity.

During downtime, a Wizard can repeatedly use available spell slots to process stockpiled materials.

This becomes increasingly useful when the Wizard controls:

- A workshop
- Raw-material storage
- Tool sets
- Hirelings
- Transportation
- A Bastion
- Reliable markets

See [[Downtime]].

# Fabricate + Tool Acquisition

Tool proficiency should be considered part of Fabricate optimization.

When gaining access to another Artisan's Tool proficiency, ask:

> **What new high-craftsmanship objects can Fabricate now create?**

A Wizard intending to use Fabricate heavily gains disproportionate value from having several relevant tool proficiencies.

### Particularly Valuable

**Smith's Tools**

Excellent access to:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Metal equipment

**Carpenter's Tools**

Excellent infrastructure and construction utility.

**Mason's Tools**

Useful for permanent stone infrastructure.

**Jeweler's Tools**

Potential economic and precision-manufacturing value.

**Weaver's / Leatherworker's Tools**

Broad access to finished equipment and materials.

# Salvage Optimization

Destroyed or abandoned locations can contain valuable raw materials.

Potential sources:

- Weapons
- Armor
- Metal fittings
- Doors
- Furniture
- Lumber
- Stone
- Scrap metal
- Abandoned equipment

When appropriate material can legally and practically be recovered, Fabricate may allow it to be converted into something immediately useful.

This can make otherwise inconvenient salvage substantially more valuable.

# Fabricate as a Problem-Solving Spell

Before dismissing an environmental problem, ask:

> **Is there enough material nearby to manufacture the solution?**

Examples:

**Need to cross something?**

→ Bridge / platform

**Need cover?**

→ Barricade

**Need storage?**

→ Container

**Need equipment?**

→ Manufacture it

**Need restraints?**

→ Chains / cage / barriers

**Need construction?**

→ Structural components

**Need a specialized mundane object?**

→ Determine material + craftsmanship + size

This is where Fabricate's flexibility becomes difficult to quantify with a simple spell ranking.

---

# Preparation Checklist

Before using Fabricate, determine:

### Material

- What is the object made from?
- Is that material available?
- Is there enough?
- Is the material visible and within the spell's requirements?

### Size

- Is the object Large or smaller?
- If mineral/metal/stone, is it Medium or smaller?

### Craftsmanship

- Does this require a high degree of skill?
- If yes, do I have the appropriate Artisan's Tool proficiency?

### Legality

- Is the desired object mundane?
- Is it actually being made from the supplied material?
- Does it violate any explicit Fabricate restriction?

### Economics

- What did the material cost?
- What is the finished object actually worth locally?
- Is there a buyer?
- Is manufacturing it more valuable than selling it?

---

# Important Limitations

Fabricate does **not**:

- Create magic items
- Create creatures
- Create material from nothing
- Perform true transmutation
- Ignore raw-material requirements
- Ignore craftsmanship requirements
- Ignore size restrictions
- Guarantee market value
- Guarantee buyers
- Automatically bypass the normal economy
- Replace every possible crafting process

---

# Magic Item Crafting

Fabricate cannot directly create a magic item.

This remains true even though 2024 magic-item crafting uses:

- Arcana proficiency
- Tool proficiency
- Materials
- Downtime

Fabricate's explicit restriction against creating magic items still applies.

However, Fabricate may potentially be useful for creating **mundane objects or infrastructure associated with a larger crafting project**, subject to the normal rules and DM adjudication.

See [[Crafting]].

---

# Wizard Optimization

Fabricate is best understood as:

> **A portable magical manufacturing system.**

Its strength comes from combining:

**Raw Materials**

+

**Tool Proficiencies**

+

**Creativity**

+

**Preparation**

+

**10 Minutes**

The spell can compress manufacturing time, solve environmental problems, support Bastion construction, process salvage, produce equipment, and enable long-term Wizard infrastructure.

Its economic potential can be enormous, but should be separated from its much more reliable **manufacturing and utility value**.

---

# Acquisition Priority

Fabricate is extremely useful but rarely needs to compete with the strongest immediate 4th-level combat selections.

For an optimized Wizard:

> **Prefer acquiring Fabricate through a spell scroll, captured spellbook, or other spellbook acquisition when possible.**

It is an excellent **spellbook acquisition target** because once learned, its value remains available whenever downtime, infrastructure, materials, or an unusual problem makes it relevant.

See [[Spell Bank]].
