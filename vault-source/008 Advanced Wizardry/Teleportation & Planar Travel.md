# Teleportation & Planar Travel

Wizard mobility progresses from tactical escape to campaign-scale infrastructure.

## Progression

| Spell                | Level | Main Job                                   |
| -------------------- | ----: | ------------------------------------------ |
| Misty Step           |     2 | Personal tactical teleport                 |
| Vortex Warp          |     2 | Reposition another creature when available |
| Dimension Door       |     4 | Rescue / escape with one ally              |
| Teleportation Circle |     5 | Strategic fixed-destination travel         |
| Arcane Gate          |     6 | Battlefield / local mass repositioning     |
| Teleport             |     7 | Long-range same-plane travel               |
| Plane Shift          |     7 | Planar travel                              |
| Etherealness         |     7 | Border Ethereal movement                   |
| Demiplane            |     8 | Extraplanar storage / infrastructure       |
| Gate                 |     9 | Precise planar portal / creature calling   |


## Teleport

**Reliable anchors:** a permanent circle whose sigil you know, or an object taken from the destination within the last **six months**, gives an on-target result on every entry of Teleport's table. Merely seeing the destination is only "Very familiar" and still carries risk. Teleport is Action-speed and transports you plus up to eight willing creatures you can see within 10 feet, or one eligible object. See [[Teleport - Spell]].

Before casting:

1. Classify how familiar you actually are with the destination.
2. Prepare for every listed arrival outcome.
3. Keep communication and healing available.
4. Have a second route if the destination is dangerous.

A known permanent anchor is safer than vague description-based travel.

## Plane Shift

The revised spell is willing group travel, **not an offensive banishment attack**. The ordinary fork costs **250+ GP** and must be attuned to the destination plane. A general destination arrives in or near the requested place at the DM's discretion; a known permanent-circle sigil on that plane supplies the precise circle alternative. See [[Plane Shift - Spell]].

Before casting:

- Obtain the correct plane-attuned fork.
- Research the destination.
- Decide how precise the arrival can be.
- Plan the return route.
- Keep the party physically coordinated.

A successful Plane Shift is not useful if the party arrives somewhere hostile with no way home.

## Teleportation Circle

Good for:

- Permanent headquarters
- Regular trade routes
- Allied cities
- Bastions
- Strategic extraction points

Repeated route access turns geography into infrastructure.

## Etherealness

Use for:

- Bypassing physical obstacles
- Infiltration
- Planar movement where the Border Ethereal exists

Confirm that the destination plane actually has the required Ethereal relationship.

## Demiplane

Use as:

- Secure storage
- Clone vault
- Archive
- Component cache
- Emergency supplies

See [[Demiplane]].

## Gate

Gate is not just transportation.

It can create a precise portal to another plane and can call a named creature under its rules.

Calling a creature does **not** automatically control it.

It must connect **different planes**. Calling needs the creature's actual name, not a title, pseudonym, or nickname; deities and planar rulers can prevent the portal in their domains. A 5,000+ GP diamond is retained, but the spell uses Concentration up to 1 minute. Do not spend your ninth-level slot assuming the called creature is an obedient reinforcement.

Sources: [Teleport](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Teleport), [Plane Shift](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#PlaneShift), [Gate](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Gate).

## Wish

Safe Wish replication can duplicate eligible level-8-or-lower travel spells and bypass their normal casting requirements.

This can make [[Teleportation & Planar Travel#Plane Shift|Plane Shift]] useful when the correct tuning fork is unavailable, but the replicated spell's targeting and destination rules still matter.

> Every strategic teleport needs a **destination plan and a return plan**.
