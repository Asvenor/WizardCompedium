# Magic Item Attunement
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


A character can normally attune to **three magic items at once** unless a feature changes that limit.

Forming or voluntarily ending an attunement bond normally requires completing a **Short Rest focused on the item**. A creature that cannot take a Short Rest cannot normally attune to a new item.

Attuning requires physical contact and focus on **only that item**, and cannot share the same Short Rest used to identify it. Trying to attune to a fourth item fails rather than automatically dropping an existing bond. You cannot attune to two copies of the same item. Cursed items can prevent voluntary unattunement; ending their curse is a separate problem.

Wearing compatibility matters too: normally one pair of footwear, gloves/gauntlets, bracers, one suit of armor, one headwear item, and **one cloak**. Two available attunement slots do not automatically let Cloak of Protection and Mantle of Spell Resistance work together. The DM can allow exceptions. [2024 PHB magic-item rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#MagicItems).

For a Wizard, those three slots should be treated as a **package**, not three isolated picks.

Use [[Item Finder]] to filter a curated set by rarity, attunement, tactical role, and exact source. A free attunement slot does not waive class restrictions, hand requirements, clothing compatibility, or campaign access.

[[Evolving Magic Items]] describes an optional upgrade route. Compare the resulting item as a whole; evolution is not automatic extra attunement capacity or permission to discard the underlying item's access and use restrictions.

> [!tip] Core Question
> **Which of my current three attuned items does this replace?**

## Attunement Priorities

| Priority | What It Should Improve |
|---|---|
| **1. Spell DC / Core Casting** | Makes control and other save-based spells harder to resist |
| **2. Survival / Saving Throws** | Keeps the Wizard alive and protects Concentration |
| **3. Resource Expansion** | Adds spell slots, stored spells, charges, or extra casting |
| **4. Mobility** | Provides flight, teleportation, or escape without consuming Concentration |
| **5. Specialist Utility** | Senses, anti-magic, stealth, campaign-specific answers |

## Package Evaluation

Compare the full three-item setup across:

- Spell Save DC
- Spell Attack Bonus
- AC
- Saving Throws
- Concentration protection
- Initiative
- Mobility
- Reactions
- Extra spell resources
- Charges
- Resistances / immunities
- Senses
- Campaign-specific utility

A new item is worth attuning only if the **entire package becomes better**.

## Resource and defense traps

- **Ring of Spell Storing:** holds five spell levels, not five spells of any level. It uses the original caster's slot level, DC, spell attack bonus, and spellcasting ability, but the wearer is the caster when releasing the spell and supplies any required Concentration. Filling the ring still uses the original casting's resources; storing a spell that will not fit wastes it.
- **Rod of Absorption:** its **50 spell levels are a lifetime absorption limit**, not a rechargeable daily capacity. Held use and a Reaction are required to absorb an eligible spell targeting only the bearer and creating no area. Stored energy supports the bearer's prepared/known spells only up to level 5 and no higher than their own slots.
- **Staff of the Magi:** its held +2 applies to **spell attacks, not spell DC or AC**. Spell absorption uses a Reaction; absorbing beyond 50 charges triggers its destructive Retributive Strike. Do not copy Staff of Power's defensive numbers onto it.
- **Mantle of Spell Resistance:** Advantage on saves **against spells**, not automatically against all magical effects or every damage-triggered Concentration save. The robe's broader wording is a distinct benefit.
- **Bracers of Defense:** +2 AC requires no armor and no Shield. Mage Armor is not worn armor, but a shield-using multiclass route cannot keep the bracers' bonus.

Sources: [Ring](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#RingofSpellStoring), [Rod](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#RodofAbsorption), [Staff](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#StaffoftheMagi), [Mantle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#MantleofSpellResistance), [Bracers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#BracersofDefense). ## Concentration item choices

These all compete for an attunement slot. Preventing damage, raising the save, and repairing a failed save solve different problems; none of these three repair ordinary Concentration loss from becoming Incapacitated or starting another Concentration spell.

| Item | Cost after a failed CON Concentration save | When it is attractive |
|---|---|---|
| **Ring of Dedicated Focus — Uncommon, Arcana Unleashed** | Spend one or two unexpended Hit Dice and add their rolls; no Reaction specified | Preserves the Reaction for Shield/Counterspell. Can rescue near misses repeatedly while Hit Dice last, but not guaranteed success. |
| **Mind Sharpener — Uncommon, revised Eberron** | Reaction and one charge to succeed instead; four charges, regains 1d4 at dawn | Reliable rescue when the Reaction is available. Revised Artificer replication unlock is level 6, not a legacy level-2 infusion. |
| **Scholar's Anchoring Bangle — Rare, Arcana Unleashed** | Reaction to succeed instead, once per dawn | Combines one guaranteed rescue with reliable proficient Study checks. Its Rare price is not automatically better Concentration protection than the two Uncommon choices. |

Sources: [Ring of Dedicated Focus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-5-magic-items#RingOfDedicatedFocus), [Mind Sharpener](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/10886045-mind-sharpener), [Scholar's Anchoring Bangle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-5-magic-items#ScholarsAnchoringBangle). These are item effects, not feats; compare with [[Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List]] and [[Concentration Strategy]].

These compare jobs within a three-item package. Their precise operating rules and evidence are linked in [[Items for Wizard#Source and edition scope]]; the Ravnica, Blackstaff, Wyrmreaver, and 2023 Staff of Defense summaries use explicitly labeled secondary support.

| Candidate | The job it can replace | Why the replacement can lose value |
|---|---|---|
| [[Mizzium Apparatus - Item\|Mizzium Apparatus]] | A narrow resource or utility item when class-list flexibility is the bottleneck | Uses personal slots and normal components, with paid misfires on failed Arcana checks. It is not free spell fuel or automatic access to all spell lists. |
| [[Illusionist's Bracers - Item\|Illusionist's Bracers]] | A damage or control-support item in a cantrip-heavy plan | Repeating the same cantrip costs that turn's Bonus Action. Normally cannot wear them together with Bracers of Defense; the extra casting must justify both costs. |

| [[Blood Amulet - Item\|Blood Amulet]] | A burst-damage/control specialist slot | Three charges, Necrotic/Exhaustion defenses, and fixed DC 15 limit reliability; compare [[Arcana Unleashed Combinations#Blood Amulet + Scorching Ray]]. It does not protect Concentration passively. |
| [[Blackstaff - Item\|Blackstaff]] | A high-end resource/anti-caster staff, if the campaign actually grants succession | Designated Wizard heir, sentience, and the legacy base profile are essential. It is not a generic acquisition route or an INT-setting item. |

Its five fixed clothing designs do not provide armor or copied magical properties. Neither no-attunement status nor Common rarity guarantees that an item is available for purchase.

### Worked equipment comparisons

**Fixed comparison assumptions:** Wizard, INT 20 (+5), DEX 14 (+2), Proficiency Bonus +6, three attunement slots, no armor or shield, no other numerical bonuses. The first two packages use an active **Mage Armor**; the third uses the robe's base AC instead. These are static examples, not a character tracker or an assumption that a particular level guarantees these items. Tasha's **Arcane Grimoire** must be permitted by the campaign.

| Package | Spell save DC | Spell attack | AC | Bonus to all saves | Main tradeoff |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Uncommon resource mix:** Grimoire +1, Cloak of Protection, Pearl of Power | **20** | **+12** | **16** | **+1** | One recovered slot of level 3 or lower per dawn; gives up a second defensive item |
| **Save-focused mix:** Grimoire +2 (Rare), Cloak of Protection (Uncommon), Ring of Protection (Rare) | **21** | **+13** | **17** | **+2** | Passive save/AC protection, but no extra slot from a Pearl or automatic Concentration rescue |
| **High-end control mix:** Robe of the Archmagi (Legendary), Staff of Power (Very Rare), Grimoire +3 (Very Rare) | **24** | **+18** | **19** | **+2** | Strong casting and staff resources, but expensive access and both hands occupied for the listed bonuses |

The DC and attack columns are for **the Wizard's own Wizard spells**, not a blanket ruling about spells cast from magic items. All three items in every row require attunement. The values exclude a temporary **Shield** spell bonus; add its +5 AC only when its Reaction and casting requirements can actually be met.

**Arithmetic:** base spell DC is 8 + 6 + 5 = 19; base spell attack is +11. For the high-end package, the robe adds +2 to both, the held +3 Grimoire adds +3 to both for Wizard spells, and the held Staff adds +2 to spell attacks **but not DC**. AC is the robe's 15 + DEX 2 + Staff 2 = 19. Mage Armor is an alternative base-AC calculation, not another bonus to add to the robe. A Cloak and Ring of Protection are different items, so their +1 bonuses combine.

**Resources:** the Grimoire also increases the total spell-slot levels recovered by Arcane Recovery by one; it does not raise the maximum individual slot level recoverable. The Pearl needs to be on the caster's person and takes a Magic action to restore an expended slot of level 3 or lower, once per dawn. The robe additionally grants Advantage on saves against spells and other magical effects; this is not Advantage on every saving throw or on ordinary damage-triggered Concentration saves.

#### Held items and spell components

- **Grimoire:** the focus permission and Wizard spell attack/DC bonus require holding it. Wearing or carrying a closed book in a bag does not preserve those casting bonuses.
- **Staff of Power:** its +2 AC, saving-throw, and spell-attack bonuses require holding it. Putting it away to free a hand changes the package's numbers immediately.
- Holding the staff in one hand and the book in the other uses both hands. The Grimoire can replace eligible costless, unconsumed Material components, and the Material hand can also perform that spell's Somatic components. Its focus permission is **not a general waiver of Somatic requirements for spells without Material components**.
- **War Caster** expressly permits Somatic components with weapons or a Shield in the hands. The Staff of Power is a Quarterstaff, so it provides a weapon hand for that permission. War Caster does not waive required costly or consumed Material components; access to those must still be legal. Do not assume an off-turn Reaction grants a free opportunity to rearrange equipment.

The first two packages need only the Grimoire held, leaving the other hand available. The high-end package is attractive only when its hand requirements, item access, and opportunity costs fit the build. For higher-INT versions, add the change in INT modifier to the spell DC and spell attack columns; see [[Intelligence above 20]].

Sources: [Arcane Grimoire — Tasha's](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/2411893-arcane-grimoire), [2024 Cloak](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#CloakofProtection), [Ring](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#RingofProtection), [Pearl](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#PearlofPower), [Robe](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#RobeoftheArchmagi), [Staff](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#StaffofPower), [PHB spell components](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spells), [War Caster](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#WarCaster). Compare [[Spellcasting Rules]] and [[Concentration Strategy]] before treating a numerical package as a complete casting plan.

### Core Caster

**Goal:** maximize spell reliability while staying alive.

- Spell DC item
- Defensive / save item
- Resource or utility item

Example:

**Arcane Grimoire + Cloak of Protection + Ring of Spell Storing**

---

### High-Level Control Wizard

**Goal:** make encounter-defining spells land and preserve Concentration.

- High-end Spell DC item
- Strong defensive item
- Staff / resource item

Example:

**Robe of the Archmagi + Staff of Power + Arcane Grimoire**

> [!warning] Check Compatibility
> Always verify whether effects actually stack and whether all items require attunement.

### Defensive Wizard

**Goal:** survive focus fire and protect Concentration.

- Cloak / Ring of Protection
- Constitution or save support
- Anti-spell item

Useful candidates:

- **Amulet of Health**
- **Mantle of Spell Resistance**
- **Cloak of Protection**
- **Ring of Protection**
- **Staff of Power**

### Resource Wizard

**Goal:** maximize total magical resources.

Useful candidates:

- **Pearl of Power**
- **Ring of Spell Storing**
- **Rod of Absorption**
- **Staff of Power**
- Other charge-based casting items

## Non-Attunement Value

Non-attunement items are especially valuable because they provide power **without competing for one of the three slots**.

Examples:

- **Tome of Clear Thought** — permanent Intelligence increase after study; no attunement slot
- **Carpet of Flying**
- **Portable Hole**
- **Cape of the Mountebank**
- **Immovable Rod**
- **Bag of Holding**
- **Boon Companions' Bands** — one shared friendly-fire protection use per dawn
- **Spell Component Ring** — one prepared spell's costless, unconsumed Material component; not a Somatic-component bypass
- Other utility and storage items

> [!tip] Opportunity Cost
> A slightly weaker non-attunement item can be more valuable than a stronger attunement item because it leaves all three slots available.

## Situational Swapping

Attunement should not be treated as permanently locked.

Swap specialist items when the mission is known in advance.

| Situation | Consider |
|---|---|
| Illusions / Invisible enemies | **Gem of Seeing** |
| Enemy spellcasters | **Mantle of Spell Resistance**, **Rod of Absorption** |
| Dangerous saving throws | **Cloak of Protection**, **Ring of Protection** |
| Long adventuring day | **Pearl of Power**, **Ring of Spell Storing** |
| Extreme mobility needs | Attuned flight / teleportation options |
| Known elemental threat | Relevant resistance item |

---

## What Usually Loses an Attunement Slot

Items become weaker candidates when they:

- Duplicate something already covered by another item
- Only matter in rare situations
- Require attunement for a small numerical benefit
- Compete with a major Spell DC item
- Consume the same Reaction or action economy as stronger options
- Provide utility easily replaced by a spell or non-attunement item

---

## Attunement Principle

> **Do not ask whether an item is good. Ask whether the three-item package is better with it.**
