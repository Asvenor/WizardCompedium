# Mind Sharpener

[[Item Finder]] · [[Magic Item Attunement]] · [[Magic Item Tier List]]

**Uncommon · Attunement required · Revised Eberron Artificer**  
**Access:** No class restriction

## Practical value

After failing a CON Concentration save, use a Reaction and a charge to succeed instead. Four charges; regains 1d4 at dawn.

## Limiting cost

Competes with Shield and Counterspell for the Reaction. The revised Artificer replication unlock is level 6, not the legacy level-2 infusion.
