# Robe of the Archmagi

**Legendary · Attunement required · Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)**  
**Access:** Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard

## Practical value

Adds +2 spell DC and spell attacks; offers unarmored base AC and Advantage against spells and other magical effects.

## Limiting cost

Its base AC is an alternative to Mage Armor, not a bonus added on top. It does not give Advantage on every Concentration save.
