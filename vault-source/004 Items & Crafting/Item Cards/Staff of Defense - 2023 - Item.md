# Staff of Defense - 2023

[[Item Finder]] · [[Magic Item Attunement]] · [[Magic Item Tier List]]

**Rare · Attunement required · Phandelver and Below (2023)**  
**Access:** Bard, Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard

## Practical value

Held +1 AC; ten charges cast Mage Armor for one or Shield for two. The revised wording uses normal spell timing, including Shield's triggered Reaction.

## Limiting cost

Recovers 1d6+4 charges at dawn; final-charge destruction risk. Do not combine its timing with legacy unrestricted attunement.
