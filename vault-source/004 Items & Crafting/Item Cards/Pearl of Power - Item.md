# Pearl of Power

[[Item Finder]] · [[Magic Item Attunement]] · [[Magic Item Tier List]]

**Uncommon · Attunement required · Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)**  
**Access:** Spellcaster

## Practical value

A Magic action restores an expended spell slot of level 3 or lower, once per dawn, while the pearl is on your person.

## Limiting cost

No benefit when those slots are not the bottleneck; it still uses attunement.
