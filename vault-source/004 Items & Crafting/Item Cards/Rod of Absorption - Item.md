# Rod of Absorption

[[Item Finder]] · [[Magic Item Attunement]] · [[Magic Item Tier List]]

**Very Rare · Attunement required · Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)**  
**Access:** No class restriction; spellcaster for conversion

## Practical value

A held Reaction can absorb an eligible single-target spell; stored energy can fuel known/prepared spells up to level 5, within your own slot access.

## Limiting cost

Its 50 absorbed levels are a lifetime limit, not a renewable daily allowance. Area spells are excluded.
