# Prismatic Rune

[[Item Finder]] · [[Magic Item Attunement]] · [[Magic Item Tier List]]

**Rare · Attunement required · Arcana Unleashed**  
**Access:** Spellcaster

## Practical value

After a Long Rest select Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder; spend a charge when casting a damaging spell to change its damage type to that selection.

## Limiting cost

Six charges, regaining 1d6 at dawn. Choosing a type is not permission to ignore all enemy resistances.
