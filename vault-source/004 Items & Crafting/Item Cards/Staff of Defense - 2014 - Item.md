# Staff of Defense - 2014

## Practical value

Held +1 AC; ten charges for Mage Armor (one) or Shield (two), restricted to spells on the user's class list.

## Limiting cost

Legacy casting explicitly takes an Action. Do not silently turn that into reactive Shield. Uses 1d6+4 dawn recovery and final-charge destruction risk.
