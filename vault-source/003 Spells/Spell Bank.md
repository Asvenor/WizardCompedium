# Master Spellbook Acquisition Table

More detailed list is [[Spells]]

**How to use the rankings:** “Must-Own” means a high-value **acquisition shortlist**, not a requirement to learn or prepare every entry. Free level-up choices are limited; copying needs access, time, and money. Choose complementary answers, not six Concentration spells that solve the same encounter. [[Worked Wizard Builds]] shows a legal no-copy spellbook progression; [[Prepared Loadouts]] explains how to select a day's preparations from a larger book.

**Cantrips are learned, not copied into the spellbook.** Choose within the class's known-cantrip allowance. Expanded and Dunamancy spells are candidates only when the campaign grants access; a tier label never overrides that gate.

Some high-ranked spells are **strategy-dependent**: Conjure Minor Elementals rewards repeated attacks at close range, not a back-line controller's ordinary turn; Planar Binding needs a legal available target, containment, casting time, and a costly consumed component; Contact Other Plane carries a dangerous caster INT save. Their cards and dedicated notes give the prerequisites before the payoff.
