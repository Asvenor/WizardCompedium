## Decision

A flexible visual decoy or screen when a cantrip's static limits are too restrictive. Its value depends on believable placement and how observers react; it is not a guaranteed lost enemy turn.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Create a visual image of an object, creature, or phenomenon fitting a 15-foot Cube at a point within range. It has no sound, smell, or other sensory output. A Magic action moves it within range and can make that movement look natural.

Physical interaction exposes the illusion because things pass through. A Study action with successful INT (Investigation) against your spell save DC also identifies it; a creature that discerns it can see through it.

An apparent wall does not become physical cover or block projectiles. Whether the sight deception changes an enemy's decisions is situational adjudication; do not promise restraint, fear, or forced movement. Concentration also means giving up another control spell.

## Upcasting

No listed scaling. [[Major Image - Spell|Major Image]] supplies additional senses and, with a level-4+ slot, persistence without Concentration.
