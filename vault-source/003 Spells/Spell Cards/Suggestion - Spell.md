## Rules that decide the play

The visible target must hear and understand an achievable instruction of no more than 25 words. It cannot obviously damage the target or its allies. A failed WIS save Charms the target; task completion or damage by the caster/allies ends the effect. It is not unrestricted domination or permission to bypass Charm immunity.
