## Decision

An immediate non-Concentration opening when a target is below the HP threshold. Valuable against initial-save defenses, but poor if its HP is too high or it is immune to Stunned.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose one visible creature. At **150 current HP or fewer**, it becomes Stunned without an initial save. Above 150 HP, its Speed instead becomes 0 until the start of your next turn.

A Stunned target repeats a CON save at each turn's end to end the condition. Legendary Resistance cannot replace a nonexistent initial save, but can be used on eligible later failed saves. Stunned immunity is not bypassed.

Use the current [[Conditions]] entry: Stunned is not Paralyzed, does not grant automatic Critical Hits, and does not itself set Speed to 0 in 2024. The high-HP fallback stops ordinary movement, not actions or teleportation.
