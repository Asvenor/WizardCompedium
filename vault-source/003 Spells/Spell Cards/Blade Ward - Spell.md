## Decision

Useful protection before an expected fight when no stronger Concentration spell is running. Its Action and Concentration costs make it less attractive after combat starts.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Every creature attacking the caster subtracts 1d4 from its attack roll. It does not reduce saving-throw damage or grant damage resistance. Beginning another Concentration spell ends it; repeatedly casting it during exploration is observable spellcasting, not a silent always-on feature.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].
