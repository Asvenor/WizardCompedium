## Decision

A concentration-free execution spell with an Undead-creation rider. Its seventh-level slot cost makes routine blasting questionable; it is most relevant to a Necromancer's longer-term creature plan.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature makes a CON save against **7d8 + 30 Necrotic**, halved on success. A **Humanoid killed by this spell** becomes a Zombie at the start of your next turn and obeys verbal orders.

Reducing a creature to 0 HP is not always killing it; a later death from something else does not satisfy this rider. Non-Humanoid kills do not create Zombies, and the result is the specified Zombie rather than the victim's former stat block.

The current entry specifies verbal obedience and gives no 24-hour renewal requirement. It does not reproduce the legacy sentence promising permanent control. Keep creature handling, target access, campaign consequences, and any specific control-ending effects in view rather than treating this as an unrestricted army button.
