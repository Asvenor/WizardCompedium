## Decision

Preloaded defense; 1,500+ gp statuette, not consumed.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Store an action-cast spell of level 5 or lower that can target you; cast both spells and expend both slots. The first matching circumstance triggers it automatically. Keep the statuette on your person; only one Contingency can be active. [[Contingency Optimization]]
