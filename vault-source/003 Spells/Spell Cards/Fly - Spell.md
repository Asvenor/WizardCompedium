Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Grants a willing touched creature a 60-foot Fly Speed and hover. Hover protects against ordinary flight-related falling triggers while the effect exists; it does not preserve flight after the spell ends. An emergency Feather Fall still needs its own legal Reaction and components.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 3.
