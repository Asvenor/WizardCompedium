## Decision

Create or maintain Skeletons/Zombies; Bonus Action commands and 24-hour upkeep.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Initial casting animates one Small/Medium Humanoid corpse or bone pile. Renewal controls up to four existing creations for another 24 hours. Bonus Action commands reach 60 feet; creatures follow a general order until it is complete.

Control must be renewed **before** the current 24 hours end. The spell's 10-foot casting range and its 60-foot command range are different limits.

### Command wording and action cost

[[100 Assets/Statblocks for Compendium/Animate Dead/Commands|German command examples]] provide possible order wording, not additional rules or free actions. On the caster's turn, a **Bonus Action** can mentally command creatures made by this spell within **60 feet**. A group command gives the **same order to each selected creature**; it is not a separate custom action for every minion.

An existing order continues until its task is complete. With no command, the 2024 default is **Dodge and movement only to avoid harm**, not automatic attacking. Shouting a code word does not itself grant a new off-turn mental command; conditional standing orders must be agreed with the DM rather than treated as unlimited remote reprogramming.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 3 adds two creatures to either animation or control renewal. Each needs a separate corpse or pile of bones.
