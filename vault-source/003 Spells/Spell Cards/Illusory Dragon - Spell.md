## Decision

## Rules that decide the play

The Huge, tangible illusion occupies its space and is immune to all damage and conditions. On appearance, enemies that can see it make Wisdom saves; failure drops held items and Frightens. A frightened enemy repeats the save at turn end only when it lacks line of sight to the dragon. Your Bonus Action moves it up to 60 feet and allows a 60-foot Cone dealing 6d6 damage, Intelligence save for half; choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Necrotic, or Poison when creating it. The Cone affects allies too. A Study action with Investigation against your spell DC reveals the illusion and grants Advantage against its effects, not immunity.
