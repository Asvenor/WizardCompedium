## Decision

Emergency self-healing when a short rest is unavailable. It consumes both a spell slot and Hit Dice, so it is not free resource recovery.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Roll one or two unexpended Hit Point Dice, regain the total plus the spellcasting ability modifier, and expend those dice. The spell does not add CON per die as a normal Short Rest does. Its slot expenditure still counts toward the one-slot-per-turn casting restriction.

## Upcasting

Each slot above 2 allows one additional unexpended Hit Die to be rolled.
