## Decision

A long-lasting ambush, concealment, or route-deception tool without ongoing Concentration. It changes perception, not the actual traversability of terrain.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Alter the look, sound, and smell of natural terrain within a 150-foot Cube. Manufactured structures, equipment, and creatures are unchanged, as are tactile properties. Touch can make the discrepancy obvious; otherwise a Study action and Investigation check against the caster's DC can expose it. A creature that discerns the illusion sees the real terrain beneath a faint overlay.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].
