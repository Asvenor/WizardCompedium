## Decision

Cheap corpse preservation and insurance for later resurrection. Useful in the book even when rarely prepared, but urgent timing can require an actual slot casting.

**Preparation priority:** Strong spellbook ritual (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a corpse or remains to prevent decay and becoming Undead. Days protected by this spell do not count toward resurrection time limits, such as Raise Dead's limit. It does not itself resurrect anyone or supply a resurrection spell's other requirements.

The material is **two consumed Copper Pieces** in 2024, not reusable coins that must remain on the body's eyes.

A ritual adds 10 minutes and requires Concentration while casting; the completed ten-day effect does not. Do not assume ritual casting can rescue an already missed short resurrection deadline. See [[Spellcasting Rules]] for Wizard ritual access.

## Upcasting

No listed scaling. Renewing preservation still requires another legal casting and its components.
