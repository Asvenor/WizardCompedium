## Decision

Reliable same-plane travel to a known permanent circle. The minute of casting makes it a prepared retreat, not an instant emergency button.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Draw a 5-foot-radius circle linked to a known same-plane permanent destination. The portal lasts until the end of the caster's next turn; creatures entering emerge near the destination. Initially learn two Material Plane destination sequences chosen by the DM; an additional sequence takes one minute of study to memorize. Casting at one location daily for 365 days creates a permanent circle, costing at least 18,250 GP in inks through this method. A permanent circle is a destination, not free outbound casting; an optional Bastion facility offers a separate acquisition route.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].
