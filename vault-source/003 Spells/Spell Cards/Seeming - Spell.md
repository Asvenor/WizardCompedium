## Decision

Long-duration group disguise without occupying Concentration. Best when the party needs a shared cover story; appearance does not supply documents, knowledge, behavior, or cooperation from observers.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating). Illusion Adept can supply it always prepared, separately from the normal Wizard count.

## Rules that decide the play

Choose visible creatures in range, giving them matching or different illusory appearances. An unwilling creature can resist with a CHA save. Bodies and equipment can look different; apparent height changes by at most 1 foot either way, and limb arrangement must remain compatible.

Touch can expose the illusion. A creature can also take the Study action and pass an INT (Investigation) check against your spell DC to recognize a disguise. The spell changes appearance, not physical equipment or game statistics.
