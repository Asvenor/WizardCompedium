## Decision

Clusters enemies for hazards or follow-up area spells without requiring Concentration.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

A 20-foot-radius Sphere centered on a visible point forces Constitution saves. Failure deals 5d10 Force and pulls in a straight line toward the center, ending in the closest possible unoccupied space; the ending space may be in the air. Success halves damage and prevents the pull. Positioning is therefore save-dependent, and allies inside are affected too. Fall damage or a later area attack is a separate consequence, not guaranteed extra damage in the spell's printed roll.

## Upcasting

Damage increases by 1d10 per slot level above 4; the radius and pull destination rule do not change.
