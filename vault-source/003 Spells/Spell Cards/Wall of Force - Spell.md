## Rules that decide the play

Choose a radius-up-to-10-foot globe/dome or ten connected 10-foot-square panels. No initial save is listed; creatures intersected at creation are pushed to a chosen side. Damage and Dispel Magic do not remove it; Disintegrate does. It blocks physical and Ethereal passage but is not automatically a ban on teleportation. Visibility through it is not the same as an unobstructed spell path.
