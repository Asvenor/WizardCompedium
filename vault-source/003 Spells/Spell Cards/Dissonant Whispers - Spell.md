## Decision

A useful low-slot follow-up while another control spell occupies Concentration. Its movement can create an opening, but only when the target still has a Reaction and a usable route away.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating). When a feature always prepares it, it does not spend an ordinary Wizard preparation.

## Rules that decide the play

Choose one visible creature. A failed WIS save deals **3d6 Psychic** and immediately spends the target's available Reaction on retreat by the safest route, as far from you as possible. Success gives half damage without movement. No available Reaction means no reaction movement.

The 2024 text requires sight and has no legacy automatic-success clause for Deafened targets. It does not impose Charmed or Frightened.

## Upcasting

Add **1d6 damage** per slot level above 1, not another target.
