## Rules that decide the play

The Medium door needs a visible flat solid surface and opens into a 30-foot-per-side room. When the door vanishes, creatures can choose to be shunted outside and land Prone; objects stay. Knowing another caster's demiplane's nature and contents can permit access, so secrecy is not an absolute security guarantee. See [[Demiplane]].
