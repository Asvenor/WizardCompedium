## Rules that decide the play

Create a visible-point 20-foot-radius Sphere. The Sphere and space within 100 feet of it are Difficult Terrain. A creature starting its turn within 100 feet of the Sphere makes a Strength save against being pulled toward the center. First entry on a turn or starting inside the inner Sphere deals 5d10 Force with no damage save and Restrains; an action and Strength check against your spell DC can free oneself or a reachable creature. Leaving the Sphere also ends that restraint. At 0 HP, the creature and nonmagical carried/worn gear are annihilated. Unsecured objects are pulled and eligible nonmagical objects inside are destroyed; collateral loss is a real cost.
