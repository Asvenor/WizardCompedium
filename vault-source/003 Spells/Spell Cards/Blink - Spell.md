## Decision

Non-Concentration defense through intermittent Ethereal removal.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

At each turn's end, a d6 result of 4–6 moves the caster to the Ethereal Plane; return at the next turn's start to a visible free space within 10 feet of the departure point. The protection is random, and being on another plane also prevents ordinary interaction and Reactions against creatures left behind. It is not immunity on demand.
