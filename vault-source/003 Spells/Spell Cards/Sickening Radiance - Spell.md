## Decision

Persistent radiant/exhaustion pressure; infamous containment synergy.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for primary concentration (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A 30-foot-radius Sphere spreads around corners. First entry on a turn or starting there calls for a Constitution save; failure causes 4d10 Radiant, one Exhaustion level, and a glow preventing the benefits of invisibility. Success avoids these effects. Merely creating the area around a creature does not immediately trigger that save. Its Exhaustion and glow disappear when the spell ends; apply the campaign's current Exhaustion rules rather than importing the old exhaustion table. A concentration-based containment spell normally needs a separate caster or another explicit legal source.
