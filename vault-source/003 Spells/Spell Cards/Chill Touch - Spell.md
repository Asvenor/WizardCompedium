## Decision

A specialist anti-healing hit, but its revised melee range exposes a conventional Wizard. Do not choose it assuming the old 120-foot ranged spell.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A melee spell attack against a target within reach deals 1d10 Necrotic damage on a hit and prevents that target from regaining HP until the end of the caster's next turn. The prevention requires the hit; it is not a blanket aura or an ongoing Concentration effect.

## Scaling

Damage becomes 2d10/3d10/4d10 at character levels 5/11/17.
