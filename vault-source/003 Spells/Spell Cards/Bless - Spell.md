## Decision

An efficient support option when several allies make repeated attacks or important saves. Its benefit does not depend on high Wisdom, making it attractive to a Cleric dip—but it competes with your best Wizard Concentration spell.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose up to three creatures in range. Each adds **1d4 to attack rolls and saving throws** while the spell lasts; this includes Concentration saves, not ability checks or damage rolls.

The priced Holy Symbol is required even for a free Magic Initiate casting; it is not consumed. A normal Arcane Focus does not substitute for a priced component.

Acquiring this through another class or feat does not make it a normal Wizard spellbook selection. Use the preparation and casting rules of the feature that grants it.

## Upcasting

Add one target per slot level above 1. Covering a fourth relevant ally can be worthwhile; extra slot expenditure does not improve the d4.
