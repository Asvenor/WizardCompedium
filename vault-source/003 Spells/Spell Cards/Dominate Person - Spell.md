## Decision

High upside when controlling one important Humanoid can resolve an encounter or objective. Less reliable once fighting has begun, and friendly fire against the controlled target undermines the investment.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible Humanoid makes a WIS save against being Charmed; it has Advantage if you or your allies are fighting it. Each instance of damage permits another save, ending the spell on success. There is no automatic end-of-turn repeat save.

While on the same plane, issue telepathic commands on your turn without an action; the target does its best to obey on its turn. After finishing an order without further direction, it acts freely to protect itself. Ordering its Reaction spends **your Reaction too**. Neither creature gains an extra Reaction.

Charm immunity remains a hard limit. The 2024 spell has no separate legacy action for total, precise control.

## Upcasting

Duration with Concentration: slot 6 up to 10 minutes, slot 7 up to 1 hour, slot 8+ up to 8 hours. Upcasting adds duration, not extra targets.
