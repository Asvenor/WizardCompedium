## Decision

Split-target or focused attack-roll damage without Concentration. Compare expected hits and Fire defenses; three attacks are not three guaranteed damage instances.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Create three rays, allocating them to one or several targets in range. Each uses a separate ranged spell attack and deals 2d6 Fire on a hit. Multiple attacks remain one spell casting; per-cast, per-turn, and per-hit riders must be distinguished.

## Upcasting

One additional ray per slot above 2.
