## Decision

A major party defense against enemy magic and a current Wizard spell. It rewards coordinated positioning but competes with offensive Concentration.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A 30-foot Emanation centered on the caster grants the caster and allies Advantage on saves against spells and other magical effects. When such an effect normally allows a save for half damage, success instead deals no damage. It does not grant this protection against every nonmagical attack/effect, and a failed save still has its ordinary consequences.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].
