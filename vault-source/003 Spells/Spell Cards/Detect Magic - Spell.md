## Rules that decide the play

Sensing nearby magical effects is different from identifying them. A further Magic action reveals auras on visible creatures/objects and a spell effect's school. One foot of stone, dirt, or wood; one inch of metal; or a thin lead sheet blocks detection. It does not automatically locate every invisible creature or explain a magic item's properties.
