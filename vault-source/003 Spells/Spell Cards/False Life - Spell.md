## Decision

A pre-fight buffer when survival matters more than preserving the slot. Compare it with cheaper prevention and existing Temporary HP: spending an Action during combat can be costly.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Gain **2d4 + 4 Temporary Hit Points**: 6–12, averaging 9. This is not healing, does not raise current or maximum HP, and does not stack with another Temporary HP pool.

The 2024 spell is instantaneous, not a one-hour buff. Its Temporary HP follow the normal rules, remaining until depleted or a Long Rest unless another rule ends them. See [[Rules Desk]].

## Upcasting

Gain 5 additional Temporary HP per slot above 1. The fixed increase can be efficient emergency padding, but an unused pool is not banked healing and repeated castings do not accumulate.
