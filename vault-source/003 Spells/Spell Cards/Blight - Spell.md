## Decision

A concentration-free single-target damage option, especially against Plant creatures. The short range and common strong Constitution saves limit its general-purpose value.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature makes a CON save against **8d8 Necrotic**, halved on success. A Plant creature automatically fails that save, but does **not** automatically take maximum damage.

Alternatively, a nonmagical plant that is not a creature simply withers and dies. This does not create a general object-destruction spell.

The 2024 entry does not exclude Undead or Constructs; their actual Necrotic resistance or immunity can still defeat the damage. An automatic failed save is not a promise to bypass a target's specific Legendary Resistance.

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 per slot above 4. Compare that modest increase with spending the slot on control or a stronger summon.
