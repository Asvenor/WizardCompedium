## Decision

Suppresses magic, including much of your own kit.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Its 10-foot Emanation suppresses ordinary ongoing magic and magic-item properties and prevents casting, Magic actions, teleportation, and planar travel inside. Suppressed durations continue to elapse. The field harms a Wizard's own spell/gear plan too. Read specific exceptions—such as Prismatic Wall and the spell's artifact/deity wording—instead of assuming every effect is equally suppressed.
