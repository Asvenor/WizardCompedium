## Decision

Persistent triggered illusion for security, deception, and infrastructure.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A scripted visible/audible performance fits within a 30-foot Cube and runs for at most five minutes per activation, followed by ten minutes dormant. The trigger must use sight or sound phenomena within 30 feet of the area. It is not an intelligent sentry or unrestricted remote-information detector; touch or a successful Study check can expose it.
