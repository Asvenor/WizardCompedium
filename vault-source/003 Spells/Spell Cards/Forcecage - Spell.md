## Rules that decide the play

There is no initial save, but teleportation or interplanar escape requires a CHA save. The 10-foot solid box blocks spells; the 20-foot barred cage is a different enclosure. Dispel Magic cannot end it. Concentration and consumed ruby dust are real costs.
