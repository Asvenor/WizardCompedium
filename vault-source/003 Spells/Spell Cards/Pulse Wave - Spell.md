## Decision

Directional Force damage and group repositioning; choose a single push or pull mode.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

Every creature in the 30-foot Cone makes a Constitution save against 6d6 Force, half on success. Failed saves also move 15 feet in the chosen direction: toward you or away from you. The choice applies to the spell, not separately to each creature. Unsecured objects wholly inside the Cone move the same way. Allies receive no automatic exemption; choose an angle that does not sabotage their positions.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 3 adds 1d6 damage and 5 feet of push/pull distance.
