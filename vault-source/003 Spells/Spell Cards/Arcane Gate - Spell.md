## Decision

Two linked portals for rapid local mass movement and extraction.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Choose two visible, Large, unoccupied ground spaces: one within 500 feet and the other within 10 feet of you. The portals are one-sided and block sight through them; a Bonus Action changes their facing.
