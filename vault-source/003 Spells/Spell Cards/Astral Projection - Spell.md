## Decision

A specialized Astral expedition tool, not the default planar transport pick. Compare the component bill and exposed bodies with Plane Shift before dedicating your ninth-level slot.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

You and up to eight willing creatures project into the Astral Plane; casting there ends the spell immediately. Bodies remain Unconscious in suspended animation, without aging or needing food or air. Astral forms replicate statistics and possessions; body and form do not share damage or other effects.

Leaving the Astral Plane brings the original body and possessions along, reuniting the traveler with them. This is not a spare body left safely at home on every plane.

A silver cord can be severed only by an effect explicitly allowing that, killing both body and projection. Either body or projection reaching 0 HP ends that target's spell. Your Magic action dismisses it for everyone; a living target returns to its body when its effect ends.

The one-hour casting requires Concentration while casting, despite the completed effect not requiring it. Cost: **1,100+ GP per target**, including the caster. No listed upcasting.

Protect the unattended bodies; projection is not immortality or permission to duplicate expendable resources.
