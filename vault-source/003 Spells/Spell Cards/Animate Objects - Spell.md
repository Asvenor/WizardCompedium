## Rules that decide the play

Choose up to your spellcasting ability modifier in nonmagical objects: Large costs two choices and Huge costs three. The current text does not specify a minimum of one. No worn, carried, fixed, or Gargantuan objects. Bonus Action commands reach 500 feet and give the same command to the selected objects; their turn follows yours. They have Speed 30 feet, not the old automatic flight. Without commands they Dodge and move only to avoid harm.

All sizes have **AC 15**, use **your spell attack modifier** for a 5-foot-reach Slam, and have Blindsight 30 feet. Base-slot combat numbers:

| Object size | Choices used | HP | Slam Force damage |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Medium or smaller | 1 | 10 | 1d4 + 3 |
| Large | 2 | 20 | 2d6 + 3 + your spellcasting ability modifier |
| Huge | 3 | 40 | 2d12 + 3 + your spellcasting ability modifier |

Object availability and space matter: more small attackers are not automatically the highest-damage allocation under the revised statistics.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 5 increases each Slam by 1d4 for Medium-or-smaller objects, 1d6 for Large, or 1d12 for Huge. It does not increase the number animated.
