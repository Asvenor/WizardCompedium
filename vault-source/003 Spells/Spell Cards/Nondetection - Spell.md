## Decision

A non-Concentration anti-surveillance layer for a sensitive mission. Its eight-hour coverage costs a slot and 25 GP for each casting; it does not replace ordinary stealth.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a willing creature, or a place or object whose dimensions are each no more than 10 feet. For the duration, Divination spells cannot target it, and magical scrying sensors cannot perceive it. This is not invisibility, protection from ordinary witnesses, or a blanket defense against every magical detection feature: apply the actual spell/sensor wording.
