## Decision

A multisensory deception tool; the level-4 casting is the standout infrastructure option. Spend the higher slot when an enduring disguise, display, or distraction frees Concentration for combat.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Create an image fitting a 20-foot Cube at a visible point within range. It includes suitable sounds, smells, and temperature but **cannot deal damage or impose conditions**.

While within range of the image, use a Magic action to move it to another point within range with natural-looking motion; its sounds can vary, including conversation. Physical interaction reveals its insubstantial nature. A successful Study action using INT (Investigation) against your spell save DC also exposes it: that observer sees through it and perceives its other sensations faintly.

This is not a real barrier or an autonomous creature. An enemy's response to apparent danger is adjudication, not a guaranteed control effect.

## Upcasting

A **level-4+ slot** makes the duration until dispelled and removes Concentration. The base-level metadata in filters remains Concentration: use this upcast exception when planning layered effects.
