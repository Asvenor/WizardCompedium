## Decision

Paralyzed shutdown; powerful when the save sticks and allies can exploit it.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for primary concentration (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

One visible creature makes a WIS save, repeating at the end of each of its turns. Failed saves impose Paralyzed; immunity and Legendary Resistance still matter. The current spell has no general Undead exclusion. The automatic critical-hit benefit of Paralyzed requires an attack to hit from within 5 feet.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 5.
