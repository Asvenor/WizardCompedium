## Decision

A strategic weather intervention for travel, sieges, or environmental objectives. It is too slow to function as an ordinary combat opener, and it monopolizes Concentration for a potentially long period.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Cast outdoors to influence weather within 5 miles; going indoors ends the spell. The DM establishes starting conditions. Change each chosen weather category by **one stage** up or down; the new conditions take **1d4 × 10 minutes** to arrive before another change. Wind direction can also change.

| Category | Ordered stages |
|---|---|
| Precipitation | Clear → light clouds → overcast/ground fog → rain/hail/snow → torrential rain/driving hail/blizzard |
| Temperature | Heat wave → hot → warm → cool → cold → freezing |
| Wind | Calm → moderate → strong → gale → storm |

Weather gradually returns to normal when the spell ends. Its ten-minute casting also requires Concentration.

The spell does not assign automatic damage, destroy buildings, or select enemies only. Apply the relevant environmental rules and DM adjudication; plan for consequences to allies and civilians too.

## Upcasting

No listed scaling; a ninth-level slot does not remove the outdoor, timing, stage, or Concentration limits.
