## Decision

A downtime security purchase with no Concentration cost. Secure the surrounding structure too; a magical lock is not indestructible material.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Magically lock a closed door, window, gate, container, or hatch. Nonmagical means cannot unlock it. Designated creatures and the caster can operate it; a chosen password spoken within 5 feet unlocks it for 1 minute. This is not the older +10 lock DC rule. Knock suppresses Arcane Lock for 10 minutes rather than permanently dispelling it.
