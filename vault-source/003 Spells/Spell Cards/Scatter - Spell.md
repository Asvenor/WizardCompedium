## Decision

Mass repositioning of allies and enemies; excellent rescue/control tool.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Choose up to five visible creatures within 30 feet; unwilling targets receive Wisdom saves. Each destination must be unoccupied, visible within 120 feet of you, and on a floor or ground. It is not an airborne drop spell. Rescue several allies, separate enemy support, or rearrange a formation while preserving Concentration; the short target-selection range is the main exposure cost.
