## Decision

No-Concentration multilayer battlefield barrier with extreme punishment for crossing.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for control (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Crossing **each of the seven layers** starts with a DEX save, including Indigo and Violet. Indigo then uses end-of-turn CON saves against Petrified; Violet uses a later WIS save against planar displacement. A separate CON save handles nearby blindness. There is no single save to negate the whole wall. Layer-specific counters, immunity exceptions, and order matter. You can designate creatures to pass safely, but creating the wall in a creature-occupied space makes the spell end without effect.
