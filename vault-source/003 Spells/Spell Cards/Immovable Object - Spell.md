## Decision

A fixed anchor or platform with explicit load limits; high slots buy long-lived infrastructure.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

Touch one object weighing no more than 10 pounds. You and designated creatures can move it normally; a password spoken within 5 feet suppresses the spell for 1 minute. At base level, it supports up to 4, 000 pounds in the air; exceeding that makes it fall. Otherwise, an action and a Strength check against your spell DC can move it up to 10 feet. It does not explicitly give a wearer Restrained or automatically win a grapple. Applying it to worn objects or vehicle parts needs the actual targeting and movement consequences resolved with the DM.

## Upcasting

Slots 4–5: 24 hours, 8, 000-pound load, and +5 to the movement-check DC. Slots 6+:until dispelled, 20, 000-pound load, and +10 DC. A level 3 slot adds no listed improvement; slots above 6 do not add further scaling.
