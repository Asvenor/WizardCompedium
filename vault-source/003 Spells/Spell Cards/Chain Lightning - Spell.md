## Decision

Selective damage helps when allies are mixed into a group. Compare its expensive slot with cheaper area damage and decisive control rather than choosing it solely for the largest damage dice.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose an initial visible target within range, then up to **three different additional targets**, each within **30 feet of the first**. Targets may be creatures or objects; each receives at most one bolt. Every target makes a Dexterity save against **10d8 Lightning**, halved on success.

The geometry branches from the initial target: secondary targets do not extend a successive 30-foot chain. Selecting targets does not damage everything between them.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 6 adds **one secondary target**, not more damage per bolt.
