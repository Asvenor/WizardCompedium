## Decision

Two-stage Incapacitated/Unconscious control; immunity matters.

## Rules that decide the play

Select creatures in the small Sphere. First failed WIS save: Incapacitated until the end of the target's next turn; failing the repeat then causes Unconscious for the remaining duration. Damage or an adjacent creature's action can end the effect. Non-sleepers and creatures immune to Exhaustion automatically save. This is not the 2014 hit-point-pool spell.
