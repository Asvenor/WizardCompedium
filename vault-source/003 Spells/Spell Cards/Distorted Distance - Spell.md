## Rules that decide the play

Create a 60-foot-radius Sphere. On casting, choose an effect separately for each creature you can see there: an Intelligence save against 2d10 Psychic and Difficult Terrain until its turn ends, or +20 Speed until its next turn ends with no save. A successful Intelligence save avoids both hostile effects. On entry or turn end inside, you may choose an effect again, at most once per turn per creature. It does not immobilize targets or require one globally friendly/hostile mode; preserve ally routes and compare it with cheaper control before spending Concentration.
