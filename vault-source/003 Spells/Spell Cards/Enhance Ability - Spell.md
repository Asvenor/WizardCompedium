## Decision

Broad ability-check support for an important sequence of tasks. It does not improve saves or attacks and competes with other Concentration utility.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a creature and choose STR, DEX, INT, WIS, or CHA; the target gains Advantage on checks using that ability. There is no CON option and none of the legacy animal-themed extra effects. Advantage from another source does not stack into extra dice.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot above 2; each can use a different selected ability.
