## Decision

A flexible infiltration or aquatic-access tool. Its ordinary Concentration cost makes it situational beside stronger combat plans; natural weapons alone are rarely the reason to select it for a Wizard.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose one mode; a Magic action can switch modes:

- **Aquatic:** underwater breathing and Swim Speed matching your Speed.
- **Appearance:** physically change your appearance and voice, retaining size, basic body arrangement, and statistics. Another Magic action changes the disguise again.
- **Natural weapons:** claws deal Slashing, fangs/horns Piercing, or hooves Bludgeoning. Their damaging Unarmed Strikes use **1d6 plus your spellcasting modifier**, with that modifier for the attack too, replacing Strength. This does not replace Grapple/Shove save DCs.
