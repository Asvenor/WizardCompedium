## Decision

Long-term death insurance; expensive setup and 120-day maturation.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A 120-day maturation delay means it is not immediate resurrection. At later death, the soul must be free and willing; equipment does not transfer. The vessel must remain undisturbed. The diamond is consumed, but the vessel is not listed as consumed. See [[Clone]] for protected storage, recovery gear, and soul-related failure cases.
