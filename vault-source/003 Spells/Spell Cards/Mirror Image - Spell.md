## Decision

Non-Concentration attack defense.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

After an attack hits, roll one d6 per remaining duplicate; any die of 3+ diverts the hit and destroys one duplicate. With three/two/one duplicates the diversion chances are 26/27, 8/9, and 2/3. Blinded attackers and attackers with Blindsight or Truesight ignore it. No protection against save-based damage; no Concentration needed.
