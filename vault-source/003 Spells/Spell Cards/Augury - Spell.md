## Decision

Thirty-minute course-of-action omen; repeated castings add uncertainty.

**Preparation priority:** Spellbook utility (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Asks about a proposed course of action within the next 30 minutes, not arbitrary future facts. Later changes can invalidate the omen. After the first casting before a Long Rest, the chance of receiving no answer rises cumulatively by 25 percentage points per casting; repeated rituals do not guarantee certainty.
