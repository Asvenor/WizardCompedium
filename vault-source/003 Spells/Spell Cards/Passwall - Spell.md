## Decision

A clean non-Concentration bypass through appropriate construction, especially when a lock is not the real obstacle.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Open a passage on a visible wooden, plaster, or stone surface, up to 5 feet wide, 8 feet high, and 20 feet deep. Metal is not a listed valid surface. It does not destabilize the structure; when the passage ends, remaining occupants/objects are safely ejected to the nearest unoccupied space by the casting surface. It is not a damaging wall-collapse effect.
