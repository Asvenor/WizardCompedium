## Decision

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a willing creature for Mask, or an object not worn/carried for False Aura. Mask chooses another creature type: spells and other magical effects treat the creature as that type. It does not rewrite the creature's entire stat block or grant that type's monster traits. False Aura instead changes how an object registers to magic-aura detection, including apparent magic status or school. Repeating the casting on the same target daily for 30 days makes the illusion last until dispelled.
