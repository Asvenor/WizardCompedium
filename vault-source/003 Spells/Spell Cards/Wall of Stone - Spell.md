## Rules that decide the play

Must merge with and be supported by existing stone. An enclosed creature can make a DEX save and, on success, use its Reaction to escape up to its Speed. Ten minutes of maintained Concentration makes it permanent; earlier ending removes it. Panels have AC 15 and 30 HP per inch of thickness, so it is destructible rather than a second Wall of Force.
