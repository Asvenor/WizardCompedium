## Decision

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature takes 10d6 + 40 Force on a failed DEX save and no damage on success. Reduction to 0 HP by this damage causes disintegration; it is not triggered merely by losing Temporary HP. Nonmagical objects and magical-force creations use separate size/destruction rules. Ordinary magic items are not valid objects for automatic deletion.

## Upcasting

Add 3d6 damage per slot level above 6.
