## Rules that decide the play

On casting and with later Bonus Actions, move the hand up to 60 feet and choose an effect. Fist: melee spell attack for 5d8 Force. Push: Huge-or-smaller target, STR save. Grapple: Huge-or-smaller target, DEX save; later crushing uses a Bonus Action for 4d6 + spellcasting modifier. The object has AC 20 and HP equal to the caster's maximum; it can be destroyed. Its defensive mode grants Half Cover through its space, not universal cover.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 5 adds 2d8 to Clenched Fist and 2d6 to Grasping Hand damage.
