## Decision

One-hour no-Concentration Speed increase.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Adds 10 feet to the target's Speed for an hour without Concentration. Casting before a known expedition avoids spending a combat action, but the duration and slot still matter. Multiple castings on the same creature do not add their identical Speed bonuses.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 1.
