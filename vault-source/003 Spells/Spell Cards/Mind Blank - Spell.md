## Decision

Twenty-four-hour psychic/divination defense.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Protects a willing target from Psychic damage, Charmed, specified mental/location detection, magical information-gathering, remote observation, and mind control. It does not make the creature invisible to ordinary sight, erase mundane evidence, or grant immunity to every mental-ability saving throw.
