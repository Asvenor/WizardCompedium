## Rules that decide the play

Creates a simple melee weapon with which you are proficient, dealing 2d8 Psychic damage on a hit before the normal weapon-attack ability modifier. It has Finesse, Light, and Thrown 20/60. Advantage requires the target to be in Dim Light or Darkness, not merely the caster. After being thrown or dropped, it disappears at that turn's end; a later Bonus Action can recreate it while the spell continues. It has no listed monetary value, so do not assume it satisfies a costed weapon component. It competes with control for Concentration and with other Bonus Actions for recall.

## Upcasting

Weapon damage is 3d8 with a level-3/4 slot, 4d8 with a level-5/6 slot, and 5d8 with a level-7+ slot.
