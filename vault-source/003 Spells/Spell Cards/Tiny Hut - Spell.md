## Decision

Eight-hour shelter; Ritual casting available, but not automatic safety.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A stationary 10-foot Emanation that must fully encapsulate **all creatures in its area** when cast, not merely the intended occupants. Creatures and objects already inside when cast can pass freely; others cannot pass through. It blocks casting spells of level 3 or lower through the boundary and prevents those spells' effects extending inside, not every spell. It is opaque from outside but transparent from inside, with comfortable dry air. Leaving it or recasting ends your hut. Do not import the old nine-creature cap.
