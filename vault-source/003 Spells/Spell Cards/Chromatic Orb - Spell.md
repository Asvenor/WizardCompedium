## Decision

Flexible damage type with a chance of chaining; the reusable diamond is an access cost. Leaps improve its ceiling, not the reliability of hitting one crucial target.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, or Thunder, then make a ranged spell attack for 3d8 of that type. Matching results on at least two damage d8s allow a leap to a different chosen target within 30 feet of the previous one, with a new attack and damage roll. Each creature can be targeted only once per casting.

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 per slot above 1. The maximum number of leaps equals the slot level; each further leap still needs matching damage dice. A level-1 cast can leap once.
