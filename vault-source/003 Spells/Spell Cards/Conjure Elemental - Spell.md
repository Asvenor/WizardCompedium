## Decision

A fixed-area restraining threat, not a summoned monster with a stat block or action economy. Position it at a route the enemy must use.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Place a Large intangible spirit in an unoccupied space. Choose air/Lightning, earth/Thunder, fire/Fire, or water/Cold. When a visible creature enters its space or starts its turn within 5 feet, the caster may force a DEX save if the spirit currently restrains nobody. Failure deals 8d8 and Restrains. The restrained target repeats the save at each turn's start: failure deals 4d8, success releases it. No damage is specified on a successful save. The spirit has no granted movement command, attacks, or creature stat block; it is not automatically eligible for monster-control or Planar Binding tricks.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].

## Upcasting

The damage increases by 1d8 per slot above 5, applying to initial and repeat damage.
