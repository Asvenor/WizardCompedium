## Decision

An area disruption option against enemies immune to Charmed or Frightened. Its strength is bypassing those condition gates; its weakness is randomness and repeated saves.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Each creature in a 10-foot-radius Sphere makes a WIS save. Failure removes its Bonus Actions and Reactions, and it rolls d10 at each turn's start:

| d10 | Behavior |
|---|---|
| 1 | No action; use all movement in a random cardinal direction (d4: north, east, south, west) |
| 2–6 | No movement or actions |
| 7–8 | No movement; one melee attack against a random creature in reach, otherwise no action |
| 9–10 | Chooses its behavior; still no Bonus Action or Reaction |

An affected creature repeats WIS at each turn's end, ending its own effect on success. Damage does not grant a new save. Allies in the area are not exempt.

## Upcasting

The radius grows by 5 feet per slot above 4. This enlarges the affected area rather than adding a chosen target independently of that area.
