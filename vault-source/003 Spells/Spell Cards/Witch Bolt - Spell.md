## Decision

A revised sustained-damage option when the target cannot readily escape range or cover. It competes with control Concentration and later Bonus Actions.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A ranged spell attack deals 2d12 Lightning on a hit. On subsequent turns, a Bonus Action deals 1d12 automatically even if the first attack missed. The spell ends immediately if the target ever leaves 60 feet or has Total Cover from the caster. The current wording does not require taking the follow-up every turn to keep the spell.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].

## Upcasting

Each slot above 1 adds 1d12 to the initial hit only; repeat damage stays 1d12.
