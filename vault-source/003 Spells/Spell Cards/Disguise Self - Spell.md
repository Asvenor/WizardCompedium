## Decision

One-hour appearance disguise; physical inspection can expose it.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

The illusion changes appearance and belongings, not physical dimensions, voice, or touch. Apparent height can shift by one foot, but the basic limb arrangement must match. Physical inspection exposes mismatches; a Study action with INT (Investigation) against the spell DC can discern the disguise.
