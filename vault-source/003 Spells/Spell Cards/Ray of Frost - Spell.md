## Decision

Cold damage plus Speed pressure.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A ranged spell attack against a creature deals 1d8 Cold before scaling and reduces Speed by 10 feet until the start of the caster's next turn. The hit is required for both effects. Compare that short movement window with ally positioning and hazard timing.

## Upcasting

Cantrip damage becomes 2d8 at character level 5, 3d8 at 11, and 4d8 at 17. The Speed reduction remains 10 feet.
