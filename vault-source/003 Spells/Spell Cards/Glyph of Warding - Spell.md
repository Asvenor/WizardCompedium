## Decision

Fixed magical defense or stored spell; 200+ gp consumed diamond.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Moving its surface or container more than 10 feet from the casting location breaks the glyph without triggering it. A stored prepared spell must meet the target/area and level limits, and is cast as part of inscription. Triggered Concentration spells last their full duration under the glyph's specific rule. See [[Glyph of Warding]] for costs, trigger limits, and why a portable buff bag is not automatic.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 3 adds 1d8 to Explosive Runes. A Spell Glyph can instead store a spell up to the level of the slot used for Glyph; cast and pay for the stored spell as well.
