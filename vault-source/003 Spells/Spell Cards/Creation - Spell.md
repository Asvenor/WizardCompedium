## Decision

Temporary physical materials and equipment when their lifetime fits the problem. It cannot supply valid components for another spell.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Create a vegetable- or mineral-matter object fitting a 5-foot Cube, in a form and material previously seen. Vegetable matter lasts 24 hours; stone/crystal 12 hours; precious metals 1 hour; gems 10 minutes; adamantine/mithral 1 minute. Mixed materials use the shortest duration. Using any created object as another spell's Material component causes that other spell to fail. Do not mistake temporary manufactured value for permanent wealth.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Upcasting

The Cube's size increases by 5 feet per slot above 5.
