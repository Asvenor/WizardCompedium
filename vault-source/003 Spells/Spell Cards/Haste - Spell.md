## Rules that decide the play

Doubles Speed, adds +2 AC and Advantage on DEX saves, and gives a restricted extra action: one Attack, Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Utilize. It does not grant a general Magic action. When it ends, the target becomes Incapacitated with Speed 0 until the end of its next turn, which can also break that target's own Concentration.
