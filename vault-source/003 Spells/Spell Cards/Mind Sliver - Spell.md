## Decision

Psychic damage; subtract 1d4 from the target's next save before the end of your next turn.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Choose a visible creature. A failed INT save deals 1d6 Psychic before scaling and penalizes its next save by 1d4 before the end of the caster's next turn. A successful save gives neither damage nor penalty. Coordinate the next save with allies; the penalty can be consumed by an incidental save.

## Upcasting

Cantrip damage becomes 2d6 at character level 5, 3d6 at 11, and 4d6 at 17. The save penalty remains 1d4.
