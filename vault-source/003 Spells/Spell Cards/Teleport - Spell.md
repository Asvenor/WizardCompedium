## Rules that decide the play

Transports the caster and up to eight willing visible creatures within 10 feet, or one eligible Large-or-smaller object, to a known same-plane destination. A permanent circle whose sigil you know or a linked object taken from the location in the last six months guarantees an on-target result on the table. Merely seeing a place once does not guarantee arrival.

| Familiarity | Mishap | Similar area | Off target | On target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Known permanent circle | — | — | — | 01–00 |
| Linked object | — | — | — | 01–00 |
| Very familiar | 01–05 | 06–13 | 14–24 | 25–00 |
| Seen casually | 01–33 | 34–43 | 44–53 | 54–00 |
| Viewed once or described | 01–43 | 44–53 | 54–73 | 74–00 |
| False destination | 01–50 | 51–00 | — | — |

These are **d100 results**, with 00 meaning 100. Very familiar includes a frequently visited, carefully studied, or currently visible location. Seen casually means seen more than once without that familiarity; viewed once or described includes magical viewing or a description/map. A false destination does not actually exist as intended.

A mishap deals **3d10 Force** to each traveler/object and rerolls the table, so repeated mishaps are possible. Off target means **2d12 miles** away in a random direction; similar area sends you to the nearest thematically or visually similar place. Neither is controlled fallback routing. See [[Teleportation & Planar Travel]] for broader transport choices.
