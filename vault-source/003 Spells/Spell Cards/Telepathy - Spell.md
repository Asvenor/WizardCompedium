## Decision

Twenty-four-hour two-way same-plane sensory communication with one willing familiar creature.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Links the caster and one familiar, willing creature on the same plane for 24 hours; leaving that plane ends the link. It exchanges words and sensory messages, not unrestricted mind reading or remote spellcasting. Compare the lower-level Telepathic Bond when a whole party needs ordinary same-plane communication.
