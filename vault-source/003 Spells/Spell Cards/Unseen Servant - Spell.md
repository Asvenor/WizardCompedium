## Decision

One-hour simple-task force; Bonus Action commands and a 60-foot tether.

**Preparation priority:** Spellbook utility (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

A Bonus Action on the caster's turn commands up to 15 feet of movement and an object interaction. It continues a simple task until finished, cannot attack, and has only 1 HP. A commanded task that would take it beyond 60 feet ends the spell. It is not a creature with a full adventurer's action economy.
