## Decision

A very large Radiant burst can blind many enemies without occupying Concentration. The radius creates a serious friendly-fire problem, and its costly slot merits comparison with smaller control spells.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment).

## Rules that decide the play

Sunlight fills a **60-foot-radius Sphere** centered within range. Creatures inside make Constitution saves: failure deals **12d6 Radiant** and inflicts **Blinded for up to 1 minute**; success deals half damage without blindness.

Affected creatures repeat the Constitution save at each turn's end, ending their blindness on success. The burst also dispels spell-created Darkness in its area. The current entry does not impose the legacy special saving-throw Disadvantage on Undead or Oozes.
