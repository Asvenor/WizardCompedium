## Decision

A costly fixed-site defense with devastating repeated exposure. Position it around a genuine chokepoint and plan ally evacuation; trigger exemptions do not say that allies inside the activated area ignore its effect.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Inscribe a glyph no wider than 10 feet on a surface or inside a closable object. An object moved more than 10 feet from the casting location breaks its glyph harmlessly. Noticing the glyph takes a Wisdom (Perception) check against your spell save DC. Set the trigger, including creature-type restrictions or exceptions such as a password.

Activation produces a dimly lit 60-foot-radius Sphere for 10 minutes. Every creature there on activation, entering for the first time on a turn, or ending its turn there is targeted, at most once per turn. Choose the effect when casting:

| Effect | Save | Failed-save result |
|---|---|---|
| Death | CON | 10d10 Necrotic damage; success takes half |
| Discord | WIS | 1 minute arguing, unable to communicate meaningfully, with Disadvantage on attacks and ability checks |
| Fear | WIS | Frightened for 1 minute; move at least 30 feet away each turn if able |
| Pain | CON | Incapacitated for 1 minute |
| Sleep | WIS | Unconscious for 10 minutes; damage or an action shaking the target wakes it |
| Stunning | WIS | Stunned for 1 minute |

The non-damage modes list no repeat saves to end their individual effect early. Entering/staying can cause new targeting; this is not a portable glyph grenade.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].
