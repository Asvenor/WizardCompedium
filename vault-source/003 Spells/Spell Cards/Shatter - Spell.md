## Decision

Thunder area damage; Constructs save with Disadvantage and unattended nonmagical objects take damage.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

The 10-foot-radius Sphere deals 3d8 Thunder, with CON for half damage. Constructs have Disadvantage; other creatures do not gain that disadvantage merely from being made of a hard substance unless another rule says so. Unworn, uncarried nonmagical objects in the area also take damage.

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 damage per slot level above 2.
