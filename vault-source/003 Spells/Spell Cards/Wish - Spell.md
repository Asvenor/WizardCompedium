## Decision

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Duplicating a spell of level 8 or lower avoids Wish stress and its normal casting requirements, including costly components; the resulting effect still uses its own ongoing rules, including Concentration when required. Every listed non-replication option, not just an extravagant custom wish, incurs stress and a 33% chance of permanently losing Wish. See [[Wish]] and [[Intelligence above 20]] for feat replacement and infrastructure limits.
