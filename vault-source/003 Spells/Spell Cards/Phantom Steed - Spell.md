## Decision

Fast travel/mobility; fragile and temporary.

**Preparation priority:** Spellbook utility (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Uses Riding Horse statistics with Speed 100 feet and travel of 13 miles per hour. Any damage ends the spell; the one-minute fade is a dismount provision, not a guaranteed extra minute of full-speed combat use. See [[Movement & Travel]] for mounted-action limits.
