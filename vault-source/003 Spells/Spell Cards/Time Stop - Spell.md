## Decision

Grants extra personal turns for setup; ends early when affecting other creatures.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Gain 1d4+1 personal turns. It ends when your actions/effects affect another creature or something another creature wears/carries, or you move over 1,000 feet from the casting point. It does not let you damage enemies throughout all the extra turns.
