## Decision

Links two creatures' damage and healing; powerful only when the paired targets and save odds justify the slot.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

Choose two visible creatures within range. Both make Constitution saves, with Disadvantage if they are within 30 feet of each other; either may willingly fail. If either succeeds, the spell has no effect. If both fail, damage dealt or HP restored to one is mirrored to the other regardless of their separation. The tether ends on both if either reaches 0 HP or the spell ends on either. Do not assume unlimited recursive damage from mirrored damage; resolve that extreme reading with the DM before building around it. Two unwilling saves, Concentration, and the consumed 250 GP component are substantial costs.
