## Decision

Expensive single-target aging and damage; not a universal replacement for Wish or encounter-wide ninth-level options.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature makes a Constitution save against 10d12 Necrotic damage, half on success. Failure also ages it to within 30 days of death by old age, imposes Disadvantage on attacks/checks/saves, and halves walking speed. Only Wish or Greater Restoration cast with a level 9 slot reverses these aging effects. It is not immediate old-age death, and ordinary Greater Restoration is insufficient. Legendary Resistance or a successful save avoids the aging rider after you have spent the component.
