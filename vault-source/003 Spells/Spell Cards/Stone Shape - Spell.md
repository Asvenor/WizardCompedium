## Decision

Reshape stone for access, escape, fortification, or environmental utility.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

Reshapes a Medium-or-smaller stone object or a stone section at most five feet in every dimension. Up to two hinges and a latch are possible, not precise machinery. It does not create missing stone, reshape metal, or open an arbitrarily thick wall.
