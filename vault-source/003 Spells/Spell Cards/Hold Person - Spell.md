## Decision

A low-slot shutdown when dangerous Humanoids are expected and allies can exploit the brief opening. Creature type is the gate: human-shaped is not the same as Humanoid.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

One visible Humanoid makes a WIS save; failure gives it the Paralyzed condition. It repeats the save at each turn's end, ending its own effect on success. Damage does not itself grant another save.

Paralyzed immunity and Legendary Resistance remain counters. Under [[Conditions]], attacks have Advantage, but automatic Critical Hits require a hit from within 5 feet; ranged attacks from farther away do not gain that benefit. Arrange allied follow-up before the target's next end-of-turn save.

## Upcasting

One additional Humanoid per slot level above 2. Each must meet the spell's range and sight requirements; the 2024 entry has no separate 30-foot spacing limit.
