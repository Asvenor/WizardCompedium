## Decision

Persistent heavily obscured area damage; Poison defenses, strong wind, and the drifting cloud limit its reliability.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Create a 20-foot-radius Sphere. Creatures initially inside save for 5d8 Poison, half on success. The same save is triggered when the Sphere moves into a creature's space, a creature enters, or it ends its turn there, but only once per turn per creature. The Sphere moves 10 feet away from the caster at the start of each caster turn. Strong wind disperses it and ends the spell. Holding one's breath is not a listed immunity.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [[Concentration Strategy]].

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 per slot above 5.
