## Decision

Buy reliable lore when it can change a major decision. Research the subject first: existing knowledge improves the detail returned, while an obscure subject can waste the casting.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Name or describe a famous person, place, or object. The DM supplies accurate significant lore, potentially including secrets, but may phrase it figuratively. More prior knowledge yields greater precision. If the subject is not actually famous, the spell fails. It is neither a ritual nor a universal answer to any question; budget 250 GP consumed per attempt plus the reusable 200 GP minimum ivory set.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].
