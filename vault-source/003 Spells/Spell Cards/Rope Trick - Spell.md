## Decision

One-hour extradimensional refuge.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

The extradimensional space holds eight Medium-or-smaller creatures. Attacks, spells, and other effects cannot cross its portal, so it is not a protected firing position. Its contents drop out on expiry. A one-hour duration leaves no slack for a full one-hour Short Rest once entry/setup time is included; do not assume a guaranteed completed rest.
