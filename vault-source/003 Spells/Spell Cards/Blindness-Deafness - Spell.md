## Decision

Non-Concentration debuff that can run beside a major control effect. CON saves are often a weak target choice against physically tough enemies.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

One visible creature saves against Blinded or Deafened, chosen on casting. It repeats the CON save at the end of each turn, ending the spell on success. Blindness can deny sight-dependent abilities but does not erase other senses or automatically immobilize a target. The current spell is Transmutation and has 120-foot range.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot above 2.
