## Decision

Close-range Emanation damage plus an action-fired lightning Line; two different saves and targeting patterns.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [[Concentration Strategy]] Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [[Spellcasting Rules]].

## Rules that decide the play

The 10-foot Emanation can force a Constitution save when it enters a creature's space, a creature enters it, or a creature ends its turn there: failure takes 3d6 Lightning plus 3d6 Thunder and is Deafened for 1 minute; success takes half damage only. You choose whether to force this save. A separate Magic action fires a 60-by-5-foot Line: every creature in that Line makes a Dexterity save against 6d6 Lightning, half on success, with no printed ally exemption. The current Emanation text has no explicit once-per-turn cap; repeated-entry optimization needs exact movement/trigger adjudication. Close-range exposure and the eighth-level Concentration cost are significant.
