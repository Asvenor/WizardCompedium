## Decision

A flexible debuff with a strong level-5 non-Concentration breakpoint. Touch range, an initial Wisdom save, and the setup Action are the price; do not treat it as free damage added to an opening spell.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a creature; on failed WIS save, choose one curse:

- Disadvantage on one chosen ability's checks and saving throws.
- Disadvantage on attacks against you.
- In combat, a WIS save at each turn's start; failure forces the Dodge action that turn.
- Your damage from an attack roll or spell adds 1d8 Necrotic to the target.

The extra-damage option belongs to **your** damage, not every ally's or summoned creature's damage. It does not establish every proposed simultaneous-damage multiplier. The 2024 spell supplies no custom-curse option; invented effects require the DM.

The recurring Dodge save controls that turn, not the curse's duration.
