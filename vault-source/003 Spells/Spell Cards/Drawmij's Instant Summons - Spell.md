## Decision

Expensive insurance for a valuable spellbook or other qualifying object. It does not forcibly steal the marked item from a holder.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch the sapphire and an object weighing at most 10 pounds and no longer than 6 feet in any dimension. Each casting needs a different sapphire. Later, a Magic action naming the object and crushing its sapphire recalls it across physical/planar distance and ends the spell. If another creature holds or carries the object, recall fails and instead reveals that creature's identity and current location. The sapphire is spent on activation, not reusable recall fuel.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [[Spellcasting Rules]].
