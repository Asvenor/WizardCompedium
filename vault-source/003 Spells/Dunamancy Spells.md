# Dunamancy Spells
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Spell Finder]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


Dunamancy manipulates the fundamental forces of **time, probability, gravity, and possibility**.

This reference supports the vault's **Chronurgy** builds. The source's Chronurgy/Graviturgy themes and access distinctions below describe the spell collection, not additional supported build variants; gravity-themed spells can still be legal Chronurgy choices.

> [!info] Access
> Dunamancy spells have special access restrictions depending on **Chronurgy, Graviturgy, source permissions, and campaign rules**.  
> Both Wildemount Wizard subclasses have ready access to the chapter's Dunamancy spells. The Chronurgy/Graviturgy labels describe the source's themes, not exclusive spell lists. Other spellcasters require DM-approved access; finding a listing does not automatically add the spells to their class list. [Official Dunamancy rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/egtw/character-options-subclasses#DunamancySpells).
>
> Pulse Wave is unmarked/shared in the source; it is grouped with gravity effects below for navigation, not restricted to Graviturgy.
