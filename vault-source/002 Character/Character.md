Static starting frameworks for **Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer**. Every subclass supports pure Wizard and Artificer, Fighter, or Cleric dips; no subclass or class structure is the flagship. The normal caster arrays apply to the six non-Bladesinger subclasses. Bladesinger has an unarmored array and a separately costed Cleric array.

## Starting Stat Blocks

> Assumes **Point Buy** before Background increases.

| Build                                        | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| -------------------------------------------- | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| **Pure Wizard**                              |   8 |  14 |  15 |  15 |  10 |   8 |
| **Artificer 1 / Wizard X**                   |   8 |  14 |  15 |  15 |  10 |   8 |
| **Cleric 1 / Wizard X**                      |   8 |  14 |  13 |  15 |  13 |   9 |
| **Fighter 1 or 2 / Wizard X**                |   8 |  14 |  15 |  15 |  10 |   8 |
| **Bladesinger — pure / Artificer / Fighter** |   8 |  15 |  14 |  15 |  10 |   8 |
| **Bladesinger — Cleric dip**                 |   8 |  15 |  12 |  15 |  13 |   8 |

Every listed row costs 27 points. The Cleric rows explicitly fund WIS 13 rather than silently adding it to another array. For heavy armor, only invest in STR when the chosen armor and acceptable speed tradeoff require it; it is an equipment choice within a supported dip, not an additional build family. Bladesong still permits no worn armor or Shield.

For legal static ability/feat schedules across all seven subclasses and every class structure, see [[Build Decision Engine#Static ability and feat frameworks]]. [[Worked Wizard Builds]] adds four detailed illustrations rather than four preferred routes. [[Spell Progression Hub]] supplies every supported combination's exact no-copy spell acquisitions.

## Pure Wizard

**Priority:** INT > CON ≈ DEX

### Example Start

**8 STR / 14 DEX / 15 CON / 15 INT / 10 WIS / 8 CHA**

Background increases should normally push:

**INT → 17**  
**CON → 16**

### Main Advantage

Fastest possible access to:

- Higher-level Wizard spells
- Subclass features
- Feats
- Spell Mastery
- Signature Spells

## Artificer 1 / Wizard X

**Start Artificer.**

### Example Start

**8 STR / 14 DEX / 15 CON / 15 INT / 10 WIS / 8 CHA**

Target:

**17 INT / 16 CON / 14 DEX**

### Gains

- Constitution saving throw proficiency
- Medium Armor
- Shields
- Additional prepared spells
- Tool proficiency

### Cost

**Wizard spell progression delayed by 1 level.**

## Fighter 1 or 2 / Wizard X

**Start Fighter.**

### Example Start

**8 STR / 14 DEX / 15 CON / 15 INT / 10 WIS / 8 CHA**

Target:

**17 INT / 16 CON / 14 DEX**

### Gains

- Constitution saving throw proficiency
- Armor
- Shields
- Fighting Style
- Weapon proficiency

### Cost

**Wizard spell progression delayed by 1 or 2 levels, matching the Fighter dip depth.**

A second Fighter level provides **Action Surge**, **Tactical Mind**, and another Wizard-level delay. Its additional action cannot be the **Magic action**. Fighter 1 / Wizard 19 can reach the Wizard 19 feat; Fighter 2 / Wizard 18 cannot reach that feat or Signature Spells within character levels 1–20. Both spell calendars are printed in [[Multiclass Spell Timing]].

## Cleric 1 / Wizard X

**Wizard-first reference timing:** reach Wizard 5, then take Cleric 1. This retains starting INT/WIS saves and reaches the first level-3 Wizard spells before the dip; it does not grant CON-save proficiency.

### Example Start

**8 STR / 14 DEX / 13 CON / 15 INT / 13 WIS / 9 CHA**

A background adding +2 INT and +1 CON produces **17 INT / 14 CON / 14 DEX / 13 WIS**. WIS 13 meets the Cleric prerequisite and is paid for with lower starting CON.

### Gains and cost

Cleric 1 adds light/medium armor and shields, four separately prepared level-1 Cleric spells, three Cleric cantrips, and Divine Order. Protector can add heavy armor and martial weapons; Thaumaturge instead adds a Cleric cantrip and its WIS-based skill benefit. Cleric spellcasting uses **WIS**, while Wizard casting still uses INT. There is **no Cleric subclass at level 1**.

Cleric contributes a full level to combined slots, but the dip still delays subsequent Wizard spells/features one character level. Bless competes with Wizard Concentration; slot-cast Healing Word obeys the one-slot-per-turn rule. Compare these specific support benefits with the other supported class structures in [[Build Decision Engine#Is Cleric 1 still optimal in 2024]].

## Bladesinger

**Priority:** INT ≈ DEX > CON

This framework uses the revised **Heroes of Faerûn (2025)** subclass. Bladesong requires no armor and no Shield. While it is active, attacks with proficient weapons can use INT for both attack and damage rolls; DEX remains important for unarmored AC, Initiative, Dexterity saves, and attacks outside Bladesong. Do not import Tasha's light-armor permission into this version. [Revised Bladesinger](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#BladesingerWizard).

### Bladesinger example start

**8 STR / 15 DEX / 14 CON / 15 INT / 10 WIS / 8 CHA**

Target:

**17 INT / 16 DEX / 14 CON**

Pure Wizard and Artificer/Fighter-start Bladesingers can use this 27-point foundation. The Cleric alternative needs **8 / 15 / 12 / 15 / 13 / 8** before background, reaching **8 / 16 / 12 / 17 / 13 / 8** after +2 INT/+1 DEX. Its lower CON is a genuine cost, not a free addition of WIS 13.

Dip armor may support a separate non-Bladesong mode, but cannot be combined with active Bladesong. Artificer/Fighter starts still add CON saves; Cleric still adds divine support. These are options to compare, not a default dip recommendation.

Bladesinger values Dexterity considerably more than an armored caster because it contributes to:

- AC
- Initiative
- Weapon attacks outside Bladesong when using DEX; active revised Bladesong can use INT instead
- Dexterity saves
