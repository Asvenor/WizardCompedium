# Arcana Unleashed Builds

Three static examples for the supported subclasses that use **Arcana Unleashed** options alongside the **2024 Player's Handbook**: revised Conjurer, revised Necromancer, and the 2024 Illusionist with optional Illusion Adept. These are illustrative spell/feat configurations, not flagship builds. All seven supported subclasses have pure Wizard, Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric routes in [[Spell Progression Hub]]; using a pure example here does not favor pure Wizard over a dip.

For exact interaction boundaries and ruling-sensitive combinations, see [[Arcana Unleashed Combinations]]. For full baseline spellbook progression and ordinary Wizard play, see [[Worked Wizard Builds]] and [[Prepared Loadouts]].

For equal-level, equal-slot damage comparisons with accuracy and combat setup included, see [[Summon Damage Benchmarks]].

## Choose the payoff

| Route | First distinctive payoff | Major breakpoint | Best fit / main cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjurer 20** | Teleportation at 3; durable summons at 6 | Damage-safe Conjuration Concentration at 10; two eligible spirits at 14 | Sustained summoned damage; the signature doubling arrives late and consumes substantial slots |
| **Necromancer 20** | Undead familiar at 3; improved Undead at 6 | Compact two-companion package at 8 | Strong repeat attacks without requiring a huge army; book-in-hand, positioning, components, and minion handling matter |
| **Illusionist 20 + Illusion Adept** | Improved illusions at 3; component omission at 4 | Illusory Self at 10; Illusory Reality at 14 | Covert control, infiltration, and creative obstacles; illusion discovery and spell-specific limits remain |

## Common foundation and legal feats

These three examples remain **pure Wizard from character level 1 to 20** so their spell and feature budgets can be read directly. Their supported Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric alternatives have complete acquisition tables in [[Spell Progression Hub]], with legal ability/feat frameworks in [[Build Decision Engine#Static ability and feat frameworks]]. A dip buys different features and delays the named Wizard milestones; it is neither mandatory nor disfavored.

- **27-point buy:** STR 8, DEX 14, CON 15, INT 15, WIS 10, CHA 8. Criminal adds +2 INT and +1 CON, producing **8 / 14 / 16 / 17 / 10 / 8**. Criminal supplies Alert. Human supplies **Tough** and Perception proficiency. This is a durable, simple baseline, not an assumption of extra General feats.
- **Skills:** Arcana and Investigation from Wizard; Arcana Expertise through Scholar at Wizard 2.
- **Defense:** no armor or magic items assumed. [[Mage Armor - Spell|Mage Armor]] starts at AC 15 with DEX 14; Shield, cover, range, and positioning remain important. Tough adds HP, not better saving throws.
- **Spell acquisition:** use the baseline book as a starting point, but spend legal level-up choices on the route's priority spells below. Savant additions must match that subclass's school. A spell being in the book does not automatically make it prepared; an explicitly always-prepared feature is separate. These priority lists are not extra free spells or a complete daily preparation list.
- **Components:** expensive materials must actually be obtained. No guaranteed loot, free high-level components, bonus ASIs, or above-20 INT items are built into the examples.

| Wizard level | Conjurer / Necromancer | Illusionist + optional Adept |
|---:|---|---|
| 4 | War Caster: **INT 18** | Illusion Adept: **INT 18** |
| 8 | ASI: **INT 20** | War Caster: **INT 19** |
| 12 | Resilient (CON): **CON 17**, CON-save proficiency | Telekinetic: **INT 20** |
| 16 | Mage Slayer: **DEX 15**, mental-save protection | Resilient (CON): **CON 17**, CON-save proficiency |
| 19 | Boon of Dimensional Travel: **DEX 16** | Boon of Dimensional Travel: **CON 18** |

The optional Adept example purchases its specialty before reaching INT 20: INT 19 still has a +4 modifier, so its spell attacks/DCs trail the INT-20 examples by 1 from levels 8–11. Telekinetic adds a useful Bonus Action option, not another action or a second Concentration spell. For earlier INT 20, choose ASI at 8 and rebuild the later feat/ability choices; do not count both progressions at once. The Conjurer's Resilient choice protects general CON saves and other spell schools even after Focused Conjuration; it is not required to prevent damage from breaking a Conjuration spell at level 10+.

At Wizard 17 all three examples obtain level-9 Wizard spell access; at 18 and 20 they retain Spell Mastery and Signature Spells. Use the legal Action-time Mastery examples in [[Worked Wizard Builds]], rather than treating Shield or Misty Step as eligible. Different Epic Boons can replace the example at 19; see [[Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List]] and recalculate the ability point.

These are complete static examples using the acquisition routes above, not blanks to fill or lists to maintain. Replace priorities for the actual mission; one prepared spell does not entitle the Wizard to concentrate on several effects simultaneously.

|---|---|---|
| Conjurer | Battle Familiar; Summon Fey; Hypnotic Pattern; Dispel Magic | None |

| Illusionist | Invisibility; Mirror Image; Hypnotic Pattern; Dispel Magic | Silent Image; Phantasmal Force; Major Image; Summon Beast; Summon Fey |

### Wizard 9 — fourteen ordinary preparations

Every route prepares these **nine**: **Shield, Mage Armor, Feather Fall, Web, Misty Step, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Dimension Door, Hypnotic Pattern**. Add its **five** entries below. This retains emergency defense and non-specialist control even when the signature tactic is inappropriate.

| Route | Five further ordinary preparations | Feature preparations available in addition |
|---|---|---|

## Conjurer — two durable spirits

**Plan:** preserve full Wizard progression, develop one strong summon first, and reach Splintered Summons at Wizard 14. Prioritize [[Summon Fey - Spell|Summon Fey]] when level-3 spells become available, with [[Battle Familiar - Spell|Battle Familiar]] as a separate non-Concentration option. Keep control spells available: summoning is not automatically the best answer to every encounter.

| Wizard level | What changes in play |
|---:|---|
| 3 | Bonus Action teleport up to 30 feet, or swap with a willing Medium-or-smaller creature; INT-modifier uses per Long Rest |
| 6 | Teleport range becomes 60 feet. A creature summoned/created by a slot-cast Conjuration spell receives **2 × Wizard level Temporary HP**, with broad Resistance while that particular pool remains |
| 10 | Taking damage cannot break Concentration on Conjuration spells |
| 14 | One eligible slot-cast Conjuration spirit spell produces **two same-kind creatures**, each at half normal current/max HP; once per Long Rest, or restore the use with an additional level-5+ slot |

### Wizard 14 example: two Fuming Fey

Use a **level-6 slot** for Summon Fey and apply Splintered Summons. Choose Fuming for both spirits. Each has **AC 18, 30 HP, and 28 Temporary HP**. Halving current/max HP does not halve the separate Temporary HP grant. Each makes three attacks; each hit deals **2d6 + 9 Force** damage. Six non-critical hits average **96 damage in total**. This is an **all-hit illustration, not expected DPR**: accuracy, enemy defenses, lost turns, and critical hits are not modeled. Fuming Fey Step gives Advantage on only the next attack that turn, not all three.

A separately precast level-4 Battle Familiar can add two hits averaging **23**, for **119 all-hit damage** across the three companions, before the Wizard's own action. This requires a second casting and slot, a legal familiar, and favorable timing; the familiar acts on its own Initiative, while the Fey act immediately after the Wizard. Do not budget Conjurer Temporary HP for merely empowering an existing familiar: that mode does not newly summon/create it.

**Costs and limits:** Summon Fey needs a reusable **300+ GP gilded flower**, Battle Familiar a reusable **25+ GP diamond**, and the underlying Find Familiar consumes **10+ GP incense** when cast. Both Fey share the same Concentration spell; Incapacitated, another Concentration effect, or other normal ending conditions can still remove them. Durable Summons does **not** resist Force, Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant, and its Resistance ends when its specific Temporary HP are gone. At Wizard 14, the level-6 slot is scarce; an additional level-5+ slot restores the split feature, not the spent summoning slot.

**Do not combine:** Splintered Summons with Summon Undead or Animate Dead; those are Necromancy. Do not assume two Battle Familiars: the one-familiar restriction remains. Conjuration Adept is optional; its concentration-save benefit overlaps heavily with Focused Conjuration after level 10.

Sources: [revised Conjurer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#Conjurer), [Summon Fey](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#SummonFey), [Battle Familiar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/3360242-battle-familiar), [Find Familiar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#FindFamiliar).

## Necromancer — compact companions or an army

**Plan:** at Wizard 3 choose an explicitly **Undead** normal-form familiar, such as an Undead Owl. Learn Battle Familiar normally; it is Conjuration, so Necromancy Savant cannot supply it. Acquire [[Summon Undead - Spell|Summon Undead]] at Wizard 5. At 6 the subclass adds the damage and durability that make repeated Undead attacks valuable; [[Animate Dead - Spell|Animate Dead]] becomes always prepared with one free casting per Long Rest.

### Wizard 8 example: two companions, one Concentration

With **INT 20**, hold the spellbook and keep the controlled Undead within **60 feet** for Withering Strike. Empower the existing Undead familiar with a **level-4 Battle Familiar**, then cast a **level-4 Summon Undead**, choosing Skeletal. These are two separate castings and consume both ordinary level-4 slots available at Wizard 8.

| Companion | Attacks and arithmetic | All-hit average |
|---|---|---:|
| Existing Undead familiar empowered by Battle Familiar | Two Rends, each **1d8 + 3 + 4 Force + 5 Necrotic** | **33** |
| Skeletal Undead Spirit | Two Grave Bolts, each **2d4 + 3 + 4 + 5 Necrotic** | **34** |
| **Combined** | Four successful non-critical attacks across their turns | **67** |

The familiar retains its Undead creature type when empowered, allowing Withering Strike. The Skeletal Spirit has **39 HP**: 30 from the level-4 spell plus **INT 5 + half Wizard level 4**. A Flyer familiar receives **30 Temporary HP** from the level-4 empowerment, but retains its original underlying HP/Hit Dice. It does **not** receive the subclass's Necromancy-created HP bonus: Battle Familiar is Conjuration. Healing underlying HP does not refill its Temporary HP, and losing that empowerment pool ends the upgrade.

The **67 is not accuracy-adjusted DPR** and excludes the Wizard's action. The familiar uses its own Initiative; the spirit acts immediately after the Wizard. Summon Undead takes Concentration; Battle Familiar does not. Budget a reusable **300+ GP gilded skull**, the **25+ GP diamond**, and the original familiar's consumed incense. One prepared control spell cannot be concentrated on simultaneously with the spirit.

### Army alternative from Wizard 6

Undead Thralls raises Animate Dead's effective level by one, including its free casting. A base/free cast therefore creates **three new Undead or renews control of six existing ones**—not six new creatures. At Wizard 6 / INT 18, a current Skeleton created with the book held has **20 HP** (13 + 4 + 3); its Shortbow hit averages **10.5** damage including Withering Strike. Three hits average **31.5**, before accuracy and enemy mitigation.

This trades compact handling for more bodies, corpses/bones, equipment, commands, and renewal logistics. Control must be renewed **before** its 24-hour period expires. More sustained summons are not free daily resources: slot-based renewals compete with adventuring magic, and army access must fit the campaign. See [[Animate Dead - Necromancer]].

**Later levels:** Harvest Undead at 10 converts a controlled Undead into healing when its precise Bloodied trigger occurs; this uses the Reaction. At 14, Bolster Undead helps creatures created/summoned by **Necromancy**, not the Conjuration familiar. Extinguish Undead has a saving-throw-DC omission in the published feature; do not base this blueprint on an assumed automatic explosion chain. Resolve that narrow question through [[Arcana Unleashed Combinations]]. The revised subclass does not grant the legacy Command Undead feature.

## Illusionist — subtle control and credible deception

**Plan:** use the 2024 Illusionist's range and illusion features, then add **Illusion Adept at 4**. Prioritize [[Phantasmal Force - Spell|Phantasmal Force]], [[Hypnotic Pattern - Spell|Hypnotic Pattern]], and useful image/infiltration spells. These are alternatives for different encounters, not effects all maintained at once.

| Wizard level | Practical breakpoint |
|---:|---|
| 3 | Illusions can omit Verbal components; qualifying Illusion spells with range 10+ feet gain **60 feet of range**. Improved Minor Illusion combines sound/image and can use a Bonus Action |
| 4 | Illusion Adept permits broader component omission on **slot-cast Illusions**, and imposes Disadvantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to discern the illusions |
| 5 | Hypnotic Pattern supplies a strong area-control option; its initial saving throw is unchanged by the feat |
| 6 | Summon Beast and Summon Fey are always prepared and can be cast as Illusions; the subclass's free versions halve creature HP |
| 10 | Illusory Self can turn one qualifying attack hit into a miss with the Reaction; it is a limited-use defense, not permanent protection |
| 14 | Illusory Reality can temporarily make an eligible object in a slot-cast illusion real |

**What the Adept actually adds:** omit V/S and unpriced, unconsumed Material components for the qualifying slot cast. A cast with no remaining components does not meet the 2024 Counterspell component trigger. This is not blanket immunity to antimagic, later dispelling, or visible consequences. An Illusion-converted Summon Fey still has a costly material requirement; neither the feat nor changing its school erases that requirement. The Adept's slot condition also does not cover a free subclass casting.

**Checks are not saves:** the feat makes the relevant Investigation checks harder, but does not impose Disadvantage on Hypnotic Pattern's initial WIS save or Phantasmal Force's initial INT save. Read each spell's own interaction/discovery clause. A convincing image is not automatically a physical wall. At 14, Illusory Reality's object lasts one minute and cannot deal damage or impose conditions; do not present an illusory object as an unrestricted lethal trap or free permanent item.

**Trade-off:** the specialty is available early, but the feat schedule reaches INT 20 at 12, not 8. If the campaign mostly rewards direct saves and rarely uses deception, a broader control Wizard may get more from earlier INT or a different feat. This is an optimization choice, not a rules weakness in the subclass.

Sources: [2024 Illusionist](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Illusionist), [Illusion Adept](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#IllusionAdept), [Counterspell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Counterspell), [Hypnotic Pattern](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#HypnoticPattern), [Phantasmal Force](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#PhantasmalForce).

## Selection rule

Compare **Conjurer** for durable spirit summons and teleportation, **Necromancer** for Undead attacks with accepted companion logistics, and **Illusionist** for deception and subtle control. These are three roles within the seven-subclass compendium, not a ranking of its builds. None needs an exploit to function. Preserve a broad Wizard spellbook, and treat an opponent's immunity, terrain, or a short campaign horizon as a reason to change tactics rather than forcing the signature combination.
