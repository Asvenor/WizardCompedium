# Wizard Subclasses

This compendium supports **Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer**. Each has pure Wizard, revised Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric spell routes in [[Spell Progression Hub]]; Fighter 1 and Fighter 2 are distinct options. **There is no flagship subclass or preferred class structure.** The table is alphabetical, not a tier ranking.

## Supported subclass comparison

The role and tradeoff columns are optimization analysis, not additional rules. Compare the exact source version and the levels the campaign will actually reach.

| Subclass and source | Tactical role | Milestones affected by a dip | Practical cost or limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Abjurer — 2024 PHB** | Damage buffering, ally protection, and anti-spell defense | Arcane Ward at 3, Projected Ward at 6, Spell Breaker at 10, Spell Resistance at 14 | Ward size follows Wizard level; refilling spends slots. Defensive Reactions compete with Shield and Counterspell. |
| **Bladesinger — Heroes of Faerûn (2025)** | Mobile unarmored casting and weapon follow-up | Bladesong at 3, Extra Attack at 6, Song of Defense at 10, Song of Victory at 14 | Bladesong requires no armor and no Shield. Dip armor is an alternate inactive mode, not an AC increase during Bladesong. |
| **Chronurgy — Explorer's Guide to Wildemount** | Initiative, reactive rerolls, stored spells, and outcome control | Initial legacy features at 3 on the revised base, later features at 6/10/14 | Campaign permission and legacy compatibility matter; no revised Savant spell grants. Stored spells and Convergent Future have resource limits. |
| **Conjurer — Arcana Unleashed** | Teleportation, durable summons, and sustained companion damage | Teleportation at 3, summon defense at 6, damage-safe Conjuration Concentration at 10, summon split at 14 | Slot costs, components, positioning, and handling remain; the split applies only to qualifying spirit spells. |
| **Diviner — 2024 PHB** | Predetermined D20 outcomes, information, and slot recovery | Portent at 3, Expert Divination at 6, The Third Eye at 10, Greater Portent at 14 | Portent must be chosen before the roll and does not bypass Legendary Resistance. Recovery needs an eligible spent Divination slot. |
| **Illusionist — 2024 PHB** | Deception, ranged illusion control, summon access, and reactive defense | Improved Illusions at 3, Phantasmal Creatures at 6, Illusory Self at 10, Illusory Reality at 14 | Each illusion retains its own discovery rules. Optional Illusion Adept is a feat configuration within this subclass, not a separate eighth subclass. |
| **Necromancer — Arcana Unleashed** | Controlled Undead, repeat attacks, and necromancy support | Familiar/resistance support at 3, Undead Thralls at 6, Harvest Undead at 10, army support at 14 | Several benefits require the book in hand. Corpses, commands, control renewal, components, and campaign acceptance are actual costs. |

**All seven support the same four class structures.** Pure Wizard keeps Wizard milestones on schedule. Artificer 1 and Cleric 1 contribute to combined slots but delay Wizard spell access and features. Fighter 1 delays both; Fighter 2 adds a second Wizard-level delay. A subclass's role does not establish a universally superior dip. Use [[Build Decision Engine#Static ability and feat frameworks]] for legal ability/feat examples and [[Multiclass Spell Timing]] for the calendar.

## Core subclass operating limits

| Current subclass | What matters when comparing builds | Common overstatement to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Abjurer** | Ward HP uses **Wizard level**; slot-cast Abjuration or its Bonus Action slot expenditure restores it. Projected Ward uses a Reaction. | At-will rituals/free feat casts do not refill the 2024 Ward. Level-14 Spell Resistance is against spells, not every magical feature. |
| **Diviner** | Portent is chosen **before** the D20 Test, once per turn; two rolls become three at Wizard 14. Expert Divination restores a lower expended slot when a level-2+ slot is spent on Divination. | It is not an after-failure reroll, bypass of Legendary Resistance, or an unlimited slot engine. |
| **Illusionist** | Improved Illusions omits V, extends qualifying ranges, and improves Minor Illusion. Its summoned creatures and reactive miss have specific resource rules. | Omitting V alone does not remove S/M. Phantasmal Creatures' free casts halve the summon HP; Illusory Reality cannot directly deal damage or impose conditions. |

## Revised Bladesinger boundaries

Use **Heroes of Faerûn**, not a mixture with Tasha's Bladesinging. The revised Bladesong needs **no armor and no Shield**, lasts one minute, and has INT-modifier uses per Long Rest, with one expended use restored by Arcane Recovery. While active it permits INT attacks/damage with proficient weapons and adds INT to AC and concentration saves. Making a two-handed weapon attack, becoming Incapacitated, or donning armor/Shield ends it.

At Wizard 6, one eligible action-time Wizard cantrip can replace one Extra Attack attack. At 10, Song of Defense spends a Reaction and slot to reduce damage. At 14, Song of Victory grants a Bonus Action weapon attack after an Action-casting-time spell; it is not the legacy flat INT damage rider. The Bonus Action still competes with starting Bladesong and teleporting. [Current subclass](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#BladesingerWizard), [[Worked Wizard Builds#D — Revised Bladesinger without an armor dip|worked unarmored example]].

Artificer/Fighter starts can still supply starting CON saves; Cleric can supply divine support. These benefits remain distinct from their armor training. An unarmored Bladesinger must budget hands, materials, and any required held tools rather than assuming a dip removes those restrictions.

## Chronurgy and legacy compatibility

Chronurgy retains its **Explorer's Guide to Wildemount** source identity. When used with the 2024 Wizard, select the subclass at **Wizard 3**, not legacy Wizard 2; receive the applicable initial features under the revised class's compatibility instruction. Do not combine the older Wizard base class with the revised base class's best features. Chronurgy does **not** have a revised Savant feature granting extra spellbook entries.

| Feature package | Useful role | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| INT to Initiative and Chronal Shift | Act earlier and force a qualifying reactive reroll | Chronal Shift is twice per Long Rest and consumes the Reaction; it is not automatic failure or a new Portent roll. |
| Arcane Abeyance at 10 | Store a spell for later release, including useful action/Concentration delegation | A level-4-or-lower **slot-cast** spell; one bead per Short/Long Rest; one-hour lifespan. The releasing creature is the caster except for the stored attack/DC, so it handles Concentration. |
| Convergent Future at 14 | Change a qualifying outcome at a decisive moment | Reaction; special Exhaustion removable only by a Long Rest. Do not price it as unlimited consequence-free control. |

Sources: [D&D Beyond Wizard subclass entries](https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/8-wizard), [Chronurgy and Dunamancy source](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/egtw/character-options#WizardSubclasses). The route's Dunamancy access and timing remain attached to the campaign's permission.

## Arcana Unleashed choices

Build explanations: [[Arcana Unleashed Builds]]. Spell/item interaction limits: [[Arcana Unleashed Combinations]].

The supported revised Arcana Unleashed subclasses are **Conjurer and Necromancer**. Illusionist uses the 2024 PHB subclass, with Arcana Unleashed's Illusion Adept available as an optional feat configuration. All are published options, not Unearthed Arcana.

Conjuration Savant and Necromancy Savant at Wizard 3 add two matching-school Wizard spells of level 2 or lower, then one matching-school Wizard spell when the Wizard class unlocks a new spell-slot level. These additions do not waive normal spell-level eligibility, preparation limits, or materials.

| Subclass | Early payoff | Later payoff | Important limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjurer** | Level 3 Bonus Action teleport or willing Medium-or-smaller swap within 30 feet; INT-modifier uses per Long Rest. At 6, range becomes 60 feet and a level-3+ slot can restore one use. | Level 6 summons created by a slot-cast Conjuration spell gain 2 × Wizard level Temporary HP and broad Resistance while those specific Temporary HP remain. Level 10 damage cannot break Concentration on Conjuration spells. Level 14 can split an eligible statblock-spirit summon into two. | Resistance excludes Force, Necrotic, Psychic, and Radiant. Split summons each have half normal HP, share one spell's Concentration, and require a slot-cast Conjuration spell containing the spirit's statblock. Split is once per Long Rest, or restored with a separate level-5+ slot. It does not double every creature-creating spell. |
| **Necromancer** | Level 3 supplies Necrotic Resistance, an Undead familiar option, and healing for a visible Undead when spending a slot on Necromancy. Level 6 improves Animate Dead and controlled Undead. | Level 10 can sacrifice a controlled Undead for healing when newly Bloodied; level 14 supports an Undead force and explosions when Undead reach 0 HP. | Several benefits require **holding the spellbook**, not just owning/carrying it. Attack support has a 60-foot limit, and Undead access/logistics remain campaign constraints. See [[Animate Dead - Necromancer]] for the exact army rules. |

**Build implications:** Conjurer has meaningful summon breakpoints at 6/10/14; Necromancer needs enough legal Undead access to justify its handling cost. Staying pure reaches those features earlier. A dip buys a different package and remains a supported alternative, not a mandatory improvement.
