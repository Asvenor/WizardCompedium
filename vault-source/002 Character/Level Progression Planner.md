# Level Progression Planner

This planner serves **Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer**, with pure Wizard, revised Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric structures presented equally. Legal static ability and feat schedules for every structure are in [[Build Decision Engine#Static ability and feat frameworks]].

Use Wizard class level for the table below. Its Proficiency Bonus and slot columns describe a **single-class Wizard**; a multiclass character uses total character level for Proficiency Bonus and the applicable multiclass rules for slots.

## What to decide at a level

| Milestone | Rules and optimization consideration |
|---|---|
| Every Wizard level after 1 | Choose two legal Wizard spells for the book; compare separate subclass additions, available copies, and otherwise inaccessible choices |
| 2 | Choose Scholar expertise based on actual skill proficiency and campaign use; Arcana is useful when copying scrolls or identifying magic matters |
| 3 | Confirm the exact subclass edition and feature progression; see [[Subclasses]] |
| 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 | Solve the current bottleneck with a feat or ASI; use [[Build Decision Engine#Feat decision table]] |
| 5 | Begin using Memorize Spell after Short Rests to replace one prepared level 1+ Wizard spell from the book |
| 7 / 9 / 11 / 13 / 15 / 17 | Price components before learning spells whose main value depends on them; consult [[Expensive Components]] |
| 18 | Select one level 1 and one level 2 book spell with an **Action** casting time for Spell Mastery; Shield and Misty Step do not qualify under 2024 wording |
| 19 | Compare qualifying Epic Boons against the campaign's real remaining threats; [[Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List]] |
| 20 | Select two level 3 book spells for Signature Spells; evaluate each spell's separate free-casting benefit |

Scholar's expertise and Memorize Spell are class features, not automatic knowledge of every spell. A Short Rest swaps one eligible prepared spell at Wizard 5+, not the entire list.

**Supported expanded-source milestones:** revised Conjurer and Necromancer use Wizard 3/6/10/14 features, as does the Heroes of Faerûn Bladesinger. Legacy Chronurgy receives its initial features at Wizard 3 on the 2024 base, then its later 6/10/14 features; it does not gain revised Savant spell additions. Read [[Subclasses#Arcana Unleashed choices]] before importing a legacy feature or deciding that every build needs the same defensive dip. At level 19 compare the new [[Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List#Arcana Unleashed Epic Boons|Arcana Unleashed boons]]; after level 20, use the optional reward rules in [[Intelligence above 20#Advancement after level 20]], not an invented level-21 class table.

## Multiclass distinction

Keep four numbers separate: **character level**, **Wizard level**, **combined spellcaster level**, and **highest Wizard spell level you can prepare**.

| Build at character level 5 | Wizard preparation | Highest Wizard spell | Slot result |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Wizard 5 | 9 | 3 | Wizard 5: 4 / 3 / 2 |
| Fighter 1 / Wizard 4 | 7 | 2 | Wizard 4: 4 / 3; Fighter 1 supplies no Spellcasting |
| Artificer 1 / Wizard 4 — revised 2025 Artificer | 7, plus 2 level-1 Artificer spells separately | 2 | Combined spellcaster level 5: 4 / 3 / 2; the Artificer contribution is half its class level, rounded up |
| Cleric 1 / Wizard 4 | 7, plus 4 level-1 Cleric spells separately | 2 | Combined spellcaster level 5: 4 / 3 / 2; level 3 slots only upcast spells this build can actually prepare |

Spell preparation is determined for each class individually as though single-classed. Higher multiclass slots do not unlock higher-level Wizard spells or copying them early. Proficiency Bonus follows total character level. Starting-class saving-throw proficiencies are not granted retroactively when you dip into that class.

### Artificer / Wizard

With the **revised 2025 Artificer**, combined spellcaster level is **Wizard level + half Artificer level, rounded up**. Artificer 1 / Wizard 4 therefore has level-3 slots, but can prepare only level-2 Wizard spells and level-1 Artificer spells. Neither Fireball nor Counterspell is unlocked until Wizard 5, at character level 6 with a one-level dip. Artificer 2 / Wizard 3 instead has combined spellcaster level 4: a second Artificer level delays slots as well as Wizard progression. [Final revised class](https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/2656866-artificer).

| Revised Artificer checkpoint | What it provides or requires |
|---|---|
| Starting class | d8 Hit Die, CON/INT saving-throw proficiency, simple weapons, light/medium armor and shields; Thieves' Tools, Tinker's Tools, and one Artisan's Tools proficiency |
| Entering after Wizard | d8 Hit Die, light/medium armor and shields, Tinker's Tools proficiency, and one skill from the Artificer list; not the starting save proficiencies or full starting tool package |
| Level-1 Spellcasting | INT-based casting, two chosen cantrips, and two prepared level-1 Artificer spells; the single-class table grants two level-1 slots, but multiclass slots use the combined table instead |
| Cantrip flexibility | One of the chosen Artificer cantrips can be replaced after a Long Rest |
| Tool-in-hand requirement | Use Thieves' Tools, Tinker's Tools, or another kind of Artisan's Tools with which the caster has proficiency as the focus, held in hand; every spell cast through Artificer Spellcasting gains a Material component; armor and a shield do not waive this requirement |
| Tinker's Magic at level 1 | Adds Mending separately; holding Tinker's Tools, a Magic action creates one item from the feature's bounded mundane list within 5 feet; INT modifier uses, minimum one, per Long Rest; the items vanish at the next Long Rest |
| Replicate Magic Item at level 2 | Four plans and two magic items at this level; not a level-1 benefit and not Tasha's Infuse Item feature |

Cleric 1 supplies **four** level-1 preparations and WIS-based divine support; revised Artificer 1 supplies **two** and uses INT. Spell lists remain separate: do not infer Healing Word or another Cleric spell from the existence of Artificer Spellcasting, or import an Alchemist subclass spell into Artificer 1. Assess the required class list before making a particular support spell the reason for a dip.

**Version boundary:** the supported Artificer dip uses the revised published class, not Tasha's older preparation/Infuse Item package or an Unearthed Arcana draft. Do not mix features between versions.

### Cleric / Wizard

Cleric 1 contributes a full caster level to slots and grants four prepared level-1 Cleric spells, separately from Wizard preparations. Its spellcasting ability is WIS, not INT. The 2024 class grants Divine Order at level 1, but its subclass begins at Cleric 3. A single Cleric level is therefore an armor/support choice, not access to a domain's signature package. See [[Build Decision Engine#Is Cleric 1 still optimal in 2024]] for the decision comparison.

### Fighter / Wizard

Fighter 1 and Fighter 2 do not add caster levels. Starting Fighter supplies STR/CON saves; taking it after Wizard does not retroactively grant those saves. Every Fighter level delays Wizard spell tiers and Wizard features. At character 20, Fighter 1 / Wizard 19 reaches Spell Mastery and the Wizard 19 feat but not Signature Spells. Fighter 2 / Wizard 18 reaches Spell Mastery but neither the Wizard 19 feat nor Signature Spells.

For 2024 Fighter 2, Action Surge's additional action cannot be the Magic action: budget that dip for its actual benefits, not a second ordinary spellcasting action. Use [[Multiclass Spell Timing]] for both calendars and [[Spell Progression Hub]] for their exact spell choices across all seven subclasses.

**Revised Bladesinger interaction:** from Wizard 6, an eligible surged Attack action can replace one attack with an Action-time Wizard cantrip under Extra Attack. This is an application of the [published Extra Attack wording](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#BladesingerWizard), not permission for Action Surge to grant a Magic action or a leveled-spell replacement. See [[Fighter 2 Bladesinger - Spell Progression]] for its actual character-level cost.

For revised Bladesinger, the armor/Shield training from any dip cannot be used during active Bladesong. For every other subclass, armor still requires training, acquisition, and legal spell components; it does not erase the Wizard-level delay.
