# Spell Progression Hub

[[Home]] · [[Character]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Worked Wizard Builds]] · [[Spell Finder]] · [[Prepared Loadouts]]

**“I levelled up. Which spells do I take?”** Choose your route below, then read its next level row. Each route prints its complete acquisition sequence: no shared baseline to reconstruct, no assumed scrolls, libraries, purchased spells, borrowed books, or friendly Wizards to copy from.

The recommendations prioritize a strong character **at the levels actually played**: defense and escape, decisive control, a way to contribute while concentrating, useful rituals, and the subclass's practical role. All seven subclasses and all four class structures have equal coverage. **There is no flagship, default subclass, or preferred dip.** The spell lists are concrete optimization examples under stated assumptions, not a claim that every route performs identically or that one wins every campaign.

## How to read a level

1. **Learn normally:** take every spell in that cell—six at Wizard 1, exactly two at each later Wizard level. Do not also take the old shared table's picks.
2. **Extra book spells:** take these separately only because the specified subclass grants them. They are not replacements for the normal two.
3. **Always prepared gained:** a separate feature grant, not another unrestricted book selection. Later rows usually show only newly gained grants; earlier ones remain available.
4. **Prepare for play:** use the route's Wizard-5 and Wizard-9 examples as starting loadouts. Acquiring a spell and preparing it are different decisions. The entire accumulated book is not a legal daily preparation list.

Wizard 18 Mastery choices appear on every route. Wizard 20 Signature choices appear only on pure routes; dip routes explicitly show their absence. These selections reuse spells already in the book. The routes also give Wizard cantrips and their actual character-level timing. No current-character input or tracking is required.

## Assumptions

- **Rules:** 2024 Wizard base class. Each route names its subclass/source; restricted additions need that source permitted. Updated and legacy subclass features are never combined.
- **Acquisition:** only six initial book spells, two per later Wizard level, and explicit subclass/feat grants. No gold, downtime, research project, or spellcasting focus manufactures a new known spell outside an actual rule. Owning your initial functioning spellbook is still necessary; multiclass equipment details remain in [[Worked Wizard Builds]].
- **Optimization:** a mixed adventuring campaign and a party that benefits from control, survival, utility and damage. No magic items, special loot, free extra feats, friendly Simulacrum army, unlimited downtime, or cooperative enemy behavior is assumed.
- **Components:** no copied spells is not the same as a zero-gold campaign. Summons and infrastructure still require their materials. Costly picks have alternatives below or on their route; campaign access is not guaranteed.
- **Preparation:** Wizard table counts—not Wizard level plus INT. At Wizard 5 the ordinary count is 9; at Wizard 9 it is 14. Feature preparations and other-class spells are separate. Ritual Adept permits book rituals without ordinary preparation, not without time or materials.
- **One plan at a time:** a strong summon and a strong control spell often compete for the same Concentration. Their presence in the book gives options, not simultaneous effects.

The older [[Worked Wizard Builds#Shared no-copy spellbook progression]] and [[Arcana Unleashed Builds#Exact no-copy spellbook routes]] remain intact as alternative example acquisitions. **Follow one acquisition route**, not their combined union. This section is the direct “tell me what to take” entry point.

## If components are unavailable

For the Diviner, Abjurer, and Bladesinger presets—and their printed dip derivatives—the following substitutions are fully counted. Choose them **when reaching that acquisition row**, instead of the named spell. They do not grant later retraining or an automatic free copy of the skipped spell. None duplicates a later acquisition in these routes.

| Cannot fund / use | Exact replacement in these presets | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Contingency | Diviner / Abjurer: [[Mirror Image - Spell\|Mirror Image]]. Bladesinger: [[Suggestion - Spell\|Suggestion]]. | Learn at the same Wizard row. This is a cheaper useful tool, not an identical emergency trigger. |
| Normal acquisition of Forcecage | [[Slow - Spell\|Slow]] | Lose premium containment; keep a useful charm-independent control option. |
| Simulacrum | [[Major Image - Spell\|Major Image]] | Buy broad utility without duplicate infrastructure; image effects still follow their own limits. |
| Clone | [[Etherealness - Spell\|Etherealness]] | Escape/infiltration, not resurrection insurance. |
| Shapechange | Diviner: [[Time Stop - Spell\|Time Stop]]. Abjurer / Bladesinger: [[True Seeing - Spell\|True Seeing]]. | True Seeing still has a much smaller consumed component cost; Time Stop has its own action limits. |
| Diviner's Savant Augury / Clairvoyance / True Seeing / Scrying | Respectively [[Comprehend Languages - Spell\|Comprehend Languages]] / [[See Invisibility - Spell\|See Invisibility]] / [[Locate Object - Spell\|Locate Object]] / [[Contact Other Plane - Spell\|Contact Other Plane]] | Each remains a distinct Divination pick at the original milestone. Contact Other Plane has a real failed-save danger. |
| Abjurer's Savant Symbol / Imprisonment | Respectively [[Remove Curse - Spell\|Remove Curse]] / [[Arcane Lock - Spell\|Arcane Lock]] | Legal lower-level Abjuration choices, not equally powerful replacements. Arcane Lock still consumes its much smaller 25+ GP component. |

Conjurer, Illusionist, Necromancer, Chronurgy, and the optional Illusion Adept configuration have their own counted alternatives. Do not apply this table blindly to them: they may already own its replacement. Summoners should price the chosen spirit's component **before** selecting its spell. The armored starting Conjurer routes choose Longstrider rather than Mage Armor so their cheaper Savant Grease alternative remains distinct. See [[Expensive Components]] and the individual spell card; keeping a spell as a future goal is legal, but it does not make the spell castable today.

**Already partway through a campaign?** Earlier picks cannot normally be rewritten for free. Keep the spells actually acquired. If the next recommendation is already in that book, the new choice is still available, but the exact preset has diverged; select another legal unowned spell using [[Spell Bank]] and [[Prepared Loadouts]], or obtain the DM's permission to rebuild. This section is a complete forward plan, not a claim to respec an existing Wizard automatically.
