# Cleric dip with Illusionist — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Build Decision Engine]] · [[Multiclass Spell Timing]] · [[Illusionist - Spell Progression|Pure Illusionist route]]

**Cleric 1 / Wizard 19, Illusionist from 2024 PHB.** Wizard at character 1–5, Cleric at character 6, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 42 ordinary Wizard acquisitions + 9 distinct Savant additions = **51 distinct book spells**. Keep the initial Mage Armor acquisition: it protects the Wizard-first levels before Cleric armor access. There is no automatic refund of an earlier book choice; later it can still protect an eligible ally.

## Dip features and separate spells

At character 6 select the **Thaumaturge** Divine Order, with **Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying** as Cleric cantrips and **Thaumaturgy** as its additional cantrip. Prepare **Bless, Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Sanctuary** through Cleric. These four level-1 Cleric spells use WIS, not INT, and do not consume Wizard book choices or Wizard preparations. Bless competes for Concentration with Wizard control. Choose a holy symbol and respect normal hand/component rules.

The prerequisite is **INT 13 and WIS 13**; fund that WIS in point buy rather than assuming an item. Starting Wizard retains **WIS and INT saving throws**, so Resilient (CON) remains an eligible later choice. There is no Cleric subclass or legacy level-1 domain feature from this one-level dip. Medium armor and Shield training are gained; Thaumaturge does not grant heavy armor.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 1 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Mage Hand**. Improved Illusions grants **Minor Illusion** at character 3 / Wizard 3; add **Prestidigitation** at character 4 / Wizard 4 and **Message** at character 11 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

This one-level caster dip preserves full shared-slot progression after it is taken while delaying new Wizard spell tiers by one character level.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [[Illusionist - Spell Progression]] for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

This table assumes no Illusion Adept feat. [[Illusion Adept - Spell Progression]] is an optional pure-Wizard feat configuration within Illusionist, not another supported subclass or automatic extra grants here. If adopting that feat in a dip build, use its actual feat-acquisition and slot-level unlock rules in [[Multiclass Spell Timing#Adept feats need their own timing]].
