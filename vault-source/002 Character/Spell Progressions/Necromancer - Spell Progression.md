# Necromancer — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Arcana Unleashed Builds]] · [[Summon Damage Benchmarks]]

**Pure Wizard 1–20; revised Arcana Unleashed Necromancer.** Follow this complete table by itself: no found spellbooks, scrolls, copying, magic items, or bonus feats are assumed. It prioritizes a compact Undead familiar plus spirit, with control when companions are unsuitable. It is not a promise that a corpse army fits every campaign. Existing build tables remain alternative recommendations, not additional acquisitions.

Use the existing feat schedule: War Caster at 4 (INT 18), INT 20 at 8, Resilient (CON) at 12, Mage Slayer at 16, Boon of Dimensional Travel at 19. At 3 choose an explicitly **Undead** familiar using the revised subclass; Battle Familiar is Conjuration and therefore must use an ordinary book choice. Its familiar upgrade retains the underlying creature type. Read [[Arcana Unleashed Builds]] for the held-book, range, HP, and command limits.

## Cantrips and prepared examples

At Wizard 1 take **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**; add **Mage Hand** at 4 and **Prestidigitation** at 10. Cantrips do not consume leveled-spell choices.

Find Familiar, Detect Magic, Unseen Servant, and Gentle Repose can be cast as book rituals without ordinary preparation; Phantom Steed joins by 9. Ritual time and material costs remain. Battle Familiar does not take Concentration; Summon Undead does. Animate Dead requires legal remains, orders, and timely control renewal, not a presumed army that costs no resources.

## If components are unaffordable

The compact package needs Find Familiar's **10+ GP consumed incense**, Battle Familiar's reusable **25+ GP diamond**, and Summon Undead's reusable **300+ GP gilded skull**. At Wizard 4, choose **Mirror Image instead of Battle Familiar** if its component is unavailable; it never appears as another ordinary pick. If the skull is unavailable at 5, select **Bestow Curse as the level-5 Savant choice, then Summon Undead instead of Bestow Curse at 9**. That is an explicit acquisition-order change, not a retroactive swap; casting the spirit at 9 still needs the skull. At 5 prepare Invisibility instead of an unusable Summon Undead, and Mirror Image instead of Battle Familiar if that replacement was selected.

At the level-11 decision, **Eyebite** is a component-affordable Necromancy alternative to Magic Jar and is not elsewhere in this route. For the ordinary Forcecage choice at 13, use **Arcane Gate** if repeated consumed ruby dust is unrealistic; for ordinary Simulacrum at 14, use **Etherealness**. These alternatives do not duplicate future choices. Clone and Astral Projection may remain learned but uncast until their expensive materials are acquired. No body, soul, spellbook, or minion-production exploit is assumed.
