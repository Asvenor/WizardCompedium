# Chronurgy — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Prepared Loadouts]]

**Purpose:** act early, force favorable control exchanges, and use a limited prepared spell handoff. Requires legacy **Explorer's Guide to Wildemount** Chronurgy and its Dunamancy access to be allowed. No Dunamancy spell is selected before Wizard 3.

**Budget:** six level-1 Wizard spells at Wizard 1, then exactly two ordinary book additions at every Wizard level from 2 to 20: **44 distinct book spells**. No found spellbook, scroll, copying, magic item, background spell, or extra feat spell is required. This is a pure Wizard route; level numbers mean **Wizard level**, not multiclass character level. Use the current spell versions on the linked cards.

Legacy subclasses need the DM's permission with the **2024 Wizard base class**. Select the subclass at Wizard 3 and receive its lower-level subclass features then. There are **no revised Savant bonus spells** here; a legacy copying discount is not a free-spell grant. These are optimization recommendations under ordinary mixed encounters, not a universally best answer for every campaign.

## Why the Chronurgy choices differ

- **Gift of Alacrity at 3:** a long-duration initiative benefit with no Concentration, but it spends its slot normally. The initial level-2 spellbook choice is intentionally Web plus this level-1 spell; Misty Step and Invisibility arrive at 4. Going first matters more than collecting every spell at the newest tier.
- **Arcane Abeyance at 10:** use only a level-4-or-lower slot-cast spell and respect its one-use-per-Short/Long-Rest and one-hour bead limits. The releasing creature is the caster except for the stored attack modifier/DC and handles Concentration. Haste on a willing frontliner is a possible planned handoff, not free permanent Haste; ending it has its normal penalty. An ally capable of keeping its Concentration is not guaranteed.
- Hypnotic Pattern, Web, or a carefully chosen Polymorph are alternative eligible payloads. Preparing the bead consumes a slot; a bead does not copy a spell into anyone's book. This route does not require instantaneous Tiny Hut, a familiar concentration army, or bypassing normal targets/components.
- Chronal Shift and Convergent Future compete with Shield and Counterspell for the one Reaction. Convergent Future's special Exhaustion is a real cost, not a repeatable free forced failure.

## Source and low-component substitutions

If the table permits Chronurgy but **does not permit Gift of Alacrity**, the complete change is **Wizard 3: replace Gift of Alacrity with [[Mirror Image - Spell|Mirror Image]]**. At both preparation checkpoints, prepare Mirror Image in Gift's slot. Do not later add Gift for free. Every other default spell is from the 2024 PHB; Silvery Barbs, Temporal Shunt, and other expanded spells are not hidden additions.

If the listed materials are persistently unavailable, make any of these **one-for-one acquisition replacements**: Wizard 12 Contingency → [[Fly - Spell|Fly]]; Wizard 12 True Seeing → [[See Invisibility - Spell|See Invisibility]]; Wizard 13 Forcecage → [[Mirage Arcane - Spell|Mirage Arcane]]; Wizard 14 Plane Shift → [[Etherealness - Spell|Etherealness]]; Wizard 16 Clone → [[Tiny Hut - Spell|Tiny Hut]]; Wizard 17 Shapechange → [[Time Stop - Spell|Time Stop]]; Wizard 20 Simulacrum → [[Wall of Stone - Spell|Wall of Stone]]. None of these replacements is otherwise acquired, so the book remains 44 spells. They change the available tools rather than duplicating the unavailable effect.

## Mastery, signatures, and component limits

At **Wizard 18**, choose [[Magic Missile - Spell|Magic Missile]] and [[Invisibility - Spell|Invisibility]] for Spell Mastery. Both were learned earlier and have an **Action** casting time. They become always prepared separately; free casting is only at the selected spell's lowest level. Invisibility still needs Concentration. Shield and Misty Step are ineligible because their casting times are not an Action.

At **Wizard 20**, choose [[Counterspell - Spell|Counterspell]] and [[Dispel Magic - Spell|Dispel Magic]] for Signature Spells. These are already in the book, become always prepared separately, and each gains one free level-3 casting per Short or Long Rest. This is not unlimited Counterspell, and its trigger and Reaction still apply.

No-copy does not mean no spell components. [[Forcecage - Spell|Forcecage]] consumes 1,500+ GP of ruby dust **per casting** and requires Concentration. [[Contingency - Spell|Contingency]], [[Clone - Spell|Clone]], [[Shapechange - Spell|Shapechange]], and [[Simulacrum - Spell|Simulacrum]] have their own material/time requirements if selected; a learned spell is not a promise that those resources exist. See [[Expensive Components]]. Basic [[Wish - Spell|Wish]] spell duplication can cover eligible lower-level effects later, but does not add them to the spellbook and does not make every non-duplication wish safe.

**No dubious engine is required:** no infinite Simulacrum chain, unlimited reaction economy, or assumption that enemies never break Concentration.
