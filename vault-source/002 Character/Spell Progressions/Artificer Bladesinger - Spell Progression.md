# Artificer dip with Bladesinger — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Build Decision Engine]] · [[Multiclass Spell Timing]] · [[Bladesinger - Spell Progression|Pure Bladesinger route]]

**Artificer 1 / Wizard 19, Bladesinger from Heroes of Faerûn (2025).** Artificer at character 1, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 42 ordinary Wizard acquisitions = **42 distinct book spells**. Keep Mage Armor. Revised Bladesong requires no armor and no Shield; armor training from a dip does not change that restriction. A worn-armor fallback is a different combat mode, not an extra Bladesong AC bonus.

## Dip features and separate spells

Use the revised **Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (2025)** class, not Tasha’s. At character 1 select Artificer cantrips **Guidance + Thorn Whip**; **Mending** is a separate Tinker’s Magic grant. Prepare **Cure Wounds + Faerie Fire**, using INT. These are two separate Artificer preparations, not Wizard book entries or Wizard preparation slots. Hold a proficient tool focus when casting an Artificer spell; this adds a Material component even to spells otherwise lacking one. A tool is not automatically a Wizard focus. Replicate Magic Item starts at Artificer 2 and is not gained here.

The Artificer start supplies **CON and INT saving-throw proficiency**. Do not also select Resilient (CON); use the starting-dip feat framework in [[Build Decision Engine]]. This route assumes no Magic Initiate feat; any origin spell package must be accounted for separately.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 2 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, True Strike**. Add **Mind Sliver** at character 5 / Wizard 4 and **Mage Hand** at character 11 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [[Bladesinger - Spell Progression]] for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

Bladesong is unarmored and Shield-free. Conjure Minor Elementals is a situational Concentration engine using corrected +1d8 upcast scaling, not a mandatory opener. A dip does not remove its setup cost or permit arbitrary extra slot expenditure in one turn.
