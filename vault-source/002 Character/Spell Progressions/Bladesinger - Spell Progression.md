# Bladesinger — no-copy spell progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Worked Wizard Builds]] · [[Spell Finder]]

2024 Wizard plus revised Heroes of Faerûn (2025) Bladesinger, pure Wizard 1–20. Follow worked build D's feat/ability plan. Control is the first job; weapons supply sustained output without obliging you to enter melee. No Savant bonuses.

**No-copy budget:** six starting spells, then exactly two per Wizard level. Final book: **44 distinct spells**. This is an optimization preset under [[Spell Progression Hub#Assumptions]], not a universally best answer for every party.

## Cantrips

Start **Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, True Strike**; add **Mind Sliver at Wizard 4** and **Mage Hand at Wizard 10**. They do not use the levelled-spell budget. The Wizard's permitted Long Rest cantrip replacement remains available.

The Mastery choices have an Action casting time; Shield and Misty Step do not qualify.

## How this route wins

Bladesong requires no armor or Shield. Conjure Minor Elementals at 7 is a situational sustained-damage option for your own qualifying hits inside its 15-foot Emanation, not a mandatory opener. It needs an Action and Concentration; setup, exposure, and competing control matter. Use corrected +1d8 per higher slot, not original +2d8 scaling. No found magic weapon, Booming Blade, or unrestricted same-turn slot casting is assumed.
