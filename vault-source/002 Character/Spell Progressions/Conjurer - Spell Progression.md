# Conjurer — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Arcana Unleashed Builds]] · [[Summon Damage Benchmarks]]

**Pure Wizard 1–20; revised Arcana Unleashed Conjurer.** This is a complete acquisition recommendation: follow every row without combining it with another progression. It assumes **no found spellbooks, scrolls, copying, magic items, or bonus feats**. Ordinary starting equipment and later material-component purchases are still necessary. These are campaign-neutral optimization choices, not an official or universal ranking. The earlier shared/replacement route in [[Arcana Unleashed Builds]] remains a separate alternative.

Use that build's feat schedule: War Caster at 4 (INT 18), INT 20 at 8, Resilient (CON) at 12, Mage Slayer at 16, Boon of Dimensional Travel at 19. Keep defense and control even when summons are the specialty. Conjuration Concentration protection at 10 does not make a summon immune to every way its spell can end.

## Cantrips and prepared examples

At Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**; add **Mage Hand** at 4 and **Prestidigitation** at 10. Cantrips are separate from the table's leveled-spell budget.

- **Wizard 5 — 9 ordinary preparations:** [[Shield - Spell|Shield]]; [[Mage Armor - Spell|Mage Armor]]; [[Feather Fall - Spell|Feather Fall]]; [[Web - Spell|Web]]; [[Misty Step - Spell|Misty Step]]; [[Battle Familiar - Spell|Battle Familiar]]; [[Summon Fey - Spell|Summon Fey]]; [[Hypnotic Pattern - Spell|Hypnotic Pattern]]; [[Fireball - Spell|Fireball]]. There are no extra always-prepared leveled spells at this point.
- **Wizard 9 — 14 ordinary preparations:** [[Shield - Spell|Shield]]; [[Mage Armor - Spell|Mage Armor]]; [[Feather Fall - Spell|Feather Fall]]; [[Web - Spell|Web]]; [[Misty Step - Spell|Misty Step]]; [[Counterspell - Spell|Counterspell]]; [[Dispel Magic - Spell|Dispel Magic]]; [[Dimension Door - Spell|Dimension Door]]; [[Hypnotic Pattern - Spell|Hypnotic Pattern]]; [[Battle Familiar - Spell|Battle Familiar]]; [[Summon Fey - Spell|Summon Fey]]; [[Polymorph - Spell|Polymorph]]; [[Wall of Force - Spell|Wall of Force]]; [[Synaptic Static - Spell|Synaptic Static]]. There are no extra always-prepared leveled spells at this point.

Find Familiar, Detect Magic, and Unseen Servant are book rituals at both checkpoints; Phantom Steed joins them by 9. Ritual Adept lets you cast those as rituals from the book without ordinary preparation. Ritual time and materials still apply. Summon Fey and Web compete for the same Concentration; the familiar is the separate non-Concentration companion. These are complete static examples, not tracking lists.

## If components are unaffordable

The companion package needs Find Familiar's **10+ GP consumed incense**, Battle Familiar's reusable **25+ GP diamond**, and Summon Fey's reusable **300+ GP gilded flower**. At the level-up decision, you may choose **Grease instead of Battle Familiar at 3**, and **Sleet Storm instead of Summon Fey at 5**; both are eligible, previously unselected Conjuration spells. That route is a control Conjurer until you spend a later legal acquisition on the skipped summon: no retroactive refund or free recovery is implied. Prepare the selected replacements in the examples above. Later spirit spells have their own components, not one universally reusable focus.

For the normal level-13 Forcecage choice, **Arcane Gate** is an affordable alternative if consumed ruby dust is unrealistic; for normal level-14 Simulacrum, **Etherealness** is a separate affordable alternative. Neither appears elsewhere in this route. Other expensive infrastructure can remain in the book until funded. The route never assumes duplicate chains, self-sustaining resources, or acquisition of every component.
