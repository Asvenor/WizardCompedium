# Cleric dip with Chronurgy — Spell Progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Build Decision Engine]] · [[Multiclass Spell Timing]] · [[Chronurgy - Spell Progression|Pure Chronurgy route]]

**Cleric 1 / Wizard 19, Chronurgy from legacy Explorer’s Guide to Wildemount on the 2024 Wizard.** Wizard at character 1–5, Cleric at character 6, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 42 ordinary Wizard acquisitions = **42 distinct book spells**. Keep the initial Mage Armor acquisition: it protects the Wizard-first levels before Cleric armor access. There is no automatic refund of an earlier book choice; later it can still protect an eligible ally.

## Dip features and separate spells

At character 6 select the **Thaumaturge** Divine Order, with **Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying** as Cleric cantrips and **Thaumaturgy** as its additional cantrip. Prepare **Bless, Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Sanctuary** through Cleric. These four level-1 Cleric spells use WIS, not INT, and do not consume Wizard book choices or Wizard preparations. Bless competes for Concentration with Wizard control. Choose a holy symbol and respect normal hand/component rules.

The prerequisite is **INT 13 and WIS 13**; fund that WIS in point buy rather than assuming an item. Starting Wizard retains **WIS and INT saving throws**, so Resilient (CON) remains an eligible later choice. There is no Cleric subclass or legacy level-1 domain feature from this one-level dip. Medium armor and Shield training are gained; Thaumaturge does not grant heavy armor.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 1 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**. Add **Mage Hand** at character 4 / Wizard 4 and **Prestidigitation** at character 11 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

The Wizard-19 Epic Boon feature arrives at character 20, not character 19.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [[Chronurgy - Spell Progression]] for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

Chronurgy and Gift of Alacrity require the named legacy source and permission. There are no revised Savant extras. For an unavailable Gift, use the pure route’s counted Mirror Image replacement at the same Wizard row.
