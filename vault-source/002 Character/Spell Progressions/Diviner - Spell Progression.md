# Diviner — no-copy spell progression

[[Spell Progression Hub]] · [[Subclasses]] · [[Worked Wizard Builds]] · [[Spell Finder]]

2024 PHB, pure Wizard 1–20. General controller with Portent, information gathering, and Divination slot recovery. Use worked build A's class/feat framework. This is a new acquisition preset, not extra spells on top of its older table.

**No-copy budget:** six starting spells, then exactly two per Wizard level. Divination Savant adds the separate school choices shown. Final book: **44 normal + 9 Savant = 53 distinct spells**. This is an optimization preset under [[Spell Progression Hub#Assumptions]], not a universally best answer for every party.

## Cantrips

Start **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**; add **Mage Hand at Wizard 4** and **Prestidigitation at Wizard 10**. They do not use the levelled-spell budget. The Wizard's permitted Long Rest cantrip replacement remains available.

The Mastery choices have an Action casting time; Shield and Misty Step do not qualify.

## How this route wins

Portent supports important rolls but does not defeat Legendary Resistance. Slot-cast Divinations can recover lower expended slots through Expert Divination; ritual casts spend no slot and cannot fuel it. Arcane Eye is a useful scouting and recovery choice at 7. Rary's Telepathic Bond remains valuable as a ritual.
