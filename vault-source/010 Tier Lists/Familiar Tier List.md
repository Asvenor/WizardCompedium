# Familiar Tier List

**2024 Monster Manual forms; rankings are situational analysis.** [[Find Familiar]] changes the chosen Beast's type to Celestial, Fey or Fiend and does not permit attacks. Help requires the familiar within 5 feet of the enemy and benefits the next allied attack before its next turn—not every attack. The familiar rolls its own Initiative. A 100-foot telepathic connection is not the limit on how far it can travel, but beyond it you lose that immediate communication; touch-spell delivery also requires 100 feet. See [[Find Familiar - Spell]] for casting, disappearance and sense-sharing limits.

Sources: [Find Familiar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618877-find-familiar) · [current animals](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/animals) · [Help](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#HelpAction).
