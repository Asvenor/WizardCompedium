# Housing & Bastion Tier List
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Build Decision Engine]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


|Tier|Facility|Level|Why for an Optimized Wizard|
|---|---|---|---|
|**B at 5; A at 9+**|**Arcane Study**|5|Identify charm and modest mundane craft orders initially; Common/Uncommon Arcana-table magic-item crafting starts at 9. Value depends on actual item demand and downtime.|
|**S**|**Scriptorium**|9|Extremely valuable for a Wizard built around written magic and scroll production. Excellent long-term resource generation.|
|**S**|**Teleportation Circle**|9|Major strategic mobility. Makes the Bastion a reliable travel hub and becomes increasingly valuable as the campaign expands.|
|**S**|**Demiplane**|17|Exceptional endgame facility with powerful magical utility and adventuring benefits.|
|**A**|**Library**|5|Strong information-gathering facility. Research can turn downtime into useful knowledge before adventures.|
|**A**|**Laboratory**|9|Strong crafting facility that expands what your Bastion can produce and complements an arcane crafting setup.|
|**A**|**Greenhouse**|9|Generates useful magical consumables/resources and provides capabilities outside the Wizard's normal toolkit.|
|**A**|**Observatory**|13|Powerful information-focused facility with natural synergy for a high-level spellcaster.|
|**A**|**Archive**|13|Excellent advanced research and information utility, although it competes with other strong high-level facilities.|
|**A**|**Meditation Chamber**|13|Provides direct adventuring benefits rather than merely economic or narrative utility.|
|**B**|**Storehouse**|5|Useful economic facility that can help fund expensive Wizard activities, but its benefit is indirect rather than increasing magical capability.|
|**A for frequent returns**|**Workshop**|5|Heroic Inspiration after a complete Short Rest, once per Long Rest; useful immediately, with Implements crafting at owner level 9+.|
|**A for early resources**|**Garden**|5|Free 7-day harvests without starting capital; practical value depends on its chosen type and whether goods are used or sold.|

**Change the rank when the bottleneck changes:** Storehouse rises sharply for a gold-starved Wizard with capital at levels 13/17; Meditation Chamber falls for a party unable to remain home for seven days; Scriptorium falls if the party rarely uses low-level scrolls. Teleportation Circle supplies a destination, not free outbound casts. These contextual changes are more useful than treating S as mandatory.

These are conditional priorities, not an exhaustive ranking of every facility. In particular, **Workshop** supplies Heroic Inspiration after a Short Rest (once per Long Rest), and **Garden** supplies free weekly resources at level 5; both can outperform Arcane Study before its magic-item crafting unlock. Scriptorium is capped at Cleric/Wizard scrolls of level 3 or lower. See [[Facilities]] for actual benefits and limits, including the strong Demiplane temporary-HP feature.
