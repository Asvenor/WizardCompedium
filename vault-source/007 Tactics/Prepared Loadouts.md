# Prepared Loadouts

[[Home]] · [[Spell Finder]] · [[Combat Dashboard]] · [[Level Progression Planner]]

These ten loadouts are **pick priorities**, not fixed lists to prepare in full. Select only spells in the spellbook, of levels the Wizard can prepare, from sources the campaign permits. Count the result against the [[Level Progression Planner#Wizard level table|Wizard preparation limit]].

## Preparation principles

1. Set the day's objective and likely environment.
2. Reserve the defenses and emergency exit that you actually need. Common candidates are Shield, Mage Armor when it improves AC, Feather Fall when falls are plausible, and an appropriate escape spell.
3. Choose one likely Concentration opener and a different answer when its counter appears. You cannot maintain both yourself simultaneously.
4. Include a useful non-Concentration follow-up and patch the mission-specific gap using a loadout below.
5. Keep rituals in the book for Ritual Adept when there is time to read and ritual-cast them. Prepare one only when normal-speed slot casting is worth the preparation.
6. The prepared list can be changed after a Long Rest. At Wizard 5+, Memorize Spell permits one eligible prepared-spell swap after a Short Rest.

An existing familiar does not require Find Familiar to remain prepared. A ritual still has its casting time, components, and any Concentration requirement. Preparing a spell does not create its expensive material component.

## 1. General adventuring

**Objective:** cover unknown encounters without overcommitting to one defense or damage type.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Efficient group control | [[Web - Spell\|Web]] → choose [[Hypnotic Pattern - Spell\|Hypnotic Pattern]] or [[Slow - Spell\|Slow]] as enemy defenses suggest |
| Reliable escape | [[Misty Step - Spell\|Misty Step]] → [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]] |
| Follow-up that preserves Concentration | [[Magic Missile - Spell\|Magic Missile]], [[Fireball - Spell\|Fireball]], later [[Synaptic Static - Spell\|Synaptic Static]] |
| Remove a known category of problem | [[Dispel Magic - Spell\|Dispel Magic]] or another actual party gap |

Book utility: [[Find Familiar - Spell|Find Familiar]], [[Detect Magic - Spell|Detect Magic]], [[Comprehend Languages - Spell|Comprehend Languages]]. Upgrade only when the new spell supplies a function you will use; higher level is not automatic replacement.

## 2. Dungeon exploration

**Objective:** gain information and avoid entering bad rooms blindly.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Safe information | [[Arcane Eye - Spell\|Arcane Eye]] when available; a familiar and careful mundane scouting earlier |
| Defend a choke point | [[Web - Spell\|Web]]; later [[Wall of Force - Spell\|Wall of Force]] where geometry permits |
| Exit or bypass | [[Misty Step - Spell\|Misty Step]], [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]], [[Stone Shape - Spell\|Stone Shape]] for suitable stone |
| Solve a known ward or curse | [[Dispel Magic - Spell\|Dispel Magic]] or [[Remove Curse - Spell\|Remove Curse]] as the actual effect permits |

Book utility: Detect Magic, Identify, Comprehend Languages, Tiny Hut. Blind corners, doors, total cover, and narrow tunnels constrain spells and forms. Tiny Hut is shelter, not proof against all threats.

## 3. Boss encounter

**Objective:** remove supporting actions and create a favorable damage window.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Separate support or reshape access | [[Wall of Force - Spell\|Wall of Force]]; earlier control aimed at susceptible minions |
| Protect the party's plan | [[Counterspell - Spell\|Counterspell]], [[Dispel Magic - Spell\|Dispel Magic]], or a specific defense |
| Survive and extract | [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]]; prearranged [[Contingency - Spell\|Contingency]] at higher levels |
| Spend a top-level slot only on a plausible payoff | [[Maze - Spell\|Maze]] for temporary removal; [[Foresight - Spell\|Foresight]] as advance support when available |

Do not make every offensive preparation depend on the same save or on Charmed. Legendary Resistance, creature size, teleportation, and condition immunity change the ranking. Use [[Legendary Resistance & Bosses]].

## 4. Anti-caster

**Objective:** interrupt the decisive cast, remove ongoing spells, and deny useful targets.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Timely interruption | [[Counterspell - Spell\|Counterspell]] if you can reach its trigger |
| End ongoing spells | [[Dispel Magic - Spell\|Dispel Magic]] |
| Deny sight and positioning | [[Fog Cloud - Spell\|Fog Cloud]] or [[Sleet Storm - Spell\|Sleet Storm]] |
| Isolate or protect | [[Wall of Force - Spell\|Wall of Force]], later [[Globe of Invulnerability - Spell\|Globe of Invulnerability]] |

The enemy's 2024 Counterspell save is CON. Fog and storms obstruct allies too; not every hostile magical attack is spellcasting. Do not assume that total cover you use defensively still allows your chosen offensive target. See [[Countering Spellcasters]].

## 5. Stealth and infiltration

**Objective:** reach the objective, avoid exposure, and leave with the party.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Blend in or conceal | [[Disguise Self - Spell\|Disguise Self]], [[Invisibility - Spell\|Invisibility]] |
| Learn the route | Familiar scouting; [[Arcane Eye - Spell\|Arcane Eye]] when suitable |
| Cross a specific obstacle | [[Misty Step - Spell\|Misty Step]], [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]], or [[Stone Shape - Spell\|Stone Shape]] |
| Plan the escape | Reserve the spell and slot that transport the required people |

Book utility: Comprehend Languages and later Telepathic Bond. Invisibility is not silence, and spell components can expose casting. Disguise Self changes appearance, not the physical reality detected by touch. Check sensors, special senses, and whether allies can follow.

## 6. Investigation and social play

**Objective:** obtain actionable information without undermining the mission.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Understand the evidence | [[Detect Thoughts - Spell\|Detect Thoughts]] where its actual modes and consequences fit |
| Verify the magical problem | [[Detect Magic - Spell\|Detect Magic]] when speed matters; otherwise ritual-cast |
| Communicate or surveil | [[Sending - Spell\|Sending]], [[Scrying - Spell\|Scrying]] |
| Influence a suitable target | [[Suggestion - Spell\|Suggestion]] or [[Mass Suggestion - Spell\|Mass Suggestion]], after reading restrictions |

Book utility: Comprehend Languages, Identify, Divination, Contact Other Plane, Telepathic Bond. Divination is not unlimited certainty; repeated use and individual spell risks matter. Mental magic has observable or explicitly described consequences. Prepare mundane questions and corroborate information. See [[Information Warfare]].

## 7. Travel and evacuation

**Objective:** move everyone and essential equipment to a known destination.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Survive the route's hazard | [[Feather Fall - Spell\|Feather Fall]], [[Fly - Spell\|Fly]], or a specific environmental answer |
| Emergency local escape | [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]] |
| Long-distance relocation | [[Teleport - Spell\|Teleport]] when destination familiarity supports its risks |
| Planar route when required | [[Plane Shift - Spell\|Plane Shift]] with the correct tuned fork |

Book utility: Phantom Steed, Water Breathing, Tiny Hut, Telepathic Bond. Check target counts, travel speed, creature sizes, carrying needs, casting time, and return access. One person's flight or teleport does not imply transportation for the whole group. See [[Teleportation & Planar Travel]].

## 8. Downtime and infrastructure

**Objective:** convert scheduled time and gold into a concrete asset.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Scheduled project | [[Fabricate - Spell\|Fabricate]], [[Glyph of Warding - Spell\|Glyph of Warding]], or [[Wall of Stone - Spell\|Wall of Stone]] |
| Security and privacy | [[Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum - Spell\|Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum]], later [[Guards and Wards - Spell\|Guards and Wards]] |
| Finite assistance | [[Simulacrum - Spell\|Simulacrum]] or [[Planar Binding - Spell\|Planar Binding]] after the full setup is legal |
| Long-term recovery | [[Clone - Spell\|Clone]], [[Demiplane - Spell\|Demiplane]] |

Prepare the scheduled spells, not every infrastructure spell every day. Keep a modest emergency kit if the location is not secure. Price component consumption, copying, containment, casting time, permanence schedules, and upkeep before spending. See [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] and [[Crafting]].

## 9. Planar expedition

**Objective:** enter, operate, and return from the chosen plane.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| Return route | [[Plane Shift - Spell\|Plane Shift]] or another verified route; retain the required component |
| Local extraction | [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]] or [[Teleport - Spell\|Teleport]] where valid |
| Environment or creature-specific defense | A defense informed by the plane; at higher levels [[Mind Blank - Spell\|Mind Blank]] or [[True Seeing - Spell\|True Seeing]] when relevant |
| Encounter separation | [[Wall of Force - Spell\|Wall of Force]], [[Banishment - Spell\|Banishment]] if the spell's creature-type and return rules actually help |

Confirm planar traits, travel restrictions, available materials, and destination knowledge. Demiplane creates access to a particular extradimensional room; it is not a universal return ticket. Gate does not automatically control a called creature. See [[Teleportation & Planar Travel]] and [[Death & Contingency Planning]].

## 10. Protect a vulnerable party through an attrition day

**Objective:** preserve actions, HP, and enough resources to retreat.

| Pick priority | Candidate progression |
|---|---|
| One efficient encounter control | [[Web - Spell\|Web]], [[Slow - Spell\|Slow]], or [[Hypnotic Pattern - Spell\|Hypnotic Pattern]] according to enemies |
| Rescue without ending current control | [[Dimension Door - Spell\|Dimension Door]] when available |
| Reaction defense | [[Shield - Spell\|Shield]], [[Feather Fall - Spell\|Feather Fall]], Counterspell when hostile casters justify it |
| Affordable follow-up | Your actual cantrips and one worthwhile non-Concentration damage spell |

Use ritual utility, protect Concentration, and spend Arcane Recovery deliberately. Reserve a practical escape resource; do not assume every room needs a new highest-level spell. Healing, revivification, and condition removal must be assigned to someone who actually has them. This loadout also suits small parties with few spare actions.
