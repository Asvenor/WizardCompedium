# Bastion Progression
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


Bastions become available at **character level 5** and expand as the character gains levels. A Wizard can gradually turn a simple base into a fully developed arcane headquarters.

The DM must enable Bastions in the campaign. The research-focused path below is a complete example, not a rule that every optimized Wizard should take the same six facilities.

## Adventuring-first alternative

| Character level | Example package | Reason to choose it |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Workshop + Garden** | Heroic Inspiration before departure and useful free harvests without starting capital |
| 9 | Add **Scriptorium + Teleportation Circle** | Commission low-level scrolls and establish a permanent destination circle |
| 13 | Add **Storehouse** | Strong funding when 5,000 GP can be invested; trade instead of research if gold is the bottleneck |
| 17 | Add **Demiplane** | 85 Temporary HP after the qualifying Long Rest at 17, rising with level |

This uses 2/4/5/6 slots. Storehouse can be selected earlier when capital exists; Arcane Study can replace a lower-priority facility at any eligible level-up from 9 onward if reusable Common/Uncommon Arcana items matter more. For lore-intensive play, keep the Library/Archive route below. For short trips, Greenhouse fruit is powerful but expires 24 hours after picking. None of these choices produces its full value without the required orders, work/rest time, and return visits. See [[Facilities]], [[Bastion Crafting]], and [[Hirelings]].

## Facility Progression

| Character Level | Special Facilities | Facilities Gained |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **5** | 2 | +2 |
| **9** | 4 | +2 |
| **13** | 5 | +1 |
| **17** | 6 | +1 |

When you gain a level, you can replace **one Special Facility** with another facility for which you meet the prerequisites.

## Level 5 — Establish the Bastion

### Recommended Facilities
- Arcane Study
- Library

This is a **research-themed example**, not a universally optimal pair. Arcane Study cannot craft either Common or Uncommon magic items until level 9. Compare Workshop (Heroic Inspiration after a Short Rest, once per Long Rest), Garden (free weekly resources), and Storehouse (trade profit) if immediate adventuring value or funding matters more. The Library produces up to three DM-selected accurate pieces of lore per 7-day Research order. [DMG facility rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions).

### Development

This is the foundation of the Wizard's headquarters.

The **Arcane Study** establishes the magical workspace, while the **Library** provides research capabilities.

The Bastion does not need to begin as an enormous Wizard tower. A relatively small structure with room for future expansion is sufficient.

### Main Capabilities
- Arcane workspace
- Research
- Early crafting
- Spellbook infrastructure
- Secure permanent base

---

## Level 9 — Arcane Headquarters

### Add
- Scriptorium
- Teleportation Circle

### Current Facilities
1. Arcane Study
2. Library
3. Scriptorium
4. Teleportation Circle

### Development

Level 9 is the largest Bastion expansion after its creation.

The **Scriptorium** expands the Bastion's written and magical production capabilities.

The **Teleportation Circle** turns the Bastion into a strategic transportation hub and makes returning home significantly easier as the campaign expands.

### Main Capabilities
- Research
- Arcane crafting
- Written magical production
- Permanent headquarters
- Long-distance transportation

---

## Level 13 — Strategic Headquarters

### Add
- Observatory

### Current Facilities
1. Arcane Study
2. Library
3. Scriptorium
4. Teleportation Circle
5. Observatory

### Development

The Bastion now becomes a high-level information and planning center.

The Wizard's growing access to teleportation, communication, divination, and other strategic magic also makes the physical distance between the Bastion and current adventures increasingly less important.

### Main Capabilities
- Advanced information gathering
- Strategic planning
- Magical research
- Long-distance transportation
- Established crafting infrastructure

---

## Level 17 — Archwizard Bastion

### Add
- Demiplane

### Final Facilities
1. Arcane Study
2. Library
3. Scriptorium
4. Teleportation Circle
5. Observatory
6. Demiplane

### Development

At level 17, the Bastion has developed into a true **Archwizard headquarters**.

The **Demiplane** adds powerful magical infrastructure and gives the Wizard a facility appropriate to endgame play.

The Bastion can now function as a permanent center for research, crafting, transportation, preparation, and high-level magical projects.

# Alternative Progression

The recommended progression is not mandatory.

Strong alternatives include:

| Facility               | Level | Good For                                |
| ---------------------- | ----: | --------------------------------------- |
| Storehouse         |     5 | Trade and economic value                |
| Laboratory         |     9 | Crafting and experimentation            |
| Greenhouse         |     9 | Magical plants and consumable resources |
| Archive            |    13 | Advanced research                       |
| Meditation Chamber |    13 | Direct adventuring benefits             |

A facility can be swapped when leveling if the Bastion's priorities change.
