# Crafting for Profit
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Wizard Operations Roadmap]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


Crafting can turn Bastion downtime into gold, but there are two different profit systems:

1. **Direct Trade** — facilities such as the Storehouse generate reliable profit.
2. **Craft and Sell** — produce magic items or Spell Scrolls below their listed value and sell them if a market exists.

> [!important]
> Selling crafted magic items depends on the campaign having buyers.  
> Listed magic-item value is not the same thing as guaranteed instant cash.

**Scope of the numbers below:** a Storehouse trade cycle means one maximum-sized purchase order followed by one sale order, normally **14 in-game days** altogether. The purchase caps are not stated caps on all goods already owned or on a larger accumulated sale. Maintain, missing orders, and a slower DM-selected Bastion cadence can extend the timeline; see [[Hirelings#Remote orders and ongoing work]].

## Choose production by the actual bottleneck

| Need | Strong facility or method | Binding limit |
|---|---|---|
| No-capital starting resources | Garden at 5; Greenhouse at 9 | Harvest time, exact outputs, and buyers if selling |
| Predictable returns on invested capital | Storehouse | Two orders per buy/sell cycle and the level-based purchase cap |
| One scroll ready before the next adventure | Scriptorium at 9 | One Cleric/Wizard spell of level 3 or lower; normal scribing cost/time |
| A reusable Arcana item | Arcane Study at owner level 9+ | DMG Arcana table, Common/Uncommon rarity; personally craft spell-casting items |
| A Rare or higher item | General personal crafting | Not unlocked in the Arcane Study just by leveling; gold, materials, spells, and workdays |

At the normal weekly cadence, Storehouse profit per two-order cycle averages **25 / 200 / 1,250 / 2,500 GP per week** at levels **5 / 9 / 13 / 17**, respectively, once the necessary capital exists. For comparison, a level-9 **Gaming Hall** has an expected **73.5 GP per 7-day Trade order** with no stated investment, but its output is random. These are comparisons of written facility benefits, not guaranteed income while no orders are issued.

# Magic Item Profit Basics

## Magic Item Values

| Rarity | Craft Cost | Listed Value | Potential Gross Profit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **Common** | 50 gp | 100 gp | **50 gp** |
| **Uncommon** | 200 gp | 400 gp | **200 gp** |
| **Rare** | 2,000 gp | 4,000 gp | **2,000 gp** |
| **Very Rare** | 20,000 gp | 40,000 gp | **20,000 gp** |
| **Legendary** | 100,000 gp | 200,000 gp | **100,000 gp** |

Consumable magic items normally have half the value, crafting cost, and crafting time. The table excludes an underlying mundane item's cost, which is added to both the relevant item value and crafting budget; it also excludes selling expenses. Basic Potions of Healing use their specific **25 GP / one-day** PHB recipe instead of the generic consumable time.

Spell Scrolls use their own crafting rules.

# Level 5

At level 5, the recommended Wizard Bastion does not yet have access to profitable magic-item production through the Arcane Study.

## Best Reliable Profit

### Storehouse

| Investment | Sale | Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Up to 500 gp | 110% | **Up to 50 gp** |

The Storehouse alternates between buying and selling goods, so maximum profit is effectively generated every **two Bastion Turns**.

### Maximum

**50 gp profit per trade cycle**

The Storehouse is a straightforward trade-income option at this level. A Garden can also produce free saleable goods, but turning those listed values into cash requires buyers; compare the campaign's actual market rather than treating this as a universal ranking.

---

# Level 9

Level 9 is where Wizard crafting becomes much more interesting.

The Arcane Study can now craft qualifying **Common and Uncommon Arcana magic items**.

The Scriptorium can produce **Spell Scrolls**.

## Uncommon Magic Items

| Craft Cost | Value | Potential Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **200 gp** | **400 gp** | **200 gp** |

An Uncommon permanent magic item therefore potentially doubles the invested materials.

### Examples

Useful Arcana items can include:

- Wand of the War Mage +1
- Arcane Grimoire +1 — expanded-source general-crafting candidate; absent from the DMG Arcana tables, so **not automatically an Arcane Study product**
- Broom of Flying
- Bag of Holding
- Other qualifying Common/Uncommon Arcana items

Exact availability depends on the relevant magic-item table and facility rules.

Source: [2024 DMG Arcana tables](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/random-magic-items#ArcanaTables).

---

## Storehouse at Level 9

| Investment | Sale | Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Up to 2,000 gp | 120% | **Up to 400 gp** |

### Maximum

**400 gp per completed trade cycle**

This is already better raw gold generation than crafting one Uncommon magic item purely for resale.

# Spell Scroll Profit

The Scriptorium is limited to Cleric or Wizard scrolls of level 3 or lower. The higher-level rows below describe general personal scribing, not Scriptorium output. A facility can perform only one Craft production at a time and orders follow Bastion Turns, normally seven days; the personal workday ratios below are not permission to issue a fresh facility order every day. Consumed spell components add to scroll costs and reduce these margins.

Spell Scroll values are **twice their scribing cost**.

Therefore:

| Spell Level | Scribing Cost | Listed Value | Potential Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cantrip | 15 gp | 30 gp | 15 gp |
| 1 | 25 gp | 50 gp | 25 gp |
| 2 | 100 gp | 200 gp | 100 gp |
| 3 | 150 gp | 300 gp | 150 gp |
| 4 | 1,000 gp | 2,000 gp | 1,000 gp |
| 5 | 1,500 gp | 3,000 gp | 1,500 gp |
| 6 | 10,000 gp | 20,000 gp | 10,000 gp |
| 7 | 12,500 gp | 25,000 gp | 12,500 gp |
| 8 | 15,000 gp | 30,000 gp | 15,000 gp |
| 9 | 50,000 gp | 100,000 gp | 50,000 gp |

For a scroll with no consumed component and a buyer paying the table value, the theoretical markup is **100% of the listed scribing cost**. The table is not a profit guarantee for spells with expensive consumed components, commissions, or discounted sale prices.

The problem is time and finding buyers.

# Level 13

At level 13, the Storehouse becomes dramatically more profitable.

## Storehouse

| Investment | Sale | Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Up to 5,000 gp | 150% | **Up to 2,500 gp** |

### Maximum

**2,500 gp per completed trade cycle**

This is a huge jump.

At this point, crafting solely for resale usually loses to Storehouse trading in raw economic efficiency.

---

# Crafting at Level 13

The Wizard can still use:

- Arcane Study
- Scriptorium
- Other crafting facilities if selected

Crafting is generally more valuable for producing items the party actually wants than for pure cash.

If reliable buyers exist, however, the same margins remain:

**Common:** +50 gp  
**Uncommon:** +200 gp

The limitation is that Bastion crafting facilities generally don't simply scale to Rare item production just because the Wizard reaches level 13.

---

# Level 17

The Storehouse becomes absurdly profitable.

## Storehouse

| Investment | Sale | Profit |
|---:|---:|---:|
| Up to 5,000 gp | 200% | **Up to 5,000 gp** |

**5,000 gp profit per completed trade cycle**

You buy 5,000 gp of goods and eventually sell them for 10,000 gp.

# Practical Wizard Strategy

## Level 5

Focus Bastion facilities on progression and utility.

Do not build the Bastion around crafting profit yet.

---

## Level 9

This is the first real crafting breakpoint.

Use:

- Arcane Study for Common/Uncommon Arcana items
- Scriptorium for Spell Scrolls
- Storehouse if pure gold production matters

---

## Level 13

The Storehouse reaches its major economic breakpoint.

**5,000 gp investment → 7,500 gp sale**

Profit:

**2,500 gp**

At this level, use crafting primarily to produce powerful resources for yourself and the party rather than selling everything.

---

## Level 17

The Storehouse reaches its maximum return.

**5,000 gp investment → 10,000 gp sale**

Profit:

**5,000 gp**

At this point your Bastion can fund:

- Spell copying
- Expensive components
- Scroll production
- Magic-item crafting
- Simulacrum
- Planar Binding
- Contingency
- Clone
- High-level Wizard infrastructure

without relying entirely on adventuring loot.
