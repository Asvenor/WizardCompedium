# Spell Finder

**Choosing level-up spells without copied books or scrolls?** Start with [[Spell Progression Hub]]; use this finder for the individual spell rules.

The progression hub covers the seven supported subclasses equally, with pure Wizard, Artificer 1, Cleric 1, Fighter 1, and Fighter 2 plans for each. Its optional pure Illusionist/Illusion Adept configuration is a feat alternative, not an extra subclass or favored route.

[[Home]] · [[Prepared Loadouts]] · [[Spell Bank]] · [[Rules Desk]]

Pick a battlefield job, then compare spell level, source, concentration, and tactical tradeoffs. The catalogue contains 208 selected spell cards, including the compendium's recommended spells, eight summon families, and all fifteen Wildemount Dunamancy spells. It is a curated optimization library, not the complete Wizard class list; source access and the exact spell's limits still apply.

Some cards describe spells granted by a particular feat or subclass rather than ordinary Wizard-list acquisitions. Check the card's access line; inclusion in this finder does not itself grant access.

## Live filters

Use the view selector to compare reactions, rituals, no-concentration control, Intelligence saves, long-range options, and source groups. The table is a spell reference, with no character setup or tracking required. The static index below always works.

## Using the reference

Use each spell card's linked source and rules version for the exact wording. The catalogue mixes the 2024 core rules with labeled expansions and legacy options; availability in a sourcebook does not automatically grant campaign or subclass access. Save/test shorthand and tactical roles are not a complete targeting specification. Off-list build-support spells such as Bless, Healing Word, and Summon Beast require the specific access route shown on their cards; catalogue inclusion does not make them ordinary Wizard spellbook choices. Concentration filters show the base casting: Major Image at slot 4+ and Bestow Curse at slot 5+ are important exceptions. The Contact Other Plane saving throw is made by the caster and is excluded from enemy-save filters. Expanded sources require campaign permission; Scatter is a legacy Xanathar spell. The original [[Spells]] table is preserved, including visibly flagged discrepancies. The cards supply the rules metadata for the filtered reference views.
