## Encounter decisions

| Situation | First decision | Exact reference |
|---|---|---|
| Combat is starting | Identify the turn that removes the most enemy actions. | [[Initiative & First-Turn Strategy#First-Turn Priority]] · [[Combat Dashboard]] |
| Pick concentration | Choose by enemy count, immunity, map, party, and escape options. | [[Concentration Strategy#Choosing the Concentration Spell]] |
| My control is already working | Protect the effect and use compatible actions. | [[Concentration Strategy#After Control Lands]] |
| A boss has Legendary Resistance | Compare bypass, deliberate depletion, and isolating its allies. | [[Legendary Resistance & Bosses#Strategy 1 — Bypass It]] |
| I need the weakest save | Use observed traits and information; a creature's appearance is not proof. | [[Legendary Resistance & Bosses#Save Targeting]] · [[Spell Finder#Live filters]] |
| An enemy is casting | Check sight, range, components, trigger, and reaction value. | [[Countering Spellcasters#3. Counterspell]] · [[Reaction Management]] |
| A caster is concentrating | Compare damage, disruption, sight denial, and removal. | [[Countering Spellcasters#4. Break Concentration]] |
| A creature is hidden/invisible | Separate finding its location from being able to see/target it. | [[Vision & Senses]] · [[See Invisibility - Spell]] |
| Several enemies are clustered | Choose control or damage while avoiding ally interference. | [[Spell Tactics#Battlefield Control]] · [[Spell Finder#Control]] |
| We are outnumbered | Divide the encounter and reduce the number of active enemies. | [[Spell Tactics#Wall of Force Tactics]] |
| I need to rescue an ally | Check willingness, visibility, carrying limits, and destination. | [[Spell Finder#Rescue]] |
| I need to escape | Pick a reachable safe destination and preserve the reaction needed to survive. | [[Spell Finder#Mobility]] · [[Teleportation & Planar Travel]] |
| I am being surrounded | Compare repositioning with a defensive action; keep an exit. | [[Combat Dashboard]] · [[Movement & Travel]] |
| I am losing concentration | Check the trigger/DC and reduce further exposure. | [[Concentration]] · [[Concentration Strategy#Protecting Concentration]] |
| I have very few slots | Preserve one escape/defense option and use book rituals when time allows. | [[Prepared Loadouts]] · [[Resting]] |
| I want to layer two concentration effects | Establish two actual concentration holders or an explicit exception. | [[Concentration Strategy#Special Cases]] · [[Rules Desk]] |
| We can push or grapple enemies | Read each hazard's timing and once-per-turn language. | [[Spell Tactics#Forced Movement]] · [[Rules Desk]] |
