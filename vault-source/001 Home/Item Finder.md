Find an item by the problem it solves, then compare its attunement, access, and operating limits. These **51 selected item cards** cover important core choices, Arcana Unleashed options, and edition-sensitive specialist items. This is not the entire magic-item catalogue or a promise of campaign availability.

## Live filters

Choose a view for no-attunement utility, Concentration, casting accuracy, resources, mobility, defense, components/teamwork, Arcana Unleashed, or legacy sources. Use the table's filter and sort controls for finer comparisons. No character setup or tracking is required. If Bases is unavailable, every card remains accessible through the static index below.
