[[Form Catalogue]] · [[Form Legality Guide]] · [[Polymorph]]

## Best use — analysis

Melee damage and control against Large or smaller enemies, with reach on both attacks.

Bite's grapple also restrains, but the grappled target cannot be targeted by this creature's Tail. A level-7 target cannot become CR8 through Polymorph.
