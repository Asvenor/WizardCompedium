# Invisible Stalker

[[Form Catalogue]] · [[Form Legality Guide]] · [[True Polymorph]]

**CR 6 · Large Elemental · AC 14 · HP 97**  
Walk 50 ft; Fly 50 ft (hover).  
Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 18.

## Best use — analysis

Invisible infiltration and pressure on casters: Vortex can grapple and stop Verbal-component spells.

## Restrictions

Vortex needs a Large or smaller target in its space and allows a Constitution save. It does not stop spells without Verbal components or automatically reveal hidden targets.
