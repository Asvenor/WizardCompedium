# Giant Scorpion

[[Form Catalogue]] · [[Form Legality Guide]] · [[Polymorph]]

**CR 3 · Large Beast · AC 15 · HP 52**  
Walk 40 ft.  
Blindsight 60 ft.; Passive Perception 9.

## Best use — analysis

Blindsight plus two claws for holding opponents, useful when visibility is the main problem.

## Restrictions

Claws grapple; the block does not say they also restrain. Sting damage partly depends on Poison, which many enemies resist or ignore.
