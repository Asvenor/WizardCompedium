[[Form Catalogue]] · [[Form Legality Guide]] · [[Find Familiar]]

## Best use — analysis

Default aerial familiar with good perception, stealth and Flyby.

## Restrictions

Flyby prevents opportunity attacks caused by flying out of reach; it does not prevent other attacks or readied actions. Normal familiars cannot attack.
