# Shapechange
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Form Finder]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

> [!important] Form eligibility and concentration
> Choose a sort of creature you have seen, with CR no higher than your level or CR; Constructs and Undead are excluded. You retain your own mental scores and Spellcasting. Only the **first** form grants temporary HP: changing forms does not refill them. Casting another concentration spell—or starting a spell with a casting time of 1 minute or more—conflicts with maintaining Shapechange. ## Arcana Unleashed choices — analysis

All eight specialist archmages are **CR 15, Medium or Small Humanoids**, not Wizard subclasses or character builds. Their complete source stat blocks are in [Arcana Unleashed, Magical Creatures](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-6-magical-creatures).

| Form | Distinctive reason to compare it | Important limit |
|---|---|---|
| Abjurer Archmage | Bonus Action temporary HP; rechargeable area banishment | Fortify recharges on 4–6; temporary HP replace rather than stack |
| Conjurer Archmage | Four Force attacks and Bonus Action teleport/swap | Benign Transposition is 3/day; swap requires a willing creature |
| Diviner Archmage | Truesight 60 ft.; speaks all languages | Listed Foresight is a separate pre-combat spell, not an automatic transformation benefit |
| Enchanter Archmage | Bonus Action enchantments alongside Force attacks | Dominate Person requires concentration; limited-use abilities retain their limits |
| Evoker Archmage | Flight and a rechargeable two-spell barrage | Barrage recharges on 5–6; follow its specific spell choices |
| Illusionist Archmage | Illusions plus Bonus Action Mirror Image | Hypnotic Pattern and other concentration spells compete with Shapechange |
| Necromancer Archmage | 60-foot aura grants chosen Undead Advantage on attacks and saves | Aura stops while Incapacitated; requires existing Undead allies |
| Transmuter Archmage | Flight, speed and at-will utility | At-will Polymorph still requires concentration |

Separate a monster's spell list from its **active spell effects**. Mind Blank and Foresight are marked as pre-combat casts, and Mage Armor is included in the listed AC. Agree with the DM how those prepared effects apply to a chosen form; the spell list alone is not an automatic casting of every buff. In particular, the Abjurer's Mind Blank defenses are conditional, not unconditional body immunities. Spells and reactions retain their normal action, component and usage requirements.

# Special Senses

![[Diviner Archmage.png]]

![[100 Assets/Statblocks for Compendium/True Polymorph/Taisus.png]]

**Taisus (CR 19):** Truesight 120 ft., broad condition immunities and repeatable Counterspell/Shield are strong comparisons, but a reaction is still required. Brain Drain expends the victim's slot; it does not restore yours. Its [Arcane Immortality](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-6-magical-creatures#TaisusStatBlock) depends on the creature's designated creation location; transformation is not a guaranteed player immortality route. Resolve that interaction and legendary benefits with the DM.
