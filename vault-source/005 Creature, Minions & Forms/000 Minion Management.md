# Minion Management
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Form Finder]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


Minions are strong because they add bodies and actions, but their value falls quickly if they slow the table or require constant correction.

## Core Rule

> Maintain control before adding more bodies.

The rules that determine a minion's value are:

- Its durability and defenses
- Positioning and movement options
- The action and range needed to command it
- Concentration requirements
- Duration and the conditions that end control
- Available reactions and their triggers

## Find Familiar / Battle Familiar

Compare forms by their senses, movement, defenses and available actions. A normal familiar rolls its own Initiative and cannot attack. Battle Familiar lasts one hour without Concentration; its separate Initiative and command rules still matter.

An empowered existing familiar keeps its underlying familiar identity and returns to its original form when the Battle Familiar temporary HP are gone.

## Summon Undead

Use one clear command and select the form for the encounter.

General roles:

- **Skeletal:** ranged pressure
- **Ghostly:** mobility/control
- **Putrid:** melee/control

Keep the Wizard safe: losing Concentration can erase the whole investment.

## Animate Dead

Best practices:

1. Create or renew control before the adventuring day when possible.
2. Use simple standing orders.
3. Keep ranged Skeletons at range.
4. Roll attacks in batches.
5. Do not create more creatures than the table can resolve quickly.
6. Control lasts 24 hours; a renewal must finish before that window ends.

## Arcana Unleashed Necromancer

At Necromancer 6:

- Maintain the required spellbook handling.
- Count **reliable hits**, not just bodies.
- Controlled Undead within 60 ft can gain the Intelligence-based Withering Strike rider when the feature applies.
- Only qualifying Necromancy-created/summoned Undead receive the subclass HP increase.
- An Undead familiar can qualify for the hit rider without automatically receiving the Necromancy-origin HP bonus.

A practical starting package is:

**1 Summon Undead + 1 Undead/Battle Familiar**

before escalating to a permanent squad.

## Planar Binding

Before binding:

- Secure a legal target.
- Secure containment.
- Secure the 1,000+ gp consumed jewel.
- Write commands that can actually be followed.
- Confirm the duration of the slot used.
- Plan what happens when control ends.

## Fast Turn Procedure

1. State the command once.
2. Move all similar creatures.
3. Roll similar attacks together.
4. Apply damage together.
5. Resolve damage and conditions using the relevant stat block.
6. End the minion turn.

Minions should increase your action economy, not consume the entire session.
