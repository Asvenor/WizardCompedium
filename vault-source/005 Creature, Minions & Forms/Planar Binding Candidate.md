## Choose value and containment together

[[Planar Binding]] requires an existing Celestial, Elemental, Fey or Fiend to remain within 60 feet for the entire one-hour casting. The 1,000+ GP jewel is consumed even when the target succeeds on the final Charisma save. The ongoing binding uses no Concentration; the **casting procedure does**. A successful binding does not change a hostile creature's attitude or remove the danger of badly phrased commands.

| Candidate | CR / binding defense | What makes it useful | Cost of using it safely |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glabrezu | 9; CHA save **+7**, Magic Resistance | Truesight 120; two Pincers plus Pummel **or Spellcasting**; at-will Dispel Magic | Difficult save target; its dispelling and spell list complicate magical containment; Pincers grapple only Medium or smaller creatures |
| Hezrou | 8; CHA save **+1**, Magic Resistance | AC 18, 157 HP, three Rends and a movement-efficient leap | Stench affects any creature starting within 10 feet, including allies; much of its damage is Poison |
| Vrock | 6; CHA save **+2**, Magic Resistance | Fly 60 and a once-daily area Stun | Spores and Screech can hit allies; Poison defenses matter; Screech is once daily, not repeatable crowd control |
| Succubus | 4; CHA save **+5**, **no Magic Resistance listed** | Deception/Persuasion +9, Stealth +7, Humanoid disguise and component-free Dominate Person | Charm is an actual Concentration spell, not unlimited simultaneous domination; damage can disrupt control; the creature remains intelligent and self-interested |

For example, against **DC 19** without other modifiers, failure chances are 55% / 85% / 80% / 65% respectively; with the first three creatures' Magic Resistance these become **30.25% / 72.25% / 64%**, while Succubus remains **65%**. These are final-save probabilities only—not the chance to find, capture, contain or safely command the creature. A natural 1 or 20 has no automatic success/failure rule on ordinary saving throws.

## Other useful branches

- **Transport:** [[Planar Binding Mount]] compares Pegasus and Nightmare.
- **Scouting or specialist movement:** [[Invisible Stalker - Creature]] and [[Earth Elemental - Creature]]; choose actual movement abilities, not a creature's name.
- **Support:** [[Couatl - Creature]] has strong senses and a spell list, but access, attitude and binding logistics still matter.
- **Object-created candidates:** [[True Polymorph#Planar Binding Assets]]; CR 9 is that spell mode's cap, not a Planar Binding cap. The initial caster cannot simply concentrate on True Polymorph while beginning another long-cast spell.
