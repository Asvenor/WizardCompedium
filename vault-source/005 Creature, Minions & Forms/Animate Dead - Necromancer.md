## Revised Necromancer — Arcana Unleashed

Use the [published revised Necromancer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#Necromancer), not the 2014 School of Necromancy feature package. The subclass does not automatically grant its old Command Undead feature or the old +Wizard-level HP / +PB weapon-damage formulas.

### Animate Dead operating limits

- Target a pile of bones or corpse of a Small or Medium Humanoid within 10 feet; the spell takes 1 minute, so it is not an instant in-combat reinforcement.
- A Bonus Action commands one or more creatures animated by this spell within 60 feet, giving the same command to a selected group. A general task continues until complete; no new command means Dodge and movement only to avoid harm.
- Control lasts 24 hours. Renewal must be cast before control ends. Ordinary level-3 renewal controls up to four existing Undead; each higher effective level adds two to either creation or renewal capacity.
- Do not assume a spellbook can simultaneously be held, replace every material component, and leave both hands free. Resolve the actual spell/focus, weapon, shield, and somatic requirements.
- [[000 Minion Management]] covers shared minion handling; [[Find Familiar]] covers familiar-specific limits. These are reference rules, not a character ledger.
