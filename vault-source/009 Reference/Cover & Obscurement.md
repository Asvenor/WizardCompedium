# Cover & Obscurement
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Rules Desk]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


## Cover

| Cover | Benefit |
|---|---|
| **Half Cover** | +2 AC and +2 to DEX saving throws. |
| **Three-Quarters Cover** | +5 AC and +5 to DEX saving throws. |
| **Total Cover** | Can't normally be targeted directly by an attack or spell from the other side. |

Only the strongest applicable degree counts; two sources of Half Cover do not automatically make +4 AC. Cover must lie between the attacker/effect and target. A creature can provide cover for another creature. An area effect may reach behind cover only as allowed by its geometry and specific text; see [[Spell Area]].

## Obscurement

| Type | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Lightly Obscured** | Disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks relying on sight. This does not itself impose Disadvantage on attacks. |
| **Heavily Obscured** | Opaque; you are effectively Blinded when trying to see something there, unless an applicable sense overcomes the obstruction. |

**Opaque is not solid:** Fog Cloud blocks ordinary sight, not arrows or a spell's clear physical path. A wall can block that path even if transparent. Darkness, fog, invisibility, and Total Cover are different obstacles; use [[Vision & Senses]] to determine which senses help.

When neither combatant can see the other, the attack's unseen-attacker Advantage and unseen-target Disadvantage cancel. That does **not** restore sight-required spells, reveal an unknown location, or remove cover. Heavy obscurement is a targeting tool, not automatic attack protection for everyone inside it.

## Wizard Use

- Total Cover can stop many targeted spells entirely.
- Breaking line of sight can be stronger than trying to win a Counterspell exchange.
- **Fog Cloud**, **Sleet Storm**, darkness effects, walls, doors, corners, and terrain can all change targeting.
- Remember that blocking enemy sight can also block your party.
- A spell requiring **"a creature you can see"** fails as an option if you can't see the target.
