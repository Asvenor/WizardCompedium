## 2024 area geometry

| Shape | Origin and dimensions |
|---|---|
| Cone | Extends in a chosen direction. Its width at any distance equals that distance: a 30-foot cone is 30 feet wide at its far end. Origin excluded unless the creator includes it. |
| Cube | Origin anywhere on a face, not at the center. Listed size is each side's length. Origin excluded unless included. |
| Cylinder | Origin at the center of the top or bottom circle; listed radius and height define the area. Origin included. |
| Emanation | Extends the listed distance from its origin creature or object in every direction, not simply from its center point. Moves with that origin unless instantaneous or stationary. Origin excluded unless included. |
| Line | Extends from an origin along the listed length and width. Origin excluded unless included. |
| Sphere | Extends the listed radius from its center origin. Origin included. |

Total Cover blocks locations when every straight line from the origin is blocked. Placing an unseen origin beyond an intervening obstruction puts it on the near side. The individual effect can make exceptions. A Self range or origin at the caster does not, by itself, make every shape follow the caster.

Source: [2024 PHB Rules Glossary — Area of Effect and the six shapes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#AreaofEffect).
