> [!warning] Rules clarification
> Grappled includes Disadvantage on attacks against targets other than the grappler. Prone includes Disadvantage on your own attacks and a half-Speed standing cost. Invisible's Initiative advantage is separate from its attack-related exception for a creature that can see you. Use [[Condition Quick Reference]] and the [2024 Rules Glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary) for the full conditions.

**Rules baseline:** 2024 Rules Glossary.

Quick reference for conditions that matter most to Wizard play.

| Condition | Key Effect |
|---|---|
| **Blinded** | Can't see. Attacks against the creature have Advantage; its attacks have Disadvantage. |
| **Charmed** | Can't attack the charmer or target it with damaging abilities or magical effects. The charmer has Advantage on ability checks to interact socially with it. |
| **Frightened** | Disadvantage on ability checks and attack rolls while the source of fear is within line of sight; can't willingly move closer to the source. |
| **Grappled** | Speed 0; Disadvantage on attacks against targets other than the grappler; the grappler can drag or carry the creature under the condition's movement rules. |
| **Incapacitated** | Can't take Actions, Bonus Actions, or Reactions; can't speak; Concentration ends; Initiative is rolled with Disadvantage while Incapacitated. |
| **Invisible** | Advantage on Initiative. Effects requiring sight cannot target it unless the observer can see it. Its attacks have Advantage and attacks against it have Disadvantage, except against a creature that can see it. |
| **Paralyzed** | Incapacitated, Speed 0, automatically fails STR/DEX saves, attacks against it have Advantage, and a hit is a Critical Hit if the attacker is within 5 feet. |
| **Petrified** | Transformed into solid material; Incapacitated, Speed 0, strong defensive changes, and severe vulnerability to being removed from play. |
| **Poisoned** | Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks. |
| **Prone** | Own attacks have Disadvantage. Attacks against it have Advantage within 5 feet and Disadvantage farther away. Must crawl or spend half its Speed (round down) to stand; cannot stand at Speed 0. |
| **Restrained** | Speed 0; attacks against it have Advantage; its attacks have Disadvantage; Disadvantage on DEX saves. |
| **Stunned** | Incapacitated, fails STR/DEX saves, and attacks against it have Advantage. |
| **Unconscious** | Incapacitated and Prone, Speed 0, unaware of surroundings, drops held items, automatically fails STR/DEX saves. Attacks against it have Advantage; a hit is a Critical Hit if the attacker is within 5 feet. |

## Wizard Notes

- **Incapacitated is a major anti-caster condition** because it normally ends Concentration. Explicit exceptions such as Boon of the Iron Mind change that part, not the condition's other restrictions; see [[Concentration#Specific feature exceptions]].
- **Restrained** is excellent when the party has many attack rolls.
- **Paralyzed** becomes much stronger with melee allies because of Critical Hits.
- **Prone** helps attackers within 5 feet and hinders those farther away; distance, not the melee/ranged label, is decisive. A ranged attack at 5 feet can still suffer its separate close-combat Disadvantage.
- Check immunity before relying on **Charmed**, **Frightened**, **Poisoned**, or **Unconscious**.
