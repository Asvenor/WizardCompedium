# Reaction Management
<!-- wizard-upgrade:start -->
[[Home]] · [[Rules Desk]] · [[Problem Navigator]]

<!-- wizard-upgrade:end -->


A Reaction is one of the Wizard's most valuable combat resources.

## Common Wizard Reactions

- Shield
- Counterspell
- Absorb Elements
- Silvery Barbs
- Temporal Shunt
- Chronal Shift
- Convergent Future
- Opportunity Attack
- Other subclass or item reactions

## Reaction Discipline

Before spending your Reaction, ask:

1. What can happen before my next turn?
2. Could I need Shield?
3. Could an enemy cast a decisive spell?
4. Is this roll important enough for Chronal Shift or Silvery Barbs?
5. Does spending the Reaction actually change the encounter?

Do not use a premium Reaction on small value if a catastrophic event could happen later in the round.

---

# Baiting Enemy Reactions

Enemy reactions can often be exhausted deliberately.

Examples:

- Force an enemy to spend Shield before another attacker acts.
- Trigger an opportunity attack with a less-important creature before moving a vulnerable ally.
- Force an enemy Counterspell on a lower-priority spell.
- Move outside Counterspell range before using the spell that matters.
- Break line of sight before casting.

## Counterspell Positioning

Counterspell is limited by:

- Range
- Sight
- Reaction availability
- Valid spellcasting trigger

Sometimes the best Counterspell strategy is simply:

**Cast from farther away or from behind Total Cover.**

Cover must still leave a legal target and clear path for your own spell; most spells cannot target through Total Cover. For a proposed Ready-behind-cover sequence, use the targeting, Concentration, and Reaction cautions in [[Countering Spellcasters]].

# Denying Reactions

Effects that remove or block Reactions can enable:

- Safe movement
- Escapes
- Forced repositioning
- Casting without certain enemy interruptions
- Safer melee disengagement

Check the exact duration: losing Reactions until the **start** or **end** of a turn can produce very different windows.

---

# Ready and Reaction Conflict

A Readied Action normally consumes your Reaction when triggered.

If you Ready something, you may be giving up:

- Shield
- Counterspell
- Absorb Elements
- Subclass reactions

Account for that before committing.

---

# Core Rule

> Treat your Reaction as a reserved resource, not a free extra action.
