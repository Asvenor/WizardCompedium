# Condition Quick Reference

[[Home]] · [[Rules Desk]] · [[Conditions]]

**Source and errata baseline:** all 15 conditions in the official [SRD 5.2.1 Rules Glossary](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=176), checked 2026-09-12. Use this updated 2024 wording rather than the 2014 glossary; recheck if later official errata appear. Page numbers below refer to that PDF. An effect can add restrictions beyond its condition.

**ADV** = Advantage; **DIS** = Disadvantage. **Speed 0** below also means it cannot increase. **Incapacitated** includes every effect in its row.

The condition summaries below also agree with the current [2024 PHB condition entries](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#BlindedCondition). Specific spell and feature text can override a general consequence.

## Common tactical mistakes

- **Distance matters:** Prone's attack modifier and Paralyzed/Unconscious critical hits use **5 feet**, not the melee/ranged label. Critical hits still require a hit.
- **Invisible Initiative is separate:** the can-see-you exception qualifies the attack benefits, not the condition's Initiative benefit. Invisible does not automatically mean hidden or an unknown location.
- **Checks are not saves:** Poisoned and Frightened do not by themselves penalize saving throws. Exhaustion affects all D20 Tests, including saves.
- **Grappled is not Restrained:** it does not itself give attackers ADV or penalize your DEX saves. An action can attempt Athletics/Acrobatics versus escape DC; the grapple also ends if the grappler is Incapacitated, releases you, or you exceed its range. Crawling costs one extra foot per foot, or two extra in Difficult Terrain. [SRD pp. 179, 182](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf#page=179).

Conditions can combine. Apply all relevant rules, then resolve Advantage/Disadvantage normally; one source of each cancels all sources of both. Check the originating effect for duration, repeat saves, removal and additional restrictions.

Specific features can override an individual consequence. For example, **Boon of the Iron Mind** can preserve Concentration through Stunned or Paralyzed, but not Petrified or Unconscious; it does not restore actions or speech. See [[Concentration#Specific feature exceptions]].
