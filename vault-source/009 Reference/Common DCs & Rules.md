A compact place for frequently needed numbers and rules.

## Concentration

**DC 10 or half the damage taken (round down), whichever is higher, to a maximum DC of 30.**

## Spell Scroll Copying

When the active Spell Scroll rules require an Arcana check to copy a scroll:

**DC = 10 + spell level**

## Higher-Level Spell Scroll Use

When the active scroll rules require a spellcasting ability check:

**DC = 10 + spell level**

## Passive Score

The current glossary explicitly defines **Passive Perception**:

**10 + Wisdom (Perception) check bonus**, with **+5 for Advantage** or **−5 for Disadvantage** on the relevant check. It is awareness without consciously making the check, not a universal minimum result on active rolls.

## Typical task DCs

| Difficulty | Very easy | Easy | Medium | Hard | Very hard | Nearly impossible |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| DC | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 |

The DM determines whether a check is appropriate; not every impossible fictional task becomes possible with DC 30. See [[Saving Throws & Checks]].

## Jumping, Carrying, Objects

| Rule | Current baseline |
|---|---|
| Long Jump | With a 10-foot immediate run-up, up to STR score in feet; standing jump half. Every foot still costs movement. Landing in Difficult Terrain requires DC 10 DEX (Acrobatics) or Prone. |
| High Jump | After moving at least 10 feet on foot, height = 3 + STR modifier feet, minimum 0; standing jump half. Reaching arms adds half your height above your head. Jumping still spends movement. |
| Carrying | Small/Medium: STR × 15 lb carried; STR × 30 lb dragged, lifted, or pushed. Tiny halves these amounts; Large doubles, Huge quadruples, Gargantuan ×8. |
| Over-capacity movement | Dragging, lifting, or pushing more than carrying capacity limits Speed to at most 5 feet. |
| Falling | 1d6 Bludgeoning per 10 feet, maximum 20d6; land Prone unless no falling damage is taken. Falling into liquid allows a Reaction DC 15 Athletics/Acrobatics check to halve the damage. |
| Suffocation | Breath held for 1 + CON modifier minutes, minimum 30 seconds. Once out of breath/choking: gain 1 Exhaustion at each turn's end; breathing again removes only Exhaustion gained from suffocating. |

**Objects:** suggested ACs are 11 cloth/paper/rope, 13 crystal/glass/ice, 15 wood, 17 stone, 19 iron/steel, 21 mithral, 23 adamantine. Suggested fragile/resilient HP: Tiny **2/5**, Small **3/10**, Medium **4/18**, Large **5/27**. Huge/Gargantuan structures use smaller sections as the DM determines; damage thresholds may make weak hits ineffective.

Objects are immune to Poison and Psychic damage and normally lack ability scores: they cannot make ability checks and fail saves unless a rule gives them scores. **A creature-only spell still cannot target an object.** Damage type, material, magic-item resilience, and explicit spell exceptions matter more than the suggested numbers alone.

Sources: [2024 PHB — glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#BreakingObjects) and [ability checks](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/playing-the-game#AbilityChecks).
