**Quick Legend**

**Atk** = Spell Attack · **S** = Sphere · **Cb** = Cube · **Cyl** = Cylinder · **E** = Emanation  
**24** = 2024 Rules · **5eX** = Expanded 5e Source · **Duna** = Dunamancy · **AU** = Arcana Unleashed (official Wizards of the Coast expansion)
