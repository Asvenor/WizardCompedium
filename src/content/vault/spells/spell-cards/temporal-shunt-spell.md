## Decision

**Dunamancy access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. This is not an automatic addition to every Wizard's available spells. [Dunamancy Spells](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/)

Reaction denial that temporarily removes the triggering creature.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Reserve your reaction and confirm the actual trigger. [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/)

## Rules that decide the play

React when a visible creature within 120 feet makes an attack roll or starts casting. Wisdom failure makes that attack miss or wastes that spell, and the creature vanishes until its next turn starts; it returns to its old space or the nearest unoccupied one and does not remember the interruption. It normally still gets its next turn after returning. This is not the 2024 Counterspell slot-refund rule. A successful Wisdom save defeats it; the level-5 slot and Reaction are a substantial cost for denying only one attack.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 5; all targets must be within 30 feet of one another.
