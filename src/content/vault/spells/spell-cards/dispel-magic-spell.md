## Decision

Ends spells; higher-level effects can require a check.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Targets one creature, object, or magical effect, ending qualifying ongoing spells—not every supernatural trait or magic item's permanent properties. At base level, spells through level 3 end automatically; each higher-level spell needs a spellcasting-ability check against DC 10 + spell level. No proficiency bonus is added merely for being a spellcaster.

## Upcasting

Automatically end spells whose level is no higher than the slot used. Higher-level spells still require the spellcasting-ability check.
