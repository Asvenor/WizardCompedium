## Decision

Versatile size manipulation and STR support; value depends on the actual size-sensitive obstacle or ally rather than the modest damage rider.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Target a visible creature or an object not worn/carried; an unwilling creature negates it with a CON save. Change size by one category. Enlarge grants Advantage on STR checks/saves and +1d4 on enlarged weapon or Unarmed Strike hits; Reduce gives Disadvantage and subtracts 1d4 from those hits, minimum 1 damage. Carried equipment changes with a creature; dropped items revert, and thrown ammunition/weapons revert after resolving their hit or miss. It does not directly increase STR or grant extra reach.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).
