## Decision

Non-Concentration terrain transformation and movement/control pressure.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for control (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible area of nonmagical rock or mud fitting within a 40-foot Cube is required. Rock-to-mud movement costs 4 feet per foot; the casting, first entry on a turn, and turn end can require Strength saves against Restrained. An action frees a creature from this mud without a check. Ceiling mud instead falls for 4d8 Bludgeoning, Dexterity save for half. Mud-to-rock affects at most 10 feet of depth and uses Dexterity against Restrained; escaping that rock needs an action and DC20 Strength check or breaking AC15/25HP rock (immune to Poison/Psychic). It does not create suitable terrain where none exists.
