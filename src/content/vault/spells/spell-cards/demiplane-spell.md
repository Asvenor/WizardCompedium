This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

The Medium door needs a visible flat solid surface and opens into a 30-foot-per-side room. When the door vanishes, creatures can choose to be shunted outside and land Prone; objects stay. Knowing another caster's demiplane's nature and contents can permit access, so secrecy is not an absolute security guarantee. See [Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/).
