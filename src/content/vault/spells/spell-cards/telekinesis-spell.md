## Rules that decide the play

Use on casting and with later Magic actions; a Huge-or-smaller creature makes a STR save, not the old contested ability check. Failure permits up to 30 feet of movement and Restrained through the end of the caster's next turn. Continuing control needs another use and failed save. Switching targets releases the previous one; worn/carried objects also allow their carrier a STR save.
