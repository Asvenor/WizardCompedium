## Decision

Useful close-area searching once the target type or individual is known. It gives direction, not a map, exact coordinates, or continent-scale tracking.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Locate a familiar named/described creature, or the nearest creature of a specific kind previously seen within 30 feet. Detection works within 1,000 feet, revealing the direction of location and movement. A target in a different form is not found. Any thickness of lead blocking the direct path defeats detection; this is the revised blocker, not the old running-water clause.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).
