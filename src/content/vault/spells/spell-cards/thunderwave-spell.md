## Decision

Close-range damage and 10-foot push; audible at 300 feet.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

The 15-foot Cube originates from the caster; it is not automatically centered on the caster. Failed CON save: 2d8 Thunder and a 10-foot push; success halves damage and prevents the push. Unsecured objects wholly inside are also pushed. The 300-foot boom makes it a poor stealth choice.

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 damage per slot level above 1.
