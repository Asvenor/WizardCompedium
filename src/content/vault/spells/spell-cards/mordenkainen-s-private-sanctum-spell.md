## Decision

Privacy, anti-scrying, teleport, and planar ward; permanent after repeated use.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose the desired security properties when casting, including teleportation and planar-travel blocks. Those blocks do not have an automatic ally exemption. Repeating the spell at the same spot daily for 365 days makes it last until dispelled, not immune to dispelling. See [Long-Term Magical Defenses](/library/advanced/long-term-magical-defenses/).

## Upcasting

Add 100 feet to the Cube size per slot level above 4.
