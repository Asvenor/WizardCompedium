## Decision

Emergency AC and Magic Missile defense.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Reserve your reaction and confirm the actual trigger. [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/)

## Rules that decide the play

The +5 AC applies to the triggering hit and lasts until the start of the caster's next turn; Magic Missile damage is prevented during that period. It does not help a saving throw or turn a critical hit into a miss. Check the V/S requirements and [Held items and spell components](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#held-items-and-spell-components) before assuming a two-handed equipment setup can always cast it.
