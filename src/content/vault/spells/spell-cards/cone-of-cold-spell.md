## Decision

A broad Cold burst provides a different damage type and save from Fireball. Getting the cone into position can expose the Wizard, and Constitution-heavy enemies may be poor targets.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment).

## Rules that decide the play

Every creature in a **60-foot Cone from you** makes a Constitution save against **8d8 Cold**; success halves damage. A creature killed by the spell freezes into a statue until thawed. This does not automatically impose Restrained or Petrified on survivors.

The spell itself does not exclude allies. Check the actual cone and any applicable subclass protection before casting.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 5 adds **1d8** damage.
