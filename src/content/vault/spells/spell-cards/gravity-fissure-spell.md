## Decision

A long Force-damage line that also threatens creatures beside it; distinguish the two save outcomes.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

The Line is 100 feet long and 5 feet wide. Creatures in it make Constitution saves against 8d8 Force, half on success. Creatures outside the Line but within 10 feet of it instead take 8d8 Force and are pulled into it only on a failed Constitution save; their successful save avoids that outside-line effect entirely. Do not apply the half-damage rule to both groups. There is no printed ally exemption, so account for the wider danger zone when aiming.

## Upcasting

Damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 6.
