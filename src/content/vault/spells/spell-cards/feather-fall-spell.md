## Decision

Emergency fall protection for multiple creatures.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Reserve your reaction and confirm the actual trigger. [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/)

## Rules that decide the play

One Reaction protects up to five falling creatures in range, slowing descent to 60 feet per round. Landing before the spell ends prevents fall damage and ends the spell for that creature. It does not grant flight, horizontal movement, or immunity after its one-minute duration expires.
