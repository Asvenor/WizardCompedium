## Rules that decide the play

Up to three non-instantaneous effects can coexist. Cleaning/soiling and warming/chilling/flavoring have one-cubic-foot limits; the latter lasts an hour. A created trinket or hand-sized image lasts only until the end of the next turn, and a trinket cannot deal damage or have monetary value. This is utility, not free saleable equipment.
