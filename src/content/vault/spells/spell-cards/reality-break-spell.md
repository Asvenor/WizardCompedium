## Decision

**Dunamancy access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. This is not an automatic addition to every Wizard's available spells. [Dunamancy Spells](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/)

Repeated reality-warping disruption; powerful but unpredictable.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A failed Wisdom save removes the target's Reactions for the duration. At each of its turn starts, roll a d10: 1–2 gives 6d12 Psychic and Stunned until turn end; 3–5 gives a Dexterity save against 8d12 Force, half on success; 6–8 teleports it up to 30 feet to a visible unoccupied space, deals 10d12 Force, and knocks it Prone; 9–10 gives 10d12 Cold and Blinded until turn end. It repeats the Wisdom save at turn end to end the spell. The damage/control outcome is random—not guaranteed Stunned every round—and the initial successful save produces no effect.
