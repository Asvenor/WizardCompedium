## Decision

A prepared containment operation, not a normal one-action combat finisher. Obtain the target-specific statuette and a safe minute to cast; a successful save blocks further attempts for 24 hours.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose a creature you can see within range. It makes a Wisdom save; success prevents the effect and grants immunity to this spell for 24 hours. Failure imprisons it without aging or needing air, food, or water. Divination spells cannot locate or perceive it, and it cannot teleport. Choose one prison:

- **Burial:** a just-large-enough underground force globe blocks passage in either direction.
- **Chaining:** ground-anchored chains impose Restrained and prevent any displacement.
- **Hedged Prison:** a chosen demiplanar enclosure bars teleportation and planar travel.
- **Minimus Containment:** the victim shrinks to 1 inch inside an indestructible gem or similar object; only light passes through.
- **Slumber:** Unconscious and unable to be awakened.

Specify an observable release trigger when casting. The DM must agree it is highly likely within the next decade; an impossible release clause is not legal. Dispel Magic ends this spell only with a level 9 slot, targeting the prison or casting component. The 5,000+ GP component depicts this particular target; it is not consumed and does not use the legacy Hit-Dice-based price or separate components for each prison.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).
