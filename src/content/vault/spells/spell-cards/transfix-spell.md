## Decision

Repeatable Charm-based attraction and Incapacitation; powerful but requires proximity.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/) Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

On casting, choose one creature you can see within 60 feet. A failed Charisma save Charms and Incapacitates it; on its turns it approaches by the most direct route, trying to reach within 5 feet of you. Ending a turn that close deals 4d8 Psychic damage. It still provokes Opportunity Attacks, but repeats its save before entering damaging terrain. Later Magic actions can add targets; a creature that has saved against this casting cannot be targeted again. Charmed immunity defeats the engine, and maintaining proximity exposes you to other enemies.

## Upcasting

Add 1d8 to the end-of-turn Psychic damage per slot level above 7.
