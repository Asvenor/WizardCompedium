## Decision

Use a choke point or allied movement tools to make the hazard matter. An enemy able to leave cheaply can reduce its value; do not spend Concentration just to imitate a burst spell.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment).

## Rules that decide the play

Place an opaque wall on a solid surface: maximum **60 feet long × 20 high × 1 thick**, or a ring **20 feet in diameter × 20 high × 1 thick**. Creatures in the wall when created save against **5d8 Fire**, halved on success.

Choose its damaging side. Later **5d8 Fire** has no stated save: first entry into the wall each turn, or ending a turn inside it or within **10 feet of its hot side**. The opposite side does not radiate damage. Starting nearby is not an additional trigger; overlapping end-turn descriptions are not two separate damage awards.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 4 adds **1d8** to the damage.
