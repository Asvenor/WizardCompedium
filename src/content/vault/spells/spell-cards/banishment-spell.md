## Rules that decide the play

A failed CHA save removes and Incapacitates the target; this spell gives no repeated save. Maintaining the full minute sends an Aberration, Celestial, Elemental, Fey, or Fiend to a random location on a DM-chosen plane associated with its type. Other creature types return when the spell ends.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 4.
