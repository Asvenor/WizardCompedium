## Decision

Non-Concentration Prone/terrain pressure.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for control (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

A 10-foot ground square becomes Difficult Terrain. DEX saves against Prone occur on creation, on entry, and at a creature's turn end in the area. The grease is explicitly nonflammable. It can coexist with a Concentration control spell.
