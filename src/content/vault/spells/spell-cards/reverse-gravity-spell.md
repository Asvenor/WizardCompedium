## Rules that decide the play

A 50-foot-radius, 100-foot-high Cylinder sends unanchored creatures and objects upward. The DEX save is specifically to grab a reachable fixed object; it is not an unconditional save against the spell. Impact and later descent can cause falling damage. Flight, available anchors, ceilings, and allies in the area determine its actual payoff.
