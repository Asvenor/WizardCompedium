## Decision

Ten-minute no-Concentration elemental defense and retaliation.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose Cold Resistance with Fire retaliation, or Fire Resistance with Cold retaliation. Retaliation is 2d8 and requires a creature within 5 feet to hit the caster with a melee attack roll; no Reaction is listed. It does not retaliate against every damage source or prevent the triggering hit.
