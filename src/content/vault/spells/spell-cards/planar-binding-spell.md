## Decision

Bind an eligible outsider; consumes a 1,000+ gp jewel and normally needs containment.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Only Celestials, Elementals, Fey, and Fiends are normal targets. The creature must stay within 60 feet for the entire hour, then fail a CHA save. Extending a summoning spell's duration is not explicit permission to ignore its other ending conditions. Read [Planar Binding](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/) for casting-Concentration conflicts, containment, and hostile command interpretation.

## Upcasting

Duration by slot: level 6 gives 10 days, level 7 gives 30 days, level 8 gives 180 days, and level 9 gives 366 days.
