## At a glance

Level 2 Divination; Action; Self; V, S, M (1 Copper Piece); Concentration up to 1 minute. Casting fields checked on 12 September 2026.

## Decision

Use for a planned investigation when ordinary conversation leaves a specific question unresolved. Sensing thoughts and reading them are different modes; each has limits. Shallow reading does not trigger the deeper-probing save, but visible components and suspicious behavior still have consequences. Deeper probing alerts the target regardless of its save outcome. Maintain concentration and follow the language/telepathy and barrier restrictions.

The Wisdom save belongs to deeper probing, not every use of the spell. A successful later Intelligence (Arcana) check can end unwanted probing. No higher-slot benefit is listed.
