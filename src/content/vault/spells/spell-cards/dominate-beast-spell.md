## Decision

A niche answer when taking control of an important Beast changes the encounter. Confirm the creature type rather than judging its appearance; many dangerous animal-like monsters are not Beasts.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating). When a feature always prepares it, it does not spend an ordinary Wizard preparation.

## Rules that decide the play

One visible Beast makes a WIS save against being Charmed, with Advantage if your side is fighting it. Damage grants another save; success ends the effect. There is no routine end-of-turn save.

While on the same plane, give telepathic orders on your turn without spending an action. The target follows them on its turn; without further orders it protects itself. Ordering its Reaction also spends **your Reaction**. Charm immunity blocks the effect.

## Upcasting

Concentration duration: slot **5 → 10 minutes**, **6 → 1 hour**, **7+ → 8 hours**. Upcasting never adds targets.
