## Decision

A useful non-Concentration weapon upgrade when the party lacks magic equipment. Existing magical weapons are not valid targets.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a nonmagical weapon to make it magical with +1 to its attack and damage rolls. Casting this spell again ends the previous instance, so the lack of Concentration does not let one caster stockpile active enchanted weapons.

## Upcasting

Slots 3–5 give +2; slots 6+ give +3.
