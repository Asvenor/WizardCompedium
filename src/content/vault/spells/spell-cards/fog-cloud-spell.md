## Rules that decide the play

A 20-foot-radius Sphere is Heavily Obscured, affecting allies as well as enemies. Strong wind disperses it. Losing sight can deny sight-dependent spells, but it does not itself silence casting or make every attack against the party miss. Compare [Cover & Obscurement](/library/reference/cover-obscurement/).
