## Decision

Ranged emergency healing that preserves your Action. Its best combat use is often restoring an ally at 0 HP before that ally loses a turn, rather than trying to outheal sustained enemy damage.

**Preparation priority:** High value when available (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

One visible creature in range regains **2d4 + your spellcasting ability modifier** HP. Use the granting feature's ability: for example, Wisdom for Cleric Spellcasting or the ability chosen for Magic Initiate. Wizard Intelligence is not automatic.

It does not resurrect the dead or clear unrelated conditions. The current spell has no blanket exclusion for Undead or Constructs; a target's own restrictions can still prevent healing.

A slot-cast Healing Word uses your one spell-slot expenditure to cast a spell that turn. A genuinely slot-free feature casting is different; see [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/). Neither case grants a second Bonus Action.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 1 adds **2d4 healing**. The 2024 spell is Abjuration, not the legacy Evocation version.
