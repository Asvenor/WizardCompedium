## Decision

Elemental damage defense; requires a campaign-approved expanded source.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Reserve your reaction and confirm the actual trigger. [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/)

## Rules that decide the play

React to taking Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Thunder damage; Resistance to that triggering type lasts until your next turn starts and mitigates the triggering damage. The first melee attack that hits on your next turn adds 1d6 of the same damage type. A ranged spell attack does not qualify, and an off-turn melee hit before your next turn does not spend the rider. The spell does not cover Force, Psychic, Radiant, Poison, Necrotic, or weapon damage outside its listed types.

## Upcasting

Each slot level above 1 adds 1d6 to the next-turn melee-hit rider; it does not improve the Resistance.
