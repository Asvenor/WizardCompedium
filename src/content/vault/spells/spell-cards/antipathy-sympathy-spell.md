## Decision

Ten-day attraction/repulsion setup.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for control (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Targets one Huge-or-smaller creature or object and a specified kind of creature. Coming within 120 feet triggers WIS against repulsion (Frightened) or attraction (Charmed). Successful saves give only one minute of immunity to that effect. It is a ten-day area-behavior tool, not unconditional control of every creature in the region.
