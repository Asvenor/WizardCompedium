Long-term concealment and suspended protection; expensive consumed component.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Works on an object or willing creature; it conceals rather than grants damage immunity. A creature becomes Unconscious in suspended animation. Any damage ends the spell. A chosen ending condition must occur or be visible within a mile, so define a usable recovery method before relying on indefinite storage.
