## Decision

Repeatable single-target disabling when several rounds of control justify the ongoing Magic actions and high-level slot.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature within 60 feet makes a WIS save against the chosen effect. On later turns a Magic action can target another creature, but not one that succeeded against this casting. Asleep gives Unconscious until damage or another creature's waking action. Panicked Frightens and forces Dash away; it ends after reaching a place at least 60 feet away where the creature cannot see the caster. Sickened gives Poisoned. Check the relevant condition immunities; these effects are not ordinary recurring-save damage.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).
