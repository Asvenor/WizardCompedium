## Decision

A low-level sustained attacker with a choice between flying withdrawal and ground/water teamwork. Slot 4 doubles its attacks. For ordinary Wizards this is not a spellbook pick; the Illusionist access route is the reason it appears here.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose Air, Land, or Water and place the allied spirit in a visible unoccupied space within range. It shares your Initiative but acts immediately after you. Verbal commands require no action; otherwise it Dodges and moves away from danger. It disappears at 0 HP or when the spell ends.

## Summoned spirit

Let **L = casting slot level**. Small Beast; AC **11 + L**; HP **20 + 5 × (L − 2)** for Air or **30 + 5 × (L − 2)** for Land/Water. Walk 30 feet; additionally Land climbs 30, Air flies 60, and Water swims 30. Darkvision 60 feet, Passive Perception 12. Ability/save modifiers: STR +4, DEX +0, CON +3, INT −3, WIS +2, CHA −3. It understands the caster's languages, uses the caster's Proficiency Bonus, and has **no CR** (0 XP).

Multiattack makes **floor(L / 2)** attacks: caster's spell attack modifier, melee reach 5 feet, **1d8 + 4 + L Piercing** per hit.

| Form | Operating limit |
|---|---|
| Air | Flyby prevents Opportunity Attacks when it flies out of an enemy's reach |
| Land / Water | Pack Tactics gives Advantage if an ally of the spirit is within 5 feet of the target and is not Incapacitated |
| Water | Breathes **only underwater**; swimming speed is not amphibious breathing |

## Upcasting and Wizard access

Higher slots improve AC, HP, damage, and attacks; attacks rise at 4, 6, and 8.

Illusionist 6 always prepares this spell and can change its school to Illusion. The subclass's free casting has half normal spirit HP; it does not waive the priced component. See [Arcana Unleashed Builds](/library/character/arcana-unleashed-builds/). Ordinary slot castings use normal HP.
