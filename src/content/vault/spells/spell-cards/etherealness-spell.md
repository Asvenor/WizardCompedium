## Decision

Long-duration non-Concentration infiltration or escape through the Border Ethereal, subject to planar access and dangers on that plane.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Enter the Border Ethereal, moving in any direction; vertical travel costs an extra foot per foot. Perceive the original plane only within 60 feet. Normally interact only with Ethereal creatures, objects, and effects; special features can override that separation. Return to the corresponding space when the spell ends. An occupied return space causes displacement and 2 Force damage per foot moved. The spell immediately ends if cast on the Ethereal Plane or a nonbordering plane.

## Upcasting

At slot 8, target up to three willing creatures total, including the caster; at slot 9, up to six total. All must start within 10 feet.
