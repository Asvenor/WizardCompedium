## Decision

A reliable non-Concentration light source; illumination also reveals position, so coordinate with stealth plans.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a Large-or-smaller object not worn or carried by someone else. It emits bright light for 20 feet and dim light for 20 feet beyond that. An opaque covering blocks the light, and casting Light again ends the previous instance. This version is not a hostile-creature tagging spell.
