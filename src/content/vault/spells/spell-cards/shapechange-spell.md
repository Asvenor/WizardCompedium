## Rules that decide the play

Requires a previously seen form within your level/CR, excluding Constructs and Undead. Only the first form grants Temporary HP; switching forms does not refresh them, and reaching 0 Temporary HP does not end Shapechange. Retain your own listed mental traits and Spellcasting.
