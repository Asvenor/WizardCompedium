## Decision

A powerful social/narrative specialist, not reliable instant combat domination. Language, time, plausibility, and immunity to Charmed all matter.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature makes a WIS save, with Advantage if fighting the caster. Failure Charms and Incapacitates it while it listens. Any damage or another spell targeting it ends this casting without a memory change. Describe the altered memory in a language it understands before this spell ends. The eligible event lasts at most ten minutes and occurred within 24 hours; erase, clarify, change, or invent its memory. Nonsensical changes need not alter behavior. Remove Curse or Greater Restoration restores true memory.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).

## Upcasting

The event can be up to 7 days old at slot 6, 30 days at 7, 365 days at 8, or any age at 9. The ten-minute event limit remains.
