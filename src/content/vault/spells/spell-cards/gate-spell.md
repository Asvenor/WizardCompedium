## Rules that decide the play

Creates a one-sided entrance to a precise location on another plane. A planar ruler can block the portal. Calling a particular creature requires its actual name and a different plane, and grants no control over it. The 5,000+ gp diamond is reusable; the arriving creature's response is not guaranteed friendly.
