## Decision

Strategic remote surveillance; target familiarity and connection matter.

## Rules that decide the play

Creature targets must be on the same plane; knowledge and physical connections modify their WIS save. Success prevents another attempt on that target for 24 hours. Failure creates a following sensor within 10 feet. Alternatively view a previously seen location with a stationary sensor. The spell is surveillance, not automatic knowledge of coordinates.
