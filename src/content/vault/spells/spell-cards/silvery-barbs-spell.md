## Decision

Forces a successful D20 Test reroll and grants ally Advantage; heavy Reaction competition.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Reserve your reaction and confirm the actual trigger. [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/)

## Rules that decide the play

React to a visible creature within 60 feet succeeding on an attack, check, or save. It rerolls one d20 and uses the lower result. A different creature you can see within 60 feet (including you) gains Advantage on its next attack, check, or save within 1 minute; only one unspent Silvery Barbs benefit can empower that creature at a time. This does not cancel a Legendary Resistance success. The real cost is your Reaction: weigh the reroll against needing Shield, Absorb Elements, or Counterspell before your next turn.
