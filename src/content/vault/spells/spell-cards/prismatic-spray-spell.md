## Decision

A wide, unpredictable burst. Its random ray assignment makes it less dependable than a targeted answer to a known defense.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Each creature in a 60-foot Cone makes a DEX save and receives a separately rolled d8 ray. Rays 1–5 deal 12d6 Fire, Acid, Lightning, Poison, or Cold, respectively, with half on a successful save. Ray 6 on failure Restrains and starts end-of-turn CON saves: three successes end it, three failures Petrify; neither series must be consecutive. Ray 7 on failure Blinds, then a WIS save at the start of the caster's next turn ends blindness either way; failure also sends the creature to a DM-chosen plane. Ray 8 applies two rerolled non-8 rays. Initial DEX success avoids the condition rays; check damage/condition defenses separately.
