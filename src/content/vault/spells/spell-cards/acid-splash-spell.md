## Decision

A small-area cantrip for clustered targets. It is not automatically selective and can hit allies.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose a point within range; every creature in a 5-foot-radius Sphere makes a DEX save, taking 1d6 Acid damage on failure and none on success. This is the revised area version, not the legacy two-creature targeting rule.

## Scaling

Damage becomes 2d6/3d6/4d6 at character levels 5/11/17.
