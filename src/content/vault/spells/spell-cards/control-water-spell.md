## Decision

An exceptional terrain specialist around substantial water, not a general-purpose attack spell. Plan which mode matters before spending Concentration.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Control water in a Cube up to 100 feet across. Later Magic actions can repeat or change the mode. Flood raises standing water up to 20 feet, or produces a 20-foot wave in a large body; Huge-or-smaller vehicles are carried and have a 25% capsize chance. Part Water opens a trench, refilling over the round after it ends. Redirect Flow changes direction even over obstacles, but normal terrain flow resumes outside the area.

Whirlpool needs at least 50 feet square of water, 25 feet deep. Creatures in the water within 25 feet are pulled 10 feet toward it. Entering the whirlpool for the first time on a turn or ending a turn inside causes a STR save for 2d8 Bludgeoning, half on success. Swimming away first requires an action and a successful Athletics check against the caster's DC; this is not a blanket prohibition on all other escape methods.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).
