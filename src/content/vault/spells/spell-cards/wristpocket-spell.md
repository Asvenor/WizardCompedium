## Decision

Conceals a small held object in an extradimensional space, but occupies Concentration.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

The object must be in your hand, held only by you, and weigh no more than 5 pounds. An action recalls it to a free hand; another action stores it again. If still stored when the spell ends, it appears at your feet. It is not long-term storage or a way to conceal a creature. Ritual casting avoids a slot but takes 10 additional minutes and still requires Concentration during casting and the effect.
