## Decision

Twenty-five-word message/reply; interplanar failure risk and target block.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

The recipient must have been met by the caster or described by someone who met it. Message and immediate reply each have a 25-word limit. Cross-plane delivery has a 5% failure chance; the caster learns of failure. A recipient can block further Sending from that caster for eight hours.
