## Decision

Major 2024 damage engine; requires close-range positioning and protection.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/) Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Bonus damage applies to your attacks that hit creatures inside your 15-foot Emanation. This requires attack rolls and close positioning; it is not bonus damage on every spell or every damage instance.

## Upcasting

Base extra damage is 2d8 per qualifying hit. Each slot level above 4 adds 1d8, using the corrected 2024 rules.
