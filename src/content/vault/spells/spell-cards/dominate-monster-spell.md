## Decision

Powerful creature control; save, Concentration, and Charm immunity limit reliability.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for primary concentration (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

Protect concentration and compare its payoff with your current effect. [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/) Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

The initial WIS save has Advantage if the caster or allies are fighting the target, and every instance of damage grants another save. Commands through the same-plane link need no action on the caster's turn, but forcing a Reaction also costs the caster's Reaction. Charm immunity and hostile focus fire can defeat an expensive control investment.

## Upcasting

A level-9 slot extends the maximum Concentration duration to 8 hours.
