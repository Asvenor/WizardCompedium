## Decision

One-hour Truesight; consumes 25+ gp materials.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Grants a willing touched creature Truesight 120 feet for an hour. The 25+ gp mushroom powder is consumed. It counters the things specified by Truesight, not solid walls or every source of obscuration; see [Vision & Senses](/library/reference/vision-senses/).
