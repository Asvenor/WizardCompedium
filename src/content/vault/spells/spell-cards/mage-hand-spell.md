## Decision

Remote object manipulation; not an attack.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

The hand can manipulate an object when first cast; later control uses a Magic action and can move it 30 feet. It vanishes beyond 30 feet from the caster or when recast. Its 10-pound limit and bans on attacks and magic-item activation rule out many combat shortcuts.
