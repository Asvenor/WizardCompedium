## Decision

**Dunamancy access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. This is not an automatic addition to every Wizard's available spells. [Dunamancy Spells](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/)

One-hour d20 insurance; consumes a 100+ gp white pearl.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The finished effect needs no Concentration, but the 1-minute casting requires it. Confirm targets, components, and setup time in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

One willing visible recipient within 60 feet gains a one-use extra d20 for an attack roll, ability check, or save; it chooses between that d20 and the original result. It can instead use the effect on an attack against itself and choose which d20 applies. Resolve existing Advantage/Disadvantage first. Dismissing the effect uses it up. The 100+ GP pearl is consumed even if the recipient never spends the benefit before the hour expires.

## Upcasting

One additional target per slot level above 2. The casting still consumes the listed 100+ gp white pearl.
