## Decision

Small-area Force damage with a short speed penalty, without ongoing Concentration.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

Choose a visible point for a 10-foot-radius Sphere. Creatures in it on the casting turn make Constitution saves: failure takes 2d8 Force and halves speed until the end of the creature's next turn; success halves damage only. Until your next turn starts, moving or picking up an unattended object in the area requires a Strength check against your spell DC. It is not a persistent damage zone that retriggers whenever a creature enters.

## Upcasting

Damage increases by 1d8 per slot level above 1. The area and speed penalty do not scale.
