## Rules that decide the play

Only Aberrations, Celestials, Elementals, Fey, Fiends, and Undead trigger the protections. They attack the target with Disadvantage and cannot newly Charm, Frighten, or possess it. Existing effects are not automatically removed; the target instead has Advantage on new saves against them. Every casting consumes the listed Holy Water.
