## Decision

Two-creature rescue/escape; destination risk.

**Preparation priority:** High baseline value (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Moves the caster and optionally one willing creature within 5 feet; the revised spell has no size-matching clause. The destination need not be visible. An occupied arrival space or one completely filled by objects makes the teleport fail and deals 4d6 Force to each traveler. It cannot take a second passenger just because both are Small.
