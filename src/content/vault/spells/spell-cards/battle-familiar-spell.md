## Decision

A non-Concentration combat companion with strong even-slot breakpoints.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

One familiar only; choose Brute, Flyer, or Stalker. It acts on its own Initiative and can attack. At a level-2 slot the Brute has AC15/30HP, the others AC13/20HP; all are Medium with Speed40 and Swim30, and Flyer also has Fly30 (hover) and Flyby. Rend uses your spell attack modifier and deals 1d8+3+slot level Force; attacks equal half the slot level rounded down. Talented adds half the slot level rounded down to its checks and saves. Stalker can replace one attack with Prowl for safe movement and a Hide attempt. When upgrading Find Familiar, retain its original HP/Hit Dice and familiar features; the battle HP become Temporary HP, and losing all of those ends the upgrade. [Find Familiar](/library/creatures/find-familiar/)

## Upcasting

Use the slot level throughout the stat block: AC rises by 1 and HP by 5 per slot level; attacks equal half the slot level, rounded down. Each Rend also adds the slot level to damage.
