## Rules that decide the play

Choose a Sphere of up to 40-foot radius. Its magical Darkness defeats ordinary Darkvision; it is Difficult Terrain and blocks sound and Verbal spellcasting. Creatures or objects entirely inside are immune to Thunder damage, and creatures entirely inside are Deafened. First entry on a turn or starting there triggers 8d10 Force damage, Constitution save for half. Reaching 0 HP from this damage disintegrates the creature and its nonmagical gear. Allies share these hazards; obscuring a target can also disable your party's sight-dependent follow-up.
