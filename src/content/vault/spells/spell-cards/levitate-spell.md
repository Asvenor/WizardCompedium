## Rules that decide the play

An unwilling creature gets one initial CON save; the spell lists no repeating save. Vertical movement is up to 20 feet, with a Magic action needed to adjust another target. Horizontal movement requires a reachable fixed surface to push or pull against. The 500-pound cap is for an object in the revised text. The target descends gently when the spell ends.
