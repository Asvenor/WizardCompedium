## Decision

Transfer repeatable area damage to a willing ally with a spare action. A familiar can use a non-attack Magic action, but its survivability and positioning still matter.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Touch a willing creature and choose Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, or Poison. It can use a Magic action to breathe a 15-foot Cone: 3d6 damage, DEX save for half, affecting every creature in that area. The recipient spends the action for each breath; the original caster maintains Concentration. Breathing is not an attack roll and does not make the familiar immune to retaliation.

Maintain Concentration and compare the effect with the spell it would replace. See [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).

## Upcasting

Breath damage gains 1d6 per slot above 2.
