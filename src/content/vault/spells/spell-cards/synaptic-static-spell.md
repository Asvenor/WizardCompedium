## Rules that decide the play

A 20-foot-radius Sphere deals 8d6 Psychic, INT for half. A failed save also applies a one-minute 1d6 penalty to attacks, ability checks, and CON saves to maintain Concentration—not to every saving throw. An INT save at each turn's end can end the debuff. It needs no Concentration despite that lasting rider.
