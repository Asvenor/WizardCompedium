## Decision

Willing-group planar travel; requires a 250+ gp attuned fork.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Transports the caster plus up to eight willing linked-hand companions to another plane. A general destination leaves exact arrival to the DM; a known teleportation-circle sequence offers a specific arrival route. The revised spell has no offensive unwilling-target banishment mode. The reusable fork must be attuned to the destination plane, not merely cost 250 gp.
