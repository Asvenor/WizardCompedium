## Decision

A specialized distraction tool for a planned infiltration. Budget the caster's exposure and Concentration before treating a reduced lookout score as a complete stealth solution.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating). When a feature always prepares it, it does not spend an ordinary Wizard preparation.

## Rules that decide the play

Choose visible creatures within range; each makes a WIS save. Creatures already fighting you or your companions succeed automatically. Failure applies **−10 to WIS (Perception) checks and Passive Perception** for the duration.

This is the **2024 Concentration version**, not the legacy disadvantage effect. It does not impose Charmed, order cooperation, or directly improve an ally's Stealth roll. A creature's immunity to Charmed is not an automatic-success rule in this entry.

## Upcasting

No listed benefit. The initial casting already allows multiple selected creatures.
