## Decision

A reliable access tool when mundane picking fails or speed matters. Its noise can defeat an otherwise stealthy plan.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Choose a visible access-blocking object. It opens one mundane lock, releases a stuck closure, or unbars it; multiple locks require additional solutions. Against Arcane Lock it suppresses that spell for 10 minutes. The target emits a knock audible up to 300 feet; casting from within Silence would itself prevent this Verbal-component spell unless a feature removes that component.
