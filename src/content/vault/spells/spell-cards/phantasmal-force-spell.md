## Rules that decide the play

One creature, INT save, and a 10-foot Cube illusion perceived only by it. Study (INT Investigation) can end it. The spell can deal 2d8 Psychic per turn under its conditions; it does not explicitly impose Blinded, Restrained, or any other condition you describe.
