## Decision

Cheap single-target removal that does not require a Humanoid or use Charmed. Best against a dangerous enemy the party can leave undamaged while defeating something else.

**Preparation priority:** Consider for primary concentration (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

A visible creature makes a WIS save. Failure imposes Prone and Incapacitated; the target cannot end Prone on itself while affected. Laughter happens if it is capable of laughing, but laughter capability is not a targeting requirement. The 2024 text has no Intelligence minimum.

It repeats the save at each turn's end and whenever damaged, with Advantage on damage-triggered saves. Do not treat this as damage-proof paralysis or automatic Critical Hits. Incapacitated and Prone immunities must be checked separately.

## Upcasting

Add one target per slot level above 1.

**Wording-sensitive:** the repeat-save sentence says a success ends the spell, without specifying only that target. With multiple targets, agree whether a success releases everyone or only the successful target; do not assume independent persistence as guaranteed RAW.
