## Decision

Repositions another creature; excellent rescue and forced-positioning utility.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

A different visible creature within range makes a Constitution save, which it can choose to fail; you cannot target yourself. Its destination must also be visible, unoccupied, and within the spell's range, on a supporting surface or liquid without squeezing. It can rescue an ally or reposition an enemy, but cannot place a creature unsupported in midair. Compare it with Dimension Door when the destination is unseen or the caster also needs extraction.

## Upcasting

Add 30 feet to the spell range per slot level above 2, affecting both target selection and destination.
