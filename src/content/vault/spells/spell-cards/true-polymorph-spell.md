## Rules that decide the play

Maintaining a full hour removes the Concentration requirement but leaves the effect dispellable. Losing form Temporary HP does not end it. Creature-to-creature transformation forbids speaking and spellcasting; object-created creatures are capped at CR 9 and cease being controlled after the hour.
