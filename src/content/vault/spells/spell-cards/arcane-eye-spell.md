## Rules that decide the play

An Invisible, invulnerable visual sensor with 30-foot Darkvision; move it up to 30 feet using a Bonus Action. It can pass through a one-inch opening but not a solid barrier. It supplies sight, not hearing or a remote origin for the caster's spells.
