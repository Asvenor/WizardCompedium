## Decision

Campaign-scale tactile terrain illusion; Truesight caveat and table judgment.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Changes terrain and structures across up to one mile square, including tactile terrain effects, but does not add, disguise, or conceal creatures. Removed illusory material vanishes outside the area. Truesight reveals the real terrain without removing the illusion's other elements. Arbitrary damage or instant-kill terrain is not a listed damage mechanic; agree on such uses with the DM.
