## Rules that decide the play

Affects creatures in the Cube that can see the pattern. Failed WIS save: Charmed, Incapacitated, Speed 0, with no automatic repeating save. Any damage or another creature's action to shake the target ends its effect. Avoid waking disabled targets with incidental area damage while active enemies remain.
