## Decision

Spoken, signed, and written-language translation; ten-minute ritual tax.

**Preparation priority:** Spellbook utility (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Provides literal understanding, not the ability to speak a new language. Written text requires touching its surface and takes about a minute per page; codes and secret messages are not decoded. Ritual access is valuable when there is time to read from the spellbook.
