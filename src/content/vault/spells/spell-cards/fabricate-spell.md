## Decision

Converts raw material into finished goods; tool proficiency matters for skilled products.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The completed effect does not require Concentration. **Casting for 1 minute or longer—including casting as a Ritual—still requires Concentration throughout the casting.** Starting that casting ends an existing Concentration effect. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Creates an object from sufficient visible raw material of the same substance. General size limit: Large; metal, stone, and other minerals: Medium. It cannot create creatures or magic items, and skilled products require the appropriate Artisan's Tools proficiency. The ten-minute casting still needs Concentration even though the completed result does not. See [Fabricate Optimization](/library/advanced/fabricate-optimization/).
