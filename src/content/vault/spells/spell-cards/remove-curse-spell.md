## Decision

Important mission utility against curses and cursed equipment.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Normally ends curses on the touched target. A cursed magic item's own curse remains: the spell instead breaks its owner's attunement so it can be discarded. Specific curses can require stronger solutions; this is not a way to manufacture a curse-free version of every cursed item.
