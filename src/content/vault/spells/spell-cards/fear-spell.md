## Rules that decide the play

Failed WIS saves in the 30-foot Cone cause dropped held items and Frightened. Affected creatures Dash away by the safest route unless nowhere is available. The repeat save occurs at turn end only when line of sight to the caster is broken. Corners can help enemies recover; allies in the Cone are not automatically exempt.
