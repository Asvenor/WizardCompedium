## Decision

A low-damage cantrip that trades damage for a chance to knock a visible target Prone.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

**Source access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. Being listed here does not automatically add it to every Wizard's available spells.

## Rules that decide the play

One visible creature within 30 feet makes a Constitution save. Failure takes 1d4 Necrotic and falls Prone; success avoids both. There is no damage-on-success fallback. Coordinate with nearby melee allies; knocking a target Prone can make distant ranged attacks worse. Constitution is often a strong monster save, and the short range is a real positioning cost.

## Upcasting

Cantrip damage scales with total character level: 2d4 at level 5, 3d4 at level 11,and 4d4 at level 17. It does not gain more targets.
