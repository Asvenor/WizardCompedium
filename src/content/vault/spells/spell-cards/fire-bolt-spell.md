## Decision

Ranged damage; ignites unattended flammables.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Make a ranged spell attack against a creature or object; a hit deals 1d10 Fire before cantrip scaling. Only an unworn, uncarried flammable object ignites. Unlike Ray of Frost, it can target an object, but it does not slow a creature.

## Upcasting

Cantrip scaling uses character level, not a slot: 2d10 at level 5, 3d10 at 11, and 4d10 at 17.
