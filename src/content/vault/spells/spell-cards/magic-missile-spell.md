## Decision

Reliable Force damage; Shield interaction.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

This card lists no concentration requirement for the effect. Confirm targets, sight, clear path, components, and available actions in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

Three darts hit visible creatures without attack rolls, each dealing 1d4 + 1 Force; they arrive simultaneously and can be divided among targets. Shield prevents their damage. They do not qualify as attack-roll hits for Conjure Minor Elementals. Multiple Concentration-check adjudication should be settled with the DM, not assumed from the number of darts.

## Upcasting

One additional dart per slot level above 1.
