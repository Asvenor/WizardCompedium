## Rules that decide the play

An illusory double appears at a location you have seen within 500 miles. Your Magic action can move it up to 60 feet and make it speak or gesture; you can perceive through it. Physical interaction or Study (INT Investigation) can reveal it. It cannot be treated as your body for casting remote spells.
