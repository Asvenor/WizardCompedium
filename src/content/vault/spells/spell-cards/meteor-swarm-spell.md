## Rules that decide the play

Four visible points each create a 40-foot-radius Sphere. A creature takes 20d6 Fire plus 20d6 Bludgeoning, DEX for half, but overlapping spheres affect it only once. Unworn, uncarried nonmagical objects also take damage. Allies, collateral destruction, range of sight, and damage resistances matter more than the impressive total alone.
