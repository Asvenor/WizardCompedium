## Decision

A strong weapon-based cantrip when a suitable weapon and proficiency are already available. Compare its weapon die, range, and hand requirements with a ranged spell cantrip.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

Make one attack with the component weapon, using the spellcasting ability for both attack and damage instead of STR/DEX. The hit may use Radiant or the weapon's usual damage type. It remains a weapon attack, not a spell attack, and casting it is not the Attack action unless another feature expressly changes the interaction. Normal weapon requirements still apply.

## Scaling

At character levels 5/11/17, add 1d6/2d6/3d6 Radiant damage, regardless of the chosen base damage type.
