## Decision

**Dunamancy access:** Chronurgy and Graviturgy Wizards have access through the Wildemount rules; other spellcasters require DM consent. This is not an automatic addition to every Wizard's available spells. [Dunamancy Spells](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/)

Eight-hour Initiative support; requires permitted Dunamancy access.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

The finished effect needs no Concentration, but the 1-minute casting requires it. Confirm targets, components, and setup time in [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

## Rules that decide the play

One willing touched creature adds 1d8 to Initiative for 8 hours. The effect needs no Concentration, but its 1-minute casting does. No upcast scaling and no automatic Dunamancy access for every Wizard. Multiple copies of this spell do not add multiple d8s. Compare the value of acting first against the slots and preparation time needed to cover several allies. [Dunamancy Spells](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/)
