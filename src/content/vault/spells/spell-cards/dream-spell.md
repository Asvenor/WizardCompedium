## Decision

Long-distance same-plane communication with a sleeping known target, or a hostile rest-denial attempt. Protect the messenger's motionless body.

**Preparation priority:** Mission-dependent (optimization judgment, not an official rating).

## Rules that decide the play

The caster or a willing touched creature becomes the messenger, Incapacitated with Speed 0 while in trance. A known target on the same plane can converse in its dreams and remembers them perfectly. If awake, the messenger can wait or end the trance; ending the trance ends the spell. A terrifying message is limited to ten words and prompts a WIS save: failure denies the target its rest benefits and deals 3d6 Psychic when it wakes. This current entry supplies no target-body-part Disadvantage rider.

The longer casting time requires Concentration while casting, independently of the completed effect's duration. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).
