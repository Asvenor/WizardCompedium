## Use when

State the actual encounter problem.

## Requirements

Spells/features, legal targets, source access, actions, components, geometry, and concentration holders.

## Sequence

1. Setup and information.
2. Execute with exact timing.
3. Sustain or exit.

## Payoff and alternative

Compare expected advantage and resource cost to the best simpler legal option.

## Counters and failure

Immunity, saves, sight/path, initiative, teleportation, broken concentration, and ally interference.

## Evidence

Separate printed rule, interpretation, optimization judgment, and DM-dependent assumptions.
