Circle Magic allows multiple spellcasters to cooperate on a single spell and enhance it beyond its normal limits.

**Source:** Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn

---

## Requirements

A spell can be cast as a Circle spell if:

- It is cast using a spell slot.
- Its Casting Time is **1 Action**, **1 minute**, or longer.
- At least **1 Secondary Caster** participates.

Bonus Action and Reaction spells do not normally qualify.

Only **one Circle Casting Option** can be applied to a spell at a time unless a specific spell says otherwise.

---

## Primary Caster

The Primary Caster initiates the Circle spell.

Unless a specific option changes something, the Primary Caster:

- Expends the spell slot.
- Provides the spell's components.
- Chooses the targets.
- Makes the spell's choices.
- Determines the spell's point of origin.
- Maintains Concentration when required.
- Is the primary controller of the spell.

Initiating the Circle spell can trigger reactions that trigger when a creature casts a spell, such as Counterspell.

**The Primary Caster must maintain Concentration from initiation until the casting completes, even for a spell with an Action casting time.** This ends any earlier Concentration effect immediately; an Action Circle spell is not a way to keep old control running while waiting for secondary casters. [Heroes of Faerûn: Circle Magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/magic-of-faerun#CircleMagic).

---

## Secondary Casters

A Secondary Caster must have:

**Spellcasting or Pact Magic**

After the Primary Caster initiates the spell, each Secondary Caster must:

1. Be within **30 feet** of the Primary Caster.
2. Take the **Magic action** to contribute.
3. Contribute before the start of the Primary Caster's next turn.

Some Circle Casting Options also require the Secondary Casters to expend spell slots.

At the DM's discretion, an NPC spellcaster can expend a limited use of a spell of the required level or higher instead of a required spell slot. This is an explicit optional accommodation, not a general rule that every innate-casting creature qualifies for Circle Magic.

---

## Action-Cast Circle Spells

For a spell with a Casting Time of **1 Action**:

1. Primary Caster takes the Magic action and initiates the spell.
2. Secondary Casters contribute with their Magic actions.
3. The spell does not resolve immediately.
4. Once the final required Secondary Caster contributes, the spell resolves.

The Primary Caster chooses which participating Secondary Caster is the final contributor.

---

## Long Casting Times

If the spell has a Casting Time of **1 minute or longer**:

- Primary and Secondary Casters take the Magic action each turn.
- Every participating caster must maintain Concentration during the casting.
- This continues for the entire Casting Time.

If any participating caster loses Concentration during the casting:

**The Circle spell fails.**

---

## Spell Failure

If the Circle spell fails during casting:

- The Primary Caster's spell slot isn't expended.
- Material components aren't consumed.
- Secondary Caster spell slots required by the Circle option aren't expended.

The casting must be started again.

---

## Circle Casting Options

| Option | Secondary Casters | Extra Slot Cost | Effect |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Augment** | 1+ | None | +1,000 ft range per Secondary Caster, maximum +1 mile |
| **Distribute** | 1+ | None | All participating casters can maintain the spell's Concentration |
| **Expand** | 1+ | 1 slot each | +10 ft to one AoE dimension per Secondary Caster |
| **Prolong** | 1+ | 1 slot each | +1 / +8 / +24 hours depending on Secondary Caster count |
| **Safeguard** | 1+ | None | Creates safe 5-ft Cubes inside the spell's AoE |
| **Supplant** | 1+ | Slot ≥ spell level each | Reduces one consumed costly component by 50 gp per Secondary Caster |

---

## Augment

**Requirement:** Spell has a range of at least 5 feet.

Each Secondary Caster increases the spell's range by:

**+1,000 feet**

Maximum increase:

**+1 mile**

## Secondary Caster Breakpoints

| Secondary Casters | Range Increase |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | +1,000 ft |
| **2** | +2,000 ft |
| **3** | +3,000 ft |
| **4** | +4,000 ft |
| **5** | +5,000 ft |
| **6+** | **+1 mile maximum** |

### Cost

Secondary Casters do **not** expend spell slots for Augment.

### Example

A spell with a normal range of:

**120 ft**

with 3 Secondary Casters becomes:

**3,120 ft**

---

## Distribute

**Requirement:** Spell requires Concentration.

After the spell is successfully cast:

**Every caster who participated can maintain Concentration on the spell.**

The spell continues as long as **at least one participating caster still maintains Concentration**.

## Secondary Caster Breakpoints

| Secondary Casters | Creatures Maintaining Concentration |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | Primary + 1 = **2** |
| **2** | Primary + 2 = **3** |
| **3** | Primary + 3 = **4** |
| **4** | Primary + 4 = **5** |
| **5** | Primary + 5 = **6** |

There is no special numerical breakpoint.

Every additional Secondary Caster adds another Concentration backup.

### Cost

Secondary Casters do **not** expend spell slots for Distribute.

---

## Why Distribute Is Powerful

Normally:

**Primary loses Concentration → Spell ends**

With Distribute:

**Primary loses Concentration → Spell continues if another participant still maintains it**

This makes important Concentration spells dramatically harder to remove.

---

## Concentration Handoff

Once the spell is active, the Primary Caster can lose or end their own Concentration while another participating caster continues maintaining the spell.

This potentially allows the Primary Caster to use their Concentration on something else while the original Circle spell remains active through another participant.

### Example

**Circle Spell:** [Wall of Force](/library/spells/spells/)

Primary Wizard + Secondary Caster establish it using Distribute.

Afterward:

**Secondary Caster**
→ maintains Wall of Force

**Wizard**
→ is free to Concentrate on another spell

This is one of the strongest optimization applications of Circle Magic.

---

## Expand

**Requirement:** Spell creates an Area of Effect.

Each Secondary Caster increases **one dimension** of the spell's AoE by:

**+10 feet**

You choose the dimension being increased.

## Secondary Caster Breakpoints

| Secondary Casters | Increase |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | +10 ft |
| **2** | +20 ft |
| **3** | +30 ft |
| **4** | +40 ft |
| **5** | +50 ft |
| **6** | +60 ft |
| **7** | +70 ft |
| **10** | +100 ft |

There is no listed maximum.

### Cost

Every Secondary Caster must expend:

**1 spell slot**

Unless another rule specifies otherwise, the slot can be **any level**.

---

## Sphere Example

Normal spell:

**20-ft-radius Sphere**

### With 1 Secondary Caster

**30-ft radius**

### With 2

**40-ft radius**

### With 4

**60-ft radius**

Because radius determines the entire size of a Sphere, Expand can massively increase total affected area.

---

## Line Example

Normal:

**300 ft long × 5 ft wide**

With 2 Secondary Casters you could increase one dimension:

**320 ft long × 5 ft wide**

or

**300 ft long × 25 ft wide**

Increasing the **width** is usually dramatically more valuable.

---

## Prolong

**Requirement:** Spell has a duration of at least **1 minute**.

Secondary Casters increase the spell's duration according to specific breakpoints.

## Secondary Caster Breakpoints

| Secondary Casters | Duration Increase |
|---:|---:|
| **1–3** | **+1 hour** |
| **4–6** | **+8 hours** |
| **7+** | **+24 hours** |

### Important Optimization

The useful breakpoints are therefore:

**1 → 4 → 7**

Using:

- 2 instead of 1
- 3 instead of 1
- 5 instead of 4
- 6 instead of 4

provides **no additional duration**.

### Optimal Secondary Caster Counts

| Desired Increase | Use |
|---|---:|
| +1 hour | **1 Secondary Caster** |
| +8 hours | **4 Secondary Casters** |
| +24 hours | **7 Secondary Casters** |

### Cost

Every Secondary Caster must expend:

**1 spell slot**

Unless specifically stated otherwise, the slot can be **any level**.

---

## Examples

### 1-Minute Spell + 1 Secondary Caster

Duration:

**1 hour + 1 minute**

### 10-Minute Spell + 4 Secondary Casters

Duration:

**8 hours + 10 minutes**

### 1-Hour Spell + 7 Secondary Casters

Duration:

**25 hours**

Prolong adds duration rather than replacing the spell's normal duration.

## Safeguard

**Requirement:** Spell creates an Area of Effect.

Safeguard creates areas inside the spell that are completely unaffected by it.

The safe area consists of:

**5-ft Cubes = Spellcasting Ability Modifier + Secondary Casters**

Minimum 1 Cube.

## INT 20 Wizard Example

INT 20 gives:

**+5 Spellcasting Ability Modifier**

| Secondary Casters | Safe 5-ft Cubes |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | **6 Cubes** |
| **2** | **7 Cubes** |
| **3** | **8 Cubes** |
| **4** | **9 Cubes** |
| **5** | **10 Cubes** |
| **6** | **11 Cubes** |
| **7** | **12 Cubes** |

The Cubes can be arranged as desired, but each Cube must be contiguous with at least one other safe Cube.

If the spell's AoE moves:

**The safe zone moves with it.**

### Cost

Secondary Casters do **not** expend spell slots for Safeguard.

---

## INT Above 20

Higher Intelligence directly improves Safeguard.

### INT 22

Modifier:

**+6**

1 Secondary Caster:

**7 safe Cubes**

### INT 24

Modifier:

**+7**

1 Secondary Caster:

**8 safe Cubes**

See [Intelligence above 20](/library/advanced/intelligence-above-20/).

## Supplant

**Requirement:** Spell requires a Material component that:

1. Has a specified GP cost.
2. Is consumed by the spell.

Each Secondary Caster reduces the minimum component cost by:

**50 gp**

## Secondary Caster Breakpoints

| Secondary Casters | Component Reduction |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | -50 gp |
| **2** | -100 gp |
| **3** | -150 gp |
| **4** | -200 gp |
| **5** | -250 gp |
| **10** | -500 gp |
| **20** | -1,000 gp |
| **30** | -1,500 gp |

There is no special breakpoint.

Every additional Secondary Caster saves another **50 gp**.

---

## Secondary Caster Cost

Every Secondary Caster must expend a spell slot that is:

**equal to or higher than the Circle spell's level**

This makes Supplant substantially more expensive in caster resources than Expand or Prolong.

---

### Glyph of Warding

**200 gp consumed**

|---:|---:|
| 1 | 150 gp |

### Planar Binding

Normal component:

**1,000 gp consumed**

| Secondary Casters | New Minimum |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 950 gp |
| 5 | 750 gp |
| 10 | 500 gp |
| 15 | 250 gp |
| 20 | **0 gp** |

However, every Secondary Caster must expend a slot of at least the spell's level.

For Planar Binding:

**Level 5+ slot per Secondary Caster**

So eliminating the full 1,000 gp cost requires **20 casters each spending a level-5+ slot**.

### Simulacrum

Normal consumed component:

**1,500 gp powdered ruby**

To reduce this to zero:

**30 Secondary Casters**

Each would need to expend a slot of at least:

**Level 7**

This is theoretically possible but requires an extraordinary magical organization.

## Wizard Optimization

## Best General Options

### Distribute — S+

Potentially the strongest option.

Use it for major Concentration spells where transferring or protecting Concentration changes the encounter.

Strong candidates:

- [Web](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Hypnotic Pattern](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Sleet Storm](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/)
- [Wall of Force](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Telekinesis](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Reverse Gravity](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Maze](/library/spells/spells/)
- [Shapechange](/library/creatures/shapechange/)

---

### Prolong — S

The important breakpoints are:

**1 / 4 / 7 Secondary Casters**

Especially strong on spells normally balanced around short durations.

---

### Expand — S

Particularly powerful when increasing a small dimension dramatically increases total area.

Best examples are often:

- Radius
- Width
- Emanation size
- Narrow Lines

Do not automatically increase the largest dimension.

---

### Safeguard — A

Excellent when a huge AoE would normally hit allies.

Especially useful when:

- Allies are already surrounded.
- The AoE is persistent.
- The spell's area moves.
- Repositioning the party is impossible.

---

### Augment — A

Very strong for:

- Siege-scale magic
- Long-range control
- Avoiding Counterspell range
- Extreme-range utility

One Secondary Caster already gives **+1,000 ft**.

---

### Supplant — B / Campaign Engine

Weak in ordinary adventuring because the required Secondary Casters must spend appropriately high-level slots.

Extremely powerful for:

- Magical organizations
- Wizard guilds
- Downtime
- Large-scale infrastructure
- Very expensive consumed components

---

## Quick Reference

| Option | Best Secondary Count | Why |
|---|---:|---|
| **Augment** | 1–6 | +1,000 ft each until +1 mile |
| **Distribute** | 1+ | Every caster is another Concentration holder |
| **Expand** | As many as useful | +10 ft per caster |
| **Prolong** | **1 / 4 / 7** | +1h / +8h / +24h |
| **Safeguard** | 1+ | Ability Mod + caster count safe Cubes |
| **Supplant** | Depends on component | -50 gp per caster |

> **The three Circle Magic numbers worth memorizing are: 1 / 4 / 7.**
>
> They are the efficient Prolong breakpoints.
