**Rules baseline:** 2024 Rules Glossary.

Rest Casting is the practice of interrupting a Long Rest to cast long-duration spells, then resuming and finishing the extended rest so the effects continue into the adventuring day while the spent spell slots are recovered.

It is primarily useful for spells with durations long enough to remain active after the rest finishes.

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**2024 Long Rest Rule**

Casting a spell other than a cantrip **stops the Long Rest**. You can resume the rest immediately afterward, but it requires **1 additional hour per interruption** to finish.

If several spells are cast before the rest is resumed, confirm with the DM whether the sequence counts as one interruption or several.

</aside>

---

## How Rest Casting Works

The basic strategy is:

1. Begin a Long Rest.
2. Keep the spell slots intended for Rest Casting available.
3. Near the intended end of the rest, cast the desired long-duration spell; this interrupts the rest.
4. Resume the Long Rest.
5. Rest for the additional hour required by the interruption.
6. Complete the Long Rest and recover your expended spell slots.
7. The spell remains active for the remainder of its duration, reduced by the extra resting time.

### Example

A spell lasts **8 hours**.

You cast it shortly before the original eight-hour point, interrupting your Long Rest.

After the rest:

- Your spell slots are restored.
- The spell is still active.
- About 7 hours of its duration remain after one interruption and the required additional hour.

The result is effectively a **precast spell with its slot recovered**, at the cost of extending the rest and reducing the spell's remaining duration.

## Mage Armor

**Duration:** 8 hours  
**Concentration:** No

### Sequence

1. Keep one level-1 slot available.
2. Cast Mage Armor near the end of the Long Rest.
3. Resume the interrupted rest and complete the required additional hour.
4. Recover the level-1 slot when the extended rest finishes.
5. Begin the adventuring day with roughly 7 hours of Mage Armor remaining.

This recovers the spell slot but extends the rest and uses about 1 hour of Mage Armor's duration.

---

## Gift of Alacrity

**Duration:** 8 hours  
**Concentration:** No

One of the best Rest-Casting targets for a Chronurgy Wizard.

### Sequence

1. Keep the necessary level-1 slots.
2. Near the end of the Long Rest, cast Gift of Alacrity.
3. Target yourself or important party members.
4. Resume the interrupted rest and complete the required additional time.
5. Recover the slots when the extended rest finishes.
6. Begin the day with the initiative bonuses active for their remaining duration.

### Good Targets

Prioritize characters whose opening turn matters:

- Wizard
- Control casters
- Characters with powerful opening abilities
- Characters who need to establish positioning early

---

## Water Breathing

**Duration:** 24 hours  
**Concentration:** No  
**Ritual:** Yes

Water Breathing is particularly easy to maintain because of its long duration and Ritual casting.

### Sequence

1. Ritual-cast Water Breathing during safe downtime.
2. Affect the party.
3. Continue normal activity.
4. Refresh before the duration expires when useful.

Because it lasts 24 hours, it can provide essentially permanent coverage during aquatic adventures without consuming spell slots.

---

## Mind Blank

**Duration:** 24 hours  
**Concentration:** No

Mind Blank becomes one of the strongest high-level long-duration precasts.

### Sequence

1. Cast Mind Blank before completing the Long Rest.
2. Resume the interrupted rest and complete the required additional hour.
3. Recover the level-8 slot.
4. Retain about 23 hours of the spell's duration after one interruption.

This provides extremely strong psychic and information protection without sacrificing the next day's level-8 slot.

---

## Foresight

**Duration:** 8 hours  
**Concentration:** No

Foresight is one of the most powerful possible Rest-Casting targets.

### Sequence

1. Preserve your level-9 slot before resting.
2. Near the end of the Long Rest, cast Foresight.
3. Resume the interrupted rest and complete the required additional hour.
4. Recover the level-9 slot.
5. Begin the day with roughly 7 hours of Foresight remaining.

You now potentially have:

**Foresight active + a fresh level-9 spell slot.**

That can mean starting the adventuring day with Foresight while still retaining access to:

- Wish
- Shapechange
- True Polymorph
- Meteor Swarm
- Another level-9 spell

This is one of the strongest possible outcomes of Rest Casting when permitted.

## Concentration and Rest Casting

Concentration spells are usually less attractive Rest-Casting targets.

Sleep normally gives the Unconscious condition and ends Concentration. A Concentration effect cannot survive further sleep without an explicit exception; satisfying sleep earlier does not waive the remaining rest requirements. See [Concentration and Unconscious](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary).

Even if the spell survives the resting interaction, it occupies your Concentration immediately after the rest.

For a Wizard, this prevents opening combat with spells such as:

- Web
- Hypnotic Pattern
- Sleet Storm
- Polymorph
- Wall of Force
- Telekinesis
- Maze

Non-Concentration spells are therefore generally the best Rest-Casting targets.

## Rest-Casting Multiple Spells

Multiple long-duration spells can potentially be established before the extended rest ends, but the table must decide how multiple consecutive castings count for interruptions.

### Example

Near the original end of the Long Rest:

1. Cast Mage Armor.
2. Cast Gift of Alacrity.
3. Cast another qualifying long-duration spell.
4. Resume the Long Rest.
5. Complete the additional time required for the interruption or interruptions.
6. Recover the expended slots.

The Wizard begins the day with several active magical effects and refreshed resources.

The primary limitation becomes:

- Available spell slots before resting
- Spell durations
- Concentration
- Material components
- Casting times
- One additional hour per interruption
- The DM's ruling on multiple consecutive castings

---

## Rest Casting vs Precasting

These should be treated separately.

## Precasting

Cast a spell **after** completing the Long Rest but before combat.

**Result:** Spell is active, but the spell slot remains spent.

## Rest Casting

Cast a spell **before** completing the Long Rest.

**Result:** The casting interrupts the rest. After the rest is resumed and its additional time is completed, the spell remains active for its remaining duration and the slot is restored.
