Initiative is unusually valuable for a Wizard because **control before enemy actions prevents more damage than recovery afterward**.

## Initiative Boosters

| Option | Use |
|---|---|
| **Alert** | Adds Proficiency Bonus to Initiative. Immediately after rolling, may swap with one willing ally in the same combat; neither can be Incapacitated. |
| Gift of Alacrity | +1d8 Initiative for 8 hours; 1-minute casting, one willing touched creature. Requires Dunamancy access. |
| **Temporal Awareness** | Chronurgy adds Intelligence modifier to Initiative. |
| **Weapon of Warning** | While attuned and the weapon is within your reach, you and allies within 30 feet have Initiative Advantage and awaken from natural sleep when combat begins. |

Stack only effects that are simultaneously legal under the active entries.

**2024 surprise is Initiative Disadvantage, not a lost first turn or a separate surprise round.** Multiple sources of Advantage do not stack; Advantage and Disadvantage cancel normally. An early turn is an opportunity, not permission to complete an unopposed two-spell setup.

Sources: [Alert](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#Alert), [Gift of Alacrity](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/egtw/character-options-subclasses#GiftofAlacrity), [Weapon of Warning](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#WeaponofWarning), [Surprise](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#Surprise).

## First-Turn Priority

1. Identify the encounter's real threat.
2. Decide whether the fight is best solved by **control, separation, anti-caster pressure, rescue, or damage**.
3. Cast before enemy positioning improves.
4. Move to a location that protects Concentration and your Reaction.

## Opening Spell Guide

| Encounter | First-Turn Plan |
|---|---|
| Large grounded group | Web |
| Charm-susceptible group | Hypnotic Pattern |
| Mixed allies/enemies | Slow |
| Enemy caster formation | Sleet Storm |
| Boss + dangerous minions | Separate with Wall of Force / later Maze |
| Fragile clustered enemies | Fireball when damage removes actions immediately |
| Unknown defenses | Terrain, walls, summons, buffs, or another low-risk test |

## First Turn vs Bosses

Do not automatically open with your strongest save-or-suck spell.

Against Legendary Resistance:

- Split the encounter.
- Remove minions.
- Use no-save control.
- Buff allies.
- Force serious saves only if the party is deliberately burning Legendary Resistance.

See [Legendary Resistance & Bosses](/library/reference/legendary-resistance-bosses/).

## Position After Casting

After the opener:

- Break line of sight when possible.
- Stay outside enemy Counterspell range if practical.
- Keep a path for Misty Step or Dimension Door.
- Avoid standing beside another priority target.
- Preserve your Reaction when hostile spellcasting is plausible.

> **First-turn goal:** remove or distort enemy actions before they happen.
