These combinations use **Arcana Unleashed plus the revised core rules**. Campaign access to the book, feats, and magic items is a separate requirement. **Rules interaction** identifies the printed mechanics; **optimization** explains their tactical use; **ruling-sensitive** identifies an interpretation that must be agreed before building around it.

## Blood Amulet + Scorching Ray

**Access:** a Rare attunement item and a level-2 Wizard spell; no particular subclass required.

**Rules interaction.** Blood Amulet has three charges and regains **1d3 at dawn**, not necessarily all three. When you deal damage to a creature, you can spend a charge to add **2d10 Necrotic** and force that target to make a **DC 15 CON save**; failure gives it one Exhaustion level. No action cost or once-per-turn limit is printed.

[Scorching Ray](/library/spells/spell-cards/scorching-ray-spell/) makes three separate ranged spell attacks at its base level. If all three rays deal damage, spending one charge on each produces:

- The rays' ordinary **6d6 Fire** damage, plus **6d10 Necrotic** from the amulet, before critical hits, resistance, or other adjustments.
- **Three separate DC 15 CON saves**, directed at the creatures damaged by the respective rays.
- All three charges spent. A miss or a ray that deals no damage does not provide that damage trigger.

**Optimization.** Concentrating the rays on one eligible creature can combine burst damage with escalating Exhaustion penalties. Each Exhaustion level subtracts 2 from all D20 Tests and reduces Speed by 5 feet, so an early failure worsens later saves. Splitting targets trades that escalation for broader disruption.

**Limits.** Three failed saves on a fresh target produce **three Exhaustion levels, not instant death**; death occurs at six. Exhaustion immunity blocks the condition, strong CON saves and Legendary Resistance resist it, and the fixed DC does not scale with the Wizard. Necrotic defenses affect the extra damage; they are not automatically Exhaustion immunity. A summon dealing its own damage is not automatically damage dealt by its caster. This is a charge-limited item combination, not an infinite engine.

Sources: [Blood Amulet](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-5-magic-items#BloodAmulet), [Scorching Ray](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#ScorchingRay), [Exhaustion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#ExhaustionCondition). See [Items for Wizard](/library/items/items-for-wizard/).

## Transfix + allied attacks and Opportunity Attacks

**Access:** Wizard 13 for the level-7 spell; Concentration, up to one minute.

**Rules interaction.** [Transfix](/library/spells/spell-cards/transfix-spell/) makes one visible creature within 60 feet attempt a CHA save. Failure imposes Charmed and, while Charmed, Incapacitated. On its turns the target approaches the caster by the most direct route, trying to get within 5 feet. Ending its turn there deals **4d8 Psychic**. Later Magic actions can target additional creatures.

The spell has **no routine end-of-turn repeat save and no clause ending it merely because the target takes damage**. Allies can therefore attack the affected creature without the automatic wake-up found in Hypnotic Pattern. The spell expressly permits Opportunity Attacks during the approach.

**Optimization.** Position the caster so the direct approach leaves an ally's reach; that ally can spend its Reaction for the usual Opportunity Attack. Other allies can attack the Incapacitated target on their turns. Incapacitated does **not itself** grant attack Advantage or automatic Critical Hits.

**Limits and rulings.** An Opportunity Attack still needs its normal trigger, sight, and an available Reaction; entering reach alone does not trigger it. Each additional target costs another Magic action. Charmed immunity, successful saves, Legendary Resistance, lost Concentration, and the caster's exposed position remain answers. A creature that succeeds on a save against this casting cannot be targeted again by it.

Before moving into damaging terrain, the target repeats its CHA save. Do not advertise a guaranteed lava walk or hazard loop. Agree what counts as damaging terrain and the direct route with the DM. A second Concentration hazard such as Spike Growth needs another caster or a specific exception; one Wizard cannot ordinarily maintain both spells.

Source: [Transfix](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-2-spells#Transfix). See [Timing reminders](/library/reference/action-economy/#timing-reminders), [Condition Quick Reference](/library/reference/condition-quick-reference/), and [Concentration Strategy](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/).

## Lightning Ring + movement

**Access:** Wizard 15 for the level-8 spell; Bonus Action casting, Concentration up to ten minutes.

**Rules interaction.** [Lightning Ring](/library/spells/spell-cards/lightning-ring-spell/) creates a **10-foot Emanation**. When it enters a creature's space, a creature enters it, or a creature ends its turn there, the caster can force a CON save. Failure deals **3d6 Lightning + 3d6 Thunder** and Deafens for one minute; success takes half damage only. The caster can decline this Emanation save for an ally.

While the spell is active, a **Magic action** fires a 60-foot-long, 5-foot-wide Line for **6d6 Lightning**, DEX save for half. That Line affects every creature in it, with no printed ally exemption. Casting the spell as a Bonus Action and using this Magic action on the same turn is possible: operating the already-active spell does not cast another slotted spell.

**Ruling-sensitive optimization.** The Emanation text has **no explicit once-per-turn damage cap**. Actual exit-and-reentry or moving the Emanation across a creature can therefore be much more valuable than leaving it stationary. Establish the table's treatment of repeated crossings, grid boundaries, carried movement, and forced movement before relying on a repeated-entry plan.

There must be a real qualifying entry: moving around while a creature remains continuously inside is not repeated entry. Movement, actions, positioning, Concentration, defenses, and the risk of standing close to enemies still limit the plan. The absence of a printed cap is **not a guarantee of unlimited movement or an infinite damage loop**.

Source: [Lightning Ring](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-2-spells#LightningRing). See [Movement & Travel](/library/reference/movement-travel/), [Spell Area](/library/reference/spell-area/), and [One spell slot per turn](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/#one-spell-slot-per-turn).

## Boon of Magic School Mastery: Shield + Heal

**Access:** level 19+ and Spellcasting/Pact Magic, with an available legal Epic Boon choice. Choose **Abjuration**.

- **Rote Casting — Shield:** choose the level-1 Abjuration on the Wizard list. It becomes always prepared and can be cast without a slot or components.
- **Signature Arcanum — Heal:** choose this level-6 Abjuration. This choice is **not restricted to the Wizard spell list**. It becomes always prepared, can be cast once without a slot per Long Rest, and can also use appropriate spell slots.

**Optimization.** [Shield](/library/spells/spell-cards/shield-spell/) becomes a repeatable slot-free defense; Heal adds an off-list **70 HP** restoration and ends Blinded, Deafened, and Poisoned on a visible creature within 60 feet. Heal remains an Action with its normal V/S components; a higher slot adds 10 healing per level above 6.

**Limits.** Shield still requires its normal Reaction trigger—being hit by an attack roll or targeted by Magic Missile—and lasts until the start of the caster's next turn. It does not provide permanent +5 AC or extra Reactions. Spending that Reaction still competes with Counterspell and other defenses. The boon removes components from the level-1 Rote Casting, not from the higher-level Signature Arcanum.

A **free Shield does not create or recharge the revised Arcane Ward**, because that feature requires casting with a spell slot. It likewise does not trigger Abjuration Adept's slot-dependent Temporary HP. Choosing to spend a slot instead can meet those triggers. The boon can be taken again only for a **different school**.

Sources: [Boon of Magic School Mastery](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#BoonOfMagicSchoolMastery), [Shield](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Shield), [Heal](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Heal), [Arcane Ward](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Level3ArcaneWard). See [Arcana Unleashed Epic Boons](/library/tiers/feats-epic-boon-tier-list/#arcana-unleashed-epic-boons).

## School Adepts: component-free control

**Access:** Enchantment Adept or Illusion Adept requires level 4+ and Spellcasting/Pact Magic; each can increase INT by 1, up to 20.

**Rules interaction.** When casting a spell of the matching school **with a spell slot**, the feat can remove Verbal, Somatic, and ordinary Material components. Costly or consumed Material components remain required. If all components are removed, the revised [Counterspell](/library/spells/spell-cards/counterspell-spell/) trigger—seeing a creature cast a spell with V, S, or M components—is absent.

**Optimization.** Component-free Hypnotic Pattern, Hold Person, or another eligible control spell is harder to interrupt with that specific reaction. These feats are available to any of the supported Wizard subclasses when their prerequisites are met; a matching subclass is not required.

**Limits.** This does not make the spell's effects invisible, erase witnesses' memories, waive targeting or Concentration, overcome immunity, or defeat every anti-magic effect. It does not automatically apply to a slot-free casting. [Suggestion](/library/spells/spell-cards/suggestion-spell/) still requires a target that can hear and understand the actual suggestion; removing its Verbal component does not remove that separate requirement.

Illusion Adept also imposes Disadvantage on **INT (Investigation) checks** to discern the true nature of its caster's spell illusions. That is not Disadvantage on initial saving throws: Phantasmal Force's initial INT save is unaffected, while its subsequent Study/Investigation check is affected. Different illusions have different interaction and discovery rules.

Sources: [Enchantment Adept](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#EnchantmentAdept), [Illusion Adept](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#IllusionAdept), [Counterspell](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Counterspell), [Suggestion](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Suggestion).

## Extinguish Undead — missing save DC

The revised Necromancer's level-14 feature calls for a **DEX saving throw but does not state its DC** in the published feature text. Using the Wizard's spell save DC is a reasonable **table clarification**, not an explicit DC printed in that feature. Agree the DC before relying on its explosions; no automatic-failure or guaranteed chain is assumed here. The [compact Necromancer build](/library/character/arcana-unleashed-builds/#necromancer--compact-companions-or-an-army) does not need this level-14 interaction to function.

Source: [revised Necromancer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#Necromancer). A DM clarification can resolve play at one table; it does not become official errata.

## Combinations that do not automatically work

| Proposed shortcut | Actual restriction |
|---|---|
| Conjurer's Durable Summons or Splintered Summons on Summon Undead | Both require a **slot-cast Conjuration**, and Splintered Summons additionally requires an eligible spirit statblock. Summon Undead is **Necromancy**; its name does not change its school. |
| Free casting triggers every school benefit | Read the trigger. “Using a spell slot” excludes slot-free casting even if the spell has a level and appears on the right class list. |
| Magic Missile automatically multiplies any added bonus by every dart | The darts strike simultaneously, but an added bonus still follows its own wording. Check whether the granting feature applies to one damage roll, one damage event, or every applicable hit; agree any wording-sensitive roll procedure with the DM rather than assuming a multiplier. |
| Two strong Concentration spells form one personal combo | Normally starting the second ends the first. A second caster or an explicit exception is needed; describing a sequence does not create one. |

Sources: [Durable Summons](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#Level6DurableSummons), [Summon Undead](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#SummonUndead), [Magic Missile](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#MagicMissile).
