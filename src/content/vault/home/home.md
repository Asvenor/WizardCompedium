Use this vault as a reference: compare tactics, spell roles, build tradeoffs, and legal forms without entering or tracking a character.

**Build coverage:** Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer. Each has pure Wizard, Artificer 1, Cleric 1, Fighter 1, and Fighter 2 spell progressions: **35 core routes**, plus one optional pure Illusionist/Illusion Adept feat configuration. Subclasses appear alphabetically, with no flagship or favored class plan. Use [Spell Progression Hub](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/) for the full comparison.
