## Which Transformation Spell?

| Goal | Use |
|---|---|
| **Give an ally a strong Beast body** | [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/) |
| **Temporary HP buffer / combat transformation** | [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/) — not healing; form ends when its granted buffer runs out |
| **Become a powerful creature while retaining core Wizard capabilities** | [Shapechange](/library/creatures/shapechange/) |
| **Switch between forms during one spell** | [Shapechange](/library/creatures/shapechange/) |
| **Transform another creature into a powerful creature** | [True Polymorph](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/) |
| **Create a CR ≤9 creature from an object** | [True Polymorph](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/) |
| **Create a long-term transformed asset** | [True Polymorph](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/) |
| **Turn a creature into an object** | [Creature → Object](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/#creature--object) |

## Fast Decision Tree

**Do I want to transform myself?**

→ [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/) for a temporary Beast body from spell level 4; [Shapechange](/library/creatures/shapechange/) at spell level 9 when retaining core Wizard capabilities matters.

**Do I want to temporarily transform an ally into a Beast?**

→ [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/)

**Do I want to transform another creature into something much more powerful?**

→ [True Polymorph](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/)

**Do I want to manufacture a creature from an object?**

→ [Created Utility Assets](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/#created-utility-assets)

**Do I need a long-term creature or servant?**

→ [True Polymorph](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/) can make an ongoing creature, but its initial control does not continue after the full hour. [Planar Binding](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/) supplies time-limited service by an eligible existing creature, not permanent loyalty.

**Do I need scouting rather than combat?**

→ [Find Familiar](/library/creatures/find-familiar/)

**Do I need a specific extraplanar servant?**

→ [Planar Binding Candidate](/library/creatures/planar-binding-candidate/)

**Do I need transportation?**

→ [Planar Binding Mount](/library/creatures/planar-binding-mount/) or the relevant transformation movement section.
