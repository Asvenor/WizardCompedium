## Encounter decisions

| Situation | First decision | Exact reference |
|---|---|---|
| Combat is starting | Identify the turn that removes the most enemy actions. | [First-Turn Priority](/library/tactics/initiative-first-turn-strategy/#first-turn-priority) · [Combat Dashboard](/library/tactics/combat-dashboard/) |
| Pick concentration | Choose by enemy count, immunity, map, party, and escape options. | [Choosing the Concentration Spell](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/#choosing-the-concentration-spell) |
| My control is already working | Protect the effect and use compatible actions. | [After Control Lands](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/#after-control-lands) |
| A boss has Legendary Resistance | Compare bypass, deliberate depletion, and isolating its allies. | [Strategy 1 — Bypass It](/library/reference/legendary-resistance-bosses/#strategy-1--bypass-it) |
| I need the weakest save | Use observed traits and information; a creature's appearance is not proof. | [Save Targeting](/library/reference/legendary-resistance-bosses/#save-targeting) · [Live filters](/library/home/spell-finder/#live-filters) |
| An enemy is casting | Check sight, range, components, trigger, and reaction value. | [3. Counterspell](/library/tactics/countering-spellcasters/#3-counterspell) · [Reaction Management](/library/reference/reaction-management/) |
| A caster is concentrating | Compare damage, disruption, sight denial, and removal. | [4. Break Concentration](/library/tactics/countering-spellcasters/#4-break-concentration) |
| A creature is hidden/invisible | Separate finding its location from being able to see/target it. | [Vision & Senses](/library/reference/vision-senses/) · [See Invisibility - Spell](/library/spells/spell-cards/see-invisibility-spell/) |
| Several enemies are clustered | Choose control or damage while avoiding ally interference. | [Battlefield Control](/library/tactics/spell-tactics/#battlefield-control) · [Control](/library/home/spell-finder/#control) |
| We are outnumbered | Divide the encounter and reduce the number of active enemies. | [Wall of Force Tactics](/library/tactics/spell-tactics/#wall-of-force-tactics) |
| I need to rescue an ally | Check willingness, visibility, carrying limits, and destination. | [Rescue](/library/home/spell-finder/#rescue) |
| I need to escape | Pick a reachable safe destination and preserve the reaction needed to survive. | [Mobility](/library/home/spell-finder/#mobility) · [Teleportation & Planar Travel](/library/advanced/teleportation-planar-travel/) |
| I am being surrounded | Compare repositioning with a defensive action; keep an exit. | [Combat Dashboard](/library/tactics/combat-dashboard/) · [Movement & Travel](/library/reference/movement-travel/) |
| I am losing concentration | Check the trigger/DC and reduce further exposure. | [Concentration](/library/reference/concentration/) · [Protecting Concentration](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/#protecting-concentration) |
| I have very few slots | Preserve one escape/defense option and use book rituals when time allows. | [Prepared Loadouts](/library/tactics/prepared-loadouts/) · [Resting](/library/reference/resting/) |
| I want to layer two concentration effects | Establish two actual concentration holders or an explicit exception. | [Special Cases](/library/tactics/concentration-strategy/#special-cases) · [Rules Desk](/library/reference/rules-desk/) |
| We can push or grapple enemies | Read each hazard's timing and once-per-turn language. | [Forced Movement](/library/tactics/spell-tactics/#forced-movement) · [Rules Desk](/library/reference/rules-desk/) |
