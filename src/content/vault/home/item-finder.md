## Fast decisions

| Need | Start here | Cost to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Higher Wizard spell DC | [Grimoire +1](/library/items/item-cards/arcane-grimoire-1-item/) / [+2](/library/items/item-cards/arcane-grimoire-2-item/) / [+3](/library/items/item-cards/arcane-grimoire-3-item/), [Robe of the Archmagi](/library/items/item-cards/robe-of-the-archmagi-item/) | Attunement, held book, actual edition, and access. Staff weapon bonuses are not spell DC. |
| Protect Concentration | [Dedicated Focus](/library/items/item-cards/ring-of-dedicated-focus-item/), [Mind Sharpener](/library/items/item-cards/mind-sharpener-item/), [Anchoring Bangle](/library/items/item-cards/scholar-s-anchoring-bangle-item/) | Hit Dice versus Reaction, charges versus daily use; none stops every reason Concentration ends. |
| Slot economy | [Pearl of Power](/library/items/item-cards/pearl-of-power-item/), [Spell Storing](/library/items/item-cards/ring-of-spell-storing-item/), [2023 Staff of Defense](/library/items/item-cards/staff-of-defense-2023-item/) | Recovery, preloaded spells, and charged casting are different resources. |
| Broader spell access | [Mizzium Apparatus](/library/items/item-cards/mizzium-apparatus-item/) | Class list, components, available slots, Arcana check, and paid failures—not every spell automatically. |
| More cantrip output | [Illusionist's Bracers](/library/items/item-cards/illusionist-s-bracers-item/) | Same cantrip, same turn, one Bonus Action; loses that turn's Bonus Action escape/support. |
| Flight without Concentration | [Broom](/library/items/item-cards/broom-of-flying-item/), [Winged Boots](/library/items/item-cards/winged-boots-item/), [Carpet](/library/items/item-cards/carpet-of-flying-item/) | Revised Broom/Boots use attunement; Carpet does not. Compare activation, duration, capacity, and Speed. |
| Power without an attunement slot | [Tome](/library/items/item-cards/tome-of-clear-thought-item/), [Bands](/library/items/item-cards/boon-companions-bands-item/), [Component Ring](/library/items/item-cards/spell-component-ring-item/) | Study and rare access, one shared daily protection, or a narrow material benefit. |
| A campaign-specific prize | [Blackstaff](/library/items/item-cards/blackstaff-item/), [Universal Pantograph](/library/items/item-cards/universal-pantograph-item/) | Succession/sentience or artifact access—not ordinary planned shopping. |
