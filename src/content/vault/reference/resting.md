## Short Rest

A Short Rest takes **1 uninterrupted hour** of light activity; you must have at least 1 HP to start. Reading, talking, eating, and standing watch are allowed.

- Spend Hit Point Dice one at a time, regaining the die roll + CON modifier (**minimum 1 HP per die**), and regain features that explicitly recharge on a Short Rest.
- Rolling Initiative, taking any damage, or casting a spell other than a cantrip **stops the rest and grants no benefits**. A Ritual is still a spell.
- Wizard **Arcane Recovery** uses its own once-per-Long-Rest restriction; taking more Short Rests does not reset it.

## Long Rest

A Long Rest takes **at least 8 hours**, normally including at least 6 hours of sleep and no more than 2 hours of light activity. Species and features can provide specific exceptions. You need at least 1 HP to start and must wait **at least 16 hours after finishing** one before starting another.

It restores all lost HP and **all spent Hit Point Dice**, returns reduced maximum HP and ability scores to normal, removes one Exhaustion level, and refreshes features as specified. A specific curse or other effect can override these general benefits.

Rolling Initiative, taking any damage, casting a non-cantrip spell, or spending 1 hour walking/otherwise exerting yourself interrupts it. At least 1 hour of rest before the interruption grants Short Rest benefits. Resuming the Long Rest adds **1 hour per interruption**.

Important Wizard interactions include:

- Spell slot recovery
- Preparing spells
- Arcane Recovery timing
- Long-duration spells
- Rest Casting
- Familiar and minion preparation
- Bastion and downtime planning

## Rest Casting

Casting a leveled spell during a 2024 Long Rest stops the rest. It can be resumed immediately, but requires **1 additional hour per interruption** to finish.

Do not assume older "cast five minutes before the rest ends and immediately regain the slot" routines automatically work under the current rules.

See [Rest Casting](/library/tactics/rest-casting/) for the dedicated section.
