**Rules baseline:** 2024 Rules Glossary.

A creature can normally concentrate on only **one spell or effect at a time**.

## Concentration Ends When

- You start casting another spell requiring Concentration, or activate another effect requiring it. The old Concentration ends immediately, not only when the new casting finishes.
- You become **Incapacitated**.
- You die.
- An effect specifically ends your Concentration.
- You fail a Concentration saving throw after taking damage.

## Damage Check

After taking damage while concentrating:

**Constitution saving throw**

**DC = 10 or half the damage taken (round down), whichever is higher, to a maximum DC of 30**

Each separate source of damage can cause a separate check.

Casting a spell for 1 minute or longer requires Concentration during the casting, even if its eventual effect does not. Readying a spell also requires Concentration while holding it. Sleep normally causes Unconscious, which includes Incapacitated and therefore ends Concentration.

Source: [2024 PHB Rules Glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#Concentration).

## Specific feature exceptions

The rules above are the baseline, not a way to override a feature that explicitly protects Concentration.

- **Boon of the Iron Mind (Arcana Unleashed, level 19+):** while maintaining Concentration, only another Concentration spell/effect, Petrified, Unconscious, or death breaks it. Damage and other causes of Incapacitated do not break it through the normal rule. It still does **not** permit two Concentration effects, extend a spell's duration, or prevent the effect itself from being dispelled.
- **Revised Conjurer, Focused Conjuration (Wizard 10):** taking damage cannot break Concentration on **Conjuration spells**. This narrower protection does not remove the normal condition or replacement restrictions.

Sources: [Boon of the Iron Mind](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#BoonOfTheIronMind) and [Focused Conjuration](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#Level10FocusedConjuration). See [Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List](/library/tiers/feats-epic-boon-tier-list/) and [Subclasses](/library/character/subclasses/).

## Wizard Priorities

1. Cast the Concentration spell that best solves the encounter.
2. Protect it with positioning, cover, movement, AC, and save bonuses.
3. Use non-Concentration spells on later turns when the first spell is already winning.
4. Do not replace a successful control spell just to deal marginal damage.

## Common Concentration Protection

- Constitution saving throw proficiency
- War Caster
- Bladesong
- High Constitution
- Cover
- Range
- Defensive spells
- Avoiding damage entirely
