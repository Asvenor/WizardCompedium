| Size | Typical Space |
|---|---|
| **Tiny** | 2½ × 2½ ft |
| **Small** | 5 × 5 ft |
| **Medium** | 5 × 5 ft |
| **Large** | 10 × 10 ft |
| **Huge** | 15 × 15 ft |
| **Gargantuan** | 20 × 20 ft or larger |

This is **combat space**, not a solid cube of flesh or a creature's exact height. The 2024 PHB table uses a 20-foot space for Gargantuan; a particular creature or encounter can require larger dimensions. Shape-specific spell limits use their actual wording, not an assumption that every Large creature fits inside a 10-foot-high room.

During movement you can pass through an ally, an Incapacitated creature, a Tiny creature, or a creature at least two sizes larger/smaller than you. A creature's space is Difficult Terrain unless it is Tiny or your ally. You cannot willingly end a move in another creature's space; involuntarily ending a turn there makes you Prone unless you are Tiny or larger than the other creature.

For squeezing through narrow openings, ask the DM to apply the table's chosen rule; do not silently import the 2014 squeezing penalties as a quoted 2024 rule.

Source: [2024 PHB — Movement and Position](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/playing-the-game#MovementandPosition).

## Wizard Relevance

Creature size matters for:

- Polymorph and Shapechange forms
- Grappling
- Squeezing
- Summons
- Blocking passages
- Area placement
- Mounts
- Teleportation and occupied destination spaces
- Features limited to creatures of a certain size

Large bodies can control space even when their raw damage is not exceptional.
