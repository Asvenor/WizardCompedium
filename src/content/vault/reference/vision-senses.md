| Sense | Use |
|---|---|
| **Darkvision** | Within its range, Dim Light becomes Bright Light and Darkness becomes Dim Light, in shades of gray. Darkness therefore still imposes the normal Dim Light penalty on sight-based Perception. It does not automatically pierce magical Darkness. |
| **Blindsight** | Counts as sight within its range without relying on physical vision. Works while Blinded, in Darkness, and against Invisible creatures, but not through Total Cover. |
| **Truesight** | Within range, sees normal/magical Darkness and Invisible creatures/objects; visual illusions appear transparent and saves against them automatically succeed; reveals magically transformed true forms and the Ethereal Plane. It does not grant sight through solid walls or opaque fog. |
| **Tremorsense** | Pinpoints creatures and moving objects sharing the same surface or liquid within range. Does not detect airborne targets and **does not count as sight**. |

## Wizard Notes

- Many spells require a target **you can see**.
- Blindsight and Truesight can defeat strategies that rely on invisibility or visual illusions.
- Heavy obscurement can block ordinary vision without necessarily defeating every special sense.
- Tremorsense does not automatically mean normal sight.
- Always check the exact range of a creature's special senses.

**Targeting distinction:** knowing a location is not the same as seeing its occupant. Tremorsense can identify a space without enabling a spell that requires a creature you can see. Conversely, Blindsight can satisfy that sight requirement within range, unless another restriction blocks the effect.

**Invisible is not automatically hidden.** Noise, tracks, or an observed movement can leave a location known. See [Condition Quick Reference](/library/reference/condition-quick-reference/) and [Hide, Search, and Study](/library/reference/action-economy/#hide-search-and-study) for the separate concealment mechanics.
