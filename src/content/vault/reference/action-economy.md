## Main Resources

| Resource | Typical Uses |
|---|---|
| **Action** | Cast most spells, Attack, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Utilize |
| **Bonus Action** | Certain spells and features |
| **Reaction** | Shield, Counterspell, opportunity attacks, subclass features |
| **Movement** | Reposition without spending an Action |
| **Free Interaction** | Minor object interaction when allowed by the rules |

## Wizard Principle

The strongest Wizard turns often **remove more enemy actions than they spend**.

Examples:

- Web restrains several enemies.
- Hypnotic Pattern can remove multiple turns.
- Wall of Force divides one encounter into two.
- Maze removes one dangerous creature without an initial save.

## Ready

Take the Ready action on your turn, name a **perceivable trigger**, and choose an action or movement up to your Speed. If the trigger occurs before your next turn, you can spend your **Reaction immediately after the trigger finishes** to respond, or ignore it.

For a readied spell:

- It must normally take **an action** to cast; Bonus Action and Reaction spells cannot be readied this way.
- You **cast now**, spending the slot and other casting resources now. Releasing it later is not a second casting.
- Holding the spell requires **Concentration**, even if the spell normally does not. Under the normal Concentration rules, this means giving up an existing Concentration effect.
- You must release it before the start of your next turn. Losing Concentration or failing to release it in time wastes the spell and its spent resources.

**Optimization cost:** Ready trades your Action, Reaction, and Concentration for timing. It is not a free way to keep Web active while holding Fireball for later, and it competes with Shield and Counterspell for your Reaction.

Source: [2024 PHB — Ready](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#ReadyAction). See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/) for the one-spell-slot-per-turn limit.

## Dodge

Dodge lasts until the start of your next turn: attacks against you have Disadvantage **if you can see the attacker**, and you have Advantage on DEX saves. The benefits end if you become Incapacitated or your Speed becomes 0. A Grappled Wizard cannot keep Dodge's benefits merely because they still have an Action.

Dodge is often an excellent follow-up when:

- Your Concentration spell is already winning.
- You are being focused.
- Casting another spell adds little value.

## Help

- **An attack:** distract an enemy within 5 feet of you. The next attack roll by an ally against that enemy has Advantage before the start of your next turn. It is one attack, not the ally's entire turn; this does not require skill proficiency.
- **An ability check:** choose a skill or tool **you are proficient with** and an ally close enough for meaningful assistance. Their next check with it has Advantage before the start of your next turn. The DM decides whether the help is possible.

This makes Help useful for a familiar, but approaching the enemy, surviving until the relevant attack, and using the correct initiative order still matter. See [Familiar Tier List](/library/tiers/familiar-tier-list/).

## Hide, Search, and Study

**Hide:** requires a DC 15 DEX (Stealth) check while Heavily Obscured or behind Three-Quarters/Total Cover, outside every enemy's line of sight. Success grants Invisible while hidden; the check total becomes the Perception DC to find you. Being found, making an attack roll, casting with a Verbal component, or making more than a whisper ends hiding. It is not a license to remain concealed while openly walking in front of an observer; agree edge cases with the DM.

**Search** uses WIS: Perception for concealed creatures/objects, Insight for state of mind, Medicine for ailments, Survival for tracks/food. **Study** uses INT for information: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, or Religion according to the subject. An illusion specifies whether its disproof uses Study/Investigation, physical interaction, a save, or something else; these are not interchangeable.

## Timing reminders

- A Bonus Action exists only when a rule grants one; an unused Action cannot normally become a second Bonus Action.
- After spending a Reaction, you cannot take another until the start of your next turn unless a feature explicitly permits it.
- Opportunity attacks require seeing a creature leave your reach and happen **before** it leaves. Teleportation and movement that uses none of the target's movement, Action, Bonus Action, or Reaction do not provoke. Compelled movement that uses one of those resources can provoke.
- Ranged attack rolls, including spell attacks, have Disadvantage when an enemy within 5 feet can see you and is not Incapacitated—even if you aim at someone farther away. Saving-throw spells are not attack rolls.

Sources: [2024 PHB — action glossary](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/rules-glossary#Action) and [combat attacks](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/playing-the-game#MakinganAttack).
