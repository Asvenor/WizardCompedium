Legendary Resistance exists to prevent a boss from being removed by one failed saving throw.

The correct response is **not always to burn through it**.

A creature normally **chooses** whether to spend Legendary Resistance after failing a save. A trivial effect does not force expenditure merely because it caused a failed roll. Its actual stat block determines uses and any additional cost or limitation; do not assume every boss has exactly three.

Magic Resistance is different: apply its printed Advantage to the specified saves. It does not automatically grant Advantage on an ability check such as escaping Maze. Condition immunity, creature-type restrictions, size, senses, and movement can defeat a plan before Legendary Resistance matters at all.

## Strategy 1 — Bypass It

Use when the party cannot force enough dangerous saves quickly.

Strong Wizard answers include:

- Wall of Force
- Maze
- Terrain control
- Buffs
- Summons
- Movement control
- Sight denial
- Damage
- Effects that use ability checks instead of saving throws

### Goal

Win the encounter **without asking for the boss's key saving throw**.

---

## Strategy 2 — Burn It Intentionally

Use when several party members can force dangerous saves in a short period.

### Steps

1. Tell the party you are burning Legendary Resistance.
2. Use effects the boss cannot afford to fail.
3. Force several meaningful saves quickly.
4. Do not waste premium spells on weak effects.
5. Once Legendary Resistance is exhausted, use the strongest save-based control.

### Good Burn Effects

Use effects that are dangerous even before your strongest finisher arrives.

---

## Strategy 3 — Ignore the Boss Temporarily

If the encounter includes dangerous minions or support creatures:

1. Control or remove the boss temporarily.
2. Destroy the supporting enemies.
3. Reposition.
4. Focus the boss afterward.

**Maze** and encounter-splitting effects are excellent for this.

---

## Save Targeting

Do not blindly target the same defense.

| Defense | General Clue |
|---|---|
| **STR** | Frail or physically weak target |
| **DEX** | Bulky, slow, constrained target |
| **CON** | Often strong on monsters; use carefully |
| **INT** | Simple-minded or animal-like target |
| **WIS** | Poor discipline or awareness |
| **CHA** | Weak force of personality / some planar targets |

These are only clues, not guaranteed stat knowledge.

---

## Boss Checklist

Before using a premium save spell, ask:

- Can I use a no-save answer instead?
- Can I split the encounter?
- Can I attack an ability check rather than a saving throw?
- Is the party actually prepared to burn Legendary Resistance?
- Does the spell still do something on a successful save?
- Is the boss even the best target right now?

## Core Rule

> Do not feed premium spell slots into Legendary Resistance without a plan.
