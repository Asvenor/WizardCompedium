## Components

| Component | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **V** | Verbal |
| **S** | Somatic |
| **M** | Material |

A valid spellcasting focus or component pouch can replace many ordinary Material components.

It does **not** normally replace a Material component that:

- Has a listed cost
- Is consumed

## Somatic + Material

When a spell has both Somatic and Material components, the hand used for the Material component can normally also perform the Somatic component.

For a **Somatic spell without Material components**, merely holding a focus does not supply a free gesturing hand. A shield plus a held wand/staff can therefore obstruct Shield or Counterspell unless War Caster or another specific feature resolves the hand requirement. A focus also needs to be valid for the class casting that spell; multiclass access does not make every focus universal.

**Verbal components use a normal speaking voice**, not an undetectable whisper. Being gagged or in magical silence blocks them. A non-Verbal spell still needs its other components. Magical speech or telepathy is not automatically a substitute for performing a Verbal component.

## Costly Components

Distinguish whether a component is:

- **Required but retained**
- **Consumed**

These behave very differently economically.

## Armor

A spellcaster must have the appropriate **armor training**. Wearing Light, Medium, or Heavy armor without it prevents spellcasting and imposes Disadvantage on STR/DEX D20 Tests. The current Shield rule is different: without Shield training, you gain **no Shield AC bonus**; the glossary does not apply that armor spellcasting ban to the Shield alone. Hand requirements still matter. Multiclass armor access and Bladesong's own restrictions are separate checks—see [Build Decision Engine](/library/character/build-decision-engine/).

## Targets

Before casting, check:

- Range
- Line of sight
- Line of effect / clear path
- Target type
- Creature size
- Willing/unwilling requirement
- Concentration
- Components
- Reaction availability

**Sight and clear path are different requirements.** Not every spell requires seeing the target, but the normal rule still prevents targeting through Total Cover unless the spell provides an exception. You can target yourself with a creature-choice spell unless it requires a Hostile creature or someone other than you.

Casting at an invalid target does **not** refund the slot. For a spell that would do nothing on a successful save, an invalid target appears to have succeeded; it is not a guaranteed creature-type detector.

## Spell Slots

A spell slot is the resource used to cast many leveled spells.

Having a higher-level multiclass slot does not automatically grant access to higher-level Wizard spells.

## One spell slot per turn

You can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell on a turn. This includes a Reaction cast during your own turn; it is not a one-slot-per-round limit. Slotless casting still needs its granted action, components or explicit component exemption, and other requirements.

**Examples:** a slotted Misty Step can pair with an Action cantrip, but not a slotted Fireball on the same turn. A slotless spell granted by a feature can pair with a slotted spell if action economy and all other restrictions allow it. Casting a slotted spell on your turn still allows a slotted Shield on an enemy's later turn if your Reaction remains available.

## Longer casting times

A casting time of **1 minute or more**, including a ritual, requires your Magic action on every turn and Concentration throughout the casting. This applies even when the finished spell has no Concentration requirement. If that Concentration breaks, the casting fails without spending its spell slot; a new attempt starts over. Do not maintain a normal concentration summon while personally performing a one-hour Planar Binding cast.

## Overlapping effects

Different spells can coexist, subject to their specific text. Multiple castings of the **same spell** do not combine benefits: the most potent applies during the overlap, or the most recent if equally potent. Casting twice does not automatically double bonuses, transformation traits, or glyph-delivered buffs.

An ongoing non-instantaneous spell can be identified from observable effects with a **Study action and DC 15 INT (Arcana)** check. That is not a free identification of an enemy's spell before deciding whether to Counterspell it.
