**Rules baseline:** 2024 Player's Handbook and Dungeon Master's Guide.

Crafting converts **time, gold, materials, proficiencies, and spell access** into equipment and magic items during downtime.

<aside class="callout callout-info" data-callout="info">

**Scope**

These are the **general 2024 crafting rules**, independent of [Bastion](/library/bastions/bastion/) facilities.

</aside>

For Arcana Unleashed's optional rules for upgrading an existing magic item, see [Evolving Magic Items](/library/items/evolving-magic-items/). Those rules require DM permission and use the difference between rarity costs; they are not an automatic addition to the general crafting permissions below.

---

## Nonmagical Item Crafting

Crafting a nonmagical item requires:

- Appropriate **tool proficiency**
- Access to the required tools
- Raw materials
- Downtime

### Cost

**Raw Materials = 50% of the item's normal purchase price**

Round the material cost down. Workdays equal the normal purchase price in GP divided by 10, rounded up; each workday requires 8 hours. Qualified assistance can divide the time, normally with one assistant unless the DM permits more.

| Item Value | Material Cost |
|---:|---:|
| 10 gp | 5 gp |
| 50 gp | 25 gp |
| 100 gp | 50 gp |
| 500 gp | 250 gp |
| 1,000 gp | 500 gp |

---

## Tools

The required tool depends on what is being created.

| Item Type | Typical Tool |
|---|---|
| Metal weapons & armor | **Smith's Tools** |
| Bows & arrows | **Woodcarver's Tools** |
| Leather equipment | **Leatherworker's Tools** |
| Clothing | **Weaver's Tools** |
| Jewelry | **Jeweler's Tools** |
| Pottery | **Potter's Tools** |
| Wooden objects | **Carpenter's Tools** |
| Alchemical items | **Alchemist's Supplies** |
| Written works | **Calligrapher's Supplies** |
| Poison | **Poisoner's Kit** |

<aside class="callout callout-tip" data-callout="tip">

**Wizard**

Tool proficiencies become considerably more valuable when combined with **downtime, Fabricate, magic-item crafting, and qualified assistants**.

</aside>

## Magic Item Crafting

Magic-item crafting is primarily determined by:

- **Rarity**
- **Crafting cost**
- **Crafting time**
- Arcana proficiency
- Required tool proficiency
- Required spell access
- Raw-material availability
- The cost of any underlying nonmagical item
- Qualified assistance

---

## Crafting Cost & Time

| Rarity | Crafting Cost | Crafting Time |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Common** | 50 gp | 5 days |
| **Uncommon** | 200 gp | 10 days |
| **Rare** | 2,000 gp | 50 days |
| **Very Rare** | 20,000 gp | 125 days |
| **Legendary** | 100,000 gp | 250 days |

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**Access**

Having enough gold and time does not automatically mean every magic item is available to craft. The item's specific requirements still apply.

</aside>

### Materials and underlying items

The DM decides whether appropriate magic-item materials are available. The DMG gives a **75% availability chance in a city** and **25% in another settlement**; after an unsuccessful check, wait at least **7 days** before checking again. This is not permission to reroll by visiting every shop on the same day.

The rarity price is only the magical materials. If the item incorporates priced mundane equipment, **add the full purchase price of that equipment**, or craft the mundane item separately. For example, +1 Plate Armor needs 2,000 GP of magical materials plus a 1,500 GP suit of plate; personally crafting the plate instead has its own materials and workdays.

### Personal crafting versus Bastion production

The general crafting table has **no rarity-based minimum character level**. Access to materials, proficiencies, required prepared spells, and time are the gates. Bastion facility crafting is a different permission: an Arcane Study needs an owner of level 9+ and is limited to Common/Uncommon items on the DMG Arcana tables. An expanded-source item is not automatically added to those tables. See [Bastion Crafting](/library/bastions/bastion-crafting/) and [Facilities](/library/bastions/facilities/).

## Consumable Magic Items

Consumable magic items generally require less investment than permanent magic items of equivalent rarity.

Halve the normal rarity-based cost and time for a consumable other than a Spell Scroll. Add the cost of an underlying nonmagical item separately. Magic-item crafting also uses 8-hour workdays, which need not be consecutive.

Examples include:

- Potions
- One-use magic items
- Spell Scrolls

**Spell Scrolls use their own crafting rules.**

**A basic Potion of Healing also has a specific PHB recipe:** Herbalism Kit proficiency, use of the kit, **25 GP and one 8-hour day**. It does not require Arcana proficiency. Do not charge the generic Common-consumable time of 2.5 days for this recipe. Greater/Superior/Supreme healing potions use the appropriate other rules rather than inheriting the one-day recipe.

Sources: [2024 PHB crafting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#CraftingEquipment) and [2024 DMG magic-item crafting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/treasure#CraftingMagicItems).

See [Spellbook & Spell Scrolls](/library/advanced/spellbook-stuff/).

## Required Proficiencies & Tools

Crafting a magic item requires:

- **Arcana proficiency**
- **Proficiency with the required crafting tool**
- The required tool itself

Any creature assisting with the crafting must also have:

- **Arcana proficiency**
- **Proficiency with the required tool**

## Magic Item Tools

| Magic Item Category | Required Tool |
|---|---|
| **Armor** | Leatherworker's Tools, Smith's Tools, or Weaver's Tools depending on the armor |
| **Potion** | Alchemist's Supplies or Herbalism Kit |
| **Ring** | Jeweler's Tools |
| **Rod** | Woodcarver's Tools |
| **Scroll** | Calligrapher's Supplies |
| **Staff** | Woodcarver's Tools |
| **Wand** | Woodcarver's Tools |
| **Weapon** | Leatherworker's Tools, Smith's Tools, or Woodcarver's Tools depending on the weapon |
| **Wondrous Item** | Tinker's Tools or the tool required to make the nonmagical item on which it is based |

## Spell Requirement

If the magic item allows its user to cast one or more spells:

> **You must have all of those spells prepared every day you spend crafting the item.**

## Important

The required tool is determined primarily by the magic item's **category** and, for some categories, the underlying item's construction.

For example:

- A metal magic weapon may require **Smith's Tools**
- A wooden magic weapon may require **Woodcarver's Tools**
- Different types of magic armor can require different tools
- Wondrous Items may use **Tinker's Tools** or the tool appropriate to the underlying nonmagical item

---

## Required Spells

If a magic item allows its user to cast one or more spells, the crafter must have all of those spells **prepared on every day spent crafting the item**.

For a Wizard, this makes the **spellbook part of the crafting toolkit**: a larger spell collection can expand the range of spell-based items the Wizard is capable of creating.

<aside class="callout callout-info" data-callout="info">

**Spell Access**

Merely having a spell in a spellbook is not enough. It must be prepared on each crafting day.

</aside>

---

## Prepared Spells Are Not Cast

The general 2024 magic-item crafting rule requires relevant spells to be **prepared**; it does not tell the crafter to cast them or provide their normal spell components.

Do not add a spell's consumed or costly material components to the crafting price unless the individual item's rules or the DM explicitly require them.

---

## Multiple Crafters

Qualified characters can cooperate on a crafting project when the crafting rules permit it.

Every contributor must meet the requirements for contributing.

Additional qualified crafters reduce the **calendar time**, not the total amount of work required.

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**Normal Assistant Limit**

Normally, only **one other character** can assist. A team of three or more crafters requires DM permission.

</aside>

### Example

A project requires **50 days of work**:

| Qualified Crafters | Completion Time |
|---:|---:|
| **1** | 50 days |
| **2** | 25 days |
| **5 (DM permission)** | 10 days |
| **10 (DM permission)** | 5 days |

Two crafters also reduce a permanent Uncommon project from 10 to **5 workdays** for the same 200 GP of magical materials. This is often a better early investment than starting a Rare project requiring 2,000 GP and 25 assisted workdays. Fractional results do not create permission to skip the required hours; settle partial-workday scheduling with the DM.

<aside class="callout callout-tip" data-callout="tip">

**Scaling**

Assistance becomes increasingly important for **Rare, Very Rare, and Legendary** projects because their base crafting times are enormous.

</aside>

## Spell Scroll Crafting

Spell Scrolls use a separate cost and time progression.

| Spell Level | Cost | Time |
|---:|---:|---:|
| **Cantrip** | 15 gp | 1 day |
| **1** | 25 gp | 1 day |
| **2** | 100 gp | 3 days |
| **3** | 150 gp | 5 days |
| **4** | 1,000 gp | 10 days |
| **5** | 1,500 gp | 25 days |
| **6** | 10,000 gp | 40 days |
| **7** | 12,500 gp | 50 days |
| **8** | 15,000 gp | 60 days |
| **9** | 50,000 gp | 120 days |

For copying, casting, scribing, and scroll-specific rules, see [Spellbook & Spell Scrolls](/library/advanced/spellbook-stuff/).

## Crafting Checklist

Before starting a project:

| Requirement | Check |
|---|---|
| **Item** | What exactly are you creating? |
| **Rarity** | What rarity is it? |
| **Tools** | Which tools are required? |
| **Proficiency** | Do you meet the proficiency requirement? |
| **Gold** | Can you afford the materials? |
| **Time** | Is enough downtime available? |
| **Spells** | Does the item require spell access? |
| **Components** | Are additional costly components required? |
| **Assistants** | Can qualified crafters reduce completion time? |
