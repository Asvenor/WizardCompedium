**Uncommon · Attunement required · Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica (2018)**  
**Access:** Sorcerer, Warlock, or Wizard

## Practical value

Attempts an unknown/unprepared class-list spell with normal components and a slot. Arcana DC is 10 + twice the expended slot level.

## Limiting cost

Failure still spends the slot and uses the failure table; slots 6–9 use the level-5 table. Expertise helps, but does not guarantee high-level success.
