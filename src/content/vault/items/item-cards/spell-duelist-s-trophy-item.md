**Rare · Attunement required · Arcana Unleashed**  
**Access:** Spellcaster

## Practical value

Removes Somatic components when casting with a spell slot; also offers Mage Hand and a once-per-dawn Dispel Magic rider on a melee spell hit.

## Limiting cost

Materials still matter. Slot-free casting is not covered by its Somatic benefit, and it does not duplicate every War Caster benefit.
