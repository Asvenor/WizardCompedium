**Uncommon · Attunement required · Arcana Unleashed**  
**Access:** Spellcaster

## Practical value

Find Familiar once per dawn. A Magic action transforms the familiar within 60 feet into a Giant Octopus for ten minutes; form HP become Temporary HP.

## Limiting cost

Losing those Temporary HP ends transformation. The transformation has no separate stated usage cap, but the item does not explicitly let a familiar attack.
