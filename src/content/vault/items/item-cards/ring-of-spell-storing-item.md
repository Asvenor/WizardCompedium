**Rare · Attunement required · Dungeon Master's Guide (2024)**  
**Access:** No class restriction

## Practical value

Holds five spell levels. The wearer casts the released spell and supplies its Concentration, using the stored casting's specified statistics.

## Limiting cost

Filling it is not free. Five spell levels are not five arbitrary spells, and a casting that cannot fit is wasted.
