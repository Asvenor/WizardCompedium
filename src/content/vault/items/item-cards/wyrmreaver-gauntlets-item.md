## Practical value

+1d6 Force on Unarmed Strike hits and a selected elemental resistance. Once per dawn, Bonus Action runes provide one minute of specialist reach and Opportunity Attack benefits.

## Limiting cost

30-foot reach applies on your turn only. No extra spell damage or permanent 30-foot reaction zone; the Prone DC uses STR, not INT.
