**Arcana Unleashed offers optional, DM-controlled item evolution.** It can improve a favored item without replacing it or adding another attunement slot. It is not a player entitlement to design or purchase any combination.

## What carries forward

An evolution advances an item to the next rarity, retaining its earlier properties and its existing attunement requirement. Existing attunement continues. Legendary items cannot evolve further. Tier-of-play advancement is suggested timing, not an automatic unlock.

There are three routes:

| Route | What changes | Wizard implication |
|---|---|---|
| Ready-made evolving item | Gains its next printed rarity's abilities | Follow the exact stage of an item such as Staff of Skulls; do not combine all stages at the starting rarity. |
| Simple evolution | A listed item becomes its next stronger version | Wand of the War Mage can progress +1 → +2 → +3. This is not a universal upgrade permission for every book or wand. |
| Complex evolution | An eligible item gains a property from its new rarity's table | Can add a useful defense, spell, or ability increase to an item already worth attuning. Existing numerical bonuses do **not** also increase. |

For complex evolution, the listed eligible items are Rod of the Pact Keeper and Wand of the War Mage, plus **Uncommon through Very Rare Armor, Staves, or Weapons that require attunement and are not cursed**. The system uses DMG items; extending eligibility to another source is separately at the DM's discretion. An Arcane Grimoire is not automatically eligible merely because a Wizard holds it. A Staff of Power is a useful eligible Very Rare example; a Legendary Staff of the Magi has no further step.

## High-value properties to compare

These are selected optimization priorities, not the complete property tables. The DM rolls or chooses the granted property. Where a property casts a spell, normal spell restrictions—including Concentration unless an exception is stated—remain.

| New rarity / property | Granted benefit | Important cost or limit |
|---|---|---|
| Rare — Vigilant | Advantage on Initiative and Perception checks while held or worn | Advantage does not stack with another source of Advantage. |
| Rare — Skulking | Pass without Trace once per dawn while held or worn | Expands group infiltration options; still Concentration. |
| Very Rare — Fortunate | Reroll a failed D20 Test, twice per dawn while attuned | Must use the second roll; not automatic success. |
| Very Rare — Traversing | Dimension Door and Freedom of Movement, each once per dawn while held or worn | Separate daily spell uses; the spells keep their targeting and duration limits. |
| Legendary — Empowering | A DM-chosen ability increases by 2, maximum 24, while attuned | Ask for INT; neither the ability choice nor item access is guaranteed. It cannot take INT 24 to 26. |
| Legendary — Spellguarding | Advantage on saves against spells and other magical effects while held or worn | Not all saving throws, not automatic Concentration-save Advantage against ordinary damage. |
| Legendary — All-Seeing | Truesight 30 feet while attuned | Range-limited perception, not sight through every wall or obstruction. |

**Worked choice:** if the DM evolves Staff of Power to Legendary with Empowering and selects INT, a Wizard with INT 22 becomes INT 24 while attuned. The staff retains its existing benefits and still occupies one attunement slot. Its +2 bonuses do not become +3 as part of this complex evolution. Compare that +1 INT-modifier improvement against Spellguarding or another allowed property: the best choice depends on the defenses and resources already available.

This is an unusually efficient way to add power to an occupied slot, but the entire item is now **Legendary**. Do not count it as an ordinary Very Rare reward. Repeated evolutions do not justify adding together replacement weapon bonuses, duplicating the same benefit without checking stacking rules, or skipping rarity steps.

## Optional crafting costs

The DM may permit evolution through the normal magic-item crafting framework. If permitted, subtract the old rarity's crafting time and material cost from the new rarity's values. These examples use the standard **nonconsumable** costs, before any separately applicable assistance or specific exception.

| Evolution | Extra material cost | Extra 8-hour workdays |
|---|---:|---:|
| Common → Uncommon | 150 GP | 5 |
| Uncommon → Rare | 1,800 GP | 40 |
| Rare → Very Rare | 18,000 GP | 75 |
| Very Rare → Legendary | 80,000 GP | 125 |

The Common → Uncommon row is only a cost calculation for an otherwise permitted evolution; it does not add Common items to the complex-evolution eligibility list. The arithmetic does not waive eligibility, the DM's decision to allow crafting evolution, materials, tools, Arcana proficiency, or required prepared spells. In particular, gaining an off-list spell through an item does not automatically supply whatever spell preparation the crafting process requires. See [Crafting](/library/items/crafting/) for those gates; do not import a Bastion facility's separate permissions into personal evolution crafting.
