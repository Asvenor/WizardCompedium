Facilities are the functional parts of a Bastion. They determine what the Bastion can produce, research, craft, or otherwise provide while the character is adventuring.

## Facility Types

Bastion facilities are divided into **Basic Facilities** and **Special Facilities**.

### Basic Facilities

Basic Facilities represent the ordinary rooms and infrastructure of the Bastion.

Examples include:

- Bedrooms
- Kitchens
- Dining rooms
- Storage rooms
- Courtyards
- Sitting rooms
- Bathrooms
- Ordinary workshops

They don't provide the mechanical benefits of Special Facilities and aren't limited by the character's Special Facility progression.

Their construction and expansion can require **time and gold**.

The official Basic Facility list is Bedroom, Dining Room, Parlor, Courtyard, Kitchen, and Storage. Bathrooms and ordinary workshops above are descriptive room arrangements, not extra rules-defined Special Facilities. See [Basic facility construction](/library/bastions/construction-defense/#basic-facility-construction) for the initial free rooms and prices.

---

## Special Facilities

Special Facilities provide specific mechanical benefits and are the main progression system of a Bastion.

A character gains additional Special Facilities as they level.

| Character Level | Special Facilities |
|---:|---:|
| **5** | 2 |
| **9** | 4 |
| **13** | 5 |
| **17** | 6 |

Each Special Facility has its own:

- Level requirement
- Prerequisites, if any
- Size
- Hirelings
- Available orders
- Mechanical benefits

Some facilities require a particular feature, proficiency, or type of equipment before they can be selected.

Each Special Facility can be selected only once unless its description permits multiples. Gaining a level permits replacing one with another eligible facility; buying more ordinary rooms does not bypass the slot limit. Productive orders also require presence or magical communication: see [Remote orders and ongoing work](/library/bastions/hirelings/#remote-orders-and-ongoing-work).

## Wizard Facilities

These are the facilities most relevant to an optimized Wizard Bastion.

## Arcane Study

**Available:** Level 5

A dedicated workspace for arcane research and magical projects.

### Main Uses
- Arcane crafting
- Magical research
- Spellbook-related work
- Wizard-focused Bastion activities

At level 5 its concrete benefits are a 7-day Identify charm after a Long Rest in the Bastion, a free Arcane Focus per 7-day Craft order, or a blank book for 10 GP per 7-day order. **Common and Uncommon Arcana-table magic-item crafting starts at character level 9**, with normal crafting costs and requirements. Items that cast spells still require the owner to craft personally with those spells prepared. It is a thematic foundation, not an automatic strongest level-5 facility.

---

## Library

**Available:** Level 5

A collection of books, records, maps, and other sources of information.

### Main Uses
- Research
- Gathering information
- Lore
- Preparing for future adventures

The Library gives the Bastion an information-gathering function rather than purely producing equipment or resources.

Each Research order takes **7 days** and returns up to **three accurate, previously unknown facts**, chosen by the DM, about an eligible topic. It is useful preparation, not unrestricted answers to any question.

---

## Workshop

**Available:** Level 5; no prerequisite.

After an entire Short Rest in the Workshop, gain **Heroic Inspiration**, once per Long Rest. This is a strong benefit before an adventure without buying a consumable. It does not stack multiple held Inspiration uses.

Its three hirelings and six chosen Artisan's Tool types support normal mundane crafting. At owner level 9+, it can craft Common/Uncommon items from the **DMG Implements tables**, subject to normal costs, tool/Arcana requirements, and owner participation for items that cast spells. A 2,000 GP enlargement adds two hirelings and three tool types. It does not permit several simultaneous Craft items in this facility; apply the project assistance rules and settle with the DM how many of its hirelings can contribute to one project.

## Garden

**Available:** Level 5; no prerequisite; multiples allowed.

A Harvest order takes **7 days** and costs no money. Choose its type when adding it: Decorative (ten 5 GP bouquets, ten Perfume vials, or ten Candles), Food (100 days of Rations), Herb (herbs for ten Healer's Kits or one Potion of Healing), or Poison (plants for two Antitoxin vials or one Basic Poison vial). The Herb/Poison descriptions specify ingredients used to create those products; do not assume a further crafting-cost exemption beyond the facility's stated harvest.

Changing type takes **21 days** without another activity in the Garden. Enlarging for **2,000 GP** provides two component gardens, each yielding its own harvest. Use output for party needs first; listed product values are not an assurance of unlimited buyers.

---

## Scriptorium

**Available:** Level 9

A facility dedicated to producing and working with written material.

### Main Uses
- Written magical work
- Documents
- Scroll-related activities
- Written production

Its hireling can scribe a **Cleric or Wizard Spell Scroll of level 3 or lower**, paying the PHB scribing cost and taking its stated time; the hireling satisfies the prerequisites. It can also copy a nonmagical book in 7 days given a blank book, or make/distribute up to 50 paper products in 7 days for 1 GP each. It does not automatically duplicate a Wizard's usable spellbook notation.

---

## Teleportation Circle

**Available:** Level 9

A permanent magical transportation facility.

### Main Uses
- Long-distance transportation
- Returning to the Bastion
- Establishing the Bastion as a travel hub
- Connecting the headquarters to the wider campaign

Its value increases as the campaign becomes geographically larger.

The facility supplies a **permanent destination circle**, not free outbound teleportation. Its Recruit order has a 50% chance to bring a Friendly spellcaster for up to 14 days or one requested Wizard spell of level 4 or lower (level 8 or lower when the owner is level 17+). Costly materials must be paid; the visitor will not defend the Bastion.

---

## Laboratory

**Available:** Level 9

A dedicated facility for experimentation and specialized crafting.

### Main Uses
- Crafting
- Experimentation
- Alchemical work
- Producing specialized resources

A strong alternative when crafting is more important than one of the default progression facilities.

Its single hireling can craft things made with **Alchemist's Supplies**, following the normal PHB/DMG crafting rules. Its separate poison order produces one application of **Burnt Othur Fumes, Essence of Ether, or Torpor** in **7 days** for **half that poison's price**. It is not a general free-potion facility, and spell-granting magic items still require the general prepared-spell requirements to be met.

---

## Greenhouse

**Available:** Level 9

A controlled environment for cultivating useful and magical plants.

### Main Uses
- Growing special plants
- Producing consumable resources
- Supporting healing and other utility
- Creating renewable Bastion resources

It provides useful capabilities that don't necessarily overlap with the Wizard's normal spellcasting.

Three daily-regrowing fruits each provide Lesser Restoration; picked fruit loses its magic after 24 hours. A 7-day Harvest order produces a Greater Potion of Healing, or one application of Assassin's Blood, Malice, Pale Tincture, or Truth Serum, at no monetary cost.

---

## Observatory

**Available:** Level 13

A facility designed for observation and gathering information.

### Main Uses
- Information gathering
- Observation
- Strategic preparation
- High-level magical research

It fits naturally into a high-level Wizard headquarters focused on knowledge and preparation.

The concrete payoff is a Contact Other Plane charm after a Long Rest in the Observatory (7 days or one use; no free pass on the spell's Intelligence save), plus a 50% chance after 7 consecutive nights of Empower work to grant a Charm of Darkvision, Heroism, or Vitality. A hireling can do that work; the chosen recipient must be on the owner's plane. The facility requires the ability to use a Spellcasting Focus.

---

## Archive

**Available:** Level 13

An advanced collection of records and accumulated knowledge.

### Main Uses
- Advanced research
- Information storage
- Historical knowledge
- Strategic investigation

It is particularly useful when information and research are central to the campaign.

A 7-day Research order yields information as though its hireling cast **Legend Lore**. One selected reference book gives Advantage on a particular category of Study checks **only while both the owner and book are in the Bastion**. A 2,000 GP enlargement adds two reference books. The Arcana volume is not Advantage on every Arcana check, including ordinary scroll-copying checks.

---

## Meditation Chamber

**Available:** Level 13

A secluded facility used to prepare and empower characters before adventuring.

### Main Uses
- Personal preparation
- Temporary benefits
- Direct adventuring support

Unlike many Bastion facilities, its value is focused more directly on the character rather than crafting or information.

Fortify Self requires **7 days without leaving the Bastion** and then grants Advantage on **two randomly selected** saving-throw abilities for 7 days. It does not let the beneficiary select CON or a desired pair. Its Empower order instead improves the next Bastion event roll by allowing two rolls and a choice.

---

## Demiplane

**Available:** Level 17

A high-level magical facility existing beyond ordinary physical space.

### Main Uses
- Endgame magical infrastructure
- Magical production
- Personal empowerment
- Secure high-level headquarters functionality

It is one of the strongest final additions to an Archwizard's Bastion.

After an Empower order, spending an entire Long Rest in this facility grants temporary HP equal to **5 × character level** while its 7-day runes last (85 at level 17). Its door and space cannot be dispelled. This is a distinct Bastion facility, not merely the Demiplane spell. Fabrication is limited to one nonmagical object per Long Rest, at most 5 feet in any dimension and 5 GP in value, made from its listed nonprecious materials; it is not unlimited production.

---

## Storehouse

**Available:** Level 5

A facility centered around storing and trading goods.

### Main Uses
- Trade
- Economic activity
- Resource management
- Generating value from downtime

Its benefits are indirect for a Wizard, but additional wealth can support expensive spell components, spell copying, scroll creation, and magical crafting.
