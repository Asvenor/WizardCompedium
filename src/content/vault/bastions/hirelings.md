Hirelings are NPCs who work in a Bastion and operate its Facilities. They allow the Bastion to continue functioning while the character is away adventuring.

---

## Bastion Hirelings

Each Special Facility comes with a specified number of trained Hirelings.

These Hirelings:

- Operate their assigned facility
- Carry out the facility's orders
- Remain at the Bastion while you adventure
- Have the proficiencies required by their facility when specified
- Can sometimes craft or assist with crafting

## Cost

**Bastion Facility Hirelings cost you 0 gp in normal wages.**

The rules assume each Special Facility generates enough income to pay the Hirelings who work there.

You therefore don't normally track:

- Wages
- Food
- Lodging
- Routine living expenses

Projects performed by the Hirelings can still have their own material or gold costs.

---

## General Hireling Costs

These are ordinary Hirelings you hire outside the automatic Bastion Facility system.

| Hireling Type | Cost |
|---|---:|
| **Untrained Hireling** | 2 sp per day |
| **Skilled Hireling** | 2 gp per day minimum |
| **Messenger** | 2 cp per mile |

## Untrained Hireling

Performs work that requires no particular proficiency.

Examples:

- Laborer
- Porter
- General worker

**Cost: 2 sp/day**

## Skilled Hireling

Performs work requiring a proficiency with a skill, weapon, or tool.

Examples:

- Artisan
- Scribe
- Mercenary
- Specialist craftsman

**Cost: 2 gp/day minimum**

Highly experienced or specialized Hirelings can demand higher payment.

---

## Hireling Tiers

There are **no official Bastion Hireling tiers** such as Apprentice, Expert, Master, or different Hireling levels.

Instead, a Hireling's mechanical capabilities are determined by the **Special Facility they belong to**.

For example:

| Facility | Hirelings | Function |
|---|---:|---|
| Arcane Study | 1 | Arcane crafting |
| Library | Facility-defined | Research |
| Scriptorium | Facility-defined | Written production |
| Workshop | 3 | Crafting |
| Barrack | 1 | Recruit Bastion Defenders |
| Menagerie | 2 | Recruit and maintain creatures |

Some facilities gain additional Hirelings when enlarged.

---

## Hirelings and Crafting

Some Special Facilities allow their Hirelings to craft items.

For example, the Arcane Study Hireling can craft:

- Arcane Foci
- Blank books
- Common or Uncommon items from the DMG Arcana tables, **only once the owner is level 9+**

If a magic item allows its user to cast spells, the Wizard must craft the item personally.

The Hireling can still **assist**, reducing the required crafting time.

The Wizard must also have the required spells prepared during each day spent crafting when the item requires them.

The Arcane Study does **not** produce Common magic items at level 5. Its earlier craft orders are a free Arcane Focus in 7 days or a blank book in 7 days for 10 GP. [DMG: Arcane Study](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions#ArcaneStudy).

## Bastion Orders

Hirelings carry out the orders available to their Special Facility.

| Order | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Craft** | Create items |
| **Research** | Gather information |
| **Trade** | Buy or sell goods |
| **Harvest** | Produce resources |
| **Recruit** | Recruit creatures or defenders |
| **Empower** | Produce character benefits |

Not every facility can perform every order.

## Remote orders and ongoing work

A Bastion Turn normally occurs every **7 days**, although the DM can use a slower cadence. Owners can issue facility orders while present. While absent, the Bastion defaults to **Maintain** unless the owner can communicate with its hirelings through **Sending or similar magic**.

Maintain applies to the **whole Bastion**: its hirelings focus on upkeep, no other orders may be issued that turn, and the DM rolls a Bastion event. It is not a free additional order alongside Craft, Trade, or Harvest. For a long-running project, agree how absences and Maintain affect its work schedule; do not assume unattended unlimited production.

A Craft facility cannot start a second crafted item while its first is in production, even if another feature lets it receive two orders. Hirelings are not a roaming labor pool: combined Bastions keep their own hirelings, and those hirelings cannot be sent to or shared with another Bastion.

Sources: [DMG Bastion turns and orders](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions#BastionTurns), [combining Bastions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/bastions#CombiningBastions), and [PHB hireling prices](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#Hirelings).

## Hirelings vs Bastion Defenders

Hirelings and Bastion Defenders are separate.

| | Hirelings | Bastion Defenders |
|---|---|---|
| Operate facilities | Yes | No |
| Perform facility orders | Yes | No |
| Main purpose is defense | No | Yes |
| Automatically provided by facilities | Yes | No |
| Normal wages tracked | No | No |
| Can be recruited | Facility dependent | Yes |

Bastion Defenders are primarily used when resolving attacks against the Bastion.

## Wizard Hirelings

The actual identities of Hirelings are customizable.

A Wizard Bastion could describe them as:

| Facility | Example Hireling |
|---|---|
| Arcane Study | Arcane apprentice |
| Library | Librarian / scholar |
| Scriptorium | Scribe |
| Teleportation Circle | Circle keeper |
| Laboratory | Alchemist |
| Greenhouse | Herbalist |
| Observatory | Astronomer |
| Archive | Archivist |

These titles are thematic.

Their actual mechanical abilities come from the facility they operate.
