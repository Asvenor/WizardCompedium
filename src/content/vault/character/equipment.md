Equipment supports the compendium's seven subclasses — **Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer** — across pure Wizard, Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric structures. Choose equipment for the permitted casting mode and actual proficiencies; none of those structures is a flagship loadout.

A Wizard's equipment should **complement the build and campaign**, not follow a fixed starting loadout.

<aside class="callout callout-info" data-callout="info">

**Loadout Variables**

Armor, weapons, tools, spellcasting equipment, and starting purchases depend on **class, multiclass, subclass, background, proficiencies, campaign environment, starting level, available gold, and party composition**.

</aside>

## Equipment by class structure

| Structure or subclass requirement | Equipment implications |
|---|---|
| Pure Wizard | Unarmored defense, book/focus or pouch, and real spell components; armor is not assumed without a separate legal source of training |
| Revised Artificer dip | Light/medium armor and shields are available, but Artificer spells require a proficient tool focus held in hand. Tools do not automatically become a Wizard focus. |
| Fighter dip | Acquire the armor/Shield actually permitted by the starting or multiclass package. A class level does not supply free starting equipment. |
| Cleric dip | Holy Symbol or other allowed divine material handling is separate from Wizard casting; armor choice follows training, Strength/speed costs, and available funds. |
| Revised Bladesinger in any structure | No worn armor or Shield during Bladesong. Budget an eligible proficient weapon and components for the actual spell, not an assumed hands-free weapon focus for every effect. |
| Necromancer | Several subclass benefits need the book in hand. Book, Shield, tool, and costly component access cannot all occupy the same hand simultaneously without a legal handling sequence. |

These are casting constraints, not a ranking of class structures. [Build Decision Engine](/library/character/build-decision-engine/), [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/), and [Held items and spell components](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#held-items-and-spell-components) explain the associated choices.

## Always Useful

Mundane equipment can solve problems without consuming spell slots.

| Item | Primary Use |
|---|---|
| **Rope** | Climbing, securing objects, lowering equipment, restraints, general problem solving |
| **Chalk** | Mark routes, doors, traps, and explored locations |
| **Crowbar** | Leverage and opening objects without magic |
| **Steel Mirror** | Look around corners and inspect dangerous areas indirectly |
| **Ball Bearings** | Cheap terrain control and environmental problem solving |
| **Oil** | Fire, lighting, and environmental utility |
| **Tinderbox** | Reliable mundane fire |
| **Paper & Ink** | Maps, notes, messages, and copied information |
| **Empty Sacks** | Loot and miscellaneous object storage |
| **Healer's Kit** | Stabilize creatures without spending magic |
| **Rations** | Basic survival |
| **Waterskin** | Basic survival |
| **Pitons** | Climbing, securing ropes, and blocking doors |
| **Hammer** | Pitons and general utility |
| **10-foot Pole** | Interact with suspicious objects, floors, and hazards from a distance |

## Spellcasting Equipment

Carry whatever the specific build requires.

- **Arcane Focus**
- **Component Pouch**
- **Spellbook**
- **Backup Arcane Focus**
- Holy Symbol or other multiclass focus
- Costly material components
- Spell Scrolls

<aside class="callout callout-tip" data-callout="tip">

**Backup Focus**

A backup focus is cheap insurance against the primary focus being **lost, stolen, destroyed, or inaccessible**.

</aside>

For 2024 Wizard spells, the **spellbook itself can be a focus**. That permission does not waive costly/consumed materials, gestures, or hand access; a pouch is an alternative for eligible materials, not a universal hands-free replacement. Multiclass spellcasting can need a different focus, particularly the revised Artificer's held tools. See [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/), [Held items and spell components](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#held-items-and-spell-components), and the costed mundane example in [Worked Wizard Builds](/library/character/worked-wizard-builds/). [2024 Wizard Spellcasting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Level1WizardSpellcasting).

## Expensive Spell Components

Costly components are part of the Wizard's equipment budget.

**Reusable components** are long-term investments.  
**Consumed components** are recurring expenses.

Only carry components relevant to spells you realistically expect to use.

| Spell               | Component Type            |
| ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| [Identify](/library/spells/spells/)        | Reusable                  |
| [Fortune's Favor](/library/spells/dunamancy-spells/) | Consumed                  |

## Emergency Consumables

Consumables provide additional answers without relying entirely on Wizard spell slots.

| Item | Use |
|---|---|
| **Potion of Healing** | Emergency healing |
| **Antitoxin** | Poison protection |
| **Holy Water** | Situational anti-creature utility |
| **Acid** | Mundane damage and environmental utility |
| **Alchemist's Fire** | Persistent mundane fire |
| **Spell Scrolls** | Additional spellcasting resources |

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**Availability**

Consumable availability and usefulness are heavily campaign-dependent.

</aside>

## Tool Proficiencies

Carry tool sets when the character has both the **proficiency and realistic opportunities to use them**.

| Tool | Typical Use |
|---|---|
| **Thieves' Tools** | Locks and mechanical traps |
| **Alchemist's Supplies** | Alchemical crafting |
| **Calligrapher's Supplies** | Written work and relevant crafting |
| **Herbalism Kit** | Herbal preparations |
| **Smith's Tools** | Metalworking and crafting |
| **Tinker's Tools** | Repairs and mechanical work |
| **Poisoner's Kit** | Poison-related work |

Do not carry expensive or heavy tool sets simply because they exist.

## Storage

As wealth increases, magical storage can substantially expand the practical adventuring loadout.

- **Bag of Holding**
- **Portable Hole**
- **Handy Haversack**

Use magical storage for equipment that is useful to have available but **doesn't require immediate combat access**.

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**Extradimensional Storage**

Be aware of interactions between multiple extradimensional storage items before combining them.

</aside>

## Backup Spellbook

A backup spellbook should normally **not travel beside the primary spellbook**.

If the same event destroys or steals both books, the backup accomplished nothing.

### Good Storage Locations

- [Bastion](/library/bastions/bastion/)
- Secure residence
- Trusted location
- Protected vault
- [Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/) at high levels

<aside class="callout callout-tip" data-callout="tip">

**Redundancy**

**Primary Spellbook:** with the Wizard  
**Backup Spellbook:** somewhere else

</aside>

## Equipment Principle

> **Choose equipment around the build and campaign. Do not spend magical resources solving problems that mundane equipment can solve safely.**
