2024 Wizard plus revised Heroes of Faerûn (2025) Bladesinger, pure Wizard 1–20. Follow worked build D's feat/ability plan. Control is the first job; weapons supply sustained output without obliging you to enter melee. No Savant bonuses.

**No-copy budget:** six starting spells, then exactly two per Wizard level. Final book: **44 distinct spells**. This is an optimization preset under [Assumptions](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/#assumptions), not a universally best answer for every party.

## Cantrips

Start **Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, True Strike**; add **Mind Sliver at Wizard 4** and **Mage Hand at Wizard 10**. They do not use the levelled-spell budget. The Wizard's permitted Long Rest cantrip replacement remains available.

**Feature selections:** at Wizard 18 choose **Magic Missile + Invisibility** for Spell Mastery; at Wizard 20 choose **Counterspell + Dispel Magic** for Signature Spells. These spells are already in this route's book and become separately always prepared through those features; no new book entries. The Mastery choices have an Action casting time; Shield and Misty Step do not qualify.

Book rituals use Ritual Adept without occupying these preparations; time, reading the book, and materials still apply. Origin-feat spells in your chosen build, such as worked build A's Healing Word, are separate and are not silently included in this book budget.

## How this route wins

Bladesong requires no armor or Shield. Conjure Minor Elementals at 7 is a situational sustained-damage option for your own qualifying hits inside its 15-foot Emanation, not a mandatory opener. It needs an Action and Concentration; setup, exposure, and competing control matter. Use corrected +1d8 per higher slot, not original +2d8 scaling. No found magic weapon, Booming Blade, or unrestricted same-turn slot casting is assumed.

## Components and exact alternatives

No found books does **not** mean free components. Use [If components are unavailable](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/#if-components-are-unavailable) before choosing a spell you cannot fund. Its replacements are acquisition-time choices, not retroactive free swaps.
