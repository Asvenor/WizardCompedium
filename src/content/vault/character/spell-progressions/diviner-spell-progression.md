2024 PHB, pure Wizard 1–20. General controller with Portent, information gathering, and Divination slot recovery. Use worked build A's class/feat framework. This is a new acquisition preset, not extra spells on top of its older table.

**No-copy budget:** six starting spells, then exactly two per Wizard level. Divination Savant adds the separate school choices shown. Final book: **44 normal + 9 Savant = 53 distinct spells**. This is an optimization preset under [Assumptions](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/#assumptions), not a universally best answer for every party.

## Cantrips

Start **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**; add **Mage Hand at Wizard 4** and **Prestidigitation at Wizard 10**. They do not use the levelled-spell budget. The Wizard's permitted Long Rest cantrip replacement remains available.

**Feature selections:** at Wizard 18 choose **Magic Missile + Invisibility** for Spell Mastery; at Wizard 20 choose **Counterspell + Dispel Magic** for Signature Spells. These spells are already in this route's book and become separately always prepared through those features; no new book entries. The Mastery choices have an Action casting time; Shield and Misty Step do not qualify.

Book rituals use Ritual Adept without occupying these preparations; time, reading the book, and materials still apply. Origin-feat spells in your chosen build, such as worked build A's Healing Word, are separate and are not silently included in this book budget.

## How this route wins

Portent supports important rolls but does not defeat Legendary Resistance. Slot-cast Divinations can recover lower expended slots through Expert Divination; ritual casts spend no slot and cannot fuel it. Arcane Eye is a useful scouting and recovery choice at 7. Rary's Telepathic Bond remains valuable as a ritual.

## Components and exact alternatives

No found books does **not** mean free components. Use [If components are unavailable](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/#if-components-are-unavailable) before choosing a spell you cannot fund. Its replacements are acquisition-time choices, not retroactive free swaps. Savant replacements must still be legal Divination Wizard spells.
