**Pure Wizard 1–20; 2024 PHB Illusionist, with no Arcana Unleashed feat or spell required.** Follow every row directly: no scrolls, found spellbooks, copying, magic items, or bonus feats are assumed. This route prioritizes illusion utility while retaining dependable Wizard control and survival. It is an optimization recommendation, not a universally superior build. For the separate expansion feat version use [Illusion Adept - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/illusion-adept-spell-progression/); do not combine its free grants with this route.

Use the pure-caster foundation in [Worked Wizard Builds](/library/character/worked-wizard-builds/), with Illusionist as the subclass and **Tough** as Human's additional Origin feat: start INT 17, War Caster at 4 for INT 18, INT 20 at 8, Resilient (CON) at 12, Mage Slayer at 16, Boon of Dimensional Travel at 19. No Magic Initiate healing package is assumed by these preparation examples.

## Exact acquisitions

The extra column contains only Illusion Savant book additions. The subclass's level-6 summon grants are always prepared separately and use **neither this route's book-acquisition budget nor its ordinary preparation count**. Summon Fey is on the Wizard list; Summon Beast is accessed here through the explicit subclass grant. All named book choices here use their 2024 PHB versions. Lower-level Savant picks at later tiers are legal and avoid requiring expansion-only high-level Illusions.

## Cantrips and prepared examples

At Wizard 1 take **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Mage Hand**. At 3 the Illusionist grants **Minor Illusion** through Improved Illusions. Add **Prestidigitation** as the normal Wizard cantrip at 4 and **Message** at 10. This avoids spending a starting choice on the forthcoming subclass grant; cantrips do not use leveled-spell acquisition slots.

- **Wizard 5 — 9 ordinary preparations:** [Shield](/library/spells/spell-cards/shield-spell/); [Mage Armor](/library/spells/spell-cards/mage-armor-spell/); [Feather Fall](/library/spells/spell-cards/feather-fall-spell/); [Web](/library/spells/spell-cards/web-spell/); [Misty Step](/library/spells/spell-cards/misty-step-spell/); [Hypnotic Pattern](/library/spells/spell-cards/hypnotic-pattern-spell/); [Fireball](/library/spells/spell-cards/fireball-spell/); [Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/invisibility-spell/); [Phantasmal Force](/library/spells/spell-cards/phantasmal-force-spell/). There are no extra always-prepared leveled spells yet.
- **Wizard 9 — 14 ordinary preparations:** [Shield](/library/spells/spell-cards/shield-spell/); [Mage Armor](/library/spells/spell-cards/mage-armor-spell/); [Feather Fall](/library/spells/spell-cards/feather-fall-spell/); [Web](/library/spells/spell-cards/web-spell/); [Misty Step](/library/spells/spell-cards/misty-step-spell/); [Counterspell](/library/spells/spell-cards/counterspell-spell/); [Dispel Magic](/library/spells/spell-cards/dispel-magic-spell/); [Dimension Door](/library/spells/spell-cards/dimension-door-spell/); [Hypnotic Pattern](/library/spells/spell-cards/hypnotic-pattern-spell/); [Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/invisibility-spell/); [Greater Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/greater-invisibility-spell/); [Polymorph](/library/spells/spell-cards/polymorph-spell/); [Wall of Force](/library/spells/spell-cards/wall-of-force-spell/); [Synaptic Static](/library/spells/spell-cards/synaptic-static-spell/). **Additional always prepared:** Summon Beast, Summon Fey.

Find Familiar, Detect Magic, Unseen Servant, and Phantom Steed are book rituals by 5; Leomund's Tiny Hut joins by 9. Ritual Adept supplies book ritual access without ordinary preparation, not instant casting or free materials. Before an infiltration, trade a normal preparation for the acquired Suggestion, Major Image, or Arcane Eye as appropriate; the examples are complete examples, not every mission's ideal list.

## Costs and illusion boundaries

The core illusion/control package needs no expensive material. Find Familiar still consumes incense; the granted summons still require their costly materials, including when using the subclass's free casting. Its free versions halve the summoned creatures' HP; “always prepared” is not “always free.” Component omission from Improved Illusions removes **Verbal** requirements for eligible spells, not all components.

At acquisition time, **Eyebite instead of Contingency at 11**, or **Arcane Gate instead of Forcecage at 13**, avoids their expensive components without duplicating later choices. For the **Savant** Simulacrum pick at 15, choose **Hallucinatory Terrain** instead if you cannot fund a duplicate; it is a legal Illusion choice and is not otherwise acquired. These are explicit alternatives, not retroactive swaps or extra picks. Clone and Shapechange may remain uncast until their materials are obtained.

An image is not automatically a physical wall or an automatic failed save. Illusory Reality's eligible real object has duration and damage/condition limits; do not assume free permanent equipment. You cannot concentrate on Hypnotic Pattern, Greater Invisibility, and a summoned spirit together.
