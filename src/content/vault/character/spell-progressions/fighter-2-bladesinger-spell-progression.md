**Fighter 2 / Wizard 18, Bladesinger from Heroes of Faerûn (2025).** Fighter at character 1, Wizard at 2–6, second Fighter level at 7, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 40 ordinary Wizard acquisitions = **40 distinct book spells**. Keep Mage Armor. Revised Bladesong requires no armor and no Shield; armor training from a dip does not change that restriction. A worn-armor fallback is a different combat mode, not an extra Bladesong AC bonus.

## Dip features and separate spells

The Fighter start supplies **CON and STR saving-throw proficiency**, armor training, Weapon Mastery, Second Wind, and a Fighting Style. One concrete style choice is **Dueling for a one-handed Rapier mode (respect its weapon conditions); do not select Defense expecting it to help unarmored Bladesong**. Fighter 1 or 2 adds no spellcasting, spells, or caster levels. The multiclass prerequisite is **INT 13 and either DEX 13 or STR 13**. Use the starting-dip feat framework in [Build Decision Engine](/library/character/build-decision-engine/) rather than duplicating CON proficiency with Resilient (CON).

**Action Surge at character 7 cannot grant a Magic action under the 2024 rules.** It can supply an eligible non-Magic action; it is not two leveled Wizard casts through extra Magic actions. A Bladesinger’s eligible Attack-action spell substitution still uses that feature’s own limits and the one-slot-per-turn rule. The second Fighter level delays every later Wizard milestone another character level.

**Rules application at character 8 / Wizard 6:** a surged Attack action can use Extra Attack's replacement of one attack with an Action-time Wizard cantrip. Each eligible Attack action has its own one-cantrip replacement; this is not a Magic action. Thus the dip can add real weapon/cantrip output, while still costing two Wizard levels. It does not permit a leveled-spell replacement. [Revised Extra Attack](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#BladesingerWizard) · [2024 Action Surge](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes#Fighter).

## Wizard cantrips

At character 2 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, True Strike**. Add **Mind Sliver** at character 5 / Wizard 4 and **Mage Hand** at character 12 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

At **character 20 / Wizard 18**, choose **Magic Missile + Invisibility** for Spell Mastery. Both are already in the book and have Action casting times; they become separately always prepared. **No Signature Spells:** the route never reaches Wizard 20. It also does not reach the Wizard-19 Epic Boon feature; character level 19 alone grants no feat.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [Bladesinger - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/bladesinger-spell-progression/) for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

Bladesong is unarmored and Shield-free. Conjure Minor Elementals is a situational Concentration engine using corrected +1d8 upcast scaling, not a mandatory opener. A dip does not remove its setup cost or permit arbitrary extra slot expenditure in one turn.
