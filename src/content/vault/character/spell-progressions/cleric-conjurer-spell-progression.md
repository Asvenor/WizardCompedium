**Cleric 1 / Wizard 19, Conjurer from Arcana Unleashed.** Wizard at character 1–5, Cleric at character 6, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 42 ordinary Wizard acquisitions + 9 distinct Savant additions = **51 distinct book spells**. Keep the initial Mage Armor acquisition: it protects the Wizard-first levels before Cleric armor access. There is no automatic refund of an earlier book choice; later it can still protect an eligible ally.

## Dip features and separate spells

At character 6 select the **Thaumaturge** Divine Order, with **Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying** as Cleric cantrips and **Thaumaturgy** as its additional cantrip. Prepare **Bless, Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Sanctuary** through Cleric. These four level-1 Cleric spells use WIS, not INT, and do not consume Wizard book choices or Wizard preparations. Bless competes for Concentration with Wizard control. Choose a holy symbol and respect normal hand/component rules.

The prerequisite is **INT 13 and WIS 13**; fund that WIS in point buy rather than assuming an item. Starting Wizard retains **WIS and INT saving throws**, so Resilient (CON) remains an eligible later choice. There is no Cleric subclass or legacy level-1 domain feature from this one-level dip. Medium armor and Shield training are gained; Thaumaturge does not grant heavy armor.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 1 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**. Add **Mage Hand** at character 4 / Wizard 4 and **Prestidigitation** at character 11 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

At **character 19 / Wizard 18**, choose **Magic Missile + Invisibility** for Spell Mastery. Both are already in the book and have Action casting times; they become separately always prepared. **No Signature Spells:** the route never reaches Wizard 20. The Wizard-19 Epic Boon feature arrives at character 20, not character 19.

These are fixed examples from the acquired book, not a tracker. Feature and other-class preparations remain separate. Book rituals use Ritual Adept without ordinary preparation, but still need time, access to the book, and components. Only one Concentration plan and the normal one-slot-per-turn limit apply across both classes. This one-level caster dip preserves full shared-slot progression after it is taken while delaying new Wizard spell tiers by one character level.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [Conjurer - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/conjurer-spell-progression/) for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

The companion choices still require their costly materials. Longstrider in armored starts leaves the pure route’s low-cost Grease-for-Battle-Familiar Savant replacement distinct and legal; summoned spirits compete with ordinary control for Concentration.
