**Pure Wizard 1–20; 2024 PHB Illusionist plus Arcana Unleashed Illusion Adept.** This is the expansion-enabled specialist, not a separate subclass. Follow every row: no scrolls, found spellbooks, copying, magic items, or bonus feats are assumed. The feat supplies specific always-prepared spells, allowing more of the book budget to go elsewhere. Existing shared/replacement build tables remain alternatives, not additional acquisitions.

Keep the established feat order: **Illusion Adept at 4 (INT 18), War Caster at 8 (INT 19), Telekinetic at 12 (INT 20), Resilient (CON) at 16, Boon of Dimensional Travel at 19**. This trades earlier INT 20 for covert specialty casting. For a PHB-only route with earlier INT 20, use [Illusionist - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/illusionist-spell-progression/). Neither route is universally stronger in every campaign.

## Exact acquisitions

Extra book choices are Illusion Savant additions. Adept grants are **always prepared separately**, not additional book picks, and their thresholds depend on available slot levels. Each grant row is cumulative. Mastery and Signature Spells later reuse existing entries instead of granting new acquisition choices.

## Cantrips and prepared examples

At Wizard 1 take **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Mage Hand**. Improved Illusions grants **Minor Illusion** at 3. Add **Prestidigitation** as the normal Wizard cantrip at 4 and **Message** at 10. Telekinetic at 12 improves the Mage Hand already known; it does not refund a past cantrip choice. Cantrips remain separate from leveled-spell acquisitions.

- **Wizard 5 — 9 ordinary preparations:** [Shield](/library/spells/spell-cards/shield-spell/); [Mage Armor](/library/spells/spell-cards/mage-armor-spell/); [Feather Fall](/library/spells/spell-cards/feather-fall-spell/); [Web](/library/spells/spell-cards/web-spell/); [Misty Step](/library/spells/spell-cards/misty-step-spell/); [Hypnotic Pattern](/library/spells/spell-cards/hypnotic-pattern-spell/); [Fireball](/library/spells/spell-cards/fireball-spell/); [Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/invisibility-spell/); [Suggestion](/library/spells/spell-cards/suggestion-spell/). **Additional always prepared:** Silent Image, Phantasmal Force, Major Image.
- **Wizard 9 — 14 ordinary preparations:** [Shield](/library/spells/spell-cards/shield-spell/); [Mage Armor](/library/spells/spell-cards/mage-armor-spell/); [Feather Fall](/library/spells/spell-cards/feather-fall-spell/); [Web](/library/spells/spell-cards/web-spell/); [Misty Step](/library/spells/spell-cards/misty-step-spell/); [Counterspell](/library/spells/spell-cards/counterspell-spell/); [Dispel Magic](/library/spells/spell-cards/dispel-magic-spell/); [Dimension Door](/library/spells/spell-cards/dimension-door-spell/); [Hypnotic Pattern](/library/spells/spell-cards/hypnotic-pattern-spell/); [Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/invisibility-spell/); [Greater Invisibility](/library/spells/spell-cards/greater-invisibility-spell/); [Polymorph](/library/spells/spell-cards/polymorph-spell/); [Wall of Force](/library/spells/spell-cards/wall-of-force-spell/); [Synaptic Static](/library/spells/spell-cards/synaptic-static-spell/). **Additional always prepared:** Silent Image, Phantasmal Force, Major Image, Hallucinatory Terrain, Seeming, Summon Beast, Summon Fey.

Find Familiar, Detect Magic, Unseen Servant, and Phantom Steed are book rituals by 5; Leomund's Tiny Hut joins by 9. Ritual casting from the book does not consume ordinary preparation space, but time and materials remain necessary. Before an infiltration, the acquired Arcane Eye or Disguise Self can replace an ordinary default preparation. Preparing multiple Concentration spells supplies choices, not simultaneous Concentration.

## Costs and subtle-casting boundaries

The core illusion package has no expensive component. Find Familiar consumes incense, while the granted summons require their costly materials even with the subclass's free casting; those free versions halve creature HP. Illusion Adept's component benefit requires a **slot-cast Illusion** and never removes priced or consumed materials. It does not apply to a free Spell Mastery casting merely because that spell is an Illusion. A no-component cast avoids Counterspell's component trigger, not antimagic, later dispelling, or visible consequences.

At the acquisition decision, choose **Eyebite instead of Contingency at 11**, **Arcane Gate instead of Forcecage at 13**, or **Etherealness instead of Simulacrum at 14** when those costly components are unrealistic. These ordinary-spell alternatives do not duplicate any future pick. This is not a retrospective rewrite of the book. Clone and Shapechange can remain learned but uncast until funded.

Illusion Adept affects relevant Investigation checks, not Hypnotic Pattern's initial Wisdom save or Phantasmal Force's initial Intelligence save. Illusory Reality is bounded by its eligible-object, duration, damage, and condition restrictions; it is not a permanent free-equipment engine. See [Arcana Unleashed Combinations](/library/tactics/arcana-unleashed-combinations/) for interaction limits.
