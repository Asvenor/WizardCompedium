**Artificer 1 / Wizard 19, Abjurer from 2024 PHB.** Artificer at character 1, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 42 ordinary Wizard acquisitions + 9 distinct Savant additions = **51 distinct book spells**. Learn Grease instead of Mage Armor at Wizard 1. This route assumes trained armor is actually obtained, not free equipment. Use armor for the base AC; Grease adds control without Concentration.

## Dip features and separate spells

Use the revised **Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (2025)** class, not Tasha’s. At character 1 select Artificer cantrips **Guidance + Thorn Whip**; **Mending** is a separate Tinker’s Magic grant. Prepare **Cure Wounds + Faerie Fire**, using INT. These are two separate Artificer preparations, not Wizard book entries or Wizard preparation slots. Hold a proficient tool focus when casting an Artificer spell; this adds a Material component even to spells otherwise lacking one. A tool is not automatically a Wizard focus. Replicate Magic Item starts at Artificer 2 and is not gained here.

The Artificer start supplies **CON and INT saving-throw proficiency**. Do not also select Resilient (CON); use the starting-dip feat framework in [Build Decision Engine](/library/character/build-decision-engine/). This route assumes no Magic Initiate feat; any origin spell package must be accounted for separately.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 2 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion**. Add **Mage Hand** at character 5 / Wizard 4 and **Prestidigitation** at character 11 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

The Wizard-19 Epic Boon feature arrives at character 20, not character 19.

Book rituals use Ritual Adept without ordinary preparation, but still need time, access to the book, and components. Only one Concentration plan and the normal one-slot-per-turn limit apply across both classes. This one-level caster dip preserves full shared-slot progression after it is taken while delaying new Wizard spell tiers by one character level.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [Abjurer - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/abjurer-spell-progression/) for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

The pure route’s component alternatives and subclass resource limits remain unchanged.
