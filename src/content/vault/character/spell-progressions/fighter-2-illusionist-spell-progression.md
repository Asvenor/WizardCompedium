**Fighter 2 / Wizard 18, Illusionist from 2024 PHB.** Fighter at character 1, Wizard at 2–6, second Fighter level at 7, then Wizard at every remaining level. This is one concrete, static no-copy progression, not a preferred build over the other supported routes. Choose a route for its tradeoffs; do not combine acquisition tables. No found books, scrolls, copying, magic items, or extra feats are assumed.

**Book budget at character 20:** 40 ordinary Wizard acquisitions + 9 distinct Savant additions = **49 distinct book spells**. Learn Grease instead of Mage Armor at Wizard 1. This route assumes trained armor is actually obtained, not free equipment. Use armor for the base AC; Grease adds control without Concentration.

## Dip features and separate spells

The Fighter start supplies **CON and STR saving-throw proficiency**, armor training, Weapon Mastery, Second Wind, and a Fighting Style. One concrete style choice is **Defense for an actually worn-armor mode**. Fighter 1 or 2 adds no spellcasting, spells, or caster levels. The multiclass prerequisite is **INT 13 and either DEX 13 or STR 13**. Use the starting-dip feat framework in [Build Decision Engine](/library/character/build-decision-engine/) rather than duplicating CON proficiency with Resilient (CON).

**Action Surge at character 7 cannot grant a Magic action under the 2024 rules.** It can supply an eligible non-Magic action; it is not two leveled Wizard casts through extra Magic actions. A Bladesinger’s eligible Attack-action spell substitution still uses that feature’s own limits and the one-slot-per-turn rule. The second Fighter level delays every later Wizard milestone another character level.

## Wizard cantrips

At character 2 / Wizard 1 choose **Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Mage Hand**. Improved Illusions grants **Minor Illusion** at character 4 / Wizard 3; add **Prestidigitation** at character 5 / Wizard 4 and **Message** at character 12 / Wizard 10. Other-class cantrips are separate pools. Scaling uses total character level where the cantrip says so. All Wizard milestones, including subclass selection at 3, use Wizard level.

At **character 20 / Wizard 18**, choose **Magic Missile + Invisibility** for Spell Mastery. Both are already in the book and have Action casting times; they become separately always prepared. **No Signature Spells:** the route never reaches Wizard 20. It also does not reach the Wizard-19 Epic Boon feature; character level 19 alone grants no feat.

## Subclass tactics and components

Use [Illusionist - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/illusionist-spell-progression/) for the subclass’s operating limits and counted component alternatives. The normal and Savant acquisition order here is the same at each Wizard milestone, apart from the stated initial armor substitution. Apply replacements **when acquiring** the spell, not as free later retraining. Retain ordinary defense and control when the specialty does not fit the encounter.

This table assumes no Illusion Adept feat. [Illusion Adept - Spell Progression](/library/character/spell-progressions/illusion-adept-spell-progression/) is an optional pure-Wizard feat configuration within Illusionist, not another supported subclass or automatic extra grants here. If adopting that feat in a dip build, use its actual feat-acquisition and slot-level unlock rules in [Adept feats need their own timing](/library/character/multiclass-spell-timing/#adept-feats-need-their-own-timing).
