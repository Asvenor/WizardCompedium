The [Spell Progression Hub](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/) supplies complete character-level spell tables for all seven supported subclasses with Artificer 1, Cleric 1, Fighter 1, and Fighter 2. Use this page to understand their calendars or compare another timing for the same supported classes. **Spell access follows Wizard level, not the largest shared slot.** No subclass or dip is the default or flagship.

## Exact calendars

Each number below is the Wizard-level row to use at that character level. “No new Wizard row” means the class dip grants no normal Wizard acquisitions or new Savant choice; it does not erase the book already owned.

Apply the same calendar to class features, normal preparation count, new cantrips, Wizard feats, and Savant. Do not take the Wizard-20 row on a build that ends at Wizard 19 or 18. A late dip grants multiclass proficiencies, **not** the starting class's saving throws.

## What remains separate

| Dip | Separate spells | Slot contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Revised Artificer 1 | Two Artificer preparations; two chosen cantrips plus Mending from Tinker's Magic; INT and tool requirements | One caster level; half Artificer levels round up |
| Cleric 1 | Four level-1 Cleric preparations, Cleric cantrips and Divine Order; WIS | One caster level |
| Fighter 1 or 2 | No spellcasting at these levels | None |

Two Artificer levels would still contribute only one caster level and delay Wizard another level; that is not the printed Artificer-1 preset. Cleric-first is mechanically possible, but the provided support route deliberately reaches Wizard 5 before dipping. A heavy-armor choice changes ability/equipment tradeoffs, not how many Wizard spells the level grants.

### Armor and subclass choices

For trained, actually armored Artificer/Fighter starts, the non-Bladesinger routes choose Grease instead of Mage Armor, except Conjurer uses Longstrider to leave Grease available as its separate low-component Savant alternative. Do not automatically remove Mage Armor from a Wizard-first book when armor later becomes available: acquisition is not preparation, and no refund is granted. It can still serve a suitable ally. Revised Bladesinger must remove armor and Shield for Bladesong, so every printed Bladesinger route keeps Mage Armor. Armor access is a situational tradeoff, not proof that a dip is always better.

### Adept feats need their own timing

School Adept feature tables can use **available spell-slot levels**, while normal Wizard acquisitions and Savant use the Wizard class's access. Their always-prepared grants can therefore unlock at different character levels in a multiclass build. Also move the actual feat acquisition to the appropriate Wizard ASI milestone. Do not shift every column of an Adept route by one mechanically and call the result exact. The printed Adept presets are pure Wizard; the printed multiclass presets do not assume those feats. Use the feat's exact table in [Feats(Epic Boon) Tier List](/library/tiers/feats-epic-boon-tier-list/) when deliberately changing that foundation.
