Four complete, static illustrations show what the choices actually cost from level 1 to 20. Their letter labels are identifiers, **not ranks or recommended defaults**. They are examples to compare, not character sheets or flagship builds.

The supported scope is **Abjurer, Bladesinger, Chronurgy, Conjurer, Diviner, Illusionist, and Necromancer**, each with pure Wizard, Artificer, Fighter, and Cleric spell routes. For the full equally presented route list, use [Spell Progression Hub](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/); for valid ability/feat frameworks across every structure, use [Static ability and feat frameworks](/library/character/build-decision-engine/#static-ability-and-feat-frameworks). The four longer illustrations below are not a claim that unillustrated combinations lack support.

For revised Conjurer and Necromancer, plus an optional Illusion Adept configuration of the supported Illusionist, use [Arcana Unleashed Builds](/library/character/arcana-unleashed-builds/). Those expansion build blueprints have their own source assumptions and link to [Arcana Unleashed Combinations](/library/tactics/arcana-unleashed-combinations/) for the exact interaction limits.

## Shared assumptions

- **Rules:** 2024 Player's Handbook. Build B additionally permits the published revised Artificer in **Eberron: Forge of the Artificer (2025)**; D uses the revised Bladesinger in **Heroes of Faerûn (2025)**. No legacy class features, custom backgrounds, bonus feats, ability-score books, or magic items are assumed.
- **Ability generation:** 27-point buy, followed by the Criminal background's +2 INT and +1 CON in A/B/C or +1 DEX in D. All four are 2024 Humans: Criminal supplies Alert; Human supplies a different Origin feat and Perception proficiency. Background and species do not grant extra General-feat choices.
- **Equipment:** ordinary armor/foci/components must still be acquired. The comparisons use no magic-item bonuses and do not assume starting characters own expensive armor or high-level spell components. A multiclass level does **not** grant another class's starting equipment.
- **Progression:** take the listed class at each character level. Feats arrive at that class's milestones; total level alone does not grant an ASI. PB and cantrip scaling follow total character level, while Wizard preparations, subclass features, and book additions follow Wizard level.
- **Spellbook:** the shared acquisition table below uses only free level-up choices. Savant additions are listed separately. Found scrolls/books can improve it, but are not required to make the build function.

Rules: [Wizard and core subclasses](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Wizard), [origins](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-origins), [feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats), [multiclassing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/creating-a-character#Multiclassing). The choices and comparisons are optimization analysis.

**Campaign horizon changes the answer.** At character level 5, A and C have Fireball/Hypnotic Pattern; B does not. Armor can be worth these delays, but full slot progression does not erase them.

## A — Pure Diviner

**Point buy:** STR 8, DEX 14, CON 15, INT 15, WIS 10, CHA 8. **After background:** 8 / 14 / 16 / 17 / 10 / 8.

**Origin:** Criminal / Human. Human feat: Magic Initiate (Cleric), using INT, with Guidance, Light, and Healing Word. Healing Word is always prepared separately, with one free casting per Long Rest and ordinary slot casting; it is not a Wizard-book spell. Choose Arcana and Investigation from Wizard, then Scholar Expertise in Arcana at Wizard 2.

| 3 | Diviner 3 | 2 | Portent and Divination Savant |
| 4 | Diviner 4 | 2 | War Caster: INT 18; fourth Wizard cantrip |
| 5 | Diviner 5 | 3 | Memorize Spell; first level-3 spells |
| 6 | Diviner 6 | 3 | Expert Divination |

| 8 | Diviner 8 | 4 | ASI: INT 20 |

| 10 | Diviner 10 | 5 | The Third Eye; fifth Wizard cantrip |
| 11 | Diviner 11 | 6 | First level-6 spells |
| 12 | Diviner 12 | 6 | Resilient (CON): CON 17 and CON-save proficiency |
| 13 | Diviner 13 | 7 | First level-7 spells |
| 14 | Diviner 14 | 7 | Greater Portent: three rolls rather than two |
| 15 | Diviner 15 | 8 | First level-8 spells |
| 16 | Diviner 16 | 8 | Mage Slayer: DEX 15; Guarded Mind mental-save insurance |

| 19 | Diviner 19 | 9 | Boon of Dimensional Travel: DEX 16 |
| 20 | Diviner 20 | 9 | Signature Spells: Counterspell and Dispel Magic |

**Why these choices:** War Caster both raises the initial odd INT and protects Concentration; the next ASI reaches INT 20. Resilient adds scaling protection to all CON saves. Mage Slayer is selected for its mental-save rescue, not for melee attacks. Its DEX point combines with the Boon's DEX point to reach 16: on a no-Tome build this immediately improves AC, Initiative, and DEX saves, whereas INT 20 → 21 does not change the INT modifier. An anticipated above-20 INT route changes that last decision; see [Intelligence above 20](/library/advanced/intelligence-above-20/).

**Operating limits:** choose Portent before the roll, at most once per turn; a low replacement roll does not bypass Legendary Resistance. Expert Divination requires spending a level-2+ slot on Divination and restores a *lower* expended slot, never above level 5. It is resource recovery, not an unlimited spell engine.

**Defense:** Mage Armor gives AC 15 initially and AC 16 after DEX reaches 16. Shield can temporarily add 5 when its trigger and casting requirements are met, but costs the Reaction and normally a slot. Magic Initiate's emergency Healing Word does not replace a full Cleric package or justify standing in melee.

## B — Revised Artificer 1 / Abjurer 19

Use the same point-buy and background increases as A: **8 / 14 / 16 / 17 / 10 / 8**. **Start Artificer** to obtain CON/INT saves; entering Artificer later does not grant CON proficiency.

**Origin:** Criminal / Human. Human feat: Magic Initiate (Cleric), using INT, with Light, Spare the Dying, and Healing Word. Starting Artificer skills: Arcana and Investigation; Human: Perception. Choose Woodcarver's Tools as the additional Artisan's Tools proficiency. At Artificer 1 prepare **Cure Wounds and Faerie Fire**; choose **Guidance and Thorn Whip**, with **Mending** added separately by Tinker's Magic. These are on the revised list; Healing Word comes from Magic Initiate, **not** Artificer 1.

One possible mundane purchase package uses the Artificer and Criminal gold options for **200 GP**: Scale Mail 50, Shield 10, Tinker's Tools 50, Component Pouch 25 = **135 GP**, leaving 65 GP for other supplies and any table-required book arrangements. Resolve how the initial spellbook from Wizard Spellcasting is obtained before relying on it; taking a multiclass level does not grant the Wizard's entire starting-equipment package. The 2024 equipment chapter does not list the legacy 50 GP Spellbook purchase, so that price is not silently assumed here. Scale Mail imposes Disadvantage on Stealth; a later Breastplate removes that armor penalty but costs more. No magic item is part of this budget.

**Why one Artificer level:** half Artificer levels round **up** for combined slots, so Artificer 1 + Wizard X has the slots of a full caster of the same total level. A second Artificer level adds Replicate Magic Item but contributes no second caster level; it is a materially different trade. The one-level build has **no** Replicate Magic Item, Flash of Genius, or extra attunement slots.

**Armor and hands:** Scale Mail + DEX 14 + Shield gives **AC 18**. Half Plate, if later purchased, gives AC 19 at DEX 14 or 16; the ordinary Medium-armor DEX contribution caps at +2. Mage Armor does not stack with worn armor. One hand holds the Shield; reserve the other for the current spell's actual tools, focus, components, and gestures. Artificer casting requires a proficient tool focus **in hand**, and adds M to its spells. Wizard Spellcasting does not automatically accept those tools as a focus; switching casting features is not permission to ignore object handling. See [Held items and spell components](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#held-items-and-spell-components) and [Spellcasting Rules](/library/reference/spellcasting-rules/).

**Ward boundaries:** maximum Ward HP is **2 × Wizard level + INT modifier**, not twice character level. At character level 20 this is **43**, not 45. Creating or replenishing it by casting requires an Abjuration spell **with a spell slot**; the free Magic Initiate Healing Word and free Spell Mastery casts are not free Ward refills. The Ward can also be replenished by the subclass's Bonus Action slot expenditure. Projected Ward competes for the same Reaction as Shield and Counterspell. Spell Resistance at Abjurer 14 protects against **spells**, not every feature that happens to be magical.

Sources: [revised Artificer features and spell list](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/efota/the-artificer#ArtificerSpellList), [2024 Abjurer](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Abjurer), [equipment](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment).

## C — Wizard-first Cleric support dip

**Point buy:** STR 8, DEX 14, CON 13, INT 15, WIS 13, CHA 9. **After background:** **8 / 14 / 14 / 17 / 13 / 9**. This is a legal 27-point array and meets the INT 13 / WIS 13 multiclass prerequisites. The WIS investment is real: compared with A/B, initial CON is two points lower.

**Origin:** Criminal / Human. Human feat: **Tough**, adding 2 HP per character level. Start Wizard for INT/WIS saves; choose Arcana and Investigation, and Human Perception. Take Cleric only after Wizard 5. Select **Thaumaturge**, then Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying, and Thaumaturgy as the four Cleric cantrips. Prepare **Bless, Healing Word, Cure Wounds, and Sanctuary** as the four separate level-1 Cleric spells. Bless and healing are the main support roles; Sanctuary's attacker save uses the lower Cleric DC, so it is less reliable here than on a WIS-focused Cleric.

**Why this remains distinct from Artificer:** it adds four divine preparations, including native Healing Word and Bless, while keeping the starting Wizard saves. Thaumaturge adds its WIS-based Arcana/Religion bonus and an extra Cleric cantrip. Tough partly compensates for the lower CON's HP cost, but does **not** improve concentration saves. INT 21 at the final level is progress toward a future increase, not an immediate spell-DC increase.

**When the dip loses:** if armor and divine support are already covered, the cost is delayed Wizard power for redundant tools. Bless competes with the Wizard's Concentration; Healing Word uses the Bonus Action and, if slot-cast, the turn's single spell-slot expenditure. Sanctuary is not a blanket license to attack or cast offensive spells while protected. Use medium armor with DEX 14; this example does not choose Protector or assume low-STR heavy armor has no speed cost. Buy armor/shield separately before relying on their AC.

## D — Revised Bladesinger without an armor dip

**Point buy:** STR 8, DEX 15, CON 14, INT 15, WIS 10, CHA 8. Criminal increases INT by 2 and DEX by 1, for **8 / 16 / 14 / 17 / 10 / 8**. Human supplies **Tough** and Perception; Wizard supplies Arcana and Investigation. This is a 27-point build, not the CON-16 array from A/B with extra DEX added.

| Character level | Class level | Highest Wizard spell | Fixed advancement / payoff |
|---:|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Wizard 1 | 1 | Alert, Tough, Wizard casting/rituals/recovery |
| 2 | Wizard 2 | 1 | Arcana Expertise |
| 3 | Bladesinger 3 | 2 | Bladesong; eligible melee-weapon proficiency/focus; Acrobatics proficiency |
| 4 | Bladesinger 4 | 2 | War Caster: INT 18 |
| 5 | Bladesinger 5 | 3 | Memorize Spell; level-3 spells |
| 6 | Bladesinger 6 | 3 | Extra Attack with one eligible Wizard cantrip substitution |
| 7 | Bladesinger 7 | 4 | First level-4 spells |
| 8 | Bladesinger 8 | 4 | ASI: INT 20 |
| 9 | Bladesinger 9 | 5 | First level-5 spells |
| 10 | Bladesinger 10 | 5 | Song of Defense; fifth Wizard cantrip |
| 11 | Bladesinger 11 | 6 | First level-6 spells |
| 12 | Bladesinger 12 | 6 | Resilient (CON): CON 15 and CON-save proficiency |
| 13 | Bladesinger 13 | 7 | First level-7 spells |
| 14 | Bladesinger 14 | 7 | Song of Victory: eligible spell → Bonus Action weapon attack |
| 15 | Bladesinger 15 | 8 | First level-8 spells |
| 16 | Bladesinger 16 | 8 | ASI: CON 16 and DEX 17 |
| 17 | Bladesinger 17 | 9 | First level-9 spells |
| 18 | Bladesinger 18 | 9 | Spell Mastery: Magic Missile and Invisibility |
| 19 | Bladesinger 19 | 9 | Boon of Dimensional Travel: DEX 18 |
| 20 | Bladesinger 20 | 9 | Signature Spells: Counterspell and Dispel Magic |

**Cantrips:** start Ray of Frost, Minor Illusion, and True Strike; add Mind Sliver at Wizard 4 and Mage Hand at 10. Follow the shared spellbook table, with **no Savant additions**. A Rapier is a suitable one-handed proficient weapon after Wizard 3; an ordinary Dagger already works before that. True Strike still requires its actual weapon component. The build does not assume Booming Blade, a particular magic weapon, or a casting workaround from an older subclass.

**The current Bladesong:** activate as a Bonus Action with **no armor and no Shield**. It lasts one minute, has INT-modifier uses per Long Rest (three initially, five at INT 20), and Arcane Recovery restores one expended use. While active, it adds INT to AC and concentration saves, adds 10 feet of Speed, and permits INT attacks/damage with proficient weapons. Incapacitated, donning armor/Shield, or making a two-handed weapon attack ends it. A second ability that adds the same named feature does not grant another copy.

**AC example:** Mage Armor with DEX 16 starts at AC 16; Bladesong at Wizard 3 adds INT +3 for **AC 19**. At INT 20 / DEX 18, those values are **AC 17 inactive / AC 22 active**, before Shield or magic items. The Mage Armor spell is not worn armor. This is an active mode, not permanent AC 22, and saving throws/area damage still threaten the character.

**Combat decision:** use a decisive control spell when it will prevent more enemy actions than attacking would remove. When a major Concentration effect is already running, Extra Attack provides a lower-slot-cost follow-up: one weapon attack plus one eligible action-time Wizard cantrip. Song of Victory checks the spell's Action casting time, so an eligible cantrip substituted into Extra Attack can trigger its Bonus Action attack; the same Bonus Action cannot also start Bladesong or cast Misty Step. It is one additional attack, not a second Attack action. A melee focus does not waive expensive materials or every Somatic requirement; War Caster and a free hand still matter.

**Reaction budget:** Song of Defense reduces damage by five times the expended slot level, but requires active Bladesong and the Reaction. Compare it with preventing an attack via Shield or interrupting a spell; do not spend the same Reaction twice. This build is a Wizard with a weapon option, not proof that entering melee is always optimal.

Source: [published revised Bladesinger](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#BladesingerWizard). Tasha's light-armor permission and its older Song of Victory are not part of this build.

**Build B substitution:** learn **Grease instead of Mage Armor** at Wizard 1 because armor already handles the base-AC job. The other builds use Mage Armor while unarmored. Starting Wizard cantrips for A/B/C: Ray of Frost, Mind Sliver, Minor Illusion; add Mage Hand at Wizard 4 and Prestidigitation at Wizard 10. D uses its stated weapon-oriented cantrip set instead. A Wizard can replace one Wizard cantrip after a Long Rest; Artificer's separate cantrip replacement is likewise separate from Wizard progression.

The deliberately late Invisibility/Phantom Steed entries are a consequence of the no-copy budget, not a low assessment of those spells. Earlier access is often worth copying or trading a situational choice. If safe travel dominates at Wizard 5, for example, learn Phantom Steed instead of Fireball and acquire Fireball later. Do not add a third free spell to that level-up.

These are **extra book entries**, not extra daily preparations. At Wizard 3 choose the first pair; the later additions occur when the Wizard class itself unlocks a new spell-slot level, not when multiclass slots advance early. D's Bladesinger does not have Savant and gains none of these extras.

### Example daily preparation at Wizard 5

The base count is **nine**, regardless of INT modifier: Shield, Mage Armor, Feather Fall, Web, Misty Step, Mirror Image, Suggestion, Hypnotic Pattern, Fireball. Build B substitutes Magic Missile for Mage Armor. After C gains armor, make the same substitution when Mage Armor has no useful target.

Find Familiar, Detect Magic, and Unseen Servant remain available as rituals from the book through Ritual Adept without taking one of those nine preparations. Ritual casting takes its extra time, requires reading the book, and does not waive materials. Magic Initiate and Cleric/Artificer preparations are separate; they do not consume these nine Wizard choices.

At Wizard 9 a fourteen-spell control preparation can be: Shield, Mage Armor, Web, Misty Step, Mirror Image, Hypnotic Pattern, Fireball, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Dimension Door, Polymorph, Arcane Eye, Wall of Force, Synaptic Static. Apply the same armored substitution. This is a broad baseline, not a command to concentrate on several spells at once. See [Prepared Loadouts](/library/tactics/prepared-loadouts/) for mission-specific changes.

**Spell Mastery and Signature Spells:** the selected Mastery spells have an Action casting time; Shield and Misty Step are not eligible in 2024. Their free castings still have normal components, casting times, and any Concentration requirement. Signature Spells grants a separate free level-3 casting of each selected spell per Short/Long Rest, and both are always prepared separately. It is not unlimited Counterspell, and using a free Counterspell still needs its trigger and Reaction.

## Expanded-source replacements

The examples deliberately make their claims testable with a narrow source set. With other books allowed, compare alternatives rather than mixing features from different printings:

- **Necromancer** needs legal Undead access and book-in-hand handling; its minion support does not remove Concentration or control-renewal costs.
- **Conjurer** reaches summon-defense and split-summon breakpoints at Wizard 6/10/14; any dip shifts those dates in exchange for that dip's actual package.
- **The Iron Mind** or **Magic School Mastery** can replace Dimensional Travel at the actual Epic-Boon milestone. Recalculate the ability point and the specific defensive/off-list spell benefit.
- **Revised Bladesinger** has an unarmored Bladesong mode in every supported structure; do not attach the armored examples' AC assumptions to active Bladesong. Artificer/Fighter CON saves or Cleric support remain distinct alternatives.
- **Chronurgy** retains legacy feature wording and has no revised Savant additions. The supported Chronurgy spell routes keep its Dunamancy permission and class-level timing explicit.
- **Abjurer, Diviner, and Illusionist** use their 2024 PHB features rather than mixed older-school packages. Optional school Adept feats modify a legal feat schedule; they are not additional subclass levels.
