**Ranking assumptions**

This compares the listed 2024 core species for a general Wizard, not every allowed expansion or every campaign. Human is a flexible default, not strictly better than Gnome against mental saves or Goliath when slotless teleportation decides an encounter. Evaluate the actual party, level, subclass, and allowed sources before following the letter rank.

</aside>

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**Dwarf Stonecunning is activated, not action-free**

The Dwarf's HP, poison defense, and Darkvision are passive. Stonecunning costs a Bonus Action, gives 60-foot Tremorsense for 10 minutes while on or touching stone, and has Proficiency Bonus uses per Long Rest. Tremorsense does not itself count as sight for a spell that requires a target you can see. [Dwarf rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-origins#Dwarf), [Tremorsense](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#Tremorsense).

</aside>

|Tier|Species|Best variant / option|Wizard value, alternatives, and caution|
|---|---|---|---|
|S|Human|Small or Medium as needed|Best general choice. The extra Origin feat, Heroic Inspiration, and a free skill create the strongest flexible package. Small helps mounts and tight spaces; Medium avoids size restrictions.|
|A+|Gnome|Forest Gnome|Gnomish Cunning protects every INT, WIS, and CHA save. Forest is the clean general option; Rock is a reasonable crafting or utility alternative but overlaps more with Wizard magic.|
|A+|Elf|Wood Elf|Best general Elf: 35-foot Speed and Pass without Trace strengthen scouting, initiative setup, and small-party stealth. High Elf rises for Bladesinger or flexible arcane utility; Drow is reserved for darkness-focused campaigns.|
|A|Dwarf|Standard 2024 Dwarf|Extra HP every level, poison defense, and 120-foot Darkvision provide passive durability. Stonecunning separately costs a Bonus Action and grants 60-foot Tremorsense for 10 minutes on or touching stone, PB uses per Long Rest; it is not sight.|
|A|Goliath|Cloud Giant ancestry|Cloud's Jaunt supplies repeatable slotless teleportation and excellent positioning. Stone ancestry is the defensive alternative, but its Reaction competes with Shield, Counterspell, and subclass reactions.|
|A-|Orc|Standard 2024 Orc|Adrenaline Rush gives emergency movement and temporary HP; Relentless Endurance prevents one collapse. Particularly strong when a two-player party has nobody available to rescue the Wizard.|
|A-|Aasimar|Heavenly Wings revelation|Two resistances, emergency healing, and concentration-free flight give broad small-party utility. The fear-based revelation is the alternative when close-range disruption matters more than mobility.|
|A-|Halfling|Standard 2024 Halfling|Rerolling a 1 on a D20 Test protects a wide range of important checks, attacks, and saves. Fear-save Advantage and creature-assisted hiding support scouting. The reroll must be used, and hiding still takes its action and remaining requirements.|
|B/A, flight route|Dragonborn|Ancestry matching likely damage|Resistance, Darkvision, and ten-minute slotless flight from level 5 can be excellent when vertical terrain matters. Flight is once per Long Rest. The CON-based breath consumes an attack within the Attack action; a normal Wizard does not simply append it to a Magic action.|
|B, resistance route|Tiefling|Infernal for Fire; Chthonic for Necrotic; Abyssal for Poison|Choose the resistance that fits the adventure, with INT for lineage spellcasting. Always-prepared lineage spells and their free castings are useful, but many overlap Wizard access. Darkness is not automatic permission to see through it, and Hellish Rebuke competes with defensive Reactions.|

## Timing and source boundaries

- Human's extra choice is an **Origin feat**, not War Caster or another unrestricted General feat.
- Gnome's mental-save Advantage is not limited to magic. It does not become extra dice when another source already supplies Advantage.
- Wood Elf gains Longstrider at character 3 and Pass without Trace at 5; High Elf gains Misty Step at 5. These are not level-1 benefits. Pass without Trace still competes for Concentration.
- Cloud Goliath's teleport uses a Bonus Action and PB uses per Long Rest. Orc's Adrenaline Rush instead refreshes on **Short or Long Rest**, while Relentless Endurance refreshes only on a Long Rest.
- Aasimar chooses a revelation **each time it transforms**, from character level 3, rather than locking one forever. The transformation lasts one minute and is once per Long Rest. Inner Radiance's area can also damage nearby allies.
- Dragonborn flight arrives at character 5 and ends if Incapacitated; unlike a Fly spell, it does not occupy Concentration. Aasimar's flight is shorter but arrives earlier.

These entries cover all ten core 2024 species, not every setting or legacy lineage. Species abilities do not replace the background's ability increases.
