PARTY-GAP RULE · Do not duplicate a skill merely because it ranks highly. After Arcana, cover the party's missing information and scouting jobs before adding a second specialist to a role already solved.

DEFAULT ORDER · Start with Arcana and Perception; add Stealth next, then choose Investigation or Insight according to the party gap. Optional B-tier skills are campaign or route upgrades, not mandatory taxes.

| Tier | Skill                  | Why it matters                                                                                                                                                                 | When to prioritize                                                                                                                               |
| ---- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| S    | Arcana                 | The Wizard's signature knowledge skill: spells, magical effects, rituals, items, planar phenomena, and magical research.                                                       | Take by default. It is the most natural Intelligence check to own and the hardest Wizard-shaped gap to justify leaving open.                     |
| S    | Perception             | Find ambushes, hidden creatures, environmental danger, and important details before the party spends actions or spell slots fixing the problem.                                | Take by default in small parties. Wisdom may be secondary, but proficiency protects the most frequently consequential awareness check.           |
| S    | Stealth                | Improves scouting, pre-combat positioning, infiltration, and the party's ability to avoid an encounter instead of paying resources for it.                                     | Highest third pick for a small party, an initiative-focused controller, or an Illusionist/infiltration route.                                    |
| A    | Investigation          | Studies clues, traps, ciphers, riddles, and gadgetry; particular illusion spells also explicitly call for Investigation. It does not replace Perception to notice every concealed object. | Prioritize when the campaign rewards deductions and the party lacks another Intelligence investigator. |
| B    | Nature · optional      | Studies terrain, flora, weather, and Beasts, Dragons, Oozes, and Plants. Uses Intelligence to fill a knowledge gap rather than replacing Survival's tracking. | Rises to A in wilderness or monster-research campaigns without another specialist. |
| B    | Religion · optional    | Useful for cults, rites, undead, fiends, celestials, sacred sites, and planar traditions—subjects that often carry tactical consequences.                                      | Rises to A in occult, undead, fiend, divine-conflict, or horror campaigns.                                                                       |
| B    | Deception · optional   | Turns disguises, illusions, false identities, and infiltration plans into a coherent story when magic alone cannot sell the lie.                                               | Rises to A for Illusionists and social infiltrators; falls if Charisma is very low or another party member already owns the role.                |
| B    | Insight · optional     | Searches for a creature's state of mind and supports social judgment; not automatic mind-reading or a guaranteed lie detector. | Prioritize over Investigation when intrigue, uncertain allies, bargains, or social horror are frequent. |
| C/A, campaign-dependent | History · optional | Covers historical events/people, civilizations, wars, and knowledge of Giants and Humanoids. | Rises in political, ancient-ruin, giant, or humanoid-enemy campaigns; do not dismiss it merely because the subject is a creature. |

## Choose the action as well as the skill

| Task | Default 2024 framework | Wizard implication |
|---|---|---|
| Notice a concealed creature or object | **Search:** Wisdom (Perception) | High INT/Investigation alone does not fill the awareness role. |
| Read a creature's state of mind | **Search:** Wisdom (Insight) | A social-information role, not the same job as persuading the creature. |
| Diagnose an ailment/cause of death; detect tracks or food | **Search:** Medicine; Survival respectively | Knowledge of Nature is not automatic tracking or diagnosis proficiency. |
| Recall/analyze information | **Study:** Intelligence with the relevant knowledge skill | Arcana: magic, planes, Aberrations/Constructs/Elementals/Fey/Monstrosities. Religion: divine matters and Celestials/Fiends/Undead. Nature and History cover the creatures listed above. |
| Conceal yourself | **Hide:** DC 15 DEX (Stealth), with the required obscurity/cover and no enemy line of sight | A high Stealth bonus does not waive the positioning prerequisite. Searchers use Perception against the successful Hide total. |
| Convince or mislead a creature | **Influence:** typically CHA with the relevant social skill | A willing request may need no roll; an unwilling creature can simply refuse. A high result is not mind control. |

The DM decides whether a check is warranted and which skill applies; specific spells/features can override the default action or check. **Scholar** only supplies Expertise in an eligible skill already proficient, not a free new proficiency. Across the supported subclasses, social-skill priority depends on the chosen role, ability scores, and party coverage rather than a presumed subclass bonus.
