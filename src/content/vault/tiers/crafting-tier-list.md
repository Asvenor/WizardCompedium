|Project type|Tool lane|Priority|Main restriction|
|---|---|---|---|
|Spell scrolls|Arcana OR Calligrapher's Supplies|S|Gold, full 8-hour inscription days, prepared spell access, Material components, and the exact Spell Scroll table.|
|Flight, storage, or broad wondrous utility|Tinker's Tools when assigned|S if accessible|Arcana, assigned tool, prepared item spells, materials, full workdays, and DM-controlled availability.|
|Rechargeable control or damage item|Woodcarver's Tools when assigned|A / S|Exact item activation, charges, attunement, and user restrictions.|
|Warning / initiative equipment|Smith's or Woodcarver's when assigned|A|Exact item wording, assigned tool, Arcana, spells, materials, and time.|
|Healing potions|Herbalism Kit|B+|Basic Potion of Healing: 25 GP and one 8-hour day, kit proficiency/use; no Arcana requirement. Higher grades use their own applicable recipe.|
|Resistance or environment potion|Alchemist's Supplies / relevant rule|B|Only exceptional when the next threat is predictable.|
|High-end spell-storage ring|Jeweler's Tools when assigned|A effect / C proficiency|High cost, long downtime, item access, exact tool assignment, and attunement.|

Priorities assume the party actually needs the output. A prepared emergency scroll can be more valuable than another redundant permanent item; a free attunement slot can be more valuable than a small item upgrade. Compare [Crafting](/library/items/crafting/) for costs and [Worked equipment comparisons](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#worked-equipment-comparisons) for complete loadouts. General personal crafting and Bastion facility permissions are different systems: see [Choose production by the actual bottleneck](/library/bastions/bastion-crafting/#choose-production-by-the-actual-bottleneck).
