These ratings are optimization judgments. [2024 DMG crafting](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/treasure#CraftingMagicItems) requires Arcana plus the assigned tool proficiency for ordinary magic-item projects: Woodcarver's Tools for rods, staffs, and wands; Jeweler's Tools for rings; Tinker's Tools or the underlying item's tool for wondrous items. [Healing-potion brewing and scroll scribing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#CraftingEquipment) have separate rules.

|Tier|Tool / access|Wizard reasoning|
|---|---|---|
|S|Arcana proficiency|Supports spell research and scroll copying; also qualifies for scribing under the separate rule. This is a skill, not a tool proficiency.|
|A|Woodcarver's Tools|A route to relevant wooden spell platforms when the specific crafting assignment and project access fit.|
|A|Tinker's Tools|Broad wondrous-item opportunity under the relevant crafting table; exact assignments control.|
|A|Herbalism Kit|An affordable healing-potion project with a separate rule, useful when downtime and materials exist.|
|A, dungeon gap|Thieves' Tools|Locks and mechanical traps without a spell slot; rises when no other party member covers the role. Proficiency does not make every obstacle unlockable or make the work silent.|
|B|Calligrapher's Supplies|Scribing access if Arcana does not already supply it, plus document work; does not automatically discount spell copying.|
|B|Smith's Tools|Rises for an armored party with a concrete armor or weapon project.|
|C|Jeweler's Tools|Valuable for a funded jewelry project; often delayed by wealth and attunement competition.|

## Tool checks are not a second proficiency bonus

When a check uses a proficient tool, add PB. If an applicable proficient skill is also used for that check, the 2024 rule supplies **Advantage**, not a second PB. Owning tools does not grant proficiency, and two proficiencies do not guarantee that both apply to a task. Tool-specific Utilize actions, crafting requirements, materials, and time still control. [2024 tools](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#Tools).

For the revised Artificer, tool access is also a casting requirement: the relevant proficient tool focus must be held in hand. That is different from a Wizard simply carrying a tool kit. See [Multiclass Spell Timing](/library/character/multiclass-spell-timing/) and the Artificer variants in [Spell Progression Hub](/library/character/spell-progression-hub/); this requirement applies regardless of Wizard subclass.
