Rankings are optimization judgments using current [Monster Manual animals](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/animals). The listed level is the **target's minimum level when it has no CR**, not the Wizard's level to learn the spell. Polymorph itself requires a level-4 slot. See [Polymorph](/library/creatures/polymorph/) and [Form Legality Guide](/library/creatures/form-legality-guide/) for temporary HP, Concentration and transformation limits.

**Ally default (optimization judgment):** Giant Ape is the strongest general 2024 combat body among the listed options at target level 7 because it combines the largest listed temporary-HP buffer with climbing, melee output, and a rechargeable ranged option. Tyrannosaurus Rex wins when its melee riders and reach matter more; Mammoth wins when a charge lane or allied Prone setup exists.

## Hostile Polymorph Forms Tier List

|Tier|Form|Containment job|Why choose it|Escape / failure warning|
|---|---|---|---|---|
|S|Rat|Best default containment form|Tiny, 1 HP, ordinary in many locations, and easy to place in a prepared smooth container.|Speed/Climb 20 and Agile make open-space pinning unreliable.|
|A|Frog|Low-offense wet-area option|Tiny, 1 HP, amphibious, and little offensive threat.|Standing Leap and Swim 20 create escape routes near gaps or water.|
|A|Lizard|Compact dry-area option|Tiny, 2 HP, slow, and easy to recognize and contain.|Spider Climb allows wall and ceiling movement; the lid must actually close.|
|B|Cat or Weasel|Socially inconspicuous fallback|Natural in settlements and easy to explain if the transformation is witnessed only briefly.|Fast, climbing, and stealthy; poor choices when physical containment is the plan.|
|C|Spider or Octopus|Narrow terrain trick|Small profile or compression can solve a very specific placement problem.|Ceiling movement, Web Walker, Compression, swimming, and water dependence add failure modes.|
|D|Bat, Hawk, Owl, or Raven|Avoid for hostile control|Flight can matter only in a sealed space designed around it.|In an ordinary room or outdoors, flight gives the target the cleanest escape route.|
