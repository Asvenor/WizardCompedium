**Feat ranking checkpoints**

**Boon of Fate** refreshes on rolling Initiative or finishing a Short/Long Rest and does not consume a Reaction. Repeated separate encounters therefore refresh it; a long single encounter or many important rolls between refreshes limits it. **Resilient** must select a save proficiency you lack: Wizard starters already have WIS, while Artificer/Fighter starters already have CON. **Mage Slayer** also protects against nonspell mental threats through Guarded Mind; that benefit is not restricted to enemy casters, but its ability increase is STR/DEX rather than INT. [2024 PHB feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats). The expanded boons use [Heroes of Faerûn](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/frhof/character-options#EpicBoonFeats); ratings are analysis, not official rankings.

## Arcana Unleashed — Origin feats

These are additional published options, subject to the campaign's allowed sources. Ratings are Wizard-focused analysis, not official rankings. An excellent feat can still be attached to a background whose ability increases are poor for a Wizard; compare [Background Tier List](/library/tiers/background-tier-list/). None grants extra feat selections by itself.

| Priority | Feat | Wizard payoff and limit |
|---|---|---|
| S/A | **Arcane Infiltrator** | Bonus Action Dodge PB times per Long Rest is powerful when attacks threaten Concentration; it competes with teleporting/commands. Also learns Friends, not a consequence-free social solution. |
| A | **Arcane Omens** | Guidance plus a Reaction d4 after a failed save by self or a visible creature within 30 feet, PB uses per Long Rest. Broad protection, but competes with Shield/Counterspell. |
| A | **Portal Jumper** | Chosen Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant Resistance and a visible 15-foot teleport costing 15 feet of movement; once per turn and PB uses per Long Rest. No spare movement means no step; it is not a universal grapple escape when Speed is 0. |
| A, familiar route | **Familiar Friend** | Slot/material-free Find Familiar once per Long Rest; familiar gains 2 × character level HP. A nearby familiar can provide Advantage on proficient-skill checks PB times per Long Rest. Strong companion durability, not automatic attack permission. |
| A/B | **Transmuted Anatomy** | +5 Speed, protection against forced shape-shifting, and Reaction d4 after failed CON saves PB times per Long Rest. Helps Concentration but consumes the Reaction and does not grant proficiency. |
| B, social route | **Arcane Eloquence** | Vicious Mockery and +1d4 to CHA Deception/Intimidation/Persuasion checks. Supports a social role regardless of Wizard subclass; social checks still cannot compel impossible cooperation. |
| B/C | **Arcane Artist** | Minor Illusion plus one ally's Heroic Inspiration per Long Rest after casting an Illusion spell. Useful, but far less party-wide Inspiration throughput than Musician when rests permit. |
| C, support niche | **Arcane Safeguard** | Resistance cantrip, including PB Bonus Action castings per Long Rest; Help on an ally's ability check adds PB Temporary HP. Resistance still requires Concentration. |
| C, knowledge niche | **Arcane Undertaker** | A Cleric/Wizard Necromancy cantrip, d4 to History/Medicine, and once-per-Long-Rest self Inspiration from stabilizing via Help. Does not improve an Undead army directly. |
| C | **Arcane Overload** | Fire Bolt and PB added to one Evocation damage roll once per Long Rest. Much narrower benefit than repeatable defense or control support. |

Source: [Arcana Unleashed Origin feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#OriginFeats).

## Arcana Unleashed — General feats

Compare complete feat routes in [Arcana Unleashed Builds](/library/character/arcana-unleashed-builds/) and the casting limits in [Arcana Unleashed Combinations](/library/tactics/arcana-unleashed-combinations/).

The eight **school Adepts** require level 4+ and Spellcasting/Pact Magic, add +1 INT/WIS/CHA (maximum 20), and always prepare their listed spells as the specified slot levels become available. They do not provide free castings or free costly components. The chosen ability matters for benefits explicitly using its modifier.

| Priority | Candidate | Decision-changing benefit | Limitation / opportunity cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| S/A, specialist | **Illusion Adept** | Slot-cast Illusions can omit V/S and unpriced, unconsumed materials; checks to discern the Wizard's illusions have Disadvantage. | Does not impose Disadvantage on saving throws. Costly/consumed materials remain; damage or physical interaction can still expose an illusion under its own rules. |
| S/A, specialist | **Enchantment Adept** | The same component omission for slot-cast Enchantments; supports unobtrusive control. | Not a blanket permission to cast every school silently or ignore consequences stated in the spell. |
| A | **Abjuration Adept** | Slot-cast Abjuration gives self or a visible creature within 30 feet Temporary HP equal to twice slot level; expanded preparations include Lesser Restoration and Mass Cure Wounds. | Temporary HP do not add together. Value depends on repeated protective casting and party healing gaps. |
| A | **Conjuration Adept** | Adds the selected ability modifier to CON saves for Concentration on Conjuration spells; expands access with options such as Entangle. | School-specific. War Caster/Resilient protect a broader spellbook; the revised Conjurer's level-10 damage protection can reduce this rider's value. |
| A/B | **Divination Adept** | Reaction Advantage/Disadvantage on a visible creature's D20 Test within 60 feet; recharges on Long Rest or a slot-cast Divination spell. | Choose when the test is made; it is not an after-failure reroll. Ritual casting without a slot does not recharge it. |
| B, blast route | **Evocation Adept** | Once per turn, spend up to two unexpended Hit Dice to add their rolls to one damage roll of a cast Evocation spell. | Consumes a finite recovery resource; no CON modifier is added by this rider. The spell still determines who takes the damage. |
| B/C | **Necromancy Adept** | Slot-cast Necromancy can spend up to two Hit Dice to heal self by their total + slot level; adds Raise Dead access at the required slot level. | Not healing without resource cost, and costly spell materials are still required. |
| B | **Transmutation Adept** | On-turn slot-cast Transmutation adds 5 × slot level to Speed for that turn; useful always-prepared buff/control suite. | The speed expires at turn end and adds no extra action. Much of its list already overlaps Wizard options. |
| A/B | **Spell Resistant** | +1 DEX/CON; chosen Necrotic/Psychic/Radiant/Thunder Resistance, plus d6 to a failed save against a spell/magical effect PB times per Long Rest. | The save correction uses no Reaction, but does not apply to every mundane CON save. No INT increase. |
| A/B, infiltration | **Spell Subterfuge** | +1 mental ability; after an action-time slot spell, Bonus Action Dash **and** Hide a limited number of times. Can preserve Hide's invisibility through verbal spells/spell attacks when ending behind sufficient cover. | Actual Hide requirements still apply. Cover must be Three-Quarters or Total for the special retention; not general invisible casting in the open. |
| A/B, party gap | **Magic Connoisseur** | +1 mental ability; with Magic Initiate, learn another level-1 and level-2 spell from its same list, each with one free casting per Long Rest plus slot casting. | Requires Magic Initiate. Compare the exact missing Cleric/Druid utility against merely duplicating Wizard access. |
| B, familiar route | **Warlike Familiar** | +1 any ability, Battle Familiar prepared with one free cast per Long Rest, and familiar Reaction AC support equal to PB for a creature within 5 feet. | Requires Familiar Friend; positioning and the familiar's Reaction constrain defense. It does not grant unlimited reactions. |
| B, familiar route | **Otherworldly Familiar** | +1 any ability; familiar gains a chosen resistance and can phase through objects as difficult terrain. | Requires Familiar Friend. Ending inside an object ejects it to its last empty space; this is not a way to remain protected inside a wall. |
| B/C, familiar route | **Elemental Familiar** | +1 any ability; chosen elemental resistance and repeatable Bonus Action / familiar Reaction pulse within 120 feet. | Requires Familiar Friend. 5-foot emanation can hit allies; failed DEX save takes 2d4 and knocks Medium-or-smaller creatures Prone, with no damage on success. |
| C, healing niche | **Soothing Familiar** | +1 any ability; nearby allied healers treat healing-die 1s/2s as 3s. | Requires Familiar Friend; allies must be in its 5-foot emanation and familiar within 120 feet. Position the healer, not automatically just the healing recipient. |

Sources and full spell tables: [General feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#GeneralFeats). Component omission prevents a casting from meeting Counterspell's component requirement only when **all** required components are actually omitted; it does not negate every way an ongoing effect can be detected or ended.

## Epic Boon Tier List
### 2024 core Epic Boons

| Tier | Epic Boon            | Wizard verdict                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | When it rises or falls                                                                                                                                                         |
| ---- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| S    | Night Spirit         | Premier damage defense in dim light/darkness; no slot, Concentration, or Reaction cost. Invisibility is a useful positioning/reset button but ends after your next action, Bonus Action, or Reaction.                                            | S whenever darkness is reliably available. Falls only when bright environments or Psychic/Radiant damage dominate.                                                             |
| S    | Dimensional Travel   | After every Attack or Magic action, teleport up to 30 feet to a visible space. Repositions after casting without a slot, Bonus Action, or limited-use counter.                                                                                   | Best universal core alternative when lighting cannot support Night Spirit; exceptional for controllers and ranged Wizards.                                                     |
| A    | Fate                 | A 2d4 correction after a D20 Test succeeds or fails within 60 feet, with no Reaction cost; refreshes when you roll Initiative or finish a Short/Long Rest. | Rises for decisive-roll reliability; limited in a long single encounter or many important rolls between refreshes, not simply a chain of separate encounters. |
| A    | Recovery             | Large self-healing reserve plus a once-per-Long-Rest recovery from dropping to 0 HP. Strong independent survival that does not compete for Concentration.                                                                                        | Rises in two-player, attrition, or low-healing parties; falls when the party already prevents damage extremely well.                                                           |
| B    | Combat Prowess       | Turn one missed attack into a hit, then wait until the start of your next turn to reuse it. This is not a separate use on every creature's turn. | A for Bladesingers and attack-roll spell plans; C for save/no-save controllers. |
| B    | Fortitude            | A large Hit Point increase is always useful on the class's smallest Hit Die, but it adds durability rather than new control, positioning, or save protection.                                                                                    | Rises for Wizard Tank and tiny parties; falls when Night Spirit or Recovery prevents more effective damage.                                 |
| B    | Speed                | Major movement increase plus Bonus Action disengage/grapple escape. Reliable, but Dimensional Travel usually gives a caster safer movement with less action conflict.                                                                            | A for Bladesinger/skirmisher movement builds; C when Bonus Actions are crowded.                                                                                                |
| B    | Spell Recall         | Each level 1–4 slot cast has only a matching-d4 chance not to be expended. Useful across a long day, but random and irrelevant to level 5–9 slots.                                                                                               | Rises when Shield, Counterspell, and other low-level slots are cast repeatedly; falls in short adventuring days. |
| C    | Energy Resistance    | Two selected resistances are useful, but the redirection consumes the Reaction and the coverage is far narrower than Night Spirit.                                                                                                               | Rises when two known damage types dominate and Reaction pressure is low.                                                                                                       |
| C    | Skill                | Broad proficiency patches party gaps, but a level-19 Wizard already has spells, tools, allies, and chosen expertise for the most important checks.                                                                                               | B in a two-character investigation campaign with severe skill gaps; otherwise opportunity cost is too high.                                                                    |
| C    | Truesight            | Always-on 60-foot Truesight is clean and convenient, but high-level Wizards can often obtain the function through spells, items, or preparation.                                                                                                 | Rises in relentless illusion, shapechanger, and invisibility campaigns where the spell tax is constant.                                                                        |
| D    | Irresistible Offense | Its ability increase is STR/DEX. Resistance bypass covers all Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage you deal, not only weapons; the extra damage requires a natural 20 on an attack roll. | B for a physical-damage or attack-heavy plan facing Resistance; low priority for a typical save-based controller. |

### Arcana Unleashed Epic Boons

| Priority | Boon | Wizard verdict | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| S, Concentration centerpiece | **The Iron Mind** | +1 any ability up to 30. While maintaining Concentration, only replacing it with another Concentration spell/effect, becoming Petrified or Unconscious, or dying ends it. | Does not permit two Concentration effects, prevent damage/death, or keep a spell past its duration. Unlike ordinary Concentration protection, Stunned/Paralyzed alone no longer end it; their other restrictions still apply. |
| S, access / endurance | **Magic School Mastery** | +1 INT/WIS/CHA up to 30. Choose a school: one matching level-1 spell **on the class list** becomes always prepared and slot/component-free; one matching spell of level 7 or lower is always prepared, with one slot-free cast per Long Rest and additional slot casts. | Level-1 free casting retains the spell's normal casting time. The higher spell is not restricted to the class list, but its components remain required. Repeatable only with a different school. Compare an at-will Shield route against a valuable off-list higher spell; the two must share the chosen school. |
| A/B, damage-control | **Erupting Spellpower** | +1 INT/WIS/CHA up to 30. A slot-cast damage spell can raise damage-die 1s/2s to 3s and knock creatures damaged by it Prone; refreshes on Initiative or Short/Long Rest. | No separate save is added for the Prone rider, but immunity still matters. It can also knock allies Prone if the spell damages them; prone enemies disadvantage distant attack rolls against them. |

The two casting boons additionally require Spellcasting/Pact Magic; all three require level 19+. Compare them alongside the core and Heroes of Faerûn choices rather than treating an older tier winner as universal.
