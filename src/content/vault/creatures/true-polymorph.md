**Choose the transformation mode first**

**Creature → creature:** form CR cannot exceed the target's CR or level; the transformed target cannot speak or cast spells, including spells in the new stat block. **Object → creature:** CR 9 maximum, and the creature cannot be larger than the nonmagical, unworn, uncarried source object. Concentrating for the full hour makes the transformation last until dispelled, but ends your control of an object-created creature. See [True Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#TruePolymorph) and [Form Legality Guide](/library/creatures/form-legality-guide/).

</aside>

## Object-created shortlist: value without assuming permanent obedience

These candidates pass the **CR 9 ceiling**; the source object must also be at least their size. The spell is still a level-9 spell. Creature → creature is a different mode and cannot borrow this mode's permission to create a spellcasting ally.

| Candidate | CR / size | Useful specialization | What prevents an automatic pick |
|---|---|---|---|
| Young Silver Dragon | 9 / Large | Flight, 168 HP, three Rends; replace one Rend with a 30-foot Paralyzing Breath | Breath first Incapacitates on a failed CON save, then requires a second failed save for Paralysis; Cone can catch allies |
| Clay Golem | 9 / Large | Physical resistance, three damage immunities and Acid Absorption | Bloodied can trigger Berserk and attacks on the nearest creature; not a safe unattended ally |
| Treant | 9 / Huge | Siege work and up to two animated trees | Fire vulnerability, Huge source-object requirement, existing trees needed; animated trees must remain within 120 feet |
| Invisible Stalker | 6 / Large | Invisible flight, one-inch Air Form, Force damage and Vortex | Not automatically Hidden; Vortex's CON save/grapple gates the Verbal-component shutdown |
| Couatl | 4 / Medium | Truesight 120, telepathy, strong defenses and component-free support spells | Smaller HP pool; actual spells retain casting-time/concentration rules unless expressly modified |
| Shield Guardian | 7 / Large | Regeneration and an amulet-based protection/spell-storage package | Do not assume transformation creates the separate control amulet or automatically binds a usable one |

Longer existence is not longer control. If the full hour is completed, the created creature's later attitude depends on how it was treated; binding, bargaining and campaign consequences remain separate problems. Constructs, Plants and Dragons cannot be Planar Bound just because the creature-creation step was legal.

Sources: [True Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#TruePolymorph) · [current Monster Manual](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024).

## Creature-to-Creature Specialists

These exceed the **CR 9 object → creature cap**. Guardian Naga requires a target with CR/level 10 or higher; Taisus requires 19 or higher. This mode does **not** permit their Spellcasting actions. For spellcasting utility, compare [Shapechange](/library/creatures/shapechange/) or independently acquired allies instead.

Sources: [Guardian Naga](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/monsters-g#GuardianNaga) · [Taisus](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-6-magical-creatures#TaisusStatBlock).

## Creature → Object

> Creature → Object does not require a creature statblock library.
>
> Use this mode for transformation, containment, transport, storage, infiltration, or other situations where turning the creature into an object solves the problem.
