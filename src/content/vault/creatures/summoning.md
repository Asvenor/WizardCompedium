## Choose the job before the creature

For worked Conjurer twin-summon and Necromancer two-companion packages, see [Arcana Unleashed Builds](/library/character/arcana-unleashed-builds/). Their subclass and spell-school restrictions matter; they are not universal bonuses to every summoned creature.

These **2024 PHB** spells create a separate allied creature. Their spirit blocks, scaling and command rules are in the linked cards; do not substitute a Monster Manual creature with a similar name. Rankings below are situational optimization judgments.

| Spell / minimum slot | Reusable component | Prefer it for | Main limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Summon Fey](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-fey-spell/) / 3 | 300+ GP gilded flower | Force damage, flight and Bonus Action teleportation; select a mood for advantage, charm or temporary darkness | Tricksy grants no special sight through its darkness; charm ends on damage |
| [Summon Undead](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-undead-spell/) / 3 | 300+ GP gilded skull | Ghostly's on-hit Frightened; Skeletal ranged pressure; Putrid's Poisoned-to-Paralyzed sequence | Match the form to condition immunities; Putrid's aura also threatens allies |
| [Summon Aberration](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-aberration-spell/) / 4 | 400+ GP platinum-inlaid vial with tentacle and eyeball | Beholderkin's ranged hovering attacks or Slaad's healing denial | Psychic immunity defeats Beholderkin's damage; Mind Flayer's aura can hurt allies |
| [Summon Construct](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-construct-spell/) / 4 | 400+ GP lockbox | A durable melee body, Stone's movement/Opportunity Attack suppression, or Clay's retaliation | Stone does not disable all Reactions; limited reach and mobility |
| [Summon Elemental](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-elemental-spell/) / 4 | 400+ GP gold-inlaid vial containing the four listed materials | Air mobility, Earth physical resistance, or terrain-specific movement | Earth has Burrow, not the Monster Manual Earth Elemental's Earth Glide |
| [Summon Dragon](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-dragon-spell/) / 5 | 500+ GP object engraved with a dragon image | Flight, multiple damage resistances, one resistance shared with the caster, and a breath in each Multiattack | Position its Cone away from allies; the breath stays 2d6 when upcast |
| [Summon Fiend](/library/spells/spell-cards/summon-fiend-spell/) / 6 | 600+ GP bloody vial | Devil's ranged flight, Yugoloth teleporting attacks, or high sustained pressure | Demon's death/spell-ending explosion can hit allies; Magic Resistance protects the spirit, not your Concentration |

All seven require **Concentration, up to 1 hour**. They take a turn immediately after yours on your Initiative count and obey verbal commands without an action from you. Without commands they Dodge and avoid danger. Protect the caster as carefully as the spirit: losing Concentration removes the summon regardless of its remaining HP.

## The useful upcast breakpoints

Their Multiattack uses **half the slot level, rounded down**: slot 3 gives one attack, 4–5 give two, 6–7 give three, and 8–9 give four. Odd slots still improve the stated AC, HP and damage formulas, but do not add another attack. Compare spending slot 4 on Fey/Undead with starting a different level-4 summon; there is no automatic rule that the higher base-level spell wins.

Summons reward time on the battlefield. Prefer immediate control when the encounter is decided by stopping an enemy *now*; prefer a summon when repeated attacks, an extra body, mobility or a particular rider can matter across several turns. Pre-casting saves a combat action only if the remaining duration, components and approaching encounter actually permit it.

## Companion options that do not use Concentration

- [Battle Familiar](/library/spells/spell-cards/battle-familiar-spell/) is the **Arcana Unleashed** option: 1 hour, own Initiative, a reusable 25+ GP diamond, and only **one familiar at a time**. Empowering an existing familiar replaces its stat block and uses temporary HP; it does not add a second familiar.
- [Find Familiar](/library/creatures/find-familiar/) supplies scouting and Help rather than attacks unless another feature expressly permits them.
- [Animate Dead - Necromancer](/library/creatures/animate-dead-necromancer/) creates a different, command-and-control package with corpses and renewed control requirements. It is not interchangeable with Summon Undead.
- [Planar Binding](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/) binds an **existing eligible creature** after a one-hour procedure and a consumed 1,000+ GP jewel; it does not summon the candidate. Undead, Aberration, Construct and Dragon spirits fail its creature-type gate. Even an eligible spirit still needs a legal duration, containment and concentration procedure; see [Binding budget and schedule](/library/advanced/wizard-operations-roadmap/#binding-budget-and-schedule).

The seven spirit blocks have **no Challenge Rating**. Slot level, caster level and XP 0 do not establish CR for transformation eligibility.
