## Pegasus or Nightmare?

| Need | Prefer | Why / limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Fast flight and a lookout | **Pegasus** | Large Celestial, CR 2; walk 60 / fly 90; Perception +6 and passive 16; CHA save +3. It has neither hover nor a special passenger teleport. |
| Ethereal access and Fire protection | **Nightmare** | Large Fiend, CR 3; walk 60 / fly 90 with hover; CHA save +2. It can grant its rider Fire resistance and use an action to move itself plus up to three willing creatures within 5 feet between the Material and Ethereal Planes. |
| Discreet nighttime travel | Usually **Pegasus**, if flight is suitable | Nightmare sheds Bright Light 10 feet plus Dim Light 10 more; it is not an inconspicuous flying horse. |

Neither block lists Magic Resistance. At DC 19 their unmodified Planar Binding failure chances are 75% (Pegasus) and 80% (Nightmare), but that comparison says nothing about acquisition or consent. Negotiating with a willing intelligent mount may be more practical than coercion.

**Binding is not mounted-combat training.** Riding still requires a willing creature at least one size larger than the rider with suitable anatomy. Controlling a mount requires training to accept a rider. The mount's turn, permitted actions, carrying capacity and fall risk still matter; a controlled mount's Dash/Disengage/Dodge action options are not a free way to obtain Nightmare's Ethereal Stride. Its passenger transfer requires willing creatures within 5 feet, not necessarily creatures riding it, and only connects the two named planes.

Use [Planar Binding](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/) for the one-hour containment procedure and [Form Legality Guide](/library/creatures/form-legality-guide/) for any transformation route. A transformation does not create a saddle, harness or pre-existing relationship with a mount.
