Walk 30 ft; Climb 30 ft.  
Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 14.

## Best use — analysis

Ceiling movement, stealth and ranged Web control.

## Restrictions

Web uses a Dexterity save and recharge 5–6; destroying its web ends the restraint. This Large form is not automatically inconspicuous.
