**CR 16 · Large Celestial · AC 19 · HP 262**  
Walk 40 ft; Fly 120 ft (hover).  
Truesight 120 ft.; Passive Perception 21.

## Best use — analysis

Powerful seen Shapechange form for flight, durability and attacks; an actual ally also offers support spells.

## Restrictions

Not legal for object→creature True Polymorph: CR16 exceeds 9. Creature→creature mode blocks spells. Natural Planar Binding eligibility does not make acquisition or the Charisma save easy.
