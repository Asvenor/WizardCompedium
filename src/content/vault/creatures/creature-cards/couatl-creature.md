## Best use — analysis

High utility for an independently acquired or object-created ally: detection, restoration and divination, with strong defenses.

## Restrictions

True Polymorph creature→creature cannot use its spells. Its own Shapechange has printed exceptions and is limited to Beast/Humanoid forms; keep those distinct from the Wizard spell.
