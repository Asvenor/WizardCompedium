## Best use — analysis

Excavation: unlike Earth Glide, Tunneler can leave a passage through solid rock.

## Restrictions

Rock movement is half Burrow Speed; it leaves a 10-foot-diameter tunnel. Gargantuan size, collateral damage and Poison-dependent offense can be liabilities. Not an ordinary binding target.
