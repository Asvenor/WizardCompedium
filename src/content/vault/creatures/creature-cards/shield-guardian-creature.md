## Best use — analysis

Amulet-linked bodyguard with regeneration and a spell-storage feature; promising only after the amulet problem is resolved.

## Restrictions

Constructs are excluded from Shapechange and ordinary Planar Binding. True Polymorph does not explicitly create the separate linked amulet; without it do not assume Bound, protection or storage benefits.
