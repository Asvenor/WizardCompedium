## Pick a legal body for this encounter

The 2024 spell is a **level-4 Concentration spell**, and the form must be a **Beast** with CR no higher than the target's CR (or level if it has no CR). The target keeps its original HP/Hit Dice and creature type, gains the form's HP as temporary HP, and cannot speak or cast spells while transformed. Polymorph ends early when its granted temporary HP run out; these are a buffer, not healing. See [Polymorph - Spell](/library/spells/spell-cards/polymorph-spell/) and [Form Legality Guide](/library/creatures/form-legality-guide/).

| Job | Default comparison | What changes the choice |
|---|---|---|
| General combat at target level 7+ | [Giant Ape](/library/creatures/creature-cards/giant-ape-creature/): 168 temporary HP, climbing, melee and a rechargeable ranged area attack | Huge space requirement and AC 12; Boulder Toss is not an unlimited ranged attack |
| Melee control at target level 8+ | [Tyrannosaurus Rex](/library/creatures/creature-cards/tyrannosaurus-rex-creature/): fast body with restraining Bite | Bite only holds Large or smaller; Tail cannot target the creature it is grappling |
| Lower-CR charge or prone support | Elephant (CR 4), then Mammoth (CR 6) | A charge can supply Prone, but an ally can also set up the Bonus Action Trample |
| Water-edge grappling | Giant Crocodile (CR 5) | Holds breath for 1 hour; not unrestricted underwater breathing |
| Sustained underwater travel | Archelon (CR 4) or [Giant Shark](/library/creatures/creature-cards/giant-shark-creature/) (CR 5) | Archelon is Amphibious with Swim 80; Shark has stronger underwater attack pressure and Blindsight but breathes only water |
| Flight | [Giant Bat](/library/creatures/creature-cards/giant-bat-creature/) (CR 1/4) | Fly 60 and Blindsight 120; Giant Eagle is a **Celestial**, not a legal Beast option |
| Quiet climbing or digging | [Giant Spider](/library/creatures/creature-cards/giant-spider-creature/) (CR 1) or Giant Badger (CR 1/4) | Spider has Spider Climb; Badger's Burrow 10 does not grant rock tunneling |

For an ally with valuable class features or active Concentration, compare the body with what the transformation makes unavailable. An injured, low-resource ally is often a better target than a fully functioning frontliner. A caster can target itself, but the Beast's limitations and the need to maintain the spell still apply.

[Compare friendly and hostile choices](/library/tiers/polymorph-friendly-hostile-tier-list/). Hostile transformation is containment, not defeat: damaging the small form can end the spell and restore the dangerous original creature.

Sources: [Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Polymorph) · [Monster Manual animals](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/animals).
