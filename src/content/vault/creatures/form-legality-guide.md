Choose the spell first, then the creature. A stat block appearing in a gallery is a research candidate, not permission to use every ability or create that creature by every method.

## Rules version matters

This guide uses the current **2024 Player's Handbook on D&D Beyond**, whose transformation wording agrees with SRD 5.2.1. Use the specific spell's rules rather than importing the 2014 versions: [Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Polymorph), [Shapechange](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Shapechange), and [True Polymorph](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#TruePolymorph).

| Spell or mode | Form restriction | Statistics and practical limits |
|---|---|---|
| Polymorph | Beast; form CR ≤ target CR, or target level only if it has no CR | Keeps original HP/Hit Dice and creature type; gains form HP as temporary HP; cannot speak, cast spells, or benefit from melded gear |
| Shapechange | Form CR ≤ your level or CR; you must have seen that sort; no Construct or Undead | Keeps own mental scores, HP/Hit Dice, proficiencies, creature type, communication and Spellcasting feature; first form sets the temporary-HP grant |
| True Polymorph: creature → creature | Form CR ≤ target CR or level | Keeps original HP/Hit Dice, alignment and personality; gains form HP as temporary HP; cannot speak or cast spells; gear melds |
| True Polymorph: object → creature | Nonmagical object, not worn or carried; creature CR ≤ 9 and no larger than the object | Initially Friendly and obeys commands; control ends if the spell lasts more than an hour |
| Planar Binding | Existing Celestial, Elemental, Fey or Fiend | Target must remain within 60 feet for the entire one-hour casting; Charisma save; this spell does not itself summon a creature |
| Find Familiar | Listed familiar form, or another Beast with CR 0 | Familiar uses the chosen statistics but its actual creature type follows Find Familiar; normal familiar cannot attack |

Source: [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), Polymorph pp. 153–154, Shapechange p. 161, True Polymorph p. 170, Planar Binding pp. 152–153, Find Familiar pp. 129–130.

## Temporary HP and concentration

**Polymorph:** the spell ends early if the target has no temporary HP left. Unused temporary HP from the spell vanish when it ends. These are not healing: the target retains its underlying HP.

**Shapechange:** SRD 5.2.1 grants temporary HP when you cast the spell, based on the **first** chosen form. Switching forms with a Magic action does not grant a new pool. The SRD 5.2.1 text does not end the spell when that pool is depleted. Remaining spell-granted temporary HP vanish when the spell ends.

**True Polymorph, creature → creature:** SRD 5.2.1 grants temporary HP and removes any remaining spell-granted temporary HP when the spell ends. Its revised text does not end the spell merely because temporary HP reach zero.

All three require concentration during their stated concentration duration. Temporary HP never stack: use the general temporary-HP rules. Ongoing conditions and other effects normally carry across a transformation unless the specific effect says otherwise.

Source: [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), the spell pages above and Shape-Shifting p. 187.

## Spellcasting and powerful monster features

Do not import the 2014 Shapechange prohibition on gaining a form's Spellcasting into the 2024 spell. The current text explicitly retains your own Spellcasting feature and does not repeat that old exclusion. Check the chosen monster's actual spellcasting, components, casting times and usage limits. A spell listed under a monster's Spellcasting action still keeps a casting time of one minute or longer unless explicitly overridden.

True Polymorph's **creature → creature** mode explicitly prohibits casting spells. A Planetar or Couatl body therefore does not give that transformed creature a usable spell list through this mode. The **object → creature** section does not contain the same prohibition; do not merge the two modes.

Before relying on transformations to refresh limited-use abilities, ask the DM how the exact interaction is resolved. Do the same for unique named NPC forms, created equipment, linked amulets, nested transformations, restoration-after-death traits, and legendary or lair benefits. The catalogue's eligibility flags only check the listed form/type/CR gates; they do not validate those extra interactions.

Source: [SRD 5.2.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf), Shapechange p. 161, True Polymorph p. 170, monster Spellcasting and limited usage p. 257.

## True Polymorph assets

After concentrating for the full hour, the spell lasts until dispelled. That is an ongoing magical transformation, not an instantaneous creation immune to Dispel Magic. In object → creature mode, continued existence and continued obedience are different: the spell explicitly ends your control after an hour.

The [Creature-to-Creature Specialists](/library/creatures/true-polymorph/#creature-to-creature-specialists) gallery includes **Guardian Naga (CR 10)** and **Taisus (CR 19)**. They exceed the object → creature cap. Their creature → creature uses require an eligible target and do not allow spellcasting.

The **Djinni (CR 11)** also exceeds that cap; its presence in the utility gallery does not make it an object → creature option. See the [2024 Monster Manual Djinni](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/monsters-d#Djinni).

The **2024 Giant Eagle is a Celestial, not a Beast**, so it is not a Polymorph form even though an image remains in the Polymorph asset folder. See the [2024 Monster Manual Giant Eagle](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/animals#GiantEagle).

A [Shield Guardian](/library/creatures/creature-cards/shield-guardian-creature/) passes the CR and size gate when the source object is Large enough. Its useful amulet-dependent functions need an actual linked amulet; the transformation spell does not explicitly provide a separate magic amulet.

[Object into creature candidates](/library/creatures/form-catalogue/#object-into-creature-candidates) provides an automatically filtered list.

## Binding and command control

For ordinary Planar Binding, filter by the creature's actual eligible type. The flags use its natural stat block, not a familiar's changed type or an assumed Magic Aura combination. [Arcane Exploit — Nystul's Magic Aura](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/#arcane-exploit--nystuls-magic-aura) is a separate interaction requiring its own target setup and campaign ruling.

Reserve containment longer than the full casting: a base one-hour Magic Circle set up before a one-hour binding is a fragile schedule. Starting a long casting requires concentration, so a lone caster cannot normally maintain a concentration summon while casting Planar Binding. A second caster or a suitable independent source can change the setup; duration extension does not expressly delete an existing concentration requirement.

Binding duration by slot: **5 → 24 hours; 6 → 10 days; 7 → 30 days; 8 → 180 days; 9 → 366 days**. A failed binding attempt can still cost the consumed 1,000+ GP jewel. Orders, location, duration and safe release all affect the value of a binding; compare the general procedures in [Wizard Operations Roadmap](/library/advanced/wizard-operations-roadmap/) and [000 Minion Management](/library/creatures/000-minion-management/).

## Using the catalogue

1. Open [Form Catalogue](/library/creatures/form-catalogue/) and choose a role or spell mode.
2. Check target level/CR, seen-form requirement, physical size and environment.
3. Read the card's restrictions, then its embedded original stat block.
4. Compare the resource cost and control/concentration requirements.
5. Resolve any remaining source or DM-dependent interaction before treating the form as a reliable option.

The catalogue contains twenty selected creatures. The full galleries remain accessible from [Form Finder](/library/home/form-finder/). Check the exact cited bestiary and edition when comparing additional forms.
