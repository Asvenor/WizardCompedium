**Familiar utility still has operating costs**

An ordinary familiar rolls its own Initiative. Telepathy requires it within 100 feet; sharing its senses costs your Bonus Action and lasts until the start of your next turn. Touch-spell delivery requires it within 100 feet and uses its Reaction when you cast. Familiar utility is excellent, but the whole package is not action-free. ## Scouting & Senses

The familiar is effectively a **remote sensor platform**.

You can use its senses according to [Find Familiar](/library/creatures/find-familiar/) to investigate locations without personally entering them.

Typical scouting pattern:

**Familiar moves ahead**

↓

**Find safe observation position**

↓

**Observe enemies / terrain / entrances**

↓

**Return or communicate information**

↓

**Party plans with information before committing**

Good scouting targets include:

- Rooms before entry
- Corridors
- Rooftops
- Enemy camps
- Caves
- Windows
- Upper floors
- Traps and suspicious terrain
- Alternate entrances
- Escape routes

<aside class="callout callout-tip" data-callout="tip">

**Tip**

Information gained **before Initiative** can be more valuable than anything the familiar does after combat begins.

</aside>

## Remote Interaction

A familiar gives you a creature that can physically occupy a location you cannot.

Depending on its anatomy and the exact task, this can potentially help with:

- Carrying tiny objects
- Delivering objects
- Reaching inaccessible spaces
- Observing mechanisms
- Triggering simple interactions
- Acting as a lookout
- Creating distractions

Do not assume a familiar can manipulate objects that its anatomy clearly cannot handle.

---

## Combat Utility

A normal familiar **cannot attack**; it supports combat through its other permitted actions.

**Help is one attack, not a round of Advantage.** The familiar must distract an enemy within 5 feet of itself; the next allied attack roll against that enemy gains Advantage before the familiar's next turn starts. Its separate Initiative can make the timing awkward. Owl's Flyby avoids Opportunity Attacks during its flight away, not ranged attacks, area effects or every triggered Reaction. For ability-check Help, the familiar needs the relevant skill/tool proficiency and a physically or verbally plausible way to assist; the DM decides whether that help is possible. [2024 Help action](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/rules-glossary#HelpAction).

Potential uses include:

- Help
- Scouting before combat
- Watching hidden approaches
- Delivering touch spells
- Positioning near allies or enemies
- Interacting with the environment
- Carrying small objects
- Providing information

The familiar's low durability makes unnecessary exposure expensive and inconvenient.

### Core Principle

> **If the familiar can create value without being attackable, prefer that option.**

## Necklace of the Beastly Familiar

This [Arcana Unleashed item](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-5-magic-items#NecklaceOfTheBeastlyFamiliar) requires attunement by a spellcaster. It casts Find Familiar once per dawn and separately lets you use a Magic action to transform your familiar within 60 feet for up to 10 minutes. That transformation has no stated daily-use limit; it ends early when its granted temporary HP run out.

| Variant | Rarity | Beast form |
|---|---|---|
| Azurite | Uncommon | Giant Octopus |
| Chrysoprase | Rare | Giant Scorpion |
| Zircon | Rare | Hippopotamus |
| Topaz | Very Rare | Mammoth |
| Fire opal | Legendary | Tyrannosaurus Rex |

The familiar keeps its original HP, Hit Dice and creature type, gaining the form's HP as temporary HP. Ill-fitting equipment falls rather than resizing. **A bigger stat block does not expressly override Find Familiar's no-attack rule:** compare it for durability, movement and utility, not as a guaranteed attacking companion. [Battle Familiar](/library/spells/battle-familiar/) is the separate combat option.

## Touch Spell Delivery

One of Find Familiar's most important Wizard interactions is delivering eligible **Touch-range spells** through the familiar.

This effectively separates:

**Your position**

from

**the point where the spell originates.**

Potential benefits:

- Remain behind cover
- Avoid approaching an ally
- Reach an ally separated by terrain
- Deliver a spell from an unusual angle
- Use a flying familiar to reach inaccessible targets

Always check the exact Find Familiar wording and the spell's casting requirements before assuming an interaction works.

## Battle Familiar

<img src="/vault-assets/100%20Assets/Statblocks%20for%20Compendium/Find%20Familiar/Battle%20Familiar.png" alt="Battle Familiar" width="1352" height="868" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

[Battle Familiar](/library/spells/battle-familiar/) changes the familiar from primarily a utility creature into a legitimate combat asset.

This is an **Arcana Unleashed** spell, not an automatic upgrade to Find Familiar. It lasts 1 hour, requires a reusable 25+ GP diamond, and rolls its own Initiative. Only one familiar can exist at a time: if one already exists, the spell empowers it rather than creating an extra creature. That route preserves its original HP/Hit Dice and uses temporary HP from the Battle Familiar form; the empowerment ends when those temporary HP run out. The spell card details the separate freshly conjured route.

**Slot breakpoints:** one Rend at slot 2–3, two at 4–5, three at 6–7, four at 8–9. Every form walks 40 and swims 30; only Flyer gains Fly 30 (hover) and Flyby. Brute adds 2 AC and starts with 10 more HP; Stalker can replace one attack with Prowl. Do not carry the old form's movement or special traits into the replacement stat block unless a specific retained feature allows it. [Battle Familiar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/3360242-battle-familiar).

Its major strategic advantage is:

> **Battle Familiar does not consume Concentration.**

This allows it to operate alongside your primary Concentration spell.

For example:

**Wizard**

→ Concentration control / summon

+

**Battle Familiar**

→ independent combat contribution

This makes Battle Familiar particularly attractive for Wizards already built around:

- Battlefield control
- Summoning
- Minions
- Action-economy advantages

## Battle Familiar Roles

Choose the Battle Familiar configuration based on what the encounter requires.

## Brute

Prioritize when you need:

- Durability
- Frontline presence
- Another body on the battlefield

## Flyer

Prioritize when you need:

- Flight
- Safe engagement
- Mobility
- Reaching vulnerable targets
- Better positioning

## Stalker

Prioritize when you need:

- Mobility
- Stealth
- Hunting
- Positioning
- Less direct confrontation

For a Wizard, the **Flyer** is often particularly attractive because mobility helps preserve the familiar while still allowing it to contribute.

## Familiar Survival

Do not treat a normal familiar as disposable without reason.

Protect it using:

- Cover
- Distance
- Elevation
- Concealment
- Staying outside obvious AoE zones
- Breaking line of sight
- Avoiding unnecessary combat exposure

Before combat begins, ask:

> **Does the familiar actually need to remain near the enemy?**

If not, move it somewhere safe after it has completed its job.

## Familiar Selection

Choose the form based on the problem.

| Problem | Form |
|---|---|
| **General scouting** | Owl |
| **Aerial reconnaissance** | Owl |
| **Blindsight needed** | Bat |
| **Tiny infiltration** | Spider |
| **Walls / ceilings** | Spider |
| **Underwater scouting** | Octopus |
| **Combat familiar** | Battle Familiar |

You do not need every possible familiar statblock.

Keep forms that provide a **distinct capability**.

---

## Familiar Doctrine

> **Owl** — default general-purpose familiar  
> **Bat** — sensory specialist  
> **Spider** — infiltration specialist  
> **Octopus** — aquatic specialist  
> **Battle Familiar** — combat specialist

The familiar is not valuable because you have a pet.

It is valuable because it gives the Wizard an **independent creature, remote senses, unusual movement, scouting capability, positioning options, and remote spell delivery** for a relatively small investment.
