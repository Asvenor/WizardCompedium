A Wizard normally reaches **20 Intelligence** through character progression. Certain features and magic items can increase Intelligence beyond this normal limit.

The absolute maximum for an ability score is **30**, unless a rule specifically states otherwise.

## Alternative Route — Adept Feats

<span id="block-dd18c4" class="block-anchor" aria-hidden="true"></span>

<aside class="callout callout-warning" data-callout="warning">

**DM-Dependent**

Requires the DM to allow feats to be learned outside normal level progression.

</aside>

Instead of using ASIs to reach **20 Intelligence**, take **two Adept feats** with your normal feat selections.

**Adept Feat**

↓

**Adept Feat**

↓

**Reach 20 Intelligence later through a learned feat, training, reward, or another available method**

This trades slower Intelligence progression for **two powerful Adept features earlier**, while preserving the option to reach 20 Intelligence later if additional feat acquisition is available. Do not plan around a guaranteed training reward: the DM must actually offer one. The published Arcana Unleashed school Adepts each add +1 INT/WIS/CHA, capped at 20; two of them take INT 17 to **19**, not 20. Compare the specific features in [Arcana Unleashed — General feats](/library/tiers/feats-epic-boon-tier-list/#arcana-unleashed--general-feats).

## Ways to Exceed 20 Intelligence

| Method | INT Increase | Maximum | Permanent | Requirement |
|---|---:|---:|:---:|---|
| **Tome of Clear Thought** | +2 | **30** | Yes | Very Rare magic item + study time |
| **Empowering item evolution (Arcana Unleashed)** | +2 to a DM-chosen ability | **24** | Only while attuned | DM permits an eligible item's Legendary evolution and chooses INT |
| **Eligible Epic Boon Feat** | +1 | **30** | Yes, while the feat is retained | Level 19+; the feat must allow INT |
| **Additional eligible Epic Boons** | +1 each | **30** | Yes, while the feats are retained | Post-20 advancement / DM; obey prerequisites and repeatability |
| **Special Rewards** | Varies | Varies | Varies | DM / campaign dependent |

### Worked routes to 24 or 26

These examples start at **INT 20**, before the listed increases. Each Tome means a different charged copy, or a copy whose century-long recharge has actually completed. Every Boon below must permit a +1 INT increase to a maximum of 30.

| Target | Route | Main constraint |
|---|---|---|
| **24** | Two Tomes: 20 → 22 → 24 | Access to two Very Rare books and the study time |
| **24 while attuned** | One Tome gives underlying INT 22; an Empowering item grants +2 to INT | DM-selected Legendary evolution; losing attunement removes the item's increase |
| **24** | One Tome + two eligible Boons: 20 → 22 → 23 → 24 | A normal Wizard gets one feat at Wizard 19; a second Boon needs another qualifying opportunity |
| **26** | Three Tomes: 20 → 22 → 24 → 26 | Access to three Very Rare books and the study time |
| **26** | Two Tomes + two eligible Boons: 20 → 22 → 24 → 25 → 26 | Both item access and an additional qualifying feat opportunity |

**Optimization:** reach 20 with ordinary increases before spending scarce above-20 increases when practical. A Tome used at 18 gives 20, but an ordinary ASI later still cannot raise that 20 to 22. Do not delay a useful immediate increase solely for a hypothetical future reward; compare the campaign's actual remaining levels and item access.

Odd scores such as 21, 23, and 25 do not improve the modifier over the preceding even score. They can still be worthwhile stepping stones, and an Epic Boon's other benefits may justify taking it immediately.

These are legal arithmetic examples, **not guaranteed Wizard progression or guaranteed loot**. See [Worked equipment comparisons](/library/items/magic-item-attunement/#worked-equipment-comparisons) for increasing casting numbers without increasing INT itself.

### Empowering evolution — an actual worn or held item route

Arcana Unleashed's **Legendary Empowering property** increases one ability score chosen by the DM by **2, to a maximum of 24, while attuned**. An eligible Very Rare item such as Staff of Power can receive it when the DM allows complex evolution to Legendary. The staff keeps its earlier properties and one attunement requirement; evolution does not also increase its existing numerical bonuses.

With INT selected, underlying INT 20 becomes 22; underlying INT 22 becomes 24. The property cannot raise 24 to 26 and is not a permanent increase independent of attunement. Do not assume multiple copies stack or that every magic item qualifies. If the DM also permits crafting evolution, Very Rare → Legendary uses the standard difference of **80,000 GP and 125 workdays**, subject to the normal crafting gates. Full eligibility and alternatives: [Evolving Magic Items](/library/items/evolving-magic-items/).

Source: [Arcana Unleashed — Evolving Magic Items, Legendary property table](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-5-magic-items).

## Advancement after level 20

The 2024 PHB offers an **optional DM-selected** advancement method: one qualifying feat for each **30,000 XP earned above 355,000 XP**. The first threshold is 385,000 XP, then 415,000, 445,000, and so on. Epic Boons are suggested, not mandatory.

This grants a feat, **not level 21**: it does not itself increase Wizard level, Proficiency Bonus, spell slots, or Hit Dice. It also does not waive feat prerequisites or repeatability. A feat can normally be taken only once; repeated copies of the same nonrepeatable Epic Boon are not an INT engine. Arcana Unleashed's Magic School Mastery is expressly repeatable, but each selection must use a different school.

An ordinary Ability Score Improvement remains capped at 20 even when selected after level 20. Choose an eligible INT-raising Epic Boon for a higher cap. Milestone campaigns need the DM to decide how, or whether, equivalent rewards are awarded; mere elapsed downtime is not a feat entitlement.

Sources: [PHB level advancement, “Bonus Feats at Level 20”](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/creating-a-character#Tier4Levels1720), [feat repeatability](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#PartsofaFeat), [Arcana Unleashed Epic Boons](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/au/chapter-1-character-options#EpicBoonFeats).

## Tome of Clear Thought

**Rarity:** Very Rare  
**Attunement:** No  
**Increase:** +2 Intelligence  
**Maximum:** 30

The Tome of Clear Thought is the most direct way for a Wizard to increase Intelligence beyond 20.

### Requirement

Study the Tome for:

**48 hours within 6 days or fewer**

After completing the study:

**Intelligence +2, to a maximum of 30.**

The Tome then loses its magic.

### Recharge

The Tome regains its magic after:

**100 years**

A different charged Tome of Clear Thought can also provide another increase. The century is the used book's recharge, not a once-per-century limit on the reader. The item imposes no once-per-person limit.

### Progression

| Starting INT | After Tome |
|---:|---:|
| 18 | 20 |
| 20 | **22** |
| 22 | **24** |
| 24 | **26** |
| 26 | **28** |
| 28 | **30** |

Multiple Tomes can therefore theoretically raise a Wizard all the way to **30 Intelligence**.

Source: [2024 DMG — Tome of Clear Thought](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#TomeofClearThought). The increase does not use an attunement slot or require continuing to carry the book.

## Epic Boons

Epic Boon feats become available at **level 19+**.

Epic Boons include an Ability Score Increase that can raise an appropriate ability score by **1, to a maximum of 30**.

For a Wizard, choose **Intelligence** when the selected Epic Boon permits it.

The **Wizard 19** feature grants one Epic Boon or another qualifying feat; it is not a free Boon at every subsequent level. **Boon of Spell Recall**, for example, permits +1 INT/WIS/CHA to a maximum of 30 and requires level 19+ and Spellcasting. Other Boons may restrict their ability increase differently.

Sources: [Wizard 19](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/character-classes-continued#Level19WizardEpicBoon), [PHB Epic Boon feats](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/feats#EpicBoonFeats).

### Example

**20 INT**

→ Epic Boon

→ **21 INT**

Unlike normal feats, the Epic Boon's increase can exceed 20.

## Tome + Epic Boon

The methods can be combined.

### Example

**20 INT**

→ Tome of Clear Thought

**22 INT**

→ Epic Boon

**23 INT**

Additional qualifying increases can continue progressing toward the absolute maximum of **30**.

## Wish feat replacement — possible, but costly

2024 [Sudden Learning](/library/advanced/complex-spells/wish/#sudden-learning) can replace **one feat the caster has**, not grant an extra feat. The replacement must meet its prerequisites, and a feat that is a prerequisite for another feat or feature cannot be replaced. All benefits of the old feat are lost.

At level 19+, swapping a feat that contributes **no INT** for an eligible +1 INT Epic Boon can produce **net +1 INT**. Swapping a +1 INT feat for a +1 INT Boon gives no net increase; swapping a +2 INT ASI for that Boon loses one point instead. These comparisons assume the new Boon's cap permits its full increase and no score-setting item masks the result.

This is **not safe spell replication**: Sudden Learning triggers Wish's stress and its **33% chance of losing the ability to cast Wish ever again**. It also costs the replaced feat's other benefits. Do not treat repeat casting as a risk-free INT engine. A custom wish for a higher ability score is DM-adjudicated, not a guaranteed listed effect; Wish's standard object creation cannot produce a magic Tome.

Source: [2024 Wish](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Wish).

## Wizard Benefits

Higher Intelligence can improve anything based on the Wizard's Intelligence modifier.

## Spell Save DC

**Spell Save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Intelligence Modifier**

At Proficiency Bonus +6:

| INT | Modifier | Spell Save DC |
|---:|---:|---:|

| 22 | +6 | **20** |
| 24 | +7 | **21** |
| 26 | +8 | **22** |
| 28 | +9 | **23** |
| 30 | +10 | **24** |

## What Does NOT Get You Above 20

## Ioun Stone of Intellect

**Increase:** +2 Intelligence  
**Maximum:** **20**

The Ioun Stone of Intellect cannot raise Intelligence above 20.

If you already have 20 Intelligence, it provides no Intelligence increase.

---

## Headband of Intellect

The Headband requires attunement and **sets Intelligence to 19 while worn**; it has no effect if Intelligence is already 19 or higher. It does not add 19 or add a bonus to an already high score.

It is therefore useful for characters with low Intelligence but is not a method for pushing an optimized Wizard beyond 20.

---

## Normal Ability Score Improvements

Normal Ability Score Improvements and most ordinary feats specify a maximum of **20**.

They cannot normally take:

**20 → 21**

A feature must specifically permit an increase beyond 20.

## Equipment bonuses versus actual Intelligence

**Robe of the Archmagi** raises spell save DC and spell attack rolls by +2 while worn. An **Arcane Grimoire** can raise the corresponding numbers for Wizard spells while held. Neither changes Intelligence, its modifier, Intelligence checks, or Intelligence saving throws. **Staff of Power** improves spell attacks while held, but does **not** grant a spell save DC bonus.

A qualifying **artifact major beneficial property** can raise a DM-chosen ability by 2, to a maximum of 24, **while attuned**. That is a real conditional ability increase if INT is selected, but neither the property nor the chosen ability is guaranteed. It is not a permanent Tome-like reward or a normal buyable gear upgrade. The **Star** card of a Deck of Many Things also permits +2 to an ability, capped at 24, but drawing that particular card is not a safe or guaranteed plan.

Sources: [Headband of Intellect](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#HeadbandofIntellect), [Ioun Stone](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#IounStone), [Robe of the Archmagi](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#RobeoftheArchmagi), [Staff of Power](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#StaffofPower), [Arcane Grimoire — Tasha's](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/2411893-arcane-grimoire), [artifact properties](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/treasure#Artifacts), [Deck — Star](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/magic-items-a-z#Star).

## Maximum Intelligence

The practical progression is:

**20 INT**
→ Tome / Epic Boon
→ **21–22+ INT**
→ Additional qualifying increases
→ **30 INT maximum**

If a Tome of Clear Thought is actually available, **22 Intelligence** from a starting 20 is a straightforward first target; its availability is a campaign reward, not a promised part of Wizard progression.

Reaching **24–30 Intelligence** requires multiple extremely rare opportunities, Epic-level progression, or exceptional campaign rewards.
