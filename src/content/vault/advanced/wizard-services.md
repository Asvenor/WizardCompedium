Wizard magic can be sold as a service when the setting has customers who need it.

The **2024 PHB provides standard spellcasting-service prices and settlement availability**. Use that baseline when buying a service; it does not guarantee an unlimited pool of customers willing to buy every player-cast spell.

## Standard service prices

| Spell level | Settlement availability | Base price |
|---|---|---:|
| Cantrip | Village, town, or city | 30 GP |
| 1 | Village, town, or city | 50 GP |
| 2 | Village, town, or city | 200 GP |
| 3 | Town or city | 300 GP |
| 4–5 | Town or city | 2,000 GP |
| 6–8 | City | 20,000 GP |
| 9 | City | 100,000 GP |

The PHB says to **add expensive components** to these prices; it does not limit that pricing surcharge to consumed components. Actual provider access, customers for a Wizard business, and negotiated contracts remain campaign matters. Source: [2024 PHB — Spellcasting Services](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#Spellcasting).

## Pricing Floor

A service should normally cover:

**Consumed components + travel + casting time + risk + spell-slot opportunity cost**

For the Wizard's own profit calculation, reusable components are investments rather than materials consumed on every cast. That accounting distinction does not override the PHB service-price surcharge above.

## Useful Services by Tier

| Service | Example Spells | Customer |
|---|---|---|
| Identification | Identify, Detect Magic | Adventurers, merchants, nobles |
| Communication | Sending | Governments, guilds, military |
| Investigation | Locate Object, Clairvoyance, Scrying | Nobles, investigators, temples |
| Translation / research | Comprehend Languages, Arcana expertise | Scholars, explorers |
| Fabrication | Fabricate | Guilds, wealthy clients |
| Security | Arcane Lock, Private Sanctum, Guards and Wards | Nobles, banks, guilds |
| Transportation | Teleportation Circle, Teleport | Governments, merchants, adventurers |
| Planar travel | Plane Shift, Gate | Extremely wealthy / powerful clients |
| Countermagic | Dispel Magic, Remove Curse access | Adventurers, institutions |

## Identification

Useful because it is:

- Low risk
- Repeatable
- Valuable to non-Wizards
- Often performed as a ritual

The reusable pearl makes the ongoing cost low after initial purchase.

**Identify is not a curse warranty.** Most identification methods, including this spell, fail to reveal a magic-item curse. A customer can also normally identify an item's properties during a Short Rest, so the service chiefly sells speed and expertise. [DMG curse rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/dmg-2024/treasure) and [PHB identification](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/equipment#MagicItems).

## Communication

Sending can replace days or weeks of courier travel.

Customers may value:

- Speed
- Privacy
- Long distance
- Immediate confirmation

The 2024 spell carries **25 words or fewer** and allows an immediate reply. The recipient must have been met by the caster or described by someone who met them. Different-plane delivery has a 5% failure chance, and a recipient can block the caster's Sending for 8 hours. Sell the actual service, not unrestricted telepathy. [Sending](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/phb-2024/spell-descriptions#Sending).

## Divination

Divination is worth more when the answer changes an expensive decision.

Examples:

- Find a missing object.
- Scout a dangerous location.
- Verify an enemy's position.
- Investigate magical activity.

## Teleportation

High-level transport can save:

- Weeks of travel
- Escort costs
- Shipping risk
- Deadlines

Do not price it like an ordinary carriage ride.

## Security

Long-duration Wizard defenses can protect:

- Vaults
- Estates
- Laboratories
- Archives
- Government buildings

Security work is especially suitable for downtime because much of the value is created before an attack ever happens.

## Risk Premium

Charge more when the job includes:

- Hostile territory
- Unknown magic
- Planar travel
- Consumed components
- Legal/political danger
- Personal combat risk

> A Wizard service is valuable because it replaces time, danger, distance, or uncertainty.
