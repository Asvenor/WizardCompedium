Downtime is the time between adventures that a Wizard can use for long-term projects, preparation, research, crafting, training, and expanding their magical resources.

For a Wizard, downtime is particularly valuable because **time and gold can be converted into permanent or long-lasting advantages**.

## Downtime Activities

| Activity | Main Resource | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Copy Spells** | Gold + Time | Permanently expand the spellbook |
| **Create Backup Spellbook** | Gold + Time | Protect the Wizard's accumulated spell collection |
| **Scribe Spell Scrolls** | Gold + Time | Store additional spellcasting for later use |
| **Craft Items** | Gold + Time | Produce mundane equipment and consumables |
| **Craft Magic Items** | Gold + Time | Create permanent or consumable magical equipment |
| **Craft for Profit** | Gold + Time | Convert crafting and Bastion resources into income |
| **Research** | Gold + Time | Gather information and prepare for future problems |
| **Training & Feats** | Gold + Time + DM Approval | Learn available training options or pursue special feat training |
| **Operate Bastion** | Bastion Turns | Produce resources, research, craft, trade, and maintain infrastructure |
| **Prepare Long-Term Spells** | Gold + Spellcasting | Establish magical effects before they're immediately needed |
| **Create / Maintain Minions** | Gold + Spells + Time | Establish Undead, Constructs, summons, or bound creatures |

## Wizard Downtime Progression

## Levels 1–4 — Build the Foundation

The early Wizard has limited gold but can already begin turning downtime into permanent advantages.

### Main Activities

- Copy discovered spells
- Expand ritual collection
- Create low-level Spell Scrolls
- Create a backup spellbook
- Research upcoming threats
- Acquire expensive spell components
- Train when useful opportunities exist

### Goal

Build the Wizard's basic resources without consuming important adventuring funds unnecessarily.

---

## Levels 5–8 — Establish Infrastructure

The Wizard now has access to significantly stronger spells and can normally begin developing a Bastion.

### Main Activities

- Expand the spellbook
- Scribe Spell Scrolls
- Begin serious crafting
- Establish Bastion facilities
- Research monsters and locations
- Prepare magical defenses
- Create or maintain Undead when relevant
- Establish permanent magical effects where possible
- Pursue training opportunities

### Useful Spells

- Animate Dead
- Glyph of Warding
- Magic Circle
- Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum
- Phantom Steed
- Tiny Hut

---

## Levels 9–12 — Magical Infrastructure

At this point downtime begins enabling strategies that aren't practical for lower-level characters.

### Main Activities

- Advanced crafting
- Bastion production
- Spell Scroll production
- Planar Binding
- Magical defenses
- Long-term summons and servants
- Information gathering
- Teleportation preparation
- Permanent magical infrastructure

### Useful Spells

- Planar Binding
- Contact Other Plane
- Telepathic Bond
- Teleportation Circle
- Contingency
- Guards and Wards

---

## Levels 13–16 — Archmage Preparation

The Wizard now has access to spells capable of changing the structure of an entire campaign.

**At Wizard 15**, Clone and Demiplane become available as level-8 spells. Start a needed Clone here: its **120-day maturation** does not wait for tier 4, and the Demiplane spell does not require the level-17 Bastion facility. See [Progression, costs and time](/library/advanced/wizard-operations-roadmap/#progression-costs-and-time).

### Main Activities

- Create Simulacrum
- Establish teleportation networks
- Advanced Bastion development
- High-level magical crafting
- Long-term planar projects
- Strengthen magical defenses
- Establish persistent illusions
- Maintain bound creatures
- Prepare emergency contingencies

### Useful Spells

- Simulacrum
- Teleport
- Plane Shift
- Mirage Arcane
- Forcecage
- Sequester

- Clone and Demiplane from Wizard 15

---

## Levels 17–20 — Endgame Infrastructure

High-level Wizard downtime can produce advantages that extend far beyond individual encounters.

### Main Activities

- Clone preparation
- Demiplane infrastructure
- Wish-assisted projects
- High-level magic-item crafting
- Powerful bound creatures
- Simulacrum management
- Permanent magical defenses
- Planar infrastructure
- High-level information gathering
- Long-duration magical preparation

### Useful Spells

- Wish
- Clone
- Demiplane
- Foresight
- Mind Blank
- Shapechange
- True Polymorph
- Gate

## Long-Term Spell Projects

Some spells are significantly more powerful when treated as downtime projects rather than ordinary combat spells.

| Spell | Downtime Use |
|---|---|
| Arcane Lock | Permanently secure important entrances and containers |
| Glyph of Warding | Establish prepared magical defenses |
| Magic Circle | Contain creatures for magical projects |
| Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum | Establish long-term privacy and magical security |
| Animate Dead | Create and maintain Undead |
| Planar Binding | Convert temporary creature access into long-duration service |
| Teleportation Circle | Establish permanent transportation infrastructure through repeated casting |
| Contingency | Preload an emergency spell |
| Guards and Wards | Establish long-term building defenses through repeated casting |
| Simulacrum | Create a long-term magical duplicate |
| Mirage Arcane | Large-scale prepared terrain manipulation |
| Sequester | Hide and protect important creatures or objects |
| Clone | Establish long-term protection against death |
| Demiplane | Create extraplanar storage and infrastructure |
| Wish | Accelerate or enable high-level magical projects |

## Training & Feats

Downtime can also be used for personal development that isn't directly related to spellcasting.

Possible training can include:

- Languages
- Tools
- Specialized instruction
- Campaign-specific training
- Other abilities made available by the DM

## Feat Training

Feats aren't normally something a character can simply purchase with downtime.

However, the DM can use **special training as a reward** and potentially grant a feat or similar benefit.

This could involve:

1. Finding an appropriate trainer.
2. Meeting any requirements set by the DM.
3. Spending the required downtime.
4. Paying training expenses where applicable.
5. Completing the training or associated quest.
6. Receiving the feat or other reward if the DM permits it.

Feat training should therefore be treated as:

**Possible Downtime Opportunity — DM Approval Required**

rather than a guaranteed method of gaining additional feats.

## Research

A Wizard can use downtime to gather information before committing adventuring resources.

Possible research subjects include:

- Monsters
- Planes
- Magic items
- Spells
- Historical events
- Locations
- Organizations
- Magical phenomena
- Enemies
- Ancient ruins
- Rituals
- Rare components

Useful sources can include:

- Libraries
- Archives
- Universities
- Wizard academies
- Temples
- Scholars
- Other Wizards
- Bastion facilities

At higher levels, magical information gathering can supplement conventional research.

## Downtime Resources

Most Wizard downtime projects consume some combination of four resources:

| Resource | Used For |
|---|---|
| **Time** | Crafting, copying, research, training and permanent projects |
| **Gold** | Materials, components, copying, crafting and services |
| **Spell Slots** | Magical preparation and repeated spell projects |
| **Access** | Trainers, libraries, creatures, formulas, components and facilities |

A powerful Wizard with no downtime can be significantly less prepared than one who has been given weeks or months to establish resources.

---

## Downtime Preparation

Before leaving for a major adventure, useful preparations can include:

- Updated spellbook
- Backup spellbook secured
- Spell Scrolls prepared
- Expensive components acquired
- Familiar active
- Minions prepared
- Contingency active
- Long-duration spells established
- Bastion orders issued
- Research completed
- Magic items crafted
- Teleportation destinations established
- Emergency resources stored
- Simulacrum available at high levels
- Clone prepared at very high levels

---

## Core Principle

> **A Wizard converts time, gold, information, and preparation into power.**

Combat optimization is only one part of Wizard optimization.

Downtime allows the Wizard to build resources **before Initiative is ever rolled**.
