A high-level Wizard should have a **recovery plan**, not just high AC.

## Layer 1 — Avoid the Failure

Keep access to:

- Shield
- Absorb Elements
- Misty Step
- Dimension Door
- Cover
- Teleportation
- Ally rescue

The cheapest recovery is the one you never need.

## Layer 2 — Contingency

Contingency is preloaded emergency insurance.

Before using it:

1. Choose a narrow, objective trigger.
2. Confirm the loaded spell can legally target you.
3. Confirm timing and Concentration.
4. Avoid triggers that fire too early or ambiguously.

Good uses are **escape, protection, or survival**, not clever wording traps.

## Layer 3 — Backup Spellbook

Keep your backup **away from the primary book**.

Good locations:

- Bastion
- Trusted vault
- Hidden secure site
- [Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/)

A backup carried in the same bag as the original is not a real backup.

## Layer 4 — Recovery Cache

A serious Wizard can store:

- Backup spellbook
- Arcane focus
- Component pouch
- Clothing / armor appropriate to the build
- Emergency gold
- Key reusable components
- Teleportation access
- Basic healing / survival gear

## Layer 5 — Clone

[Clone](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/clone/) is long-term death insurance.

Current planning from this compendium:

- 1,000+ gp diamond — consumed
- 2,000+ gp sealable vessel — retained
- Creature or at least 1 cubic inch of flesh
- 120-day maturation period
- Secure hidden location

A Clone solves **death**, not the equipment problem. Your recovery cache matters.

## Layer 6 — Demiplane

A [Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/) can protect:

- Mature clones
- Backup equipment
- Components
- Spellbooks
- Strategic supplies

Never rely on a Demiplane without a documented retrieval plan.

## High-Level Recovery Plan

Example:

**Death → Clone activates → retrieve recovery cache → recover spellbook/focus → establish transport → rejoin party**

The plan should still work if:

- Your corpse is inaccessible.
- Your normal equipment is lost.
- Your Bastion is compromised.
- One teleport route fails.

> **Survival is an infrastructure problem at high level.**
