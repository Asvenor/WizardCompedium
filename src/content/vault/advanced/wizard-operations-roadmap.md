Use spare gold and available days to build the next useful capability. The suggested order is analysis. Prices and timing below come from the named spell or Wizard rule; access to buyers, premises, creatures and campaign downtime is not automatic.

**Level convention:** spell access below assumes a single-class Wizard and the spell in the spellbook. Multiclass spell slots alone do not let you learn higher-level Wizard spells. Bastion progression uses character level and depends on the campaign using Bastions.

## Pick the next project

| Situation | First investment | Why it pays off |
|---|---|---|
| Spellbook loss would cripple recovery | [Spellbook Redundancy & Acquisition](/library/advanced/spellbook-redundancy-acquisition/) | Preserves access to hard-won spell knowledge |
| An encounter depends on an expensive focus | [Expensive Components](/library/advanced/expensive-components/) | Unlocks a spell already available to you |
| The party revisits a secure base | [Long-Term Magical Defenses](/library/advanced/long-term-magical-defenses/) | Converts downtime into repeated security benefits |
| Emergency failure can kill you before your next turn | [Contingency Optimization](/library/advanced/complex-spells/contingency-optimization/) | Stores a specific personal response |
| You can afford an additional caster with finite resources | [Simulacrum](/library/advanced/complex-spells/simulacrum/) | Adds another creature's actions and concentration |
| A campaign has months of in-world downtime | [Clone](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/clone/) | Makes long-term death recovery possible |
| You need separated storage and recovery locations | [Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/) | Adds persistent rooms with controlled access |
| You have spare ninth-level access | [Wish](/library/advanced/complex-spells/wish/) | Duplicates eligible infrastructure spells; effects still obey their own limits |

## Progression, costs and time

Costs are minimum listed GP values. Normal learning/copying, property, travel and security can add costs.

| Earliest Wizard level | Project | Setup and recurring cost | Time / resource requirement | Main limit |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Backup spellbook | 10 GP per spell level copied, plus new book | 1 hour per spell level | Only spells actually copied are preserved; a blank book is not a backup |
| 1 | Familiar | 10+ GP burning incense, consumed | 1 hour; ritual adds 10 minutes | One familiar; use the form's actual abilities |
| 3 | Arcane Lock | 25+ GP gold dust consumed | Action; lasts until dispelled | Does not strengthen a breakable wall or defeat all magical entry |
| 5 | Fixed Glyph of Warding | 200+ GP diamond powder consumed, plus stored-spell components | 1 hour, Glyph slot and the spell being stored | Warded surface/object cannot move more than 10 feet from casting location |
| 7 | Private Sanctum | No priced component | 10 minutes; normally 24 hours; same place daily for 365 days for indefinite duration | Select effects deliberately: anti-teleportation/planar protection can affect your own routes |
| 9 | Teleportation Circle journey | 50+ GP inks consumed per casting | 1 minute and a fifth-level slot | Known permanent destination on same plane; departure portal lasts only briefly |
| 9 | New permanent circle by spell | 18,250+ GP inks total | 365 daily castings at the same location | Long calendar commitment; protect access and sigil information |
| 9 | Planar Binding | 1,000+ GP jewel consumed per attempt, plus containment/acquisition | 1 hour; target within 60 feet throughout; Charisma save | Does not summon target; hostile creatures twist commands |
| 11 | Contingency | 1,500+ GP statuette, reusable and carried | 10 minutes; Contingency slot plus eligible spell slot; lasts up to 10 days | One at a time; stored spell level ≤5, Action casting, affects you only |
| 11 | Guards and Wards | 10+ GP silver rod, reusable | 1 hour per cast; daily for 365 days for lasting ward | Specific area and effects; allies need access arrangements |
| 13 | Simulacrum | 1,500+ GP ruby consumed; matching ice/snow | 12 hours, eligible Beast/Humanoid target present throughout | Cannot take rests or cast Simulacrum; recasting destroys your prior one |
| 13 | Sequester | 5,000+ GP gem dust consumed | Action; until dispelled | A creature is willing and becomes unconscious; damage ends the spell |
| 15 | Clone | 1,000+ GP diamond consumed; 2,000+ GP vessel | 1 hour casting, then 120 days maturation | No equipment transfer; soul must be free and willing |
| 15 | Demiplane | No priced component | Action; door lasts 1 hour; room persists | Visitors can opt to be shunted out when door vanishes; secrets are not absolute protection |

## Project order by level

**Levels 1–4:** protect your spellbook, buy foci for spells you will use, keep ritual utility accessible, and develop a trusted supply contact. Budget for opportunities to acquire spells.

**Levels 5–8:** establish a secure base if the campaign allows it. Add targeted wards where the party actually returns. Create a small reserve of high-value scrolls using [Crafting](/library/items/crafting/); do not count listed item value as guaranteed sales revenue.

**Levels 9–12:** learn useful destination sigils, distinguish journeys from circle construction, and design Contingency around a concrete failure scenario. Bind a creature only when acquisition, containment, saving-throw reliability and the service itself justify the cost.

**Levels 13–16:** protect the additional resources bought with Simulacrum; start Clone early enough to mature before danger. Add a separate recovery cache and secure storage. Confirm the route back to play after a resurrection, transfer or planar emergency.

**Levels 17–20:** use Wish duplication to accelerate eligible setup. A Clone still needs time to mature. Evaluate long-term True Polymorph allies separately from temporary transformations and binding. Prioritize a robust recovery plan over accumulating assets without maintenance or commands.

These priorities are adjustable: a campaign with almost no downtime changes the economics substantially.

## Bastion progression

[Bastion Progression](/library/bastions/bastion-progression/) uses **2 / 4 / 5 / 6 special facilities at character levels 5 / 9 / 13 / 17**. The campaign must award and use a Bastion; it is not a spell you can cast to demand property.

Start with the existing [Facilities](/library/bastions/facilities/), [Hirelings](/library/bastions/hirelings/), [Construction & Defense](/library/bastions/construction-defense/) and [Bastion Crafting](/library/bastions/bastion-crafting/) notes. For a Wizard, compare research/arcane work against production and transport before filling a facility slot.

Keep these concepts separate:

- **Teleportation Circle facility:** a special-facility choice with its own benefits. The spell's 365-day construction project is a different acquisition route.
- **Demiplane facility:** a level-17 special facility. The eighth-level Demiplane spell is available to a single-class Wizard at level 15; it does not by itself grant the special facility's additional benefits.
- **Hireling work:** depends on the facility's permitted orders and requirements. A Simulacrum or an ordinary hired worker does not automatically supply extra special-facility capacity.

Official overview: [Exploring Bastions from the 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1828-exploring-bastions-from-the-2024-dungeon-masters). Exact facility operations require the 2024 DMG's Bastions chapter; broad prose in the existing notes is not a substitute for those details.

## Binding budget and schedule

| Slot | Binding duration | Minimum jewel cost per day of successful service |
|---:|---|---:|
| 5 | 1 day | 1,000 GP |
| 6 | 10 days | 100 GP |
| 7 | 30 days | 33.33 GP |
| 8 | 180 days | 5.56 GP |
| 9 | 366 days | 2.73 GP |

These are amortized costs for a **successful** attempt, rounded to two decimals. Unsuccessful binding attempts (successful target saves), acquisition, containment and upkeep change the real cost. See [Binding candidates](/library/creatures/form-catalogue/#binding-candidates).

Give containment a timing margin. Casting a spell for one minute or longer requires concentration during casting, so arranging a one-hour concentration summon and personally casting one-hour Planar Binding is not a complete solo procedure.

**Glyph interaction:** an upcast Glyph can store a level-5 spell, so spell level alone does not prohibit [Glyph of Warding for Planar Binding](/library/advanced/complex-spells/planar-binding/#glyph-of-warding-for-planar-binding). An actual setup still needs to resolve Planar Binding's specific target/range/casting requirements and Glyph's trigger rules.

## Recovery chain

A prepared recovery plan has five connected parts:

1. Mature Clone or another actual recovery method.
2. Secure location that the returning character can leave.
3. Usable focus/components and clothing.
4. Backup spellbook with relevant travel and recovery spells.
5. A way to contact allies and rejoin the campaign.

See [Recovery Cache](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/clone/#recovery-cache), [Death & Contingency Planning](/library/advanced/death-contingency-planning/), [Spellbook Redundancy & Acquisition](/library/advanced/spellbook-redundancy-acquisition/) and [Teleportation & Planar Travel](/library/advanced/teleportation-planar-travel/).

With the current Demiplane wording, creatures inside may choose to be expelled as the door vanishes, landing Prone near its former location. Choosing to remain is a separate decision requiring a later exit route. This clarifies the older exit warning in [Returning to a Demiplane](/library/advanced/high-level-infrastructure/demiplane/#returning-to-a-demiplane).

## Ongoing costs and dependencies

- **Planar Binding:** the service depends on workable commands, travel distance, remaining duration and what happens when control ends.
- **Clone:** casting and maturation are separate; an immature clone is not an available recovery method.
- **Daily construction:** the required repeated castings are part of the cost, not something the first casting completes.
- **Simulacrum:** finite resources and repairs affect its long-term value. Normal repairs cost 100 GP per HP and occur during the creator's Long Rest.
