Use these as pre-session reference lists, not fixed character-sheet prep. Start from the loadout that matches the day, then adjust for party plan, known enemy type, and DM rulings.

## Concentration conflict check

<!-- source:B00171 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00171.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Rule</th><th scope="col">Use at the table</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>One main concentration slot</td><td>Pick the spell that wins the current problem: Web, Hypnotic Pattern, Sleet Storm, Slow, Banishment, Wall of Force, Telekinesis, Maze.</td></tr><tr><td>Do not stack traps</td><td>You cannot run Web + Fly + Greater Invisibility + Banishment at the same time. Choose the job before casting.</td></tr><tr><td>If control is already winning</td><td>Do not replace it with damage unless damage ends the fight faster and safely. Use cantrips, Dodge, reposition, or Counterspell.</td></tr><tr><td>If concentration is unsafe</td><td>Use non-concentration defense first: Mirror Image, Misty Step, cover, Rope Trick, Shield reaction.</td></tr><tr><td>If party needs mobility</td><td>Fly / Greater Invisibility style plans compete with battlefield control. Only choose them when movement beats control.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Prepared list, spellbook access, and reserve

<!-- source:B00175 -->
Do not spend a preparation slot merely because a spell is useful. First classify it as a daily prepared answer, a spellbook ritual, a downtime spell, or a scroll/reserve option. A spell belongs in more than one lane only when the current mission justifies it.

<!-- source:B00176 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00176" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Lane</th><th scope="col">Put here</th><th scope="col">Typical examples</th><th scope="col">Audit question</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Prepare today</td><td>Spells likely to decide the expected encounters or rescue failures.</td><td>Primary control, Shield/Absorb Elements, Counterspell, mobility, emergency escape.</td><td>Will this probably be cast under today's mission profile?</td></tr><tr><td>Spellbook ritual</td><td>Ritual-tagged spells that remain accessible without daily preparation under the active Wizard rules.</td><td>Detect Magic, Find Familiar, Identify, Phantom Steed, Tiny Hut or Telepathic Bond only when the active entry displays the Ritual tag.</td><td>Does the active D&amp;D Beyond entry actually show Ritual, and is the extra casting time safe?</td></tr><tr><td>Downtime / infrastructure</td><td>High-cost or long-cast spells used from a secure plan.</td><td>Glyph of Warding, Magic Circle, Planar Binding, Teleportation Circle, Clone.</td><td>Are the component, location, time, and failure state already secured?</td></tr><tr><td>Scroll / reserve</td><td>Rare answers that are valuable but inefficient to prepare every day.</td><td>Specialized removal, environment protection, redundant escape, campaign-specific divination.</td><td>Can the scroll be legally cast when needed, and is losing it acceptable?</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
