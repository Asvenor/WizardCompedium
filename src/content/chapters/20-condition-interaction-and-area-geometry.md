This is a Wizard-facing interaction map, not copied condition text. Use it to select a spell, identify what a condition shuts down, and avoid wasting concentration on a condition the target ignores.

<!-- source:B00279 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00279" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Condition</th><th scope="col">Wizard impact</th><th scope="col">Wizard pressure / exploit</th><th scope="col">Fast answer</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Blinded</td><td>Sight-required spells fail; attacks are impaired and attacks against the target improve.</td><td>Blindness/Deafness; obscurement can create similar sight denial without applying Blinded.</td><td>Lesser Restoration, Dispel where applicable, leave the obscured area, or use no-sight spells.</td></tr><tr><td>Charmed</td><td>The target cannot harm the charmer and the charmer gains social leverage; many creatures resist or ignore it.</td><td>Charm Person, Hypnotic Pattern, and Dominate-family effects when eligible.</td><td>Break the spell, damage/wake when the spell allows, or switch to non-charm control.</td></tr><tr><td>Frightened</td><td>Movement and d20 pressure depend on the source's visibility and exact condition text.</td><td>Cause Fear/Fear; combine with terrain and forced routing.</td><td>Break line of sight, remove the effect, or use immunity/protection.</td></tr><tr><td>Grappled</td><td>Speed becomes 0; positioning and some attacks are constrained.</td><td>Use forced movement, summons, or terrain to keep targets exposed.</td><td>Misty Step/teleport, escape check, forced separation, or remove the grappler.</td></tr><tr><td>Incapacitated</td><td>No actions, Bonus Actions, or Reactions; Concentration ends.</td><td>Hypnotic Pattern, Sleep, Hideous Laughter, and stronger conditions that include it.</td><td>End the creating effect immediately; allies should prioritize restoration/wakeup rules.</td></tr><tr><td>Invisible</td><td>Sight-required targeting fails unless you can see the creature; attack and initiative rules change.</td><td>Use area effects, hazards, detection, and readied responses to observable triggers.</td><td>See Invisibility, True Seeing, Dispel where applicable, or reveal through the environment.</td></tr><tr><td>Paralyzed</td><td>Includes Incapacitated; STR/DEX saves fail; nearby hits can become critical.</td><td>Hold Person/Monster; coordinate melee conversion before spending the slot.</td><td>Remove the spell/condition, protect the target, or break caster Concentration.</td></tr><tr><td>Poisoned</td><td>Attack rolls and ability checks are impaired, but saving throws are not automatically impaired.</td><td>Useful against weapon/skill-heavy enemies; often resisted.</td><td>Protection/neutralization, Lesser Restoration, or immunity.</td></tr><tr><td>Prone</td><td>Standing costs movement; the Prone creature’s attacks have Disadvantage. Attacks against it have Advantage from within 5 feet and Disadvantage from farther away.</td><td>Grease, Sapping Sting, and forced knockdown; strongest with nearby melee allies.</td><td>Stand if Speed is not 0, teleport, or fight from Prone only when the trade is acceptable.</td></tr><tr><td>Restrained</td><td>Speed 0; attacks against improve; own attacks and DEX saves worsen.</td><td>Web is the default early source; follow with DEX-save damage or ally attacks.</td><td>Escape/break the restraint, teleport, burn Web, or end caster Concentration.</td></tr><tr><td>Stunned</td><td>Includes Incapacitated; STR/DEX saves fail and attacks against improve.</td><td>Rare Wizard access, but convert immediately when an ally applies it.</td><td>Remove the effect or prevent follow-up focus fire.</td></tr><tr><td>Unconscious</td><td>Includes Incapacitated and Prone; nearby hits can become critical.</td><td>Sleep against eligible targets; secure rather than automatically execute when objectives matter.</td><td>Damage/wakeup/healing follows the creating effect and condition rules.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Area-of-effect geometry — exact 2024 shapes

<!-- source:B00281 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00281"><strong>2024 AREA RULE — NOT A SQUARE-COUNT TEMPLATE</strong><p>Every area has an origin and is a three-dimensional geometric shape. These are cross-sections, not grid templates. The exact effect supplies size, range, duration, targets, and exceptions. Diagram key: black/white = geometry; orange dot = origin; blue L = length; violet W = width; green S = side; red R = radius; brown H = height; teal D = Emanation distance.</p></aside>
