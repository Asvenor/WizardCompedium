## Fair save-targeting guide

<!-- source:B00044 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00044.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Defense</th><th scope="col">Observable clues</th><th scope="col">Best use</th><th scope="col">Main warning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Strength</td><td>Small, restrained, frail, or poor leverage.</td><td>Forced movement, restraint, and effects that punish weak physical power.</td><td>Large or visibly powerful creatures often excel here.</td></tr><tr><td>Dexterity</td><td>Bulky body, limited mobility, heavy equipment, confined space.</td><td>Area damage and effects where positioning matters.</td><td>Fast or evasive creatures may still have excellent Dexterity saves.</td></tr><tr><td>Constitution</td><td>Visible frailty, exhaustion, disease, or low physical resilience.</td><td>Only when the effect is worth attacking a commonly strong monster save.</td><td>Many monsters have strong Constitution; do not default here.</td></tr><tr><td>Intelligence</td><td>Animal behavior, simple tactics, poor language or planning.</td><td>Rare high-impact mental control and no-save alternatives when available.</td><td>Aberrations, constructs, and unusual creatures can break the stereotype.</td></tr><tr><td>Wisdom</td><td>Poor awareness, impulsive behavior, weak discipline.</td><td>Many premier control effects.</td><td>Bosses, priests, and disciplined magical foes may be strong here.</td></tr><tr><td>Charisma</td><td>Weak force of personality or unstable extraplanar presence.</td><td>Banishment-style and identity/existence effects.</td><td>Extraplanar does not automatically mean weak Charisma.</td></tr><tr><td>Armor Class</td><td>Visible lack of armor, poor cover, or restrained target.</td><td>Attack-roll spells and reliable finishing damage.</td><td>Attack spells can waste turns against unknown high AC.</td></tr><tr><td>No save</td><td>Legendary Resistance, uncertain defenses, or urgent encounter splitting.</td><td>Walls, terrain, movement, buffs, summons, and other effects that do not ask for the key save.</td><td>No-save does not mean no counterplay; positioning and exact wording still matter.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Campaign assumptions and sensitivity

<!-- source:B00047 -->
Optimization claims in this book are defaults under the assumptions below. Change the recommendation when the campaign changes the pressure; do not treat a tier letter as universal proof.

<!-- source:B00048 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00048" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Assumption</th><th scope="col">Reference baseline</th><th scope="col">When the answer changes</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Character legality</td><td>Selectable in the active D&amp;D Beyond builder = green light.</td><td>Recheck after a source toggle, builder update, or new release.</td></tr><tr><td>Ability generation</td><td>Point buy / standard-array style optimization with Intelligence first and useful Dexterity/Constitution.</td><td>Rolled scores can accelerate feats or make armor routes less valuable.</td></tr><tr><td>Adventuring pressure</td><td>Several meaningful encounters between Long Rests; Short Rests and safe retreats are not guaranteed.</td><td>One-fight days raise nova options; long attrition raises rituals, recovery, and durable control.</td></tr><tr><td>Magic items</td><td>Useful items may appear but are never assumed for the build to function.</td><td>Guaranteed item access can change feat, armor, and attunement priorities.</td></tr><tr><td>Downtime and gold</td><td>Components, copying, and crafting compete for finite time and money.</td><td>Abundant downtime raises scroll, glyph, binding, and infrastructure strategies.</td></tr><tr><td>Table operation</td><td>Fast turns, finite summons, transparent triggers, and conservative rulings.</td><td>A table that welcomes complex automation can raise summon and engine value.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
