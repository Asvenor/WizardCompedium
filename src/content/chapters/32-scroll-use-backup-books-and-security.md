A scroll is simultaneously an emergency cast, a possible spellbook acquisition, and a consumable asset. Use this section to choose deliberately; source versions can change who may cast, the check required, and how copying interacts with the scroll.

<!-- source:B00517 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00517"><strong>SCROLL FIRST QUESTION</strong><p>Before touching the die or spending gold, identify the scroll's source version and answer two separate questions: can this character cast it from the item, and can this Wizard legally add it to this spellbook? One yes does not guarantee the other.</p></aside>

## Scroll operations matrix

<!-- source:B00519 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00519" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Operation</th><th scope="col">Required gate</th><th scope="col">Resource result</th><th scope="col">Best use</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Cast now</td><td>The active scroll rules permit this character to use it; resolve any level/check, action, target, and spell requirements.</td><td>The scroll is normally consumed when its use resolves as the item states.</td><td>A prepared-list gap, emergency resource, or effect worth more now than permanent acquisition.</td></tr><tr><td>Copy into spellbook</td><td>It is a legal selectable Wizard spell and level; use the active copying-from-scroll procedure.</td><td>Pay the listed time/cost and apply any check or scroll-destruction rule from that source version.</td><td>Unique ritual, missing core function, campaign answer, or high-repeat spell.</td></tr><tr><td>Preserve for later</td><td>The effect remains useful but neither casting nor copying is currently worth the risk or cost.</td><td>The party retains a portable consumable that must be protected and inventoried.</td><td>Rare component, emergency transport, restoration, information, or an effect another eligible user may need.</td></tr><tr><td>Trade or sell</td><td>No party member needs the spell, copying value is low, and the market/legal situation is safe.</td><td>Convert a narrow consumable into gold, access, another scroll, or a service.</td><td>Redundant damage, prohibited source, unusable spell, or funding a higher-priority acquisition.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 2024 Spell Scroll scribing baseline

<!-- source:B00521 -->
This is the separate 2024 creation rule for Spell Scrolls. The scribe needs Arcana proficiency OR proficiency with Calligrapher’s Supplies, must have the spell prepared on every inscription day, and must keep its Material components at hand. Consumed components are consumed only when the scroll is completed. Each listed day requires 8 hours; days need not be consecutive.

## Spellbook security layers

<!-- source:B00524 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00524" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Layer</th><th scope="col">Purpose</th><th scope="col">Read-only operating rule</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Primary book</td><td>Daily preparation and normal travel access.</td><td>Keep concealed, dry, physically protected, and separate from obvious treasure when practical.</td></tr><tr><td>Travel backup</td><td>Restores the minimum viable Wizard after theft, fire, capture, or separation.</td><td>Prioritize defense, escape, control, rituals, communication, and the campaign's recurring answer.</td></tr><tr><td>Secure archive</td><td>Holds broader duplicates away from the active adventuring risk.</td><td>Use a trusted, warded, politically stable location with a recovery method that does not depend on the lost book.</td></tr><tr><td>Prepared-spell memory</td><td>Keeps the character operational after book loss under the active preparation rules.</td><td>Know what remains prepared and avoid casually replacing an irreplaceable prepared answer.</td></tr><tr><td>Recovery intelligence</td><td>Makes retrieval possible without using the document as a writable tracker.</td><td>Maintain the plan outside this compendium: trusted contacts, last location, access list, and known magical protections.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Minimum viable backup order

<!-- source:B00526 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00526" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Priority</th><th scope="col">Duplicate first</th><th scope="col">Reason</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>S</td><td>Immediate defense, self-extraction, one primary control spell, familiar/ritual engine, and campaign-critical travel or information.</td><td>Restores survival and the class's broad operating identity.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Countermagic, party rescue, scouting, communication, shelter, and a different-save or no-save alternative.</td><td>Prevents one immunity, ambush, or separated ally from collapsing the recovery plan.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Specialized damage, crafting, social, environmental, and long-downtime tools.</td><td>Strong once the essential chassis is safe.</td></tr><tr><td>C</td><td>Redundant damage and very narrow effects.</td><td>Rebuild only when the cost is trivial or the campaign repeatedly rewards the niche.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Spellbook-loss reconstruction card

<!-- source:B00528 -->
The active Wizard entry controls exact reconstruction. The traditional operational anchors below are included for planning and must be reconfirmed in D&D Beyond after any rules update.

<!-- source:B00529 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00529" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">State</th><th scope="col">What remains</th><th scope="col">Reconstruction action</th><th scope="col">Critical loss</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Primary book inaccessible</td><td>Current prepared spells and already-expended resources remain governed by the active class entry.</td><td>Recover the book first when safe; do not improvise new preparation access.</td><td>Unprepared book-only options and ritual flexibility may be unavailable.</td></tr><tr><td>Create a duplicate from your own book</td><td>All spells still present in the primary book.</td><td>Planning anchor: 1 hour and 10 gp per spell level when the active entry retains the traditional duplicate-book rule.</td><td>Time, ink, and secure storage.</td></tr><tr><td>Replace a lost book from prepared spells</td><td>Only spells the rule permits you to reconstruct from the current prepared list.</td><td>Use the active replacement procedure; create a functioning core before optional breadth.</td><td>Every nonprepared spell lacking a backup, scroll, or another source.</td></tr><tr><td>Copy from another book or scroll</td><td>Only legally accessible Wizard spells.</td><td>Planning anchor: 2 hours and 50 gp per spell level when the active entry retains the standard copying rule.</td><td>The source can be destroyed or unavailable; scroll-specific checks may apply.</td></tr><tr><td>Minimum recovery order</td><td>Defense, escape, control, ritual information, then specialist tools.</td><td>Rebuild the smallest operational spellbook first; expand only after survival is restored.</td><td>Do not spend the recovery budget on low-frequency luxuries first.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
