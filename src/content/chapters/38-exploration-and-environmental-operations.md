## Travel pace and marching order

<!-- source:B00606 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00606.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Pace</th><th scope="col">Per hour / day</th><th scope="col">2024 effect</th><th scope="col">Wizard use</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Fast</td><td>4 miles / 30 miles</td><td>Disadvantage on Perception, Survival, and Stealth checks.</td><td>Use when the deadline is the primary threat and ambush/navigation risk is acceptable.</td></tr><tr><td>Normal</td><td>3 miles / 24 miles</td><td>Disadvantage on Stealth checks.</td><td>Default when speed matters but perception/navigation cannot be sacrificed.</td></tr><tr><td>Slow</td><td>2 miles / 18 miles</td><td>Advantage on Perception and Survival checks.</td><td>Best for hostile territory, hidden routes, traps, tracking, and avoiding a disastrous first round.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<!-- source:B00607 -->

<!-- source:B00608 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00608.T001"><strong>MARCHING ORDER</strong><p>Set it before danger. In a two-player party, the melee ally usually leads through uncertain physical threats while the Wizard stays close enough for rescue and Counterspell but far enough that one trap or area effect does not catch both. Reverse the order when the Wizard’s senses, familiar, or ward interaction clearly leads the task.</p></aside>

## Exploration role assignment

<!-- source:B00610 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00610.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Role</th><th scope="col">Primary method</th><th scope="col">Failure to avoid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Route and navigation</td><td>Maps, Survival support, landmarks, divination, local guides, and recorded fallback points.</td><td>Teleporting or pushing deeper without a known safe return location.</td></tr><tr><td>Trap and hidden-space search</td><td>Describe where and how you search; combine Perception, Investigation, tools, familiar angles, and magic.</td><td>Rolling generically from the wrong location and treating one familiar pass as complete clearance.</td></tr><tr><td>Light and visibility</td><td>Darkvision, mundane light, controlled light sources, familiar scouting, See Invisibility/True Seeing when justified.</td><td>Revealing the party from extreme distance or assuming Darkvision removes all visual disadvantage.</td></tr><tr><td>Doors and barriers</td><td>Listen, inspect hinges/frames, identify magic, use tools, bypass, then force only when consequences are acceptable.</td><td>Casting loudly or breaking the door before checking alarms, occupants, and structural effects.</td></tr><tr><td>Loot transport</td><td>Identify value, evidence, weight, ownership, hazards, and extraction route before loading.</td><td>Turning mobility and stealth into a carrying-capacity crisis for low-value objects.</td></tr><tr><td>Rest location</td><td>Concealment, cover, defensible approach, escape route, watch plan, and spellbook protection.</td><td>Choosing comfort over security or assuming Tiny Hut removes every danger.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Environmental hazard matrix

<!-- source:B00612 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00612.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Environment</th><th scope="col">Primary threat</th><th scope="col">Wizard preparation</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Darkness / obscurity</td><td>Lost sight, hidden enemies, blocked targeted spells, separated party.</td><td>Light discipline, familiar angles, area control, detection, blindsight/truesight sources where earned, and a verbal regroup point.</td></tr><tr><td>Underwater</td><td>Breathing, slow swimming, rough-water checks, verbal/component questions, ranged/weapon limitations set by the active rules.</td><td>Water Breathing, movement plan, secured components, tether/signals, extraction route, and spells verified for underwater use.</td></tr><tr><td>Vertical terrain / falling</td><td>Forced movement, lost concentration, inaccessible objectives, and fall damage up to 20d6.</td><td>Feather Fall access, Fly/Levitate/teleport, anchors and rope, spread formation, and a plan for incapacitated flyers.</td></tr><tr><td>Suffocation / choking</td><td>After breath runs out, repeated Exhaustion can kill quickly.</td><td>Leave the hazard, restore breathable space, use Water Breathing or movement, and do not rely on ordinary healing to solve the air problem.</td></tr><tr><td>Heat, cold, storms, or hazardous terrain</td><td>Exhaustion, visibility, movement, lost supplies, and interrupted rests under campaign rules.</td><td>Protective gear, shelter, route timing, resistance when relevant, scouting, extra supplies, and fewer optional tests.</td></tr><tr><td>Difficult terrain</td><td>Reduced movement and failed retreat; melee ally cannot reach threats or rescue.</td><td>Phantom Steed where legal, Fly, teleportation, forced movement, alternate routes, and control that punishes pursuers.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar environment</td><td>Unknown traits, blocked travel, hostile magic, time effects, and unavailable components.</td><td>Research, planar languages/contacts, Plane Shift focus requirements, return coordinates, and redundant escape methods.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Falling and flight protocol

<!-- source:B00615 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00615.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Check</th><th scope="col">Procedure</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before ascent</td><td>Identify duration, concentration, hover, carrying limits, destination, ranged threats, and what happens if Speed becomes 0 or the flyer is Incapacitated or Prone.</td></tr><tr><td>During flight</td><td>Avoid stacking the whole party vertically over one hazard; keep landing options and reaction access; watch concentration damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Fall begins</td><td>Use Feather Fall or another legal reaction/feature immediately; falling normally deals 1d6 bludgeoning per 10 feet to a maximum of 20d6 and usually leaves the creature Prone.</td></tr><tr><td>Ally cannot fly</td><td>Use a platform, mount, rope, transformation, teleport, or separate route rather than abandoning rescue range.</td></tr><tr><td>Landing</td><td>Choose cover and exit space, not merely the closest square; a safe landing that enters a kill zone is not safe.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Expedition preparation by tier

<!-- source:B00617 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00617.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Priority</th><th scope="col">Carry or prepare</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Always</td><td>Light, rope, containers, writing materials, food/water appropriate to the campaign, backup focus/pouch, accessible healing, spellbook protection, and a clear retreat direction.</td></tr><tr><td>Dungeon</td><td>Thieves’ Tools access, chalk/markers, mirrors or poles, wedges, noise/light discipline, familiar plan, Rope Trick/Tiny Hut when appropriate.</td></tr><tr><td>Wilderness</td><td>Maps, guide/navigation support, shelter, weather gear, mounts or Phantom Steed, Water Breathing/Fly as needed, and slow-pace scouting when danger outweighs speed.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar / remote</td><td>Return method, focus/components, Sending or Telepathy plan, destination research, emergency rations, language support, and redundant transport.</td></tr><tr><td>High-value recovery</td><td>Carrying capacity, secure containers, Identify, evidence handling, ownership, transport, guards, and where the loot can actually be sold.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Teleportation and route safety

<!-- source:B00619 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00619.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Stage</th><th scope="col">Best practice</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Research</td><td>Confirm destination familiarity, legal access, hazards, wards, arrival space, and a trusted local contact.</td></tr><tr><td>Reconnaissance</td><td>Use mundane scouting, familiar/Arcane Eye, divination, maps, or prior travel before committing the party and valuable cargo.</td></tr><tr><td>Arrival</td><td>Arrive with formation, reactions, cover plan, and immediate retreat option; teleportation does not guarantee a safe initiative state.</td></tr><tr><td>Network</td><td>Protect circles, sigils, addresses, keys, schedules, and users as strategic infrastructure rather than public trivia.</td></tr><tr><td>Failure</td><td>Keep a secondary destination, return spell, mundane route, and enough resources to survive being separated or displaced.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
