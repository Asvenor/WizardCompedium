## Simple conversion rules - no calculator

<!-- source:B00730 -->
- For combat movement: count squares first. 1 square = 5 ft = about 1.5 m.

<!-- source:B00731 -->
- For spell ranges: divide feet by 5 to get squares. 30 ft = 6 squares, 60 ft = 12 squares, 120 ft = 24 squares.

<!-- source:B00732 -->
- For weight: 10 lb is about 4.5 kg; 100 lb is about 45 kg. Use the table, do not calculate exact decimals.

<!-- source:B00733 -->
- For table talk: say both when needed. Example: “The spell reaches 60 ft, so 12 Felder, about 18 meters.”
