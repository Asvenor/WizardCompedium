## Concentration replacement test

<!-- source:B00743 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00743.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Question</th><th scope="col">Keep the current spell when...</th><th scope="col">Replace it when...</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Is it still removing meaningful enemy actions?</td><td>The affected enemies remain unable to contribute efficiently.</td><td>The enemy has escaped, adapted, or become irrelevant to the objective.</td></tr><tr><td>Is concentration under real pressure?</td><td>Cover, distance, defenses and positioning keep the risk acceptable.</td><td>The spell is likely to end before creating another valuable turn.</td></tr><tr><td>Does the party need another function more?</td><td>Control already supplies the mobility, defense or isolation required.</td><td>Rescue, flight, protection or a different form of control now decides the objective.</td></tr><tr><td>Can non-concentration actions finish the job?</td><td>Cantrips, movement, Dodge, items and allies can exploit the advantage.</td><td>Only a different concentration spell can answer the new threat.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Reaction competition matrix

<!-- source:B00745 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00745.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Reaction</th><th scope="col">Primary value</th><th scope="col">Reserve it when...</th><th scope="col">Main competition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Counterspell</td><td>Stops a spell whose consequence is worse than preserving another Reaction.</td><td>Enemy casting can kill, remove allies, break control or decide the objective.</td><td>Shield, Absorb Elements and subclass Reactions.</td></tr><tr><td>Shield</td><td>Turns qualifying attacks into misses and can protect concentration.</td><td>The AC increase prevents meaningful damage or a dangerous concentration check.</td><td>Counterspell and subclass Reactions.</td></tr><tr><td>Absorb Elements</td><td>Reduces a large instance of eligible elemental damage.</td><td>The reduction changes survival or concentration odds materially.</td><td>Counterspell and Shield.</td></tr><tr><td>Chronal Shift</td><td>Forces a reroll on a decisive attack, check or save.</td><td>A limited-use correction has greater value than the defensive spell you might need later.</td><td>All other Reactions.</td></tr><tr><td>Convergent Future</td><td>Forces an encounter-defining result at an Exhaustion cost.</td><td>The result is worth both the Reaction and the lasting cost.</td><td>All other Reactions and future endurance.</td></tr><tr><td>Song of Defense</td><td>Trades a spell slot and Reaction for damage reduction while Bladesong is active.</td><td>The prevented damage exceeds the likely value of Shield or Counterspell.</td><td>Shield, Counterspell and Absorb Elements.</td></tr><tr><td>War Caster opportunity spell</td><td>Punishes movement with an eligible spell.</td><td>No more important Reaction is likely before your next turn.</td><td>Every defensive and anti-caster Reaction.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
