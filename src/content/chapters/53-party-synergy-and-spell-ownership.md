Optimization improves when each effect has one owner, one backup, and a declared handoff condition. The Wizard should solve the encounter's hardest problem, not duplicate an ally's reliable job.

<!-- source:B00818 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00818" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Party asset</th><th scope="col">Wizard normally owns</th><th scope="col">Ally normally owns</th><th scope="col">Best handoff rule</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Frontliner / defender</td><td>Terrain, escape lanes, anti-swarm control</td><td>Body blocking, threat presence, routine durability</td><td>Wizard disengages from control only when the line can hold.</td></tr><tr><td>Single-target striker</td><td>Advantage windows, target isolation, defense removal</td><td>Damage conversion on priority target</td><td>Striker calls target; Wizard confirms save/position route.</td></tr><tr><td>Healer / support</td><td>Prevention, extraction, encounter shaping</td><td>Hit-point restoration and party-wide boosts</td><td>Wizard spends rescue magic when waiting loses a turn or life.</td></tr><tr><td>Scout</td><td>Remote sensing, magical access, escape</td><td>Repeated low-cost reconnaissance</td><td>Scout leads until magical risk or sealed access appears.</td></tr><tr><td>Controller</td><td>Large geometry changes or counter-caster lane</td><td>Secondary zone and forced movement</td><td>Only one major zone unless the overlap creates a named payoff.</td></tr><tr><td>Face</td><td>Information, translation, magical leverage</td><td>Conversation, trust, routine social checks</td><td>Magic enters after consent, failure, or explicit escalation.</td></tr><tr><td>Summons / pets</td><td>Command economy, terrain, protection</td><td>Routine bodies and attacks</td><td>Cap table load before adding another creature.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
