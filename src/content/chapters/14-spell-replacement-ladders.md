A later spell does not automatically erase an earlier one. These ladders show how the function expands across tiers and when the lower-level option remains more efficient.

<!-- source:B00180 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00180.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Function</th><th scope="col">Progression ladder</th><th scope="col">What remains true</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Battlefield control</td><td>Sleep → Web → Hypnotic Pattern / Slow / Sleet Storm → Banishment / Polymorph → Wall of Force → Forcecage / Maze</td><td>Web remains efficient in confined terrain; Slow works when charm is unreliable; Sleet Storm remains elite anti-caster pressure.</td></tr><tr><td>Defense and escape</td><td>Mage Armor / Shield → Mirror Image / Misty Step → Greater Invisibility / Resilient Sphere → Contingency / Globe → Foresight</td><td>Low-level reactions and movement stay relevant because they are cheaper and often non-concentration.</td></tr><tr><td>Mobility</td><td>Misty Step → Fly → Dimension Door → Phantom Steed / Teleportation Circle → Teleport / Plane Shift</td><td>Misty Step remains the emergency tool; Phantom Steed is strategic speed, not tactical teleportation.</td></tr><tr><td>Information</td><td>Find Familiar / Detect Magic / Identify → Locate Object → Arcane Eye → Divination / Contact Other Plane → True Seeing / Telepathy</td><td>Ritual and familiar information remain efficient even after high-level divination arrives.</td></tr><tr><td>Damage</td><td>Cantrips → Thunderwave / Shatter → Fireball → Synaptic Static → Disintegrate → Meteor Swarm</td><td>Damage only climbs the priority ladder when it removes meaningful enemy actions or covers a control-resistant encounter.</td></tr><tr><td>Strategic preparation</td><td>Glyph of Warding / Planar Binding → Contingency → Simulacrum → Clone / Demiplane → Wish</td><td>Each step increases cost, setup, table pressure, and campaign impact.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Control comparison shortcuts

<!-- source:B00182 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00182.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Decision</th><th scope="col">Prefer the first option when...</th><th scope="col">Prefer the second option when...</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Hypnotic Pattern vs Slow</td><td>Valid targets are grouped and charm is reliable.</td><td>Charm immunity, ally-friendly targeting, or repeated saves matter.</td></tr><tr><td>Sleet Storm vs Counterspell</td><td>You can disrupt sight, concentration, and movement across multiple casters.</td><td>One immediate spell must be stopped now.</td></tr><tr><td>Banishment vs Polymorph</td><td>Removal from the field is the priority and the target is valid.</td><td>You need ally rescue, HP conversion, or broader transformation utility.</td></tr><tr><td>Telekinesis vs Bigby’s Hand</td><td>Repeated object/creature control and flexible checks matter.</td><td>You need a mobile, durable spell effect with multiple modes.</td></tr><tr><td>Wall of Force vs Forcecage</td><td>You want cheaper encounter splitting and concentration is acceptable.</td><td>The stronger enclosure and exact current wording justify the component and slot.</td></tr><tr><td>Shapechange vs True Polymorph</td><td>You need a caster who changes forms while retaining strategic flexibility.</td><td>Permanent or creature/object transformation is the actual objective.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Redundancy and value changers

<!-- source:B00184 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00184" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">REDUNDANCY PAIR / LADDER</th><th scope="col">VALUE CHANGER</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Hypnotic Pattern / Slow / Sleet Storm</td><td>All compete for mass-control concentration. Add redundancy when immunity profiles, friendly fire, sight, or enemy types justify it.</td></tr><tr><td>Polymorph / Banishment</td><td>Both spend concentration on a high-value single-target swing. Polymorph is broader; Banishment is cleaner removal.</td></tr><tr><td>Telekinesis / Bigby's Hand</td><td>Sustained manipulation overlaps heavily. Choose the job valued first; owning both matters more than learning both together.</td></tr><tr><td>Wall of Force / Forcecage / Maze</td><td>Hard-control ladder across tiers. Preparation pressure matters more than spellbook ownership.</td></tr><tr><td>Shapechange / True Polymorph</td><td>Both are transformation engines. Shapechange emphasizes self-flexibility; True Polymorph emphasizes target transformation and lasting outcomes under its active wording.</td></tr><tr><td>Clone + Demiplane</td><td>Long-term survival plus extradimensional infrastructure. Combination value rises sharply with downtime, wealth, and secure recovery procedures.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
