A build claim graduates from theory only when materially different sessions support its purpose, cost, failure mode, and repeatability. The standards below define the evidence required; they are reference rules, not forms to complete inside this compendium.

<!-- source:B00899 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00899" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Status</th><th scope="col">Evidence threshold</th><th scope="col">Action</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>UNTESTED</td><td>Spreadsheet/theory only</td><td>Run a representative encounter before calling it reliable.</td></tr><tr><td>ONE-SHOT</td><td>Observed once</td><td>Repeat under different map, enemy, or rest pressure.</td></tr><tr><td>CAMPAIGN TESTED</td><td>Works across at least three materially different sessions</td><td>Keep; continue checking exceptions.</td></tr><tr><td>CONDITIONAL</td><td>Strong only with stated party/map/source assumptions</td><td>Keep the conditions beside the recommendation.</td></tr><tr><td>NEEDS REVISION</td><td>Failure cost or resource use exceeds the claimed value</td><td>Change the script, build, or rating and retest.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Evidence Required for a Reliable Claim

<!-- source:B00901 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00901" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">EVIDENCE CATEGORY</th><th scope="col">REQUIRED OBSERVATION</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Build identity</td><td>Exact starting class, class order, character/Wizard level, subclass, and selectable source version.</td></tr><tr><td>Campaign scope</td><td>Enabled D&amp;D Beyond source categories plus any table ruling that materially affects the claim.</td></tr><tr><td>Party context</td><td>Composition, concentration ownership, rescue coverage, and the allies expected to convert the opening.</td></tr><tr><td>Objective and pressure</td><td>Win condition, encounter clock, map/geometry, and consequences of delay or failure.</td></tr><tr><td>Enemy evidence</td><td>Visible profile, observed defenses, counterplay, and uncertainty that existed when the choice was made.</td></tr><tr><td>Entry state</td><td>Rest state, prepared package, slots, charges, consumables, hit points, and escape reserve entering the test.</td></tr><tr><td>Predicted versus actual line</td><td>Expected opener/fallback compared with the actual opener, concentration duration, reactions, and pivots.</td></tr><tr><td>Cost and repeatability</td><td>Resources spent, turns/objectives changed, failure mode, next change, and whether a different map/enemy would alter the verdict.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Failure Interpretation Matrix

<!-- source:B00903 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00903" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">OBSERVATION</th><th scope="col">INTERPRETATION</th><th scope="col">DEFAULT RESPONSE</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Opener did not change the objective or enemy turns</td><td>The opener failed its stated job.</td><td>Move a different spell or tactic to the default ladder.</td></tr><tr><td>Concentration ended before earning its cost</td><td>Position, defense, or effect choice was mismatched.</td><td>Improve protection/position or choose a different effect.</td></tr><tr><td>Reaction missed the highest-value outcome</td><td>Reaction priority was unclear or overcommitted.</td><td>Use the <a href="/chapters/45-concentration-and-reaction-master-matrix/">Chapter 45</a> priority order before initiative.</td></tr><tr><td>Party duplicated concentration or control</td><td>Ownership was not established.</td><td>Use <a href="/chapters/53-party-synergy-and-spell-ownership/">Chapter 53</a>'s primary, backup, and handoff defaults.</td></tr><tr><td>Source/version uncertainty slowed play</td><td>The recommendation is not reproducible yet.</td><td>Apply <a href="/chapters/51-source-version-conflict-rules/">Chapter 51</a> and verify the exact active entry.</td></tr><tr><td>Fallback failed under sight, components, or resistance</td><td>The second lane shares the first lane's weakness.</td><td>Add a genuinely independent fallback.</td></tr><tr><td>Result depends on one map or enemy</td><td>The claim is conditional, not universal.</td><td>Keep the condition visible and test a contrasting case.</td></tr><tr><td>PROMOTION</td><td>Do not promote a new variant into <a href="/chapters/04-build-selection-and-priority-matrix/">Chapter 4</a>'s min-max shortlist until it passes the builder/version check and three materially different sessions, or is explicitly labeled theory/conditional.</td><td></td></tr></tbody></table></div>
