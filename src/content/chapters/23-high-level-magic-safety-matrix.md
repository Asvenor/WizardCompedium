High-level magic fails through logistics and wording more often than through raw power. Use this matrix before spending the slot, component, downtime, or political capital; it does not replace the spell entry.

<!-- source:B00371 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00371" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Spell / engine</th><th scope="col">Pre-cast gate</th><th scope="col">Main failure state</th><th scope="col">Safe operating default</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Wish</td><td>Choose ordinary spell duplication or a nonstandard request; confirm stress and future-loss rules before the latter.</td><td>A vague open request, campaign-breaking interpretation, or loss of future access.</td><td>Use legal spell duplication by default. Never use it to create Simulacrum recursion.</td></tr><tr><td>Teleport</td><td>Classify destination familiarity from evidence and prepare for every listed arrival outcome.</td><td>Mishap, wrong destination, separation, or arrival into an unsecured scene.</td><td>Carry recovery, communication, healing, and a second route; prefer prepared anchors.</td></tr><tr><td>Plane Shift</td><td>Obtain the correct tuned fork and agree on destination precision.</td><td>No legal focus, hostile arrival area, or no route home.</td><td>Research the plane, define the return plan, and keep the party physically coordinated.</td></tr><tr><td>Demiplane</td><td>Know which existing door can be reopened and how occupants leave.</td><td>Storage without retrieval, trapped allies, or dependence on one caster.</td><td>Maintain an inventory, redundant access plan, breathable assumptions, and no unlabeled doors.</td></tr><tr><td>Clone</td><td>Secure a 1,000+ gp diamond, a 2,000+ gp vessel, the creature or flesh sample, the 120-day maturation period, and a hidden location.</td><td>The clone is found, destroyed, unavailable, or creates identity and equipment gaps.</td><td>Treat it as long-term infrastructure, not immediate combat insurance.</td></tr><tr><td>Magic Jar</td><td>Secure the original body and container; define distance, possession, and ending states.</td><td>Container destruction, body death, forced return failure, or hostile control of the site.</td><td>Use a guarded site, explicit abort conditions, and a recovery team. See <a href="/chapters/22-optional-advanced-appendix-campaign-warping-wizard-engines/">Chapter 22</a>.</td></tr><tr><td>Antimagic Field</td><td>List which personal spells, items, movement, communication, and escape tools it suppresses.</td><td>The Wizard disables their own survival kit and cannot end the encounter.</td><td>Enter only with mundane protection, ally coordination, and a route out.</td></tr><tr><td>Contingency</td><td>Write a perceivable, objective trigger and test the loaded spell's target and timing.</td><td>Ambiguous trigger, premature activation, or concentration replacing the spell already winning the fight.</td><td>Use a narrow emergency trigger and rehearse the consequence, not only the activation.</td></tr><tr><td>Glyph of Warding</td><td>Fix the legal location, trigger, stored effect, cost, and movement restriction.</td><td>The glyph is moved, discovered, dispelled, triggered by the wrong creature, or never matters.</td><td>Build for one known defensive objective and document the trigger in plain language.</td></tr><tr><td>Simulacrum</td><td>Secure the casting window, component, template state, finite resource budget, and repair plan.</td><td>The copy wastes nonrenewable resources, is destroyed, or relies on prohibited recursion.</td><td>Assign a role and slot budget. The copy never creates another Simulacrum.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar Binding</td><td>Keep an eligible creature present through the casting and write commands that can actually be followed.</td><td>Containment fails, the save succeeds, duration assumptions are wrong, or commands backfire.</td><td>Use negotiated service when possible; confirm the exact summon/binding interaction.</td></tr><tr><td>Shapechange / True Polymorph</td><td>Use a legally known form and verify retained statistics, concentration, control, and ending state.</td><td>Illegal form knowledge, lost concentration, misunderstood spellcasting, or uncontrolled created creature.</td><td>Prepare a small verified form library and a safe reversion plan.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 23A. Polymorph Form Selection — Ally, Self, and Hostile Targets

<!-- source:B00374 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00374" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">CURRENT ENTRY GATE</th><th scope="col">This section uses the active 2024 Polymorph text and current selectable Beast stat blocks. If D&amp;D Beyond exposes a different selectable version, use that entry and rerank the form by the same tests; selectability is the campaign's green light.</th></tr></thead><tbody></tbody></table></div>

<!-- source:B00375 -->
Read the rules card first. The tier lists rank jobs, not flavor: a lower-tier form can be correct when terrain, target level, available space, or the objective makes its special movement decisive.

<!-- source:B00376 -->
### Polymorph rules card

<!-- source:B00377 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00377" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Check</th><th scope="col">Current 2024 rule</th><th scope="col">Optimization consequence</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Legal form</td><td>Choose a Beast whose CR is no higher than the target's CR or level.</td><td>Check the target, form creature type, CR, and active D&amp;D Beyond entry before casting.</td></tr><tr><td>Saving throw</td><td>The target makes a Wisdom save; any creature can choose to fail a save.</td><td>A willing ally or the caster can accept the spell. Hostile use still needs the failed save.</td></tr><tr><td>Statistics</td><td>The Beast stat block replaces game statistics; alignment, personality, creature type, Hit Points, and Hit Dice remain.</td><td>Use the form's movement, senses, AC, actions, ability modifiers, and saves while transformed.</td></tr><tr><td>Durability</td><td>The target gains Temporary Hit Points equal to the Beast's Hit Points; the spell ends when none remain.</td><td>Form HP is a buffer, not a second current-HP pool. Reversion can happen earlier if Concentration ends.</td></tr><tr><td>Action limits</td><td>The form's anatomy limits actions; the target cannot speak or cast spells, and melded gear gives no benefit.</td><td>Confirm that the chosen body can perform the mission without speech, hands, items, or spellcasting.</td></tr><tr><td>Self-cast</td><td>The ongoing Concentration carries into the form; full-stat replacement means using the form's Constitution save.</td><td>Self-Polymorph is an emergency body, escape tool, or utility answer—not the Wizard's default damage plan.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

### Hostile-use operating sequence

<!-- source:B00386 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00386" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Step</th><th scope="col">Do this before the target can matter again</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1. Win the save</td><td>Confirm range, sight, Wisdom-save pressure, Magic Resistance, Legendary Resistance, and whether a different Concentration spell is more reliable.</td></tr><tr><td>2. Have containment ready</td><td>Use a closed, smooth, non-damaging container or a sealed room. Do not invent the container after the spell lands.</td></tr><tr><td>3. Protect the effect</td><td>Do not attack the form or leave it inside damaging terrain. Most control forms have 1-2 THP, so trivial damage ends the spell.</td></tr><tr><td>4. Plan reversion</td><td>The hostile target retains its own Hit Points. Cover the container, concentration, exit routes, and the exact moment the original body can return.</td></tr><tr><td>5. Keep the objective visible</td><td>Polymorph removes one creature only while Concentration lasts. Use the time to escape, isolate the battlefield, secure the objective, or prepare a legal follow-up.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
