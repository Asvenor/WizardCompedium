<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00022.T001"><strong>OPTIMIZATION VERDICT</strong><p>Artificer 1 / Wizard 19 is the flagship optimizer chassis when multiclassing and Eberron are selectable. Chronurgy is the default subclass; Diviner and Illusionist are supported variants. The chassis preserves full level-20 spell-slot progression while adding medium armor, a Shield, Constitution-save proficiency, tools, healing, and crafting utility. Wizard access arrives one character level later. Use Pure Wizard only when multiclassing is unavailable.</p></aside>

## Spellbook and preparation counts

<!-- source:B00028 -->
Spellbook: Level 1 = 6 Wizard spells; every Wizard level after 1 = +2 Wizard spells; extra spellbook spells come from scrolls, enemy books, shops, rewards, or downtime.

<!-- source:B00029 -->
Prepared spells: Not based on Intelligence in 2024. Counts by Wizard level: 1-4 = 4/5/6/7; 5-8 = 9/10/11/12; 9-12 = 14/15/16/16; 13-16 = 17/18/19/21; 17-20 = 22/23/24/25.

<!-- source:B00030 -->
Wizard Ritual Adept: a Ritual-tagged Wizard spell in your spellbook need not be prepared, but you must read from the book while casting it as a Ritual.

## Core min-max principles

<!-- source:B00033 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00033.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Principle</th><th scope="col">Practical rule</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>First turn value</td><td>Initiative matters because control before enemy actions prevents more damage than later recovery.</td></tr><tr><td>One concentration slot</td><td>Choose the spell that wins the current problem, then protect it instead of replacing it for marginal damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Reaction discipline</td><td>Do not spend the reaction on small value when Shield, Counterspell, Chronal Shift, Silvery Barbs, Absorb Elements, or Song of Defense could decide the round.</td></tr><tr><td>No-save pressure</td><td>Against legendary defenses or uncertain saves, encounter splitting, terrain, walls, buffs, and movement control become premium.</td></tr><tr><td>Resource conversion</td><td>Rituals, scrolls, items, familiars, tools, and downtime turn gold and time into spell-slot preservation.</td></tr><tr><td>Damage threshold</td><td>Damage is correct when it removes meaningful enemy actions now. Medium damage that changes nothing is usually a weak Wizard turn.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
