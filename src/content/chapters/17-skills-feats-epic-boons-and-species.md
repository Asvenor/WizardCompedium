## Wizard Skill Tier List

<!-- source:B00210 -->
DEFAULT ORDER · Start with Arcana and Perception; add Stealth next, then choose Investigation or Insight according to the party gap. Optional B-tier skills are campaign or route upgrades, not mandatory taxes.

<!-- source:B00211 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00211" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Tier</th><th scope="col">Skill</th><th scope="col">Why it matters</th><th scope="col">When to prioritize</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>S</td><td>Arcana</td><td>The Wizard's signature knowledge skill: spells, magical effects, rituals, items, planar phenomena, and magical research.</td><td>Take by default. It is the most natural Intelligence check to own and the hardest Wizard-shaped gap to justify leaving open.</td></tr><tr><td>S</td><td>Perception</td><td>Find ambushes, hidden creatures, environmental danger, and important details before the party spends actions or spell slots fixing the problem.</td><td>Take by default in small parties. Wisdom may be secondary, but proficiency protects the most frequently consequential awareness check.</td></tr><tr><td>S</td><td>Stealth</td><td>Improves scouting, pre-combat positioning, infiltration, and the party's ability to avoid an encounter instead of paying resources for it.</td><td>Highest third pick for a small party, an initiative-focused controller, or an Illusionist/infiltration route.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Investigation</td><td>Supports deliberate searches, deductions, documents, mechanisms, traps, and evidence that something—including an illusion—does not add up.</td><td>Prioritize when the campaign rewards active searching and the party lacks another Intelligence investigator.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Insight</td><td>Reads motives, pressure, deception, and negotiation risk. It covers a social-information weakness that the spellbook cannot always solve safely.</td><td>Prioritize over Investigation when intrigue, uncertain allies, bargains, or social horror are frequent.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Nature · optional</td><td>Covers beasts, plants, terrain, weather, and natural hazards; it uses the Wizard’s Intelligence while filling an exploration gap that magic does not always solve efficiently.</td><td>Rises to A in wilderness, survival, monster-research, harvesting, or exploration campaigns when the party lacks a Druid or Ranger-shaped expert.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Religion · optional</td><td>Useful for cults, rites, undead, fiends, celestials, sacred sites, and planar traditions—subjects that often carry tactical consequences.</td><td>Rises to A in occult, undead, fiend, divine-conflict, or horror campaigns.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Deception · optional</td><td>Turns disguises, illusions, false identities, and infiltration plans into a coherent story when magic alone cannot sell the lie.</td><td>Rises to A for Illusionists and social infiltrators; falls if Charisma is very low or another party member already owns the role.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<!-- source:B00212 -->
PARTY-GAP RULE · Do not duplicate a skill merely because it ranks highly. After Arcana, cover the party's missing information and scouting jobs before adding a second specialist to a role already solved.

## Wizard Epic Boon Tier List

<!-- source:B00214 -->
This is a Wizard-specific ranking, not an all-class ranking. It values survival against tier-4 control, action-safe positioning, concentration protection, broad utility, and effects that matter every encounter. The normal expectation is one Epic Boon at level 19, so a narrow theme benefit must beat universal alternatives to rank highly.

<!-- source:B00215 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00215"><strong>ANCHOR VERDICT</strong><p>Boon of the Night Spirit remains S tier. In dim light or darkness, resistance to every damage type except Psychic and Radiant is the strongest verified always-on defensive package in the core list; the Bonus Action invisibility is additional utility, not the main reason it wins. Build a visibility and lighting plan before relying on it.</p></aside>
