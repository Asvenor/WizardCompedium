## Hands, focus, and component interface

<!-- source:B00290 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00290" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">State</th><th scope="col">What remains possible</th><th scope="col">Fast ruling check</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Empty hand + component pouch</td><td>The free hand can access eligible materials and perform Somatic components.</td><td>Priced or consumed materials are never replaced by the pouch.</td></tr><tr><td>Arcane focus held</td><td>The focus replaces only eligible Material components; the same hand can usually perform Somatic gestures when the spell also has Material.</td><td>A spell with Somatic but no Material component may still require a genuinely free hand.</td></tr><tr><td>Shield or object in one hand</td><td>Use the other hand if it is free and the build has the required proficiency and casting access.</td><td>Do not assume War Caster, a weapon focus, or a class feature that the character does not actually have.</td></tr><tr><td>Both hands occupied</td><td>Release, stow, or otherwise free a hand only when the action and object-interaction rules permit it.</td><td>Identify what is held, what can be released, and what must be recovered afterward.</td></tr><tr><td>Hands bound or physically controlled</td><td>Only spells whose real components can still be provided remain legal.</td><td>Grappled alone does not automatically disable hands; restraints and effects use their exact text.</td></tr><tr><td>Silenced, gagged, or unable to speak</td><td>Spells without Verbal components may remain available.</td><td>Normal telepathy does not replace a Verbal component.</td></tr><tr><td>Priced or consumed component</td><td>The exact object and quantity must be available as the spell requires.</td><td>Confirm whether the component is consumed before counting it as reusable equipment.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Sight, path, cover, and areas

<!-- source:B00292 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00292" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Spell wording asks for...</th><th scope="col">Required check</th><th scope="col">Common mistake</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>A creature or point you can see</td><td>The caster must actually see the chosen target or point when the spell is cast.</td><td>Knowing the square or hearing the creature is not the same as seeing it.</td></tr><tr><td>A target within range</td><td>Measure from the legal origin and also verify a clear path under the active rules.</td><td>Range alone does not ignore Total Cover.</td></tr><tr><td>An attack roll</td><td>Resolve visibility, cover, range, disadvantage, and target eligibility before rolling.</td><td>An unseen target is not automatically untargetable if the spell does not require sight.</td></tr><tr><td>An area point</td><td>Choose the point, confirm it is legal, then trace the area and blocked portions from that origin.</td><td>Do not select affected creatures first and invent the origin afterward.</td></tr><tr><td>A creature you know</td><td>Use only the knowledge requirement actually written in the spell.</td><td>Do not add a sight requirement—or remove one—from memory.</td></tr><tr><td>A willing creature</td><td>The creature's willingness must exist at the moment required by the effect.</td><td>Charm, friendliness, or silence does not automatically equal consent.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Ready, reaction, and timing interface

<!-- source:B00294 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00294" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Question</th><th scope="col">Operational answer</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Can it be readied?</td><td>Only use the Ready procedure with a spell whose casting time qualifies. Cast and spend the resource now, maintain Concentration, then release with the Reaction when the perceivable trigger occurs.</td></tr><tr><td>What if the trigger never occurs?</td><td>The held spell is lost when the legal release window ends; preserve this risk in the decision.</td></tr><tr><td>Can the Reaction wait?</td><td>A Reaction spell or feature uses its written trigger and window. It cannot be banked for a later event.</td></tr><tr><td>How many Reactions?</td><td>A creature normally has one Reaction before it is regained under the active rules. Shield, Counterspell, subclass features, opportunity attacks, and readied spells compete for it.</td></tr><tr><td>Does a Reaction violate the slot limit?</td><td>Apply the active one-slot-per-turn rule to the actual turn on which each spell is cast; another creature's turn is a different turn.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
