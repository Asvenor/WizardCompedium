## General opening decision tree

<!-- source:B00251 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00251.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Question</th><th scope="col">If yes</th><th scope="col">If no</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Can one spell disable multiple enemies?</td><td>Use Web / Hypnotic Pattern / Sleet Storm / Slow.</td><td>Look for encounter splitting.</td></tr><tr><td>Is there a dangerous caster?</td><td>Counterspell discipline, Sleet Storm, line-of-sight denial.</td><td>Control the biggest action-economy threat.</td></tr><tr><td>Is the party about to be surrounded?</td><td>Wall of Force / Sleet Storm / Web / movement denial.</td><td>Preserve resources and position.</td></tr><tr><td>Is damage enough to end the problem now?</td><td>Use Fireball/Synaptic Static/Meteor Swarm if it actually removes bodies.</td><td>Do not waste turns on medium damage.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Party shape: two melee + one ranged

<!-- source:B00253 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00253.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Round</th><th scope="col">Wizard plan</th><th scope="col">Avoid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before fight</td><td>Tell melee where control zones may go. Scout if possible.</td><td>Starting with everyone clumped.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 1</td><td>Use control that melee can exploit without blocking their approach.</td><td>Fog/Sleet placement that ruins your own party.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 2</td><td>Protect concentration, keep ranged sight lines open, Counterspell important magic.</td><td>Chasing damage while control is winning.</td></tr><tr><td>Cleanup</td><td>Cantrips, safe damage, reposition, utility.</td><td>Breaking your own control too early.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Party shape: one melee (two-player party)

<!-- source:B00255 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00255.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Situation</th><th scope="col">Wizard plan</th><th scope="col">Reason</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before fight</td><td>Scout whenever possible, agree on a retreat route, and decide which chokepoint or isolated target the melee ally will hold.</td><td>With only two characters, surprise or bad positioning can end the encounter before recovery is possible.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 1</td><td>Immediately reduce participating enemies with Web, Hypnotic Pattern, Sleet Storm, Slow, or encounter splitting. Leave one reachable target for the melee ally.</td><td>One melee character cannot hold an entire room, so removing enemy actions matters more than early damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 2</td><td>Protect concentration, stay mobile, and support focus fire with forced movement, a familiar, or safe damage. Do not stand beside the melee ally unless the terrain demands it.</td><td>The pair wins by controlling distance and deleting isolated enemies, not by forming a normal frontline and backline.</td></tr><tr><td>Ally overwhelmed or downed</td><td>Rescue before dealing damage: use Dimension Door, Vortex Warp if allowed, Otiluke's Resilient Sphere, Polymorph, a wall, or movement control to stop the collapse.</td><td>When the only ally falls, every remaining enemy can immediately turn on the Wizard.</td></tr><tr><td>Boss fight or retreat</td><td>Isolate minions, buff the melee carry when efficient, reserve a defensive Reaction, and keep one reliable escape option available.</td><td>A two-person party has no redundancy. Survival, recovery, and retreat are part of optimal play.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Party shape: one melee + one ranged

<!-- source:B00257 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00257.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Round</th><th scope="col">Wizard plan</th><th scope="col">Reason</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before fight</td><td>Identify who can bypass the melee or reach the ranged ally.</td><td>Small parties collapse if backline gets jumped.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 1</td><td>Split the encounter or reduce enemy numbers immediately.</td><td>One melee cannot hold an entire room.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 2</td><td>Misty Step/cover if pressured; preserve ranged ally lines.</td><td>Do not block your own archer/caster.</td></tr><tr><td>Boss fight</td><td>Buff melee carry, isolate minions, Counterspell boss magic.</td><td>Your job is structure and survival.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Bladesinger combat mode

<!-- source:B00260 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00260.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">State</th><th scope="col">Action logic</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Normal dangerous fight</td><td>Cast the best Wizard control spell first. Bladesong/defense supports the Wizard plan; it does not replace it.</td></tr><tr><td>Safe skirmish</td><td>Use Bladesong, defensive spell, and melee/cantrip routine only if the enemy cannot punish you.</td></tr><tr><td>Focused by enemies</td><td>Shield, Mirror Image, Misty Step, cover. Do not stay in melee to prove a point.</td></tr><tr><td>Concentration already winning</td><td>Stay alive, reposition, cantrip, Dodge if needed. The spell is already doing the real work.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Wizard Tank combat mode

<!-- source:B00262 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00262.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">State</th><th scope="col">Action logic</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before fight</td><td>Pre-position, know cover, scout, check escape route.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 1</td><td>Cast the spell that makes enemies waste turns: Web, Sleet Storm, Slow, Wall of Force.</td></tr><tr><td>When focused</td><td>Shield if it prevents a hit, Mirror Image/Misty Step if pressure continues, maintain concentration.</td></tr><tr><td>Against casters</td><td>Counterspell only important spells. Use Sleet Storm, Dispel Magic, Globe, and line-of-sight denial.</td></tr><tr><td>Low resources</td><td>Cantrips, rituals, positioning, familiar, mundane gear. Do not spend slots on low-value damage.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Reaction priority

<!-- source:B00264 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00264.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Priority</th><th scope="col">Use reaction when</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Counterspell</td><td>The enemy spell would swing the fight, break your plan, or kill someone.</td></tr><tr><td>Shield</td><td>It turns a hit into a miss or protects concentration from a meaningful hit.</td></tr><tr><td>Absorb Elements</td><td>Big elemental damage matters enough to spend the reaction.</td></tr><tr><td>Silvery Barbs / Chronal Shift when selectable</td><td>It flips a critical save, crit, boss roll, or fight-defining moment.</td></tr><tr><td>Do nothing</td><td>Saving the reaction is better than spending it on small value.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Expanded encounter answer matrix

<!-- source:B00266 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00266.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Encounter</th><th scope="col">Observe</th><th scope="col">Primary answer</th><th scope="col">Fallback</th><th scope="col">Avoid</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Horde</td><td>Many low-HP bodies or clustered enemies.</td><td>Area control or damage that removes actions immediately.</td><td>Choke point, difficult terrain, repeated cantrip cleanup.</td><td>Single-target save spell on one weak enemy.</td></tr><tr><td>Solo boss</td><td>Legendary defenses, high action value.</td><td>No-save splitting, buffs, terrain, coordinated save pressure.</td><td>Remove escape routes or support the party carry.</td><td>Solo premium save spell with no follow-up plan.</td></tr><tr><td>Boss plus minions</td><td>Support actions and crossfire.</td><td>Split the encounter; remove the side that creates more total actions.</td><td>Area control minions while allies pressure the boss.</td><td>Automatically targeting the largest creature.</td></tr><tr><td>Enemy caster</td><td>Visible casting, concentration, line of sight.</td><td>Sleet Storm, line-of-sight denial, Counterspell discipline, Dispel Magic.</td><td>Force movement, focus damage, cover.</td><td>Countering every low-value spell.</td></tr><tr><td>Flyers</td><td>Altitude, ranged attacks, landing options.</td><td>Flight, forced movement, ranged control, ground denial at landing points.</td><td>Cover and ready actions.</td><td>Ground-only control with no follow-up.</td></tr><tr><td>Archers</td><td>Open sight lines and spread formation.</td><td>Fog, walls, cover creation, forced movement, closing geometry.</td><td>Mobility and prone/cover play.</td><td>Standing still to trade damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Invisible enemies</td><td>Sound, tracks, spell evidence, area attacks.</td><td>See Invisibility/True Seeing when justified, area control, reveal effects.</td><td>Ready actions and protect the objective.</td><td>Pretending invisibility means impossible to interact with.</td></tr><tr><td>Teleporters</td><td>Repeated bypass of frontlines.</td><td>Control destination space, ready no-save answers, protect backline.</td><td>Spread and reserve reactions.</td><td>Static plan that assumes the enemy stays contained.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
