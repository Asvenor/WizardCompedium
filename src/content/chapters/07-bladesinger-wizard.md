## 2025 Bladesinger feature-use overview (2024 rules)

<!-- source:B00102 -->
Use the final Heroes of Faerûn entry in D&D Beyond as the rules authority. This compact table is an at-table reminder of the revised feature structure; if any exact wording differs, D&D Beyond wins.

<!-- source:B00103 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00103.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Level</th><th scope="col">Feature</th><th scope="col">Quick rules reminder</th><th scope="col">How to use it</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Wiz 3</td><td>Bladesong</td><td>Bonus Action; lasts up to 1 minute. No armor or Shield. It ends early if you are Incapacitated, equip armor/a Shield, or make a two-handed weapon attack. Uses equal your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1), return on a Long Rest, and one expended use returns when you use Arcane Recovery.</td><td>Adds INT to AC and concentration saves, raises Speed, and lets proficient weapon attacks use INT. Activate when the defense, movement, or concentration protection will matter—not for trivial cleanup.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 3</td><td>Training in War and Song</td><td>Broad melee martial weapon training excluding Heavy/Two-Handed weapons, a qualifying melee weapon can serve as the Wizard focus, and one listed physical/social skill proficiency.</td><td>This removes much of the old DEX pressure from weapon accuracy, but DEX still affects base AC, initiative, and saves.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 6</td><td>Extra Attack</td><td>Two attacks with the Attack action; one attack can be replaced by a Wizard cantrip with an action casting time.</td><td>Default safe routine: weapon attack + control/damage cantrip. Do not give up a stronger leveled control spell merely to use this feature.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 10</td><td>Song of Defense</td><td>While Bladesong is active, use a Reaction and a spell slot to reduce incoming damage by five times the slot level.</td><td>Competes with Shield, Counterspell, Absorb Elements, and other reactions. Spend it when the prevented damage protects concentration or avoids going down.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 14</td><td>Song of Victory</td><td>After casting an action spell, make one weapon attack as a Bonus Action.</td><td>This rewards Wizard-first turns: cast the spell that wins the problem, then take the free weapon follow-up if positioning is safe.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<!-- source:B00104 -->
Bladesinger warning: the revised subclass is stronger at using Intelligence for weapon play, but the core rule remains unchanged—your leveled Wizard spells usually matter more than one extra melee turn.
