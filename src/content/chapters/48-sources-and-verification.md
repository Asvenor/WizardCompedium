## Feature completeness standard

<!-- source:B00776 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00776" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Every feature reference should answer</th><th scope="col">Why it matters</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Source and character level</td><td>Prevents edition mixing and incorrect subclass timing.</td></tr><tr><td>Action, Bonus Action, Reaction, or no-action timing</td><td>Shows what the feature competes with during play.</td></tr><tr><td>Uses and recovery</td><td>Separates renewable routine from limited emergency power.</td></tr><tr><td>Target, range, save/check, and duration when relevant</td><td>Prevents a powerful summary from hiding the real gate.</td></tr><tr><td>Concentration, Reaction, slot, material, or Exhaustion conflict</td><td>Exposes the actual opportunity cost.</td></tr><tr><td>Best use, failure state, and conservative default</td><td>Turns feature text into a complete operating decision.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## D&D Beyond verification ledger

<!-- source:B00778 -->
A rule is green-lit when the exact option is selectable. Verification records which entry was used; it does not add another approval gate.

<!-- source:B00779 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00779" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Field</th><th scope="col">Record</th><th scope="col">Required action</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Option and source</td><td>Exact D&amp;D Beyond name and source label.</td><td>Do not rely on remembered print wording.</td></tr><tr><td>Builder state</td><td>Selectable / absent, plus enabled source categories.</td><td>Selectable = green light; absent = exclude until it appears.</td></tr><tr><td>Version</td><td>5.5 Core, 5.5 Expanded, 5e Core/Expanded, or Partnered.</td><td>If two versions are separately selectable, record the chosen one.</td></tr><tr><td>Verified on</td><td>Date and character-builder context.</td><td>Recheck after D&amp;D Beyond updates or a source-setting change.</td></tr><tr><td>Rules-sensitive fields</td><td>Level, casting time, range, target, save, duration, Concentration, Ritual, components, action economy.</td><td>Update every downstream table that uses a changed field.</td></tr><tr><td>Optimization impact</td><td>Affected build, tier, spell progression, item plan, or engine.</td><td>Add a changelog entry and rerun the relevant comparison.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
