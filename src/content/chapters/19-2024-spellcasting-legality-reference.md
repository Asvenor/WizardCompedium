Use this page before resolving a disputed cast. It summarizes the 2024 SRD 5.2.1 spellcasting and glossary rules; the spell's own text remains the first specific authority.

<!-- source:B00272 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00272" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Gate</th><th scope="col">2024 baseline</th><th scope="col">Fast table check</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>One slot per turn</td><td>On a turn, you can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell.</td><td>A slot spell plus a cantrip can work if only one slot is spent. A Reaction on another creature's turn is a different turn.</td></tr><tr><td>Bonus Action / Reaction</td><td>The spell's Casting Time entry defines any trigger and timing.</td><td>Do not invent a trigger or delay a Reaction beyond the listed window.</td></tr><tr><td>Ready a spell</td><td>Cast it now, spend resources now, hold it with Concentration, and release it with your Reaction before your next turn. Only an action-casting-time spell qualifies.</td><td>Existing Concentration ends when the readied spell begins. If the trigger never occurs or Concentration breaks, the spell is lost.</td></tr><tr><td>Casting 1 minute+</td><td>Take the Magic action each turn and maintain Concentration during the casting.</td><td>Interruption forces a restart; the slot is not spent when Concentration breaks before completion.</td></tr><tr><td>Verbal</td><td>The words must be spoken in a normal voice.</td><td>Gagging or magical silence blocks V components.</td></tr><tr><td>Somatic</td><td>At least one hand performs the gestures.</td><td>Confirm a usable hand before declaring the spell.</td></tr><tr><td>Material / focus</td><td>A free hand accesses materials; the same hand can perform S. A focus/pouch replaces only unpriced, unconsumed materials.</td><td>Priced or consumed materials must be present. A focus must normally be held.</td></tr><tr><td>Range</td><td>Range controls where the effect can originate; movable effects are not automatically range-tethered afterward.</td><td>Read the spell for any continuing range limit.</td></tr><tr><td>Clear path</td><td>A target cannot be behind Total Cover.</td><td>Line of sight and clear path are different tests. Apply every requirement the spell actually states.</td></tr><tr><td>Areas</td><td>The area determines the spell's targets. Total Cover can block portions of an area from its point of origin.</td><td>Choose the point first, trace blocked lines, then identify affected creatures.</td></tr><tr><td>Invalid target</td><td>The slot is still expended; the effect appears to fail as described by the invalid-target rule.</td><td>Do not use failed targeting as a free creature-type detector.</td></tr><tr><td>Ritual</td><td>Add 10 minutes and spend no slot; the ritual cannot be upcast.</td><td>Wizard Ritual Adept can change preparation access; verify the class feature.</td></tr><tr><td>Dismissal</td><td>A caster can dismiss their ongoing time-span spell without an action unless Incapacitated.</td><td>This does not automatically apply to instantaneous effects or effects controlled by different text.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Concentration break matrix

<!-- source:B00274 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00274" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Event</th><th scope="col">Result</th><th scope="col">Operational consequence</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Start another concentration effect</td><td>The previous effect ends the moment the new concentration spell starts being cast or the effect is activated.</td><td>You cannot wait to see whether the new spell succeeds before dropping the old one.</td></tr><tr><td>Take damage</td><td>CON save DC 10 or half the damage, rounded down, whichever is higher; maximum DC 30.</td><td>Roll separately for separate damage instances.</td></tr><tr><td>Become Incapacitated</td><td>Concentration ends.</td><td>Paralyzed, Stunned, and Unconscious include Incapacitated; check the applied condition.</td></tr><tr><td>Die</td><td>Concentration ends.</td><td>A delayed heal does not restore the ended effect.</td></tr><tr><td>End voluntarily</td><td>No action required.</td><td>End it immediately when it obstructs rescue, movement, or a better plan.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
