## Flagship operating rules

<!-- source:B00078 -->
This is the compendium’s primary and most-used build. The Artificer level is infrastructure, not a second identity: begin durable enough to survive, acquire the hard-to-replace tools immediately, then take nineteen uninterrupted Wizard levels.

<!-- source:B00079 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00079" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Situation</th><th scope="col">Default</th><th scope="col">Failure rule / reason</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Uncertain or close terrain</td><td>Hide Armor + Shield + Thorn Whip</td><td>AC 16. Keep the second hand available for the active spell’s tool/focus/component requirement. Donning or doffing the Shield takes a Utilize action, so choose the mode before danger is in reach.</td></tr><tr><td>Secure long range</td><td>Hide Armor + Light Crossbow</td><td>AC 14. Use the 80-foot normal range to contribute without pulling a target closer. This mode assumes the Shield is not donned; switching modes takes a Utilize action.</td></tr><tr><td>Enemy reaches melee</td><td>Shield mode, reposition, or dagger</td><td>A nearby hostile can make a ranged spell attack unreliable. Create distance, protect concentration, and use the dagger only as the emergency floor.</td></tr><tr><td>Exploration / setup</td><td>Guidance + familiar/party procedure + tools</td><td>Use Guidance when timing and concentration permit. Assign who checks, who assists, what failure changes, and whether a mundane tool solves it without a slot.</td></tr><tr><td>Ally drops</td><td>Cure Wounds or Healer’s Kit</td><td>Use the spell when immediate HP and action recovery matter; use the kit to stabilize without spending the slot when the situation permits.</td></tr><tr><td>Hands and War Caster</td><td>Shield is legal before War Caster</td><td>A free hand can perform Somatic components and access materials; the same hand can handle both S and M. War Caster solves occupied-hand Somatics and improves concentration—it does not unlock Shield proficiency.</td></tr><tr><td>First upgrades</td><td>Scale Mail → project-specific kit → spellbook economy</td><td>Scale Mail raises crossbow mode to AC 16 and Shield mode to AC 18. Tinker’s Tools do not craft Scale Mail; Smith’s Tools or a qualified craftsperson are required.</td></tr><tr><td>Wizard transition</td><td>Wizard 1 at character level 2</td><td>Add the spellbook, rituals, Shield and normal Wizard attack/utility cantrips. The crossbow becomes a no-slot range fallback rather than the long-term engine.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
