## Mission-definition procedure

<!-- source:B00580 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00580.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Question</th><th scope="col">Answer before committing resources</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Win condition</td><td>What exact state ends the mission successfully: person extracted, ritual interrupted, gate held, object stolen, target captured, or party escaped?</td></tr><tr><td>Failure condition</td><td>What loss cannot be accepted: hostage death, alarm, destroyed evidence, enemy escape, timer expiration, or civilian harm?</td></tr><tr><td>Timer</td><td>How many rounds, minutes, rests, or travel segments remain, and which actions advance or delay it?</td></tr><tr><td>Keystone</td><td>Which creature, object, route, spell, or mechanism enables the enemy plan?</td></tr><tr><td>Resource ceiling</td><td>Which slots, components, consumables, and reputation costs is this objective worth?</td></tr><tr><td>Exit</td><td>Where does the party go after success, and what prevents pursuit or immediate counterattack?</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Objective answer matrix

<!-- source:B00582 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00582.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Mission</th><th scope="col">Wizard priority</th><th scope="col">Common mistake</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Rescue / extraction</td><td>Find the target, create a safe lane, teleport or move them, then deny pursuit.</td><td>Fighting the guards while the hostage remains exposed.</td></tr><tr><td>Escort</td><td>Scout ahead, control approaches, preserve movement, and keep the protected creature out of shared areas.</td><td>Using the escort as the frontline or grouping everyone for enemy area effects.</td></tr><tr><td>Hold a location</td><td>Control entrances, reserve Counterspell/Dispel, rotate resources, and keep an evacuation trigger.</td><td>Chasing enemies away from the position you must defend.</td></tr><tr><td>Protect a ritual or caster</td><td>Split attackers, deny sight and movement, protect concentration, and identify the interruption condition.</td><td>Spending every action on damage while one enemy can still reach the objective.</td></tr><tr><td>Interrupt a ritual</td><td>Identify the keystone caster, focus, component, location, concentration, or timer; use mobility and no-save disruption.</td><td>Assuming killing all guards is required before touching the ritual.</td></tr><tr><td>Capture alive</td><td>Use control, isolation, surrender pressure, and nonlethal melee finish through an ally.</td><td>Using lethal area damage or leaving the target an escape route.</td></tr><tr><td>Steal / recover an object</td><td>Locate, reach, secure, and extract the object; use illusions and walls to avoid unnecessary combat.</td><td>Winning the room but losing the item to a runner, trap, or collapsing structure.</td></tr><tr><td>Escape / survive</td><td>Distance, blocked pursuit, obscurity, transport, and abandoning optional weight or objectives.</td><td>Spending remaining slots on retaliation while still trapped.</td></tr><tr><td>Timed defense</td><td>Trade cheap effects for enemy time, preserve one decisive answer for the final wave, and monitor the clock.</td><td>Overcasting early and entering the decisive round empty.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Round-by-round objective discipline

<!-- source:B00584 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00584.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Moment</th><th scope="col">Decision</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Before initiative</td><td>State the win condition, target priority, protected route/object, and retreat trigger in one sentence.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 1</td><td>Remove the enemy action or route that most directly threatens the objective. Initiative is valuable only if it changes the mission state.</td></tr><tr><td>Round 2</td><td>Convert control into progress: move the hostage, seize the object, reach the mechanism, reposition the escort, or establish the defense.</td></tr><tr><td>Mid-fight</td><td>Check the timer and failure condition every round. Do not continue the original plan after the mission state changes.</td></tr><tr><td>Objective achieved</td><td>Stop spending premium resources on enemies who no longer matter. Extract, secure evidence, block pursuit, or negotiate surrender.</td></tr><tr><td>Objective failing</td><td>Use the reserved rescue/escape answer or accept a lesser outcome before failure becomes irreversible.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Hostages, civilians, and nonlethal outcomes

<!-- source:B00587 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00587.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Constraint</th><th scope="col">Optimized handling</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Hostage adjacent to enemy</td><td>Use precise control, forced separation, walls, Resilient Sphere, invisibility, or teleportation; avoid uncontrolled area damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Civilians mixed into combat</td><td>Choose enemy-only targeting where possible, reshape lines and zones, create exits, and make the party announce safe areas.</td></tr><tr><td>Target must live</td><td>A melee attack that would reduce a creature to 0 HP can instead leave it at 1 HP and Unconscious; coordinate the finishing blow with the melee ally.</td></tr><tr><td>Evidence must survive</td><td>Control fire, disintegration, structural collapse, summoned creatures, and enemy destruction attempts; secure evidence before pursuing.</td></tr><tr><td>Surrender offered</td><td>Evaluate whether surrender completes the objective more safely than another round. Define disarmament, movement, custody, and escape prevention.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Two-player mission roles

<!-- source:B00589 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00589.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Wizard</th><th scope="col">Melee ally</th><th scope="col">Shared rule</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Information, route control, isolation, rescue, Counterspell, and extraction.</td><td>Physical interaction, carrying, guarding the objective, nonlethal finish, and protecting the Wizard during setup.</td><td>Do not split beyond rescue range unless the mission explicitly requires it and both have independent exits.</td></tr><tr><td>Keeps one mobility or circuit-breaker answer when the objective is fragile.</td><td>Avoids chasing controlled enemies away from the protected person/object.</td><td>When one character advances the objective, the other protects that action rather than both dealing routine damage.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## After-action conversion

<!-- source:B00591 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00591.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Review</th><th scope="col">Question</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Objective</td><td>Did the party win the stated mission, or only the combat?</td></tr><tr><td>Cost</td><td>Which slots, components, items, contacts, injuries, or reputation were spent?</td></tr><tr><td>Information</td><td>What was learned about enemy routes, defenses, motives, magic, and response time?</td></tr><tr><td>Recovery</td><td>What must be replaced before the next mission: spellbook pages, components, potions, allies, disguises, transport, or legal cover?</td></tr><tr><td>Adaptation</td><td>Which failure was caused by preparation, positioning, communication, or a wrong assumption? Change one procedure.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
