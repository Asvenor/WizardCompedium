## Circle Magic: Cooperative Spell Amplification [2025 SOURCE / BUILDER + RULING-SENSITIVE]

<!-- source:B00301 -->
Source and gate: Circle Magic is an optional rules module in Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn (2025) for the 2024 rules. A slot-cast spell with a casting time of 1 action, or 1 minute or longer, can qualify. The primary caster spends the slot, provides components, chooses targets, and maintains concentration when required; nearby secondary casters use the Magic action and may have additional slot or concentration obligations.

<!-- source:B00302 -->
Optimization use: The available enhancements can extend range or duration, widen an area, share concentration risk, protect allies, or replace costly components. Preselect the spell, enhancement, positions, secondary casters, and costs before play. Do not assume Circle Magic in a normal build, and do not apply it to Bonus Action or Reaction spells; confirm the exact option requirements with the DM.

## 1. Simulacrum Engine: Finite Dual-Caster Platform

<!-- source:B00304 -->
Why it is powerful: Simulacrum converts money, time, and a 7th-level slot into a second Wizard action, Reaction, and concentration slot. The copy has half the original Hit Point maximum, cannot gain levels, cannot take Short or Long Rests, cannot cast Simulacrum, and cannot naturally refill expended spell slots. It is a finite tactical platform, not a renewable second character.

This compendium does not permit Wish, another copied effect, or wording tricks to create a simulacrum from a simulacrum.</td><td>Any plan that requires a copy to produce another copy is rejected as illegal for this reference.</td></tr><tr><td>Replacement</td><td>If you cast Simulacrum again, a simulacrum you created with that spell is destroyed.</td><td>Replacing a healthy copy discards every unspent finite resource it still holds.</td></tr><tr><td>Repair</td><td>Repair only through the spell's listed Long Rest procedure and component cost per Hit Point.</td><td>Normal healing and resting do not restore the copy as though it were a player character.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 2. Glyph of Warding Buff Bunker

<!-- source:B00315 -->
Why it is broken: A Spell Glyph can release a stored spell when its trigger occurs. If that stored spell normally requires concentration, it instead lasts for its full duration without anyone concentrating. A fixed preparation room can therefore apply several effects that normally compete for the same concentration slot while the Wizard remains free to concentrate during the actual encounter.

<!-- source:B00316 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00316.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Gate</th><th scope="col">Execution rule</th><th scope="col">Optimization consequence</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Fixed location</td><td>The inscribed surface or object breaks if moved more than 10 feet from the casting location.</td><td>This is a bunker, base, ambush room, or recurring Demiplane setup - not a portable backpack battery.</td></tr><tr><td>Cost</td><td>Each glyph consumes 200+ gp of powdered diamond.</td><td>Reserve the engine for objectives worth the money.</td></tr><tr><td>Spell level</td><td>The stored spell can be no higher than the slot used for Glyph of Warding.</td><td>High-level packages consume both money and premium spell slots during preparation.</td></tr><tr><td>Trigger</td><td>Use objective language: named creature, password, contact, position, or opening an object.</td><td>A clear trigger prevents accidental activation and table arguments.</td></tr><tr><td>Targeting</td><td>A targeted stored spell affects the creature that triggered the glyph; an area effect is centered there.</td><td>Design the room around who triggers each glyph and where that creature stands.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 3. Planar Binding Service Engine

<!-- source:B00320 -->
Why it is broken: Planar Binding converts a failed Charisma save, 1,000+ gp, and one hour of protected casting into days or months of service from a Celestial, Elemental, Fey, or Fiend. It requires no concentration after completion, so several bindings can coexist. The engine is limited less by spell slots than by legal creature access, containment, gold, and the wording of commands. Duration is 24 hours at level 5, 10 days at level 6, 30 days at level 7, 180 days at level 8, and 366 days at level 9.

<!-- source:B00321 -->

Usually inferior to other 9th-level opportunities unless the creature is exceptional.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 4. Magic Jar Physical-Body Replacement

<!-- source:B00325 -->
Why it is broken: Possession replaces the Wizard's Hit Points, Hit Dice, Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Speed, and senses with the host's values while the Wizard otherwise keeps their game statistics. A carefully selected Humanoid body can therefore repair the class's physical weaknesses without giving up its mental scores and Wizard spellcasting. The cost is a lethal dependency on the original body and the 500+ gp container.

<!-- source:B00326 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00326.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Statistic group</th><th scope="col">Source while possessing</th><th scope="col">Optimization meaning</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Hit Points / Hit Dice</td><td>Host body</td><td>A durable host can dramatically improve survival.</td></tr><tr><td>Strength, Dexterity, Constitution</td><td>Host body</td><td>Physical saves, initiative, armor interaction, and concentration can improve.</td></tr><tr><td>Speed and senses</td><td>Host body</td><td>Flight is not automatic; only the senses and Speed actually supplied by the host apply.</td></tr><tr><td>Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma</td><td>Wizard</td><td>Spellcasting ability and mental identity remain intact.</td></tr><tr><td>Other game statistics</td><td>Wizard unless the spell explicitly replaces them</td><td>Do not assume every printed host trait, action, class feature, or legendary ability transfers. Resolve the exact stat-block interaction with the DM.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 5. Nystul's Magic Aura Exploit Tree

<!-- source:B00332 -->
Why it is broken: The 2024 Mask effect makes spells and other magical effects treat a willing creature as a creature type chosen by the caster. Repeating the spell on the same target for 30 days makes the mask last until dispelled. This does not grant the traits or immunities of that creature type; it changes how other magic classifies the target. That wording opens defensive and target-expansion interactions far beyond simple aura concealment.

<!-- source:B00333 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00333.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Branch</th><th scope="col">Sequence</th><th scope="col">Payoff</th><th scope="col">Boundary</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Defensive masking</td><td>A willing Humanoid is masked as another type.</td><td>Type-restricted magic such as Hold Person may no longer treat the target as valid.</td><td>No resistances, senses, or condition immunities are gained.</td></tr><tr><td>Magic Jar expansion</td><td>A willing non-Humanoid is masked as Humanoid, then targeted by Magic Jar.</td><td>Broad reading allows possession attempts against creatures normally excluded by type.</td><td>The target still makes the normal Charisma save; willingness for Nystul is the real access gate.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar Binding expansion</td><td>A willing creature is masked as Celestial, Elemental, Fey, or Fiend before Planar Binding.</td><td>Broad reading makes an otherwise invalid creature a legal binding target.</td><td>The target still makes the binding save, costs 1,000+ gp, and must remain contained for one hour.</td></tr><tr><td>False Aura</td><td>An object is made to appear magical, nonmagical, or associated with a chosen school.</td><td>Supports heists, decoys, hidden items, false evidence, and magical screening.</td><td>It changes magical readings, not the object's actual functions.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 6. Shapechange Spellcasting Chassis

<!-- source:B00339 -->
Why it is broken: Shapechange grants Temporary Hit Points equal to the first form's Hit Points, replaces most physical statistics with the form, and explicitly preserves the caster's Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma, proficiencies, communication, and Spellcasting feature. The Wizard becomes a high-CR physical platform while remaining a Wizard. The spell is concentration, so the best form is the one that keeps that concentration alive and adds options that do not compete with spellcasting.

<!-- source:B00340 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00340.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Selection priority</th><th scope="col">What to look for</th><th scope="col">Why</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1. Concentration survival</td><td>High Constitution, strong saves, damage mitigation, movement, or defensive traits.</td><td>Losing concentration ends the entire chassis.</td></tr><tr><td>2. Temporary Hit Points</td><td>A large Hit Point total in the form.</td><td>Provides a major buffer before the Wizard's real Hit Points are threatened.</td></tr><tr><td>3. Mobility</td><td>Flight, climb, swim, reach, or safe repositioning.</td><td>Lets the Wizard cast from angles ordinary enemies cannot pressure.</td></tr><tr><td>4. Senses</td><td>Truesight, blindsight, darkvision, or another relevant sense.</td><td>Removes encounter-specific information disadvantages.</td></tr><tr><td>5. Useful non-spell actions</td><td>Movement, control, defense, or reactions that remain valuable when a spell is not needed.</td><td>Prevents the form from being only a pile of Temporary Hit Points.</td></tr><tr><td>6. Anatomy and equipment</td><td>Communication, hands or another way to handle components, and equipment behavior accepted by the DM.</td><td>The spell preserves Spellcasting, but practical component handling should be settled before combat.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 7. True Polymorph Creature Production

<!-- source:B00342 -->
Why it is broken: A sufficiently large nonmagical object can become a creature of Challenge Rating 9 or lower. If concentration is maintained for the full hour, the transformation lasts until dispelled. The creature is Friendly and follows commands during the first hour; after that, control ends, though its attitude may remain Friendly based on treatment. Repeated use can populate bases, settlements, expeditions, or organizations with entirely new creatures.

## 8. Fabricate Economic and Infrastructure Domination

<!-- source:B00345 -->
Why it is broken: Fabricate converts visible raw materials into a finished object of the same material in 10 minutes. It does not create matter, improve material quality, or create magic items. Its power comes from replacing skilled labour and production time. With the correct Artisan's Tool proficiency, a Wizard can compress weeks or months of manufacturing into one spell slot and destabilize the price, supply, and political control of selected goods.

## 9. Force-Prison Encounter Surgery and Faithful Hound Kill Box

<!-- source:B00351 -->
Why it is advanced: Wall of Force and Forcecage do not merely disable one target; they redefine which creatures, routes, spells, and objectives exist in the current fight. The correct target is not always the largest enemy. Remove whichever participant makes the remaining battlefield easiest to solve.

<!-- source:B00352 -->

## 10. Contingency Defensive Package: Otiluke's Resilient Sphere

<!-- source:B00358 -->
Why it is advanced: Contingency moves a defensive action into downtime and fires immediately after a precise trigger. Otiluke’s Resilient Sphere is a Concentration spell. A Contingency can trigger the spell, but maintaining the sphere ends any other Concentration the Wizard was maintaining. The enclosed Wizard cannot affect creatures outside the barrier. Use this package only when emergency isolation is worth replacing the current Concentration plan. Incapacitated—including Paralyzed—ends Concentration, so such a trigger cannot sustain Resilient Sphere. Verify both active entries before finalizing the trigger.

<!-- source:B00359 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00359.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Design element</th><th scope="col">Good practice</th><th scope="col">Bad practice</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Trigger</td><td>Use a clear observable state: reduced below a chosen Hit Point threshold, grappled, restrained, engulfed, or another exact event that leaves Concentration possible.</td><td>&quot;When I am in danger&quot; or another subjective trigger.</td></tr><tr><td>Timing</td><td>Remember it fires immediately after the trigger, not before the triggering damage or effect.</td><td>Assuming it retroactively prevents the hit that activated it.</td></tr><tr><td>Component</td><td>Keep the 1,500+ gp statuette on your person for the full 10-day duration.</td><td>Storing it in extradimensional luggage or handing it to an ally.</td></tr><tr><td>Inside/outside plan</td><td>Replace existing Concentration only when isolation is worth that cost; then decide how you communicate, roll the sphere, wait, or end the spell.</td><td>Triggering while the party needs your direct spell effects or Counterspell outside the sphere.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## 11. Maze No-Save Boss Removal

<!-- source:B00361 -->
Why it is advanced: Maze has no initial saving throw. The target immediately leaves the battlefield and must spend the Study action to attempt a DC 20 Intelligence (Investigation) check to return. Even a quick escape usually costs participation and an action. The spell wins when the party converts that time into a decisive advantage rather than waiting passively.

## 12. Sleet Storm Anti-Caster Shutdown

<!-- source:B00364 -->
Why it is advanced: Sleet Storm combines long range, Heavy Obscurement, Difficult Terrain, Prone, and direct concentration loss. A creature entering the cylinder or starting its turn there must make a Dexterity save; failure causes Prone and ends concentration without using the normal Constitution concentration save. It attacks enemy casting, sight, movement, and positioning with one 3rd-level slot.
