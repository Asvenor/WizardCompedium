<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00945"><strong>BUDGET JOBS, NOT RAW SLOTS</strong><p>A slot is valuable because of the future job it can still perform. Before the session, reserve an emergency defense lane, an exit lane, and one encounter-winning lane appropriate to the mission. Re-price after every fight, Short Rest, and major reveal.</p></aside>

<aside class="callout callout--note"><p>Use the recovery budget displayed by the active Wizard entry. The strongest default restores the highest-value missing package—defense, exit, countermagic, or control—not merely the largest numerical slot total. Treat any expected Short Rest as conditional until campaign pacing confirms it.</p></aside>

## Traffic-light spending rule

<!-- source:B00950 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00950" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">STATE</th><th scope="col">DEFINITION</th><th scope="col">DEFAULT BEHAVIOR</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>GREEN</td><td>Emergency defense + exit + next-hard-fight package are intact</td><td>Spend for objective-changing control, decisive information, or prevented damage.</td></tr><tr><td>AMBER</td><td>Only one premium encounter package remains after reserves</td><td>Choose one main job; use cantrips, rituals, items, positioning, and allies for the rest.</td></tr><tr><td>RED</td><td>Defense or exit would be lost by another premium spend</td><td>Protect the retreat; spend only to prevent defeat, capture, or mission failure.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
