## Using the researched Fighter / Diviner variant legally

The optimizer's build is a useful alternative to the flagship, not an extra spell-granting system. Keep <a href="/chapters/10-compact-spell-progression-roadmap/">Chapter 10's single progression roadmap</a> as the normal allocation. Use these adjustments when information, ritual mobility and Portent suit the campaign.

- Start Fighter for its starting save proficiencies and armor training; taking Fighter later does not retroactively grant Constitution-save proficiency. The listed DEX 14 and INT 17 satisfy the Fighter and Wizard multiclass ability prerequisites.
- At Wizard 1, the spellbook starts with six first-level Wizard spells. Each further Wizard level normally adds two eligible Wizard spells. The research's longer "top picks" lists are alternatives and copying priorities, not three or four free choices.
- Divination Savant is separate: at Wizard 3 it adds two eligible first- or second-level Divinations; later new Wizard spell-slot levels grant one eligible Divination each. Do not count a spell twice if it is already in the book.
- Use the 2024 Wizard table's fixed prepared-spell count. Higher Intelligence improves attacks, save DC and relevant checks, not that table's preparation allowance.
- A Fighter 1 / Wizard 19 character reaches ninth-level Wizard spells at character level 18 and never gains Wizard 20's Signature Spells.

### Savant opportunities, separate from normal level-up choices

These are optional allocations when the spell is not already owned. They are not automatic preparation grants or mandatory replacements for the existing route.

**Expert Divination:** use recovery as a rebate on information you needed anyway, not a reason to cast Mind Spike repeatedly during combat. Rituals spend no spell slot and therefore do not create a slot-recovery loop. Divination concentration also competes with your active control.

### Optional priorities from the research

Phantom Steed can move from copy-first to a guaranteed Wizard-5 choice when open-map travel is central and no scroll is actually available. Rope Trick is a strong early acquisition when the party lacks a regrouping tool. Both replace a normal selection if learned on level-up; neither is a Divination Savant choice.

War Caster is the early concentration-protection candidate; Mage Slayer is an optional later anti-caster investment, not compulsory for every campaign. Cap Intelligence on the chosen legal feat/ASI schedule. At later feat opportunities, consider party coverage such as Musician or permitted armor investment rather than automatically choosing damage. An Epic Boon at Wizard 19 must satisfy its own prerequisites; the research's Boon of the Night Spirit is a candidate, not an extra free feat.

At Wizard 18, the 2024 Spell Mastery action-casting requirement permits utility candidates such as Unseen Servant and Locate Object; it does not make Reaction-cast Shield eligible. Locate Object still requires concentration and an eligible target. See <a href="/chapters/37-noncombat-wizard-operations/">noncombat operations</a> for the repeatable search procedure.
