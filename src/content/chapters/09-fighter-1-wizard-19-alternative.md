## Fighter / Wizard chassis and default Diviner path

<!-- source:B00115 -->
Use this only as the deliberate armored alternative to the Artificer flagship. It keeps the normal Wizard spell priorities, but starts Fighter for armor, shield, Defense Fighting Style, and CON-save durability while giving up the flagship’s tools, healing, and crafting utility.
