## Opening algorithm

<!-- source:B00934 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00934" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">STEP</th><th scope="col">QUESTION / ACTION</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>0 · Before initiative</td><td>Name the objective, exit, concentration candidate, Reaction reserve, and friendly-fire boundary.</td></tr><tr><td>1 · Read the map</td><td>Identify cover, choke points, height, doors, hazards, civilians, and spaces allies must occupy.</td></tr><tr><td>2 · Read the enemy</td><td>Separate boss, caster, skirmisher, mob, and objective pieces; do not assume unrevealed defenses.</td></tr><tr><td>3 · Choose the job</td><td>Split, deny, rescue, reveal, counter, escape, or finish—one primary job per premium spell.</td></tr><tr><td>4 · Price failure</td><td>Know the fallback if the spell is resisted, concentration breaks, or the target moves.</td></tr><tr><td>5 · Reassess</td><td>After every meaningful reveal, compare the next spell with Dodge, movement, cantrip, item, or retreat.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Seven rehearsed encounter patterns

<!-- source:B00936 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00936" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">PATTERN</th><th scope="col">OBJECTIVE</th><th scope="col">ROUND 1</th><th scope="col">PIVOT</th><th scope="col">FAILURE EXIT</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Boss / legendary defenses</td><td>Deny movement, actions, line, or objective access without depending on one save.</td><td>Open with terrain/geometry or a party-enabling effect; keep an exit.</td><td>After observed resistance, switch defense, target, or job. Do not spend the party's whole turn proving one spell.</td><td>If control cannot stick, protect allies and win the objective clock.</td></tr><tr><td>Enemy caster</td><td>Break the enemy's casting plan while preserving your own Reaction budget.</td><td>Use cover/range, threaten concentration, and keep Counterspell legality visible.</td><td>If the caster baits the Reaction, decide whether the unknown follow-up is worse than the current spell.</td><td>Disengage from the duel; block sight, move the party, or remove supporting enemies.</td></tr><tr><td>Large mob</td><td>Reduce the number of enemies who can act effectively.</td><td>Use a party-safe area, choke point, or encounter split before spending on damage.</td><td>If allies enter the zone, pivot to a smaller control, movement, or cleanup spell.</td><td>Preserve slots once the remaining enemies are safe cantrip/weapon cleanup.</td></tr><tr><td>Ambush / lost initiative</td><td>Survive the first cycle and rebuild formation.</td><td>Defense or escape first; move out of crossfire; establish sight and ally positions.</td><td>Once stable, control the most dangerous lane instead of retaliating at the nearest target.</td><td>Use the emergency protocol if the party cannot regain a safe formation.</td></tr><tr><td>Cramped room</td><td>Create usable ally lanes without trapping the party in your own effect.</td><td>Favor precise zones, doors, corners, forced movement, or non-Concentration denial.</td><td>If geometry turns against the party, end/move the plan where the active effect allows or switch jobs.</td><td>Extract through the nearest controlled route; do not defend a bad room.</td></tr><tr><td>Flying / distant threat</td><td>Restore meaningful interaction or protect the ground objective.</td><td>Use cover, range, forced repositioning, sight denial, or an effect that does not rely on reaching melee.</td><td>If grounding fails, rescue exposed allies and attack the objective or support structure.</td><td>Retreat beneath cover or out of the threat's preferred range band.</td></tr><tr><td>Magic resistance / anti-magic pressure</td><td>Keep contributing when a favorite magical route is unreliable.</td><td>Test only with a low-opportunity-cost action; lean on positioning, allies, objects, information, and objective play.</td><td>Separate resistance, immunity, dispelling, and suppression—each demands a different answer.</td><td>Leave the suppression zone or switch to the party member whose engine still works.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
