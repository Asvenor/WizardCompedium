<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00109.T001"><strong>WHY IT WINS</strong><p>The optimized tank Wizard does not imitate a Barbarian. It protects concentration, survives focus fire, and makes enemy actions fail to create value. Abjurer is the default identity; defensive shells are alternatives when the campaign demands them.</p></aside>

<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00109.T002" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Field</th><th scope="col">Answer</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Role</td><td>Defensive control Wizard. You tank by keeping concentration alive and making enemies waste turns.</td></tr><tr><td>Best chassis</td><td>Artificer 1 / Abjurer Wizard 19 for the armored defensive variant; Pure Abjurer only when multiclassing is unavailable.</td></tr><tr><td>Power ceiling</td><td>High reliability, lower greed than Chronurgy.</td></tr><tr><td>Difficulty</td><td>Medium. The concept is simple, but positioning still matters.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
