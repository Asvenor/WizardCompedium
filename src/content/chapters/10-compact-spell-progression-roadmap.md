## Spell Mastery decision matrix

<!-- source:B00131 -->
At Wizard 18, use only spells that satisfy the active feature's level and casting-time requirements. Reaction and Bonus Action favorites are not automatically eligible. Confirm both chosen entries in D&D Beyond before locking them in.

<!-- source:B00132 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00132" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Candidate family</th><th scope="col">Best job</th><th scope="col">Why it rises</th><th scope="col">Main caution</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Level 1 · Longstrider / Disguise Self</td><td>All-day mobility or repeatable infiltration</td><td>Low slot pressure and broad noncombat value.</td><td>Choose only an eligible active entry; neither wins every combat.</td></tr><tr><td>Level 1 · Grease / Magic Missile</td><td>Repeatable control test or reliable cleanup</td><td>Creates a useful default Action without consuming higher slots.</td><td>Damage scales poorly; Grease depends on terrain and enemy movement.</td></tr><tr><td>Level 1 · Fog Cloud</td><td>Repeatable sight denial</td><td>Can answer ranged pressure and hostile sight features.</td><td>Uses Concentration and can obstruct allies.</td></tr><tr><td>Level 2 · Mirror Image</td><td>Repeatable non-concentration defense</td><td>Strong when attacks repeatedly threaten survival or concentration.</td><td>Does not answer saving throws and may be inefficient against weak attacks.</td></tr><tr><td>Level 2 · Web</td><td>Repeatable battlefield control</td><td>The most aggressive renewable control candidate.</td><td>Uses Concentration; immunity, terrain, and friendly placement still matter.</td></tr><tr><td>Level 2 · Invisibility / Rope Trick</td><td>Repeatable infiltration, escape, or temporary shelter</td><td>Exceptional mission utility when the active casting-time gate permits it.</td><td>Concentration, geometry, and table rulings determine practical value.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Signature Spells decision matrix

<!-- source:B00136 -->
Pure Wizard 20 chooses two eligible level-3 Wizard spells. The one-level-dip routes do not receive this feature. Favor spells that are useful in many encounters and that you are happy to keep prepared.

<!-- source:B00137 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00137" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Package</th><th scope="col">Two-spell comparison</th><th scope="col">Best campaign</th><th scope="col">Failure check</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Default control</td><td>Slow + Sleet Storm</td><td>Mixed enemies, uncertain immunities, anti-caster pressure</td><td>Both use Concentration; free casts do not remove that conflict.</td></tr><tr><td>Control + removal</td><td>Hypnotic Pattern + Dispel Magic</td><td>Broad adventuring with frequent ongoing magic</td><td>Charm immunity and valid-dispel targeting can reduce value.</td></tr><tr><td>Damage + control</td><td>Fireball + Slow or Hypnotic Pattern</td><td>Many encounters with clustered enemies</td><td>Do not choose two spells that solve only the same easy fights.</td></tr><tr><td>Mobility + answer</td><td>Fly + Dispel Magic</td><td>Vertical exploration, rescues, hostile magic</td><td>Fly uses Concentration and can create falling risk.</td></tr><tr><td>Anti-caster</td><td>Sleet Storm + Dispel Magic</td><td>Caster-heavy campaign</td><td>Verify the active entries and preserve Reaction resources for Counterspell.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
