Outside combat, the Wizard wins by converting information, preparation, and spell access into a situation where the request, identity, route, or evidence already favors the party. Use the operating loop first, then choose an engine that solves the actual obstacle.

<!-- source:B00594 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00594.T001"><strong>NONCOMBAT RULE</strong><p>Magic changes what is possible; it does not erase witnesses, physical inspection, concentration limits, local law, or the consequences of being discovered. Build the exit before creating the deception.</p></aside>

<!-- source:B00595 -->
## Noncombat operating loop

<!-- source:B00596 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00596.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Phase</th><th scope="col">Wizard procedure</th><th scope="col">Desired output</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1. Learn</td><td>Use Study, Search/Insight, a familiar, Arcane Eye, Detect Thoughts, witnesses, maps, or legitimate research.</td><td>A real motive, route, security weakness, object location, or social pressure point.</td></tr><tr><td>2. Build cover</td><td>Prepare an alias, mundane props, Alter Self/Disguise Self, False Aura, Nondetection, or a believable reason to be present.</td><td>A story that survives the first question and does not depend on one fragile illusion.</td></tr><tr><td>3. Create leverage</td><td>Frame the request around what the target already wants. Use Major Image, evidence cues, or Suggestion only when ordinary leverage is insufficient.</td><td>A willing or hesitant target, a distraction, manufactured urgency, or apparent authority.</td></tr><tr><td>4. Execute</td><td>Bypass, locate, translate, breach, manipulate remotely, transport, or fabricate the exact solution.</td><td>The person, object, route, document, or access point needed for the mission.</td></tr><tr><td>5. Preserve the exit</td><td>Track witnesses, concentration, physical evidence, alarms, pursuit, and a retreat route. Leave before the cover story is tested repeatedly.</td><td>A completed objective without turning one successful trick into an avoidable arrest or chase.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Social and infiltration engines

<!-- source:B00598 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00598.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Engine</th><th scope="col">Execution</th><th scope="col">Limits and counters</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Detect Thoughts -&gt; tailored Suggestion</td><td>Read what is most on the target's mind, or risk a deeper probe when the information is worth exposure. End or replace concentration, then phrase a short achievable instruction around the discovered fear, duty, or desire.</td><td>The surface read is safer; a deeper probe alerts the target. Suggestion requires hearing and understanding, allows a Wisdom save, cannot obviously harm the target or allies, and competes for concentration.</td></tr><tr><td>Alter Self + Disguise Self</td><td>Use Alter Self for the real face, body proportions, and voice. Layer Disguise Self over it for clothing, armor, badges, equipment, and other visible details.</td><td>The physical body survives touch, but illusory gear does not. Both must match one believable identity; Alter Self uses concentration and neither grants documents, memories, rank, or mannerisms.</td></tr><tr><td>Disguise Self + Major Image authority play</td><td>Pose as an inspector, officer, priest, noble agent, or specialist while Major Image supplies a superior, public emergency, magical hazard, procession, messenger, or supporting scene.</td><td>Major Image can supply sound, smell, temperature, movement, and conversation, but physical interaction or a successful Study check reveals it. Visible casting and witnesses can ruin the setup.</td></tr><tr><td>Nystul's Magic Aura + Nondetection + mundane cover</td><td>Use False Aura to alter how an unattended object appears to magical detection. Use Nondetection on the infiltrator, object, or small location when divination or scrying is the real threat. Add ordinary containers, paperwork, and behavior.</td><td>Nondetection consumes diamond dust. False Aura changes magical readings, not physical ownership or evidence. The creature-type Mask is powerful and DM-sensitive; use it only after an explicit ruling.</td></tr><tr><td>Minor Illusion + Prestidigitation evidence cues</td><td>Combine one small image or sound with harmless odor, marks, soil, cleaning, temperature, flavor, sparks, or other minor sensory details. Create footprints, a voice behind a door, smoke cues, faction marks, or a false trail.</td><td>Minor Illusion creates either a sound or an object image and fails to physical interaction. Prestidigitation creates minor effects, not durable valuable evidence, forged authority, or a complete document.</td></tr><tr><td>Study -&gt; Search -&gt; Influence</td><td>Use Intelligence skills to understand the institution, clue, custom, contract, or problem. Use Insight through Search to read the creature's state of mind. Then let the best speaker frame the request around an existing desire.</td><td>A request aligned with the target's desires may need no roll; a hesitant request may. Knowledge creates leverage but does not replace social skill, proof, or roleplay.</td></tr><tr><td>Level-4+ Major Image infrastructure</td><td>During downtime, create a lasting receptionist, guard post, decoy treasure, warning apparition, false collapse, checkpoint official, or visible hazard. Operate or update it while within range.</td><td>A level-4+ casting lasts until dispelled without concentration, but physical interaction and investigation still expose it. Place it where people are discouraged from touching it and where the illusion has a believable function.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Exploration and heist engines

<!-- source:B00600 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00600.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Engine</th><th scope="col">Execution</th><th scope="col">Limits and counters</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Silence + Knock [DM-sensitive]</td><td>Place Silence so the locked target is inside the sphere while the caster stands outside it and can still provide Knock's verbal component. Knock opens one lock, bar, or stuck closure; its loud sound originates from the target.</td><td>Silence is not normally a Wizard spell, so it needs an ally, feat, item, or multiclass source. Confirm that the table suppresses Knock's target-originating sound before relying on the breach.</td></tr><tr><td>Locate Object + Phantom Steed search grid</td><td>Ritual-cast Phantom Steed, move rapidly through likely districts or terrain, then use Locate Object to follow the direction of a familiar target or the nearest object of a defined kind. Reposition to triangulate.</td><td>Locate Object reaches 1,000 feet, uses concentration, and is blocked by any thickness of lead. Phantom Steed ends early if damaged; do not ride it into a hostile search area without an extraction plan.</td></tr><tr><td>Arcane Eye + Find Familiar reconnaissance</td><td>Use the invisible, invulnerable eye to map visual routes and one-inch openings. Follow with the familiar for hearing, form-specific senses, touch-range delivery, physical interaction, a static watch point, or deliberate distraction.</td><td>Arcane Eye gives only visual information and uses concentration. A familiar is vulnerable and should receive one simple job plus a return condition; neither replaces party judgment.</td></tr><tr><td>Unseen Servant + Mage Hand interaction ladder</td><td>Use Mage Hand for precise nearby manipulation up to 10 pounds. Use Unseen Servant for repeated simple human tasks, movement, fetching, pouring, opening, cleaning, carrying light items, or operating a prepared mechanism.</td><td>Neither understands a puzzle or detects danger. The servant has Strength 2, 1 Hit Point, and cannot attack; Mage Hand cannot activate magic items. Distance reduces exposure but does not make the object safe.</td></tr><tr><td>Comprehend Languages + Study</td><td>Ritual-cast Comprehend Languages for literal spoken, signed, or written meaning, then use Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, or Religion to interpret context, coded intent, ritual meaning, authorship, or consequences.</td><td>The spell requires touching written text, takes about a minute per page, and does not decode symbols or secret messages. Translation is the first layer, not the complete solution.</td></tr><tr><td>Passwall + Rope Trick breach and hide</td><td>Open a route through a wooden, plaster, or stone surface, move the party or objective through, then establish Rope Trick on the far side as a temporary hidden observation and extraction point.</td><td>Both last one hour and neither uses concentration. Rope Trick blocks attacks, spells, and other effects across its portal; anything inside drops out when it ends. Track both timers and do not let Passwall close on a separated party.</td></tr><tr><td>Water Breathing + Alter Self expedition lead</td><td>Ritual-cast Water Breathing on the group for a full-day underwater operation. Use Alter Self for the Wizard's real Swim Speed so the Wizard can scout, guide, carry a line, operate mechanisms, or perform rescues.</td><td>Water Breathing grants respiration, not a Swim Speed. Alter Self uses concentration and protects only the Wizard unless other creatures have their own movement solution.</td></tr><tr><td>Fabricate emergency infrastructure</td><td>Convert visible raw material into the exact same-material solution: bridge, rope, clothing, barrier, ladder, platform, shelter component, container, raft part, or replacement structural piece.</td><td>Casting takes 10 minutes. Material quantity and quality still matter; metal, stone, and mineral products have the smaller size limit. Skilled objects require the relevant Artisan's Tool proficiency.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Concentration, witnesses, and failure checks

<!-- source:B00602 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00602.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Check</th><th scope="col">Static procedure</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Concentration sequence</td><td>Mark every concentration spell before the scene. Detect Thoughts -&gt; Suggestion is sequential; Alter Self prevents simultaneous concentration on another effect; Major Image becomes easier to combine only when cast with a level-4+ slot.</td></tr><tr><td>Visible casting</td><td>Verbal and Somatic components can expose the operation. Cast before entering, use cover/noise/permission, or create a mundane explanation. Do not assume observers ignore obvious spellcasting.</td></tr><tr><td>Physical inspection</td><td>Disguise Self, Minor Illusion, and Major Image all have physical or investigative failure points. Use real props, controlled distance, barriers, urgency, and an identity that does not invite touching.</td></tr><tr><td>Witness and evidence control</td><td>Know who saw the caster, what changed, which records remain, and what the target remembers after a charm ends. A successful spell can still create a later investigation.</td></tr><tr><td>Exit threshold</td><td>Leave when the objective is complete. Do not spend extra rounds proving the disguise, searching optional rooms, or improvising a second crime after the original plan has already succeeded.</td></tr><tr><td>Two-player role split</td><td>Wizard: information, bypass, misdirection, remote interaction, and exit magic. Partner: visible speaker, bodyguard, carrier, lookout, and mundane confirmation. Each should know the abort signal and meeting point.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
