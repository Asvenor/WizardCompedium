These are three subclass packages, not three chassis. Apply one to the Artificer 1 / Wizard 19 flagship when multiclassing is allowed. Use Pure Wizard only when it is not. Chronurgy maximizes ceiling, Diviner maximizes predictable outcomes, and Illusionist maximizes creative information control.

## Subclass selection matrix

<!-- source:B00087 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00087" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Field</th><th scope="col">Chronurgy</th><th scope="col">Diviner</th><th scope="col">Illusionist</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Role</td><td>Win initiative and timing; bend pivotal rolls with reaction-based control.</td><td>Force decisive outcomes with stored-roll reliability.</td><td>Win through deception, false information, fake terrain, stealth, and social control.</td></tr><tr><td>Primary engine</td><td>Temporal Awareness, Chronal Shift, Momentary Stasis, Arcane Abeyance, and Convergent Future.</td><td>Portent, Expert Divination, Third Eye, Greater Portent, and information spells that change the next decision.</td><td>Improved Illusions, Phantasmal Creatures, Illusory Self, Illusory Reality, and a permissive evidence/Study framework.</td></tr><tr><td>Best at</td><td>Going first, protecting key rolls, forcing rerolls, and converting setup into action-economy advantage.</td><td>Boss setup, saving allies, forcing failed saves, and making important rolls predictable.</td><td>Heists, scouting, misinformation, distraction, terrain tricks, and creative problem solving.</td></tr><tr><td>Resource pressure</td><td>High Reaction competition; ## Chronurgy feature-use overview

<!-- source:B00089 -->
Use Explorer's Guide to Wildemount and the active D&D Beyond entry as the rules authority. This is an operational summary; confirm compatibility with the 2024 rules and your DM.

<!-- source:B00090 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00090.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Wizard / character level</th><th scope="col">Feature</th><th scope="col">Operational reminder</th><th scope="col">Optimization use</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Wiz 3 / Char 4</td><td>Temporal Awareness</td><td>Adds your Intelligence modifier to initiative.</td><td>Act before enemies so control removes actions before they happen; stack with Alert and Gift of Alacrity only when both are selectable and their active entries permit the combination.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 3 / Char 4</td><td>Chronal Shift</td><td>Reaction-based forced reroll with limited uses.</td><td>Correct an encounter-defining attack, check, or save. <!-- source:B00094 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00094" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Level</th><th scope="col">Diviner feature plan</th><th scope="col">Illusionist feature plan</th><th scope="col">Conflict / failure check</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Wiz 3</td><td>Use Divination Savant to broaden the information library and Portent only on rolls whose outcome changes actions, survival, or the objective.</td><td>Use Illusion Savant/Improved Illusions to widen reusable deception and battlefield information tools; settle illusion evidence and Study handling with the DM.</td><td>Do not spend limited roll control on cosmetic certainty or assume an illusion changes reality beyond its text.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 6</td><td>Expert Divination rewards actual divination casting and slot planning; choose information that changes the next decision.</td><td>Phantasmal Creatures adds a defined creature-shaped illusion option; verify its action, duration, control, and repeat-use limits.</td><td>A feature is incomplete if the information arrives too late or the illusion has no planned effect on behavior, movement, or action use.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 10</td><td>Third Eye supplies selectable sensory coverage; choose the mode for the environment before exposure when possible.</td><td>Illusory Self is defensive insurance with a limited-use/recovery gate; reserve it for an attack whose prevention matters.</td><td>Third Eye is not universal omniscience; Illusory Self competes with no resource only if its use and trigger are actually available.</td></tr><tr><td>Wiz 14</td><td>Greater Portent increases the number of stored outcomes; assign likely jobs without forcing a low-value use.</td><td>Illusory Reality converts part of an eligible illusion into temporary physical utility under strict harm and object limits.</td><td>Confirm object eligibility, duration, concentration, and the active feature text before building an encounter engine.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
