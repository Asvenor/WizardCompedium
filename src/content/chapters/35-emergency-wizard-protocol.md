This is the one-page recovery order for a Wizard already in a losing state. It gives priorities, not a scripted encounter solution: verify the objective, visible threats, legal actions, and ally positions before choosing the actual spell or item.

<!-- source:B00572 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00572"><strong>EMERGENCY ORDER</strong><p>1) prevent immediate death or irreversible failure; 2) restore a legal action and safe position; 3) preserve or replace the effect still protecting the objective; 4) extract allies who cannot escape themselves; 5) conserve the minimum resources required for the next safe rest or retreat.</p></aside>

<!-- source:B00573 -->

<!-- source:B00574 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00574" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Emergency state</th><th scope="col">First question</th><th scope="col">Default response family</th><th scope="col">Do not</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Surprised / poor initiative</td><td>What can remove the most hostile actions before they resolve?</td><td>Immediate defense, area control, sight denial, separation, or retreat positioning.</td><td>Spend the first turn on low-impact damage merely because it is visible.</td></tr><tr><td>Concentration just broke</td><td>Is the original job still required, and is recasting now safe?</td><td>Recast only if it remains decisive; otherwise switch to rescue, defense, or a different control axis.</td><td>Automatically repeat the same spell into the same counter or pressure.</td></tr><tr><td>Silenced / cannot speak</td><td>Which legal non-Verbal actions, movement, items, or ally interventions remain?</td><td>Exit the effect, break its source, use legal spells/items, Dodge, Help, Hide, or reposition.</td><td>Assume telepathy supplies a Verbal component.</td></tr><tr><td>Hands disabled / focus lost</td><td>Which spells need V only, and can a hand or backup focus be recovered safely?</td><td>Free a hand, retrieve backup equipment, teleport if legal, or use noncasting actions.</td><td>Declare Somatic/Material casting without checking the actual hand state.</td></tr><tr><td>Grappled / Restrained</td><td>Is movement, attack accuracy, Dexterity defense, or casting legality the real problem?</td><td>Teleport, force separation, use save/no-attack effects, defend, or end the source.</td><td>Treat Grappled and Restrained as the same condition.</td></tr><tr><td>Isolated / surrounded</td><td>Can one action create distance, cover, a wall, sight denial, or a safe ally route?</td><td>Prioritize extraction and encounter division over trading damage.</td><td>Teleport to an unknown square or leave the only ally without a plan.</td></tr><tr><td>Ally at 0 HP</td><td>Can the enemy cause immediate death before the ally acts again?</td><td>Block follow-up damage, enable healing/stabilization, move the ally, or remove the attacker.</td><td>Assume death saves guarantee several rounds.</td></tr><tr><td>Low slots</td><td>Which remaining resource is required for escape, defense, or the objective?</td><td>Use rituals, cantrips, Dodge, familiar, items, terrain, and allies; reserve one exit/defense when possible.</td><td>Spend the final premium slot on cleanup damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Antimagic / suppression</td><td>What mundane movement, cover, tools, items, and ally abilities still function?</td><td>Leave the zone, separate its source, use physical barriers, Help/Dodge, and coordinated movement.</td><td>Base the escape on another suppressed magical effect.</td></tr><tr><td>Captured</td><td>What is physically present: voice, hands, focus, book, allies, guards, exits, and time?</td><td>Gather information, conceal capability, cooperate temporarily when useful, and act only when the escape improves.</td><td>Reveal every remaining resource in the first failed attempt.</td></tr><tr><td>Spellbook lost</td><td>What remains prepared and what is the safest recovery versus reconstruction route?</td><td>Operate from prepared spells, protect remaining gear, use contacts/divination, and rebuild the minimum package first.</td><td>Risk the character and party in a blind immediate pursuit.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00576"><strong>RETREAT IS A POWER PLAY</strong><p>A Wizard who preserves the party, spellbook access, key components, and one decisive remaining resource can rebuild the encounter on favorable terms. Survival without the objective may still be failure; confirm which loss is reversible.</p></aside>
