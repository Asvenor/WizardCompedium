## Mundane property acquisition and construction

<!-- source:B00663 -->
There is no universal campaign price that makes every house, tower, deed, or parcel identical. Obtain a campaign quote and treat ownership as a contract with recurring obligations rather than converting a lifestyle-expense line into permanent property by assumption.

<!-- source:B00668 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00668"><strong>BASTION SOURCE GATE</strong><p>Bastions are an optional campaign subsystem. Use the current 2024 Dungeon Master's Guide/D&amp;D Beyond Bastion entry when the DM activates it. ## Verified 2024 Bastion baseline

<!-- source:B00670 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00670" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">BASELINE</th><th scope="col">CURRENT D&amp;D BEYOND RULE</th><th scope="col">OPTIMIZATION CONSEQUENCE</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Activation</td><td>Bastions are optional; when the DM uses them, a character can receive one at level 5.</td><td>Do not budget facilities or outputs before the campaign activates the subsystem.</td></tr><tr><td>Turn cadence</td><td>Default: one Bastion Turn after 7 in-game days; the DM can use a slower cadence.</td><td>Tie every projected output to real campaign time, not session count.</td></tr><tr><td>Special facilities</td><td>2 at level 5, 4 at level 9, 5 at level 13, and 6 at level 17.</td><td>Build a level-gated portfolio; do not count future rooms early.</td></tr><tr><td>Replacement</td><td>On gaining a level, one special facility can be replaced if the new facility's prerequisites are met.</td><td>Use level-ups to correct a weak facility package without assuming free mid-level rebuilding.</td></tr><tr><td>Absence</td><td>If the owner cannot communicate by Sending or similar magic, the Bastion defaults to the Maintain order.</td><td>Secure a communication route before planning remote research or production.</td></tr><tr><td>Hirelings</td><td>Special facilities include the hirelings they require; the subsystem's income covers their salaries.</td><td>Do not charge a second invented wage unless the campaign adds a separate cost.</td></tr><tr><td>Turn step</td><td>Action</td><td>Wizard control</td></tr><tr><td>1. Resolve state and events</td><td>Account for attacks, absence, damaged facilities, staff, and unresolved consequences.</td><td>Protect irreplaceable records before maximizing output.</td></tr><tr><td>3. Choose facility orders</td><td>Use only orders shown by each active facility entry.</td><td>Research/crafting normally outrank generic profit when they close a real Wizard gap.</td></tr><tr><td>4. Assign people and inputs</td><td>Confirm hirelings, defenders, raw materials, gold, tools, and spell access.</td><td>No invisible labor or unrecorded component supply.</td></tr><tr><td>5. Resolve outputs</td><td>Record items, information, recruits, trade, defense, or facility progress.</td><td>Name the destination and owner of every output.</td></tr><tr><td>6. Maintain and enlarge</td><td>Repair, replace, expand, or change facilities when the rules and campaign permit.</td><td>Avoid a beautiful facility that cannot perform the next campaign job.</td></tr><tr><td>7. Update the Bastion ledger</td><td>Date, order, input, result, staff, event, unresolved risk, next turn.</td><td>A written ledger prevents double-counted production and forgotten vulnerabilities.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
