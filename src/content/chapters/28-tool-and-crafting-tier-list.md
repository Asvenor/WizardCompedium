This tier list evaluates each tool specifically for an optimized Wizard. Exact recipes, formulas, item restrictions, attunement, charges, and crafting assignments must match the active D&D Beyond rules.

<!-- source:B00450 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00450" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Tier</th><th scope="col">Tool</th><th scope="col">Wizard verdict</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>S / S+</td><td>Tinker’s Tools</td><td>Highest ceiling when the campaign supports formulas, downtime, and wondrous-item crafting. Best default for Artificer/Wizard and dedicated crafters.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Thieves' Tools</td><td>Best non-crafting utility tool when the party lacks a Rogue: locks, traps, route access, and heists. It rises to S in a small party with no other lock/trap specialist. Tool proficiency and Sleight of Hand proficiency remain separate unless the active rule explicitly calls for both.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Woodcarver’s Tools</td><td>Premier reusable spell-item lane for wands, staves, and wooden projects when assigned by the active crafting rules. Strong party-wide resource distribution.</td></tr><tr><td>A</td><td>Smith’s Tools</td><td>Eligible metal melee weapons and medium/heavy armor, warning/initiative projects when assigned, and armored-Wizard support. Shield crafting needs an exact item/tool assignment. More party value than direct pure-Wizard value.</td></tr><tr><td>B+</td><td>Herbalism Kit</td><td>Healing potions and survival consumables cover a real Wizard weakness, especially in small or healer-light parties.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Calligrapher’s Supplies</td><td>Spell Scroll scribing is an S-tier project, but a Wizard with Arcana already qualifies. This proficiency is redundancy for scrolls and rises only when Arcana is unavailable or an exact non-spell scroll/item rule requires it.</td></tr><tr><td>B</td><td>Alchemist’s Supplies</td><td>Resistance, environment-specific, and utility consumables. Strong when the next threat is known.</td></tr><tr><td>C+</td><td>Jeweler’s Tools</td><td>Potentially exceptional ring projects, but high cost, long time, formula scarcity, and attunement pressure reduce default value.</td></tr><tr><td>C</td><td>Leatherworker’s Tools</td><td>Useful for Hide, Leather, and Studded Leather armor plus leather goods, but usually overshadowed by Smith’s Tools. The public 2024 tool list does not assign Shield crafting to this tool.</td></tr><tr><td>D</td><td>Weaver’s Tools</td><td>Narrow robe category. Worth taking only when a specific strong robe formula is already available.</td></tr><tr><td>D</td><td>Poisoner’s Kit</td><td>Campaign-specific harvesting and money potential, but unreliable for Wizards and frequently defeated by resistance, immunity, or delivery problems.</td></tr><tr><td>E</td><td>Other tools for magical crafting</td><td>Not useless in general play; simply do not beat the listed tools for optimized magical crafting without a known project.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## BIS tool order by supported build

## Crafting project priority

<!-- source:B00454 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00454.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Project type</th><th scope="col">Tool lane</th><th scope="col">Priority</th><th scope="col">Main restriction</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Spell scrolls</td><td>Arcana OR Calligrapher’s Supplies</td><td>S</td><td>Gold, full 8-hour inscription days, prepared spell access, Material components, and the exact Spell Scroll table.</td></tr><tr><td>Flight, storage, or broad wondrous utility</td><td>Tinker’s Tools when assigned</td><td>S if accessible</td><td>Arcana, assigned tool, prepared item spells, materials, full workdays, and DM-controlled availability.</td></tr><tr><td>Rechargeable control or damage item</td><td>Woodcarver’s Tools when assigned</td><td>A / S</td><td>Exact item activation, charges, attunement, and user restrictions.</td></tr><tr><td>Warning / initiative equipment</td><td>Smith’s or Woodcarver’s when assigned</td><td>A</td><td>Exact item wording, assigned tool, Arcana, spells, materials, and time.</td></tr><tr><td>Healing potions</td><td>Herbalism Kit</td><td>B+</td><td>Time, materials, and campaign crafting rules.</td></tr><tr><td>Resistance or environment potion</td><td>Alchemist’s Supplies / relevant rule</td><td>B</td><td>Only exceptional when the next threat is predictable.</td></tr><tr><td>High-end spell-storage ring</td><td>Jeweler’s Tools when assigned</td><td>A effect / C proficiency</td><td>High cost, long downtime, item access, exact tool assignment, and attunement.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
