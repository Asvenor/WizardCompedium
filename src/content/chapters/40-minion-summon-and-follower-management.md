## Follower categories and control level

<!-- source:B00634 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00634.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Category</th><th scope="col">Control reality</th><th scope="col">Best role</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Familiar</td><td>Limited stat block and communication; not a complete independent scout or watch system.</td><td>One clear information, Help, delivery, or observation job with a safe return condition.</td></tr><tr><td>Temporary summon</td><td>Exact spell controls initiative, commands, duration, concentration, and disappearance.</td><td>One encounter role: block, attack, move, scout, carry, or trigger—not every role at once.</td></tr><tr><td>Undead or maintained force</td><td>Requires bodies/resources, daily or periodic maintenance, storage, transport, and social tolerance.</td><td>Guarding, labor, controlled formation, or planned attrition when the campaign supports it.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar Binding servant</td><td>Long-duration compelled service; hostile creatures can interpret commands against your intent.</td><td>Specialized transport, scouting, guarding, utility, or combat with detailed operating limits.</td></tr><tr><td>True Polymorph creation</td><td>Friendly and obedient during the first hour; afterward no automatic control.</td><td>Independent ally, citizen, specialist, mount, or guard supported by agreement and treatment.</td></tr><tr><td>Hireling / expert</td><td>An NPC with wages, safety needs, loyalty, and independent judgment.</td><td>Research, transport, security, craft assistance, translation, administration, and mundane logistics.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Command template

<!-- source:B00636 -->
<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00636.T001"><strong>STATIC COMMAND</strong><p>Role + operating area + target priority + prohibited actions + reporting method + contact schedule + retreat condition + end condition. Replace vague commands such as “protect us” or “scout ahead” with instructions that can be followed without guessing.</p></aside>

<!-- source:B00637 -->

<!-- source:B00638 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00638.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Field</th><th scope="col">Example</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Role</td><td>Scout the eastern corridor; do not engage.</td></tr><tr><td>Operating area</td><td>Remain within 300 feet and do not pass a closed marked door.</td></tr><tr><td>Priority</td><td>Report creatures, exits, active magic, and environmental hazards before treasure.</td></tr><tr><td>Prohibited acts</td><td>Do not attack, open containers, trigger mechanisms, or reveal the party.</td></tr><tr><td>Reporting</td><td>Return every ten minutes or immediately after locating a creature or blocked route.</td></tr><tr><td>Retreat</td><td>Break contact and return if detected, damaged, unable to communicate, or the route changes.</td></tr><tr><td>End</td><td>The assignment ends when the corridor is mapped, the timer expires, or the Wizard recalls you.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Combat table-speed protocol

<!-- source:B00640 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00640.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Rule</th><th scope="col">Procedure</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Preselect actions</td><td>Before the summon’s turn, know its default movement, target, action, and retreat trigger.</td></tr><tr><td>Group identical creatures</td><td>When the DM permits, use one initiative point, shared target logic, and grouped dice instead of resolving every creature as a separate tactical puzzle.</td></tr><tr><td>Use role lanes</td><td>Blockers hold a route; attackers focus one target; scouts disengage; carriers move the objective. Do not improvise complex individual plans every round.</td></tr><tr><td>Protect concentration</td><td>If the force depends on concentration, the Wizard’s positioning and defense determine whether all of those actions continue to exist.</td></tr><tr><td>Avoid friendly fire</td><td>Announce zones, forced movement, and wall geometry before allies and followers commit.</td></tr><tr><td>End low-value turns</td><td>Dismiss, withdraw, or simplify creatures that no longer affect the mission rather than spending minutes on cleanup attacks.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Formation and role assignment

<!-- source:B00643 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00643.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Role</th><th scope="col">Placement</th><th scope="col">Success condition</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Screen</td><td>Between enemies and the Wizard without blocking allied movement or sight.</td><td>Consumes enemy movement/actions and prevents direct access.</td></tr><tr><td>Flanker / attacker</td><td>On the same focus target the party has already chosen.</td><td>Converts advantage or extra attacks into an actual removal, not scattered damage.</td></tr><tr><td>Scout</td><td>Ahead only within communication, recovery, and acceptable-risk limits.</td><td>Returns actionable information before initiative rather than disappearing alone.</td></tr><tr><td>Guard</td><td>At a named door, object, person, body, container, or ritual position.</td><td>Maintains the assigned boundary instead of chasing.</td></tr><tr><td>Carrier / rescuer</td><td>Near the objective with a known route and weight capacity.</td><td>Moves the ally, object, or component to safety without becoming trapped.</td></tr><tr><td>Reserve</td><td>Out of the first area effect and close enough to respond.</td><td>Replaces a fallen screen, blocks escape, or protects retreat.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Upkeep, loyalty, and social consequences

<!-- source:B00645 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00645.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Issue</th><th scope="col">Management rule</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Wages and supplies</td><td>Budget food, lodging, equipment, transportation, hazard pay, replacement, and specialist costs; cheap labor is not free infrastructure.</td></tr><tr><td>Loyalty</td><td>Pay on time, define danger honestly, protect followers, honor agreements, and do not assume a Friendly creature accepts suicide orders.</td></tr><tr><td>Law and reputation</td><td>Undead, fiends, armed monsters, invisible scouts, and magically compelled servants may be illegal or socially catastrophic even when mechanically effective.</td></tr><tr><td>Housing and transport</td><td>Large, unusual, or numerous creatures need routes, storage, stables, concealment, guards, and permission.</td></tr><tr><td>Information security</td><td>Followers know routes, bases, clients, commands, and capabilities. Limit knowledge to the role and establish what happens if captured.</td></tr><tr><td>Casualties</td><td>Decide in advance whether the force is expendable, recoverable, revivable, replaceable, or an independent ally whose death has moral and political consequences.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Two-player party applications

<!-- source:B00647 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00647.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Party gap</th><th scope="col">Follower solution</th><th scope="col">Caution</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>No scout</td><td>Familiar, Arcane Eye, hireling guide, or specialized bound/created creature.</td><td>Information should return; a lost scout that reports nothing did not solve the gap.</td></tr><tr><td>No healer</td><td>Healing items, trained hireling/ally where available, protective summon, extraction carrier, or Magic Initiate-style emergency access.</td><td>A summon is not automatically permitted to use every item or heal; verify exact rules.</td></tr><tr><td>Only one frontline</td><td>Screening summon, controlled choke, mount, durable ally, or force-prison split.</td><td>Do not add so many creatures that the two-player campaign becomes a slow mass-combat game.</td></tr><tr><td>No face or specialist</td><td>Interpreter, researcher, guide, artisan, lawyer, temple contact, or faction representative.</td><td>The follower has independent interests and may not carry the party through every social problem.</td></tr><tr><td>No transport capacity</td><td>Mounts, Phantom Steed, carrier summon, hired wagon/crew, bound transport, or teleport network.</td><td>Plan rest, cargo, route size, legality, and what happens if the transport disappears.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## When not to use minions

<!-- source:B00649 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00649.T001" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Skip or reduce the force when...</th><th scope="col">Reason</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>The encounter is already controlled.</td><td>More creatures add time without changing the outcome.</td></tr><tr><td>Concentration is needed for stronger battlefield control.</td><td>A weaker summon can cost the spell that actually wins the encounter.</td></tr><tr><td>The environment cannot support movement or formation.</td><td>Crowded rooms, ladders, stealth missions, and narrow routes turn bodies into obstacles.</td></tr><tr><td>The campaign consequences outweigh the tactical benefit.</td><td>Visible undead, fiends, monsters, or compelled servants can destroy alliances and access.</td></tr><tr><td>Commands cannot be resolved quickly.</td><td>Unclear authority and action timing cause disputes and slow play.</td></tr><tr><td>The follower would be treated as disposable despite being an independent ally.</td><td>Mechanical optimization does not erase agency, agreements, or table expectations.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

## Summon and additional-body comparison

<!-- source:B00652 -->
Selectability decides legality; the active spell entry decides stat block, concentration, command timing, and scaling. Prefer one clearly managed body when it provides comparable value to many separate creatures.

<!-- source:B00653 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00653" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Option family</th><th scope="col">Default tier</th><th scope="col">Best job</th><th scope="col">Table-speed / failure check</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Find Familiar</td><td>S utility</td><td>Scouting, Help when legal, touch-spell delivery, alarms, communication.</td><td>No routine attack role; losing it costs time and material.</td></tr><tr><td>Summon Aberration</td><td>A when selectable</td><td>Ranged or specialized sustained pressure from one scalable body.</td><td>Concentration; verify form statistics and command timing.</td></tr><tr><td>Summon Construct</td><td>A when selectable</td><td>Durable blocker, damage, and form-specific control.</td><td>Large body and terrain can obstruct allies; concentration remains exposed.</td></tr><tr><td>Summon Draconic Spirit</td><td>A when selectable</td><td>Flight, durability, resistance support, and broad battlefield reach.</td><td>Higher slot cost; confirm source access and exact breath/resistance text.</td></tr><tr><td>Summon Undead / Shadowspawn / Elemental</td><td>A/B by mission</td><td>Fear, mobility, damage type, or environment specialization.</td><td>Choose by known mission rather than memorized reputation.</td></tr><tr><td>Animate Objects</td><td>A with table agreement</td><td>High sustained pressure and space control.</td><td>Many attacks can slow play; resistances, area damage, and command economy can collapse value.</td></tr><tr><td>Current Conjure-family spells</td><td>Source check</td><td>Area, spirits, or creatures as defined by the active 2024 entry.</td><td>Never import older creature-selection procedures into a revised spell.</td></tr><tr><td>Planar Binding pipeline</td><td>Strategic</td><td>Long-duration specialist service after legal containment and setup.</td><td>Cost, target access, save, wording, loyalty, and social consequences dominate.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
