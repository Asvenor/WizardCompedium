## Build identity and mix-and-match rule

<!-- source:B00058 -->
These seven color-coded routes are the supported build families.

<!-- source:B00059 -->
Mix & Match Rule

<!-- source:B00060 -->
Preserve class order, actual Wizard level, feat timing, spell delay, armor/shield/War Caster rules, and D&D Beyond selectability. Fighter 1 / Wizard X uses Diviner as its worked example but may use another legal Wizard subclass; replace only subclass-specific features and spell swaps. Fast conversions: Fighter/Diviner to Chronurgy keeps the Fighter start and timing but replaces Portent-specific play; Fighter/Diviner to Abjurer keeps the armored shell and shifts toward defensive control. A species swap requires a fresh check of ability scores, Origin feat access, Speed/size, and defenses.
