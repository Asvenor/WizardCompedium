## Master Spellbook Acquisition Table

<!-- source:B00141 -->
This is a spell-acquisition map, not a universal daily prepared list. Color shows the spell's ownership role under the stated optimization assumptions; underlining preserves the supplied Wizard 19 prepared baseline, and cantrips are known rather than prepared. Exact D&D Beyond selectability and the active entry always control legality and wording.

<!-- source:B00142 -->
### How to use this list

- **MANDATORY** — Optimized must-own target.
- **OPTIONAL** — Strong alternative.
- **SLEEPER** — Specialized / setup.
- **UNDERLINED** — Prepared / known baseline.
- **TAGS** — [C] Conc. · [R] Ritual · [X] source-sensitive · [D] Dunamancy · [B] blade.

**READING RULE — ACQUISITION ROLE, NOT POWER TIER**

MANDATORY means “own it in an optimized spellbook,” not “always prepare it.” OPTIONAL means a strong alternative whose value depends on party, mission, build, or rules version. SLEEPER means specialized, setup-heavy, or copy-first value. [X] and [D] never grant access by themselves; use the exact selectable D&amp;D Beyond entry.

### Acquisition guidance

**ADD A NEW JOB**

Prefer a new capability before a redundant version of a job already solved well.

**GUARANTEE ACCESS**

Spend a level-up pick when the spell is needed now and no realistic copy route exists.

**COPY LOW-PRESSURE VALUE**

Rituals, infrastructure, and narrow mission tools are premium copy targets when access is real.

**PLAY STYLE WINS**

Party, campaign, and actual play can make a lower-color option the optimized pick.

## Spellbook Strategy

<!-- source:B00147 -->
Use this page for spellbook acquisition and redundancy decisions—not encounter tactics. First secure the next mandatory job, then exploit real copying access for breadth.

<!-- source:B00148 -->
### GUARANTEE

Take on level-up if not already secured.

- Counterspell
- Hypnotic Pattern
- Dimension Door
- Wall of Force
- Contingency
- Simulacrum
- Clone / Maze
- Wish / Shapechange

**RULE** · Do not gamble a major power spike on hypothetical future loot.

### COPY-FIRST

Excellent spellbook value; delay only when access is real.

- Detect Magic
- Identify
- Unseen Servant
- Rope Trick
- Tiny Hut
- Phantom Steed
- Divination
- Telepathic Bond
- Contact Other Plane

**RULE** · Rituals and infrastructure gain value from ownership without demanding daily preparation.

### MISSION PREP

Own broadly; prepare when the mission calls for it.

- See Invisibility
- Fly
- Remove Curse
- Arcane Eye
- Greater Invisibility
- True Seeing
- Teleport
- Plane Shift
- Antimagic Field

**RULE** · High ceiling when relevant; weak reason to occupy a default preparation slot every day.

### BUILD / TABLE

Power depends on route, downtime, party, or rulings.

- Conjure Minor Elementals
- Animate Dead
- Planar Binding
- Magic Jar
- Sickening Radiance
- Demiplane infrastructure

**RULE** · Take early only when the build and campaign can reliably exploit the ceiling.

### ACQUISITION RULE

Guarantee the spells that create the build's next mandatory job; copy rituals, infrastructure, narrow mission tools, and redundancy when real access exists. Use <a href="/chapters/31-spellbook-operations-and-copy-priority/">Chapter 31</a> for the legal copying procedure, <a href="/chapters/13-prepared-spell-packages/">Chapter 13</a> for preparation packages, and the exact active D&amp;D Beyond entry for every source/version decision.
