These rules prevent a legal option from becoming an accidental hybrid. The exact entry selected on the campaign character sheet controls; similar names from another source do not donate text, scaling, or prerequisites. Builder selectability answers 'may I choose it?'; the selected version answers 'which exact rules am I using?' Both checks are required for reproducible builds.

<!-- source:B00802 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00802" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Precedence</th><th scope="col">Question</th><th scope="col">Action</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1 · Character sheet</td><td>What exact class, subclass, feat, spell, or item entry is selected?</td><td>Treat that displayed entry as the operational rule.</td></tr><tr><td>2 · Campaign sources</td><td>Which source categories are enabled for this character?</td><td>Record Core, Expanded, Partnered, Legacy/Noncore, Drops, or Homebrew.</td></tr><tr><td>3 · Source page</td><td>Does the sheet abbreviate or link to longer text?</td><td>Verify the owned D&amp;D Beyond source before play.</td></tr><tr><td>4 · Table ruling</td><td>Is an interaction still ambiguous?</td><td>Record the DM ruling, date, scope, and whether it is provisional.</td></tr><tr><td>5 · Compendium advice</td><td>Does optimization advice conflict with the selected text?</td><td>The selected rule wins; update the advice.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
