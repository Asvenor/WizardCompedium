## Starting equipment and day-one purchase plan

<!-- source:B00623 -->
<aside class="callout callout--note"><p>Infrastructure-first 150 gp purchase plan using 2024 equipment prices. It deliberately starts in Hide Armor so the rare tool kits, ranged fallback, field package, and reserve all exist on day one. Verify shop availability and the active D&amp;D Beyond class entry.</p></aside>

<aside class="callout" data-source-locator="B00626"><strong>UNKNOWN-ADVENTURE DEFAULT</strong><p>Carry mundane light, rope, containers, food/water, writing materials, backup focus or pouch, protected spellbook access, emergency healing, and one exit method. Prepare immediate defense, primary control, self-extraction, party rescue, information, and one non-concentration follow-up before adding specialized damage.</p></aside>

<!-- source:B00629 -->
<aside class="callout callout--note"><p>WIZARD-TRANSITION PURCHASE. Tinker’s Tools can serve the Artificer interface described by the active class entry, but they do not automatically replace the Wizard's arcane focus or component pouch. At character level 2 / Wizard 1, acquire a legal Wizard casting focus or component pouch and rehearse the shield-equipment/free-hand configuration. This purchase is separate from the Artificer-level 150 gp plan unless the active starting-equipment package already supplies it.</p></aside>

## Preparation portfolio gate

<!-- source:B00631 -->
<div class="table-wrap" data-source-locator="B00631" tabindex="0"><table><thead><tr><th scope="col">Before adding a specialist spell</th><th scope="col">Minimum answer already present</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Survival</td><td>One immediate defense plus positioning that reduces repeated attacks.</td></tr><tr><td>Control</td><td>One primary concentration plan and one answer that uses a different defense, geometry, or no save.</td></tr><tr><td>Movement</td><td>Self-extraction; party rescue when the tier and campaign allow it.</td></tr><tr><td>Action economy</td><td>A useful non-concentration action after the winning control spell is active.</td></tr><tr><td>Information</td><td>Ritual/scouting access that prevents avoidable slot expenditure and ambushes.</td></tr><tr><td>Mission</td><td>At least one prepared or accessible answer to the adventure's known failure condition.</td></tr></tbody></table></div>
